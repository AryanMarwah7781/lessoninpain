# Lubricate Driveshaft U-Joints—Every 100 Hours

IMPORTANT: Avoid damage to the needle bearing. Do not use grease containing more that 1% molybdenum disulfide. Use TY6341 Multi-Purpose SD Polyurea Grease or other greases that meet the following:
- John Deere specification JDM J13E4
- NLGI Performance Classification GC-LB
- NLGI No. 2 standard specification
