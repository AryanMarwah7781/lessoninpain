# Nozzle Turret | Manual Setup

Manual Setup configures the ExactApply system to your needs. Spray mode, valve pulsing mode, nozzle flow, turret position, and installed spray tips are adjustable on this page.
Modify When:
- The installed spray tips are changed.
- Changing the spray mode.
- Changing the valve pulsing mode.
- Toggling See & Spray functionality on or off.
