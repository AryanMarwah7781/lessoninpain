# Check Shim Gap on Axle Tread Adjust—250 Hours

N100281—UN—25SEP12

![[N100281.png]]
**Figure N100281** — A: Gap; B: Axle Knee; C: Lower Shim Pad

Measure gap (A) between lower machined surface of the axle knee (B) and upper surface of the lower shim pad (C). If the gap exceeds 1.5 mm (0.060 in) at the outside end of pad, adjust shim gap. (See Adjust Tread Adjust Shims—Tire Off Ground [Preferred Method] in this section.)
N101663—UN—28JAN13

![[N101663.png]]
**Figure N101663** — A: Gap

Measure gap (A) between the axle knee and side of the axle frame. Gap must be 3 mm (0.118 in) minimum on each side of axle knee. If the gap is less than required, plastic wear pads must be replaced.
