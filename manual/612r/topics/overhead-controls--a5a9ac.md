# Overhead Controls

NX638079—UN—25JUL24

![[NX638079.png]]
**Figure NX638079**

Rear Cab Light Switch (if equipped)
Rear Cab Light Switch (if equipped)—turns ON/OFF the lights on the rear side of the cab.
- Press top of the switch to turn ON rear cab lights.
- Press bottom of the switch to turn OFF rear cab lights.
NOTE: Key switch must be in the run position.
N151225—UN—27MAY20

![[N151225.png]]
**Figure N151225**

Mirror Heater Button (if equipped)
Mirror Heater Button (if equipped)—activates mirror heaters to remove ice and fog.
- Press top of the switch to activate mirror heat.
- Press bottom of the switch to deactivate mirror heat.
NOTE: Key switch must be in the run position.
N151224—UN—27MAY20

![[N151224.png]]
**Figure N151224**

Mirror Control Buttons (if equipped)
Mirror Control Buttons (if equipped)—allow you to select and adjust either the right-hand or left-hand cab mirrors.
- Select mirror to adjust using the top switch.
- Selected mirror can be moved up, down, left, or right using the bottom control. Move the control in the direction you desire the mirror to move.
NOTE: Key switch must be in the run position.

N151227—UN—27MAY20

![[N151227.png]]
**Figure N151227**

Dome Light
Dome Light—allows you to illuminate the interior of the cab.
- Switch—leave the switch in the center position (door) to set the dome light to illuminate when the door is opened. Press the switch toward the left (off) to set the dome light to remain off at all times. Press the switch to the right (on) to set the dome light to remain illuminated until switched off.
- Buttons—press the left-hand or right-hand light to toggle that light on or off.
