# Eductor

Symptom | Problem | Solution
Eductor fills with chemical after using.
Eductor rinse valve left on when eductor not in use.
Close eductor flow rate whenever eductor not being used. Close eductor valve after use.
Valves not closed in proper sequence After using eductor, close eductor flow rate valve first then close eductor sump valve.
Solution spills out of eductor when hopper is raised.
Hopper is not completely empty. | Empty hopper before raising eductor.
Eductor rinse nozzle does not rinse.
Nozzle not being depressed. | When inserting jug over rinse nozzle,
depress nozzle to activate flow.
Eductor rinse valve not turned ON. | Turn valve on.
Valve or line plugged. | Clear obstruction from line or valve.
Solution pump not primed. | Prime solution pump.
Symptom | Problem | Solution
No liquid in solution tank. | Add liquid to solution tank.
Engine speed too low. | Increase engine speed to 1800 rpm
minimum. (Manual controls only.)
Eductor hopper rinse does not rinse.
Eductor rinse valve not turned ON. | Turn valve on.
Valve or line plugged. | Clear obstruction from line or valve.
Solution pump not primed. | Prime solution pump.
No liquid in solution tank. | Add liquid to solution tank.
Engine speed too low. | Increase engine speed to 1800 rpm
minimum. (Manual controls only.)
Eductor will not extract chemicals from hopper.
Eductor rinse valve is OFF. | Turn valve to ON position.
Eductor sump valve is closed. | Open valve.
Solution pump not primed. | Prime solution pump.
No liquid in solution tank. | Add liquid to solution tank.
Eductor/Tank rinse valve in wrong position.
Select proper tank with selector valve.
Engine speed too low. | Increase engine speed to 1800 rpm
minimum. (Manual controls only.)
Venturi is plugged. | Clean venturi of obstructions.
Venturi is assembled wrong. | Verify correct assembly of venturi.
Eductor discharge hose is plugged. | Remove obstruction from discharge
hose.
Dry chemical bridged. | Turn on eductor rinse valve.
Diminished eductor performance with solution tank full.
Use eductor before solution tank gets completely full.
Eductor hose in hopper is plugged or missing.
Remove obstruction. Replace hose (if missing).
