# Troubleshooting Nozzles That Drip, Leak, Or Seep After Valve is Shut Off

Symptom | Problem | Solution
Nozzles do not automatically shut off after the boom or master valve is turned off. Nozzles continue to drip or leak out several seconds after shut off.
Air leaks in the boom sections. | Pressurize the boom plumbing to
check for air leaks on the hoses and
fittings. Fix leaks.
Issues in the nozzle bodies. | Inspect nozzle bodies. Remove debris
and replace damaged components.
Solution hose damage. | Verify that hoses are in good
condition. Replace as necessary.
