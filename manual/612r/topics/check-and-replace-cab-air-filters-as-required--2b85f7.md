# Check and Replace Cab Air Filters—As Required

NOTE: Decal is located on left-hand side of the cab on the rear window.
N154906—UN—29MAR21

![[N154906.png]]
**Figure N154906** — A: Decal

Cab air filter MUST be replaced every 500 hours or every year, whichever comes first. Check filters regularly. Replace filters sooner if they become restricted or if the manufacturer recommends. When filters are replaced, record date and engine hours on decal (A).
See Check and Replace Cab Air Filters in Cab and Air Conditioning section for replacement procedure.
