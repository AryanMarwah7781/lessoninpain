# Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually

N153553—UN—24NOV20

![[N153553.png]]
**Figure N153553** — A: Constant Tension Hose Clamp (4 used); B: Band Clamp (4 used); C: T-Bolt Clamp (3 used)

N153554—UN—24NOV20

![[N153554.png]]
**Figure N153554** — A: Constant Tension Hose Clamp (4 used); B: Band Clamp (4 used); C: T-Bolt Clamp (3 used)

Check all air intake system joints to make sure they are not damaged and have tight connections with no air leaks.
Check hose clamps at turbocharger to make sure they are not damaged and have a tight connection.

![[NX1396258.png]]

NX1396259—UN—10MAR25

![[NX1396259.png]]
**Figure NX1396259** — A: Latch; B: Filter Cover

CAUTION: Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank when replacing air filters.
1. Set the tread width to the widest setting to allow space for filter removal.
2. Open latch (A).
3. Open filter cover (B) approximately 45°. Slide the filter cover down to remove.
NX1396261—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396261.png]]
**Figure NX1396261** — A: Primary Filter; B: Lip

NX1396264—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396264.png]]
**Figure NX1396264** — A: Primary Filter; B: Lip

4. Slide primary filter (A) forward away from lip (B) of the filter housing.
5. Remove the primary filter.
IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to clean filters.
Replace secondary filter (B) every second primary filter change.
Install new secondary filter immediately to prevent dust from entering the air intake system.
N153570—UN—27JAN21

![[N153570.png]]
**Figure N153570** — A: Primary Filter; B: Secondary Filter

6. Replace primary filter (A) and secondary filter.
7. Clean dirt from inside the cover and canister.
NX1396269—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396269.png]]
**Figure NX1396269** — A: Filter Cover; B: Clip

8. With filter cover (A) at a 45° angle, slide the filter cover onto the filter housing.
9. Close the filter cover, verifying that clip (B) of the filter housing is positioned in the notch of the filter cover as shown.
NX1396280—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396280.png]]

10. Close latch (A), verifying that the latch is centered on rod (B) as shown.
