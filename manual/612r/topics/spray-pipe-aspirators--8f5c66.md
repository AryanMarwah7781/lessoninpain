# Spray Pipe Aspirators

N101652—UN—17JAN13

![[N101652.png]]
**Figure N101652** — A: Spray Pipe Aspirator; B: Spray Pipe; C: Nozzle Body; D: Wide End; E: Narrow End

Spray pipe aspirators (A) are located at each end of the spray pipes (B). Aspirators are designed to allow air trapped at the top of spray pipes to be discharged through the first and last nozzle bodies (C). The aspirators have a wide end (D) and a narrow end (E) forming a funnel to the top side of the spray pipe. This forces solution to the top of the spray pipe, lowering the possibility of air pockets forming in the spray pipes on top of the spray solution.
