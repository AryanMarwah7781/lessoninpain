# Fuse Locations

NX1439377—UN—18JAN26

![[NX1439377.png]]
**Figure NX1439377** — A: Main Power Fuse

The main power fuses (A) are located inside the battery box as shown.
Item | Measurement | Specification

NX1435664—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435664.png]]
**Figure NX1435664** — A: Fuse Box

For machines with a single solution system, nozzle body and control unit fuses and relays are located on the top right-hand side of the center frame in fuse box (A).
NX1439388—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1439388.png]]
**Figure NX1439388** — A: Fuse Box

For machines with dual solution systems, nozzle body and control unit fuses and relays are located on the left-hand side of the center frame in fuse box (A).
