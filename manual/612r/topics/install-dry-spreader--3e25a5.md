# Install Dry Spreader

CAUTION: Crane or hoist must have a rated lifting capacity of at least 2268 kg (5000 lb). Verify that any additional lifting or connecting devices, such as anchor shackles or clevises, are properly rated for the job.
Faulty strap lift points or lift straps can allow the dry spreader to fall, causing severe injury or death to you or others. Inspect the strap lifting points and lift straps for wear before attaching and lifting.
Approximate weights of the dry spreaders are as follows:
- 1650 kg (3640 lb) for the DS20
- 431 kg (950 lb) for the DS20 second product bin
- 1878 kg (4140 lb) for the DS30
- 508 kg (1120 lb) for the DS30 second product bin
- 2812 kg (6200 lb) for the DS30 NL7
The DS30 NL7 is a rear-heavy drybox application system. During installation, verify that all front hardware is installed before removing the spreader bar hoist and chains.
1. Position the chassis with adequate room around the unit. Make sure that the endgate or second product bin insert is properly installed.
IMPORTANT: Lifting tools are available as part of the conversion bundle. Lifting tools can be obtained via regular special tool order channels

18. Connect 9-pin and 23-pin connectors (A and B) to the machine.
19. Connect CAN harness (C) to machine harness (D).
20. Install the previously removed CAN termination resistor (E) to spreader CAN harness (F).
21. Retain the CAN termination resistor (as shown) and any loose harness with tie bands to prevent damage.
IMPORTANT: If a threaded connection is tightened too tightly, the fitting or housing into which the fitting is placed could be distorted and an unstoppable leak could occur.
See Hose Installation Guide in the DS20 and DS30 Dry Spinner Spreader Operator’s Manual for proper hose assembly.
NOTE: Use a thread sealer on all fittings, except O-ring adapters, JIC adapters, O-ring valves, and motors. When using a thread sealer, do not put it on the first three threads of the fitting. Too much on the fitting or on the first three threads will force it into the oil stream, where it could damage the system.
See Metric Face Seal and O-Ring Stud End Fitting Torque Chart in the Specifications section of this manual for proper fitting torque values.
