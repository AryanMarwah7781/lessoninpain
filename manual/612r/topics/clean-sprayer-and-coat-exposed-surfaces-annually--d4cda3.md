# Clean Sprayer and Coat Exposed Surfaces —Annually

Clean the sprayer annually. (See Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides in the Storage section.)
Treat exposed surfaces with Fluid Film TY22032 or TY27529.
