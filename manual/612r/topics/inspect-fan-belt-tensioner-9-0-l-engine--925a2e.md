# Inspect Fan Belt Tensioner—9.0 L Engine

1. Raise hood.
N153304—UN—08MAR21

![[N153304.png]]
**Figure N153304** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Left-Hand Engine Side Shield

2. Remove cap screws (A) and left-hand engine side shield (B).
N135793—UN—26FEB18

![[N135793.png]]
**Figure N135793** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Fan Shield

3. Remove cap screws (A) and fan shield (B).
N103030—UN—10APR13

![[N103030.png]]
**Figure N103030** — A: Tensioner; B: Belt

4. Install 13 mm (1/2 in) breaker bar in tensioner (A).
5. Rotate tensioner clockwise to release belt tension.
6. Remove belt (B) from pulleys and carefully release the tensioner.
N103031—UN—10APR13

![[N103031.png]]
**Figure N103031** — A: Mark on Tension Arm; B: Mark on Mounting Bracket

N103032—UN—10APR13

![[N103032.png]]
**Figure N103032** — A: Mark on Tension Arm; B: Mark on Mounting Bracket

Markings Aligned
7. Place mark on the tension arm at (A).
8. Measure 21 mm (13/16 in) from mark (A) and put a mark (B) on mounting bracket.
9. Rotate tension arm with torque wrench until marks (A and B) align. If torque wrench measurement is not within specification, replace tensioner mechanism. See regular spare part channels to order.
Specification
Tension Arm—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 18—23 N·m (159—204 lb·in)
10. Install belt and side shield.
11. Lower hood.
