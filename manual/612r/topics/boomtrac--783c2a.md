# BoomTrac

The BoomTrac system automatically controls boom tilt functions and center frame control relative to contours of the ground or crop canopy.
Modify When:
- Crop height changes.
- Terrain contour changes.
- Boom lower/raise response is too slow or too fast.
- Spraying in dusty or muddy conditions.
