# Boom Air Purge Procedure

Procedure Requirements:
Solution Pump | OFF
Remote Functions | Inactive
Booms | Unfolded
Reclaim | Inactive
Valve | No critical faults

Boom Pressure
Boom Pressure— displays the current pressure in the boom.
Boom Air Purge Procedure:
NOTE: Procedure pauses as soon as air pressure drops below a certain threshold. The procedure can be paused or canceled at any time.
N118434—UN—22JAN20

![[N118434.png]]
**Figure N118434**

Checkboxes
1. Select the Checkboxes under the boom sections you want to perform the boom air purge procedure. Deselect the Checkbox under any boom section you don't want to purge.
N120251—UN—04JAN17

![[N120251.png]]
**Figure N120251**

Spray System Master
2. Press the Spray System Master switch to turn on.
NOTE: Boom section purging advances automatically. Pressing the spray system master switch to OFF position pauses the procedure.
N120245—UN—23FEB16

![[N120245.png]]
**Figure N120245**

OK
3. Select OK when the procedure is complete to close the application.
N118394—UN—17FEB16

![[N118394.png]]
**Figure N118394**

Cancel
4. Select Cancel at any time to end the procedure.
If Calibration Fails:
N118122—UN—02MAY16

![[N118122.png]]
**Figure N118122**

Retry
1. Select the Retry button.
2. Verify that all requirements listed onscreen have been met.
3. If the calibration fails twice, see your John Deere dealer or service provider.
