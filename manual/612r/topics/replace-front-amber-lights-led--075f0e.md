# Replace Front (Amber) Lights (LED)

NOTE: Right-hand side is shown. The left-hand side is similar.

**Figure N153295** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Harness Connector; C: LED Light Assembly

N153294—UN—03MAR21

![[N153294.png]]
**Figure N153294** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Harness Connector; C: LED Light Assembly

1. Disconnect harness connector (B).
2. Remove and retain cap screws (A).
3. Remove the LED light assembly (C).
4. Replace the light assembly and install in reverse order of removal.
