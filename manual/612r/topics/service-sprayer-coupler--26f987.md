# Service Sprayer Coupler

TS206—UN—15APR13
CAUTION: Operator must wear appropriate personal protection equipment when servicing sprayer coupler.
CAUTION: Servicing can result in solution loss. Run evacuation and rinse procedures to prevent solution loss. Collect remaining solution.
CAUTION: Coupler cylinder is spring-loaded with compression spring and could cause serious injury to you or others.
1. Place container under coupler to collect any remaining fluid in system.
2. Evacuate and rinse Load Command system before servicing sprayer coupler. Collect remaining solution.
3. Extend poppet for service. (See Load Command Poppet Maintenance Procedure in Calibrations section.)
N108286—UN—05NOV13

![[N108286.png]]
**Figure N108286** — A: Closed Valve

4. Close shutoff valve (A) by turning knob clockwise so knob is tight.
N107970—UN—05NOV13

![[N107970.png]]
**Figure N107970** — A: Air Seal; B: Proximity Sensor (2 used); C: Poppet

Poppet shown closed.
IMPORTANT: Clean air seal (A) using mild detergent and damp cloth. Clean proximity sensors (B) using damp cloth. Do NOT pressure wash.
NOTE: Step 5 applies to only basic maintenance of coupler. When finished, proceed to Step 15.
5. Perform maintenance on the air seal, proximity sensors, and coupler poppet (C) as needed.
NOTE: Steps 6-14 apply to only full sprayer coupler service.
NOTE: Poppet retracts if machine shuts off before closing shutoff valve.
6. Return to cab and shut the engine off.
N83154—UN—02FEB09

![[N83154.png]]
**Figure N83154** — A: Poppet; B: O-Ring (2 used); C: Air Seal Retainer Plate; D: Air Seal; E: M35 Flat Head Screw (6 used)

IMPORTANT: Poppet (A) should stay extended and not drift in. If drifting occurs, tighten or service shutoff valve as necessary.
7. Remove and clean the poppet by removing cotter pin.
8. Remove and clean air seal retainer plate (C) by removing screws.
9. Slide air seal (D) off coupler and clean coupler.
10. Clean air seal using mild detergent and inspect for damage.
11. Replace O-rings (B) if necessary.
12. Reinstall clean air seal, or use new one if needed.
IMPORTANT: Use anti-seize on threads. Coupler threads are made of aluminum.
13. Reinstall retainer plate using flat head screws (E). Tighten to specification.
Specification
Retainer Plate Flat Head Screws—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 10 N·m +/- 2 (7.4 lb·ft) +/- 2
14. Install poppet on end of cylinder rod using cotter pin.
IMPORTANT: Shutoff valve must be completely opened for Load Command to function.
N108286—UN—05NOV13
15. Open shutoff valve (A) by turning counterclockwise until fully open and knob is tight.
NOTE: Step 16 applies to only coupler poppet maintenance. Machine should still be on.
16. Close poppet valve. (See Load Command Poppet Maintenance in CommandCenter Display Applications section.)
