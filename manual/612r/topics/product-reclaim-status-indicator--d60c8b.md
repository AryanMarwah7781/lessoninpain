# Product Reclaim | Status Indicator

Status Indicator displays the status of Product Reclaim procedure and a message about that status.
Available Status Indicators:
N118019—UN—22OCT15

![[N118019.png]]
**Figure N118019**

Grey
Product Reclaim Ready (grey)— turn Spray System Master switch on to start.
N118021—UN—13JAN17

![[N118021.png]]
**Figure N118021**

Blue
Product Reclaim in Progress (blue)— turn Spray System Master switch off to stop.
N118020—UN—22OCT15

![[N118020.png]]
**Figure N118020**

Amber
Product Reclaim Paused (amber)— turn Spray System Master switch on to resume.
N118022—UN—13JAN17

![[N118022.png]]
**Figure N118022**

Red
Product Reclaim Paused (red)— an error has occurred causing the procedure to be paused. A message about the error is display. To resume the procedure, correct the error and press the Spray System Master switch.
