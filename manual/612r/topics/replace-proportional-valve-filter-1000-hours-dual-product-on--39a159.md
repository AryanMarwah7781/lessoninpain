# Replace Proportional Valve Filter—1000 Hours (Dual Product Only)

NX699728—UN—10MAR26

![[NX699728.png]]
**Figure NX699728** — A: Proportional Valve Filter

Proportional Valve Filter
1. Locate the proportional valve filter (A) at the rear of the machine (under the rinse tank).
2. Place a drip pan or absorbent cloth below the filter.
3. Remove the filter by turning it counterclockwise.
4. Lubricate the new filter’s packing with John Deere Torq Gard Supreme 10W 30 or equivalent.
5. Install the new filter and hand-tighten the filter elements.
6. Check the oil level and add as required.
7. Operate the engine at a high idle.
8. Verify that the machine drive system operates properly. Move the machine in forward and reverse directions.
9. Check for leaks.
10. Check hydraulic oil level and add as required. Install reservoir cap.
