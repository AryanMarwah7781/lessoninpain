# Inspect and Adjust (As Required) T3 Link, Shims, and T6/T7 Rope

Inspect at the following intervals:
- First 50 hours
- First 100 hours
- Every 250 hours
Adjust the T3 link, shims, and T6/T7 rope using the following sequence.
NOTE: Procedure for the left-hand boom is shown. The right-hand boom is similar.
For carbon fiber boom terminology, see Carbon Fiber Boom Terminology in this section.
The inner and outer wing sections are designed to have a slight convex bow.
1. Unfold the boom completely and observe the alignment of the entire boom section.
2. Adjust the T3 Link using the following steps:

Main Fold Stops
3. Adjust the shims (if necessary):
a. Unfold the booms and loosen fold stops (A).
NOTE: A jack can also be positioned to support the boom from settling.
b. Fully fold the outer and breakaway wings to eliminate the possibility of the hydraulic cylinder leaking down while working with the shims.
c. Add or remove shims (B) as needed to vertically level the outer boom section to the inner boom section.
IMPORTANT: Verify that the fold stops are properly aligned before tightening. Unevenly aligned bumpers can lead to uneven load distribution, resulting in failure of the fold stop hardware. Trimming the shims for each fold stop individually may be necessary to achieve optimal performance.
d. Tighten the stops to specification.
Specification
Fold Stop—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 61 N·m (45 lb·ft)
NOTE: The breakaway wing can be aligned slightly downward before adjusting the T6/T7 rope.
e. Unfold the boom completely.

Center Point Deflection
4. Adjust T6/T7 Rope (if necessary):
a. Measure the length of rope (A) on the outer boom section and mark center point (B).
b. Position a tape measure on top of the boom at the center point to gauge deflection (D).
c. Use a scale (C) as shown to deflect the center point of the rope. Measure the force to deflect the rope 25 mm (1 in). See specification.
Specification
T6/T7 Rope Deflection Force—Force. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 11—15 kg (24—33 lb)
d. Use the following procedure to adjust the deflection force:
- Raise the outer wing to loosen the T6/T7 rope.
- Shorten or lengthen the rope using lock nuts (E).
- Lower the outer wing and recheck the rope tension using the same procedure.
