# Planetary Hub Oil

N64582—UN—18NOV13

![[N64582.png]]
**Figure N64582**

All hubs are filled with 85W140 Gear Lube from the factory. If oil needs to be added before the 50-hour initial oil change, use 85W140.
Use an oil viscosity based on the expected air temperature range during the period between oil changes.
The following oil is preferred for all 408R, hubs:
- John Deere GL5 85W140 Gear Lube
The following oil is preferred for all 400R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R, and 616R hubs:
- John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic Gear Lube
Hub Capacities
- 400R—1.5 L (1.6 qt)
- 408R—1.2 L (1.3 qt)
- 410R—1.5 L (1.6 qt)
- 412R—1.5 L (1.6 qt)
- 600R—2.5 L (2.6 qt)
- 612R—2.5 L (2.6 qt)
- 616R—2.5 L (2.6 qt)
