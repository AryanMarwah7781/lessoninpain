# Broadcast Pulsing Nozzle Flow

Broadcast Pulsing Nozzle Flow setting allows you to input the desired percentage of flow desired from the active tip that is broadcast spraying. This amount of flow is constantly maintained during application. Nozzle flow is represented as a percentage of the maximum amount of flow possible through the selected tip size.
Modify When:
- Performing band spraying operations.
- Desiring a large spray tip to function like a smaller spray tip.
Example: A 08 spray tip operated at a fixed 50% flow now operates as a 04 spray tip.
Procedure to Modify:
