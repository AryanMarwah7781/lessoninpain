# Replace Hydraulic Reservoir Fill Cap—1000 Hours

IMPORTANT: Fill cap contains hydraulic reservoir breather filter and mustbe replaced when hydraulic oil is changed.
N153555—UN—24NOV20

![[N153555.png]]
**Figure N153555** — A: Fill Cap

1. Remove hydraulic reservoir fill cap (A). Compress tether chain ring to remove from neck flange.
2. Wipe off dirt and oil from top of fill neck with clean rag.
3. Compress new tether ring so it fits in neck, and place horizontally under neck flange.
4. Insert chain in fill neck.
5. Install cap.
