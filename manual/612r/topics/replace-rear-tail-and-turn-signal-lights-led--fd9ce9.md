# Replace Rear Tail and Turn Signal Lights (LED)

NOTE: Right-hand side is shown. The left-hand side is similar.

N153296—UN—03MAR21

![[N153296.png]]
**Figure N153296** — A: Harness Connector (2 used); B: Cap Screw (2 used); C: LED Light Assembly

1. Disconnect harness connectors (A).
2. Remove and retain cap screws (B).
3. Remove LED light assembly (C).
4. Replace light assembly and install in reverse order of removal.
