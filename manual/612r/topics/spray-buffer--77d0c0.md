# Spray Buffer

Spray Buffer allows you to select the approximate width and length of additional spray area around the targeted plant.
NOTE: Small settings may provide the greatest amount of chemical savings, while large settings may provide the least amount of chemical savings.
Items Accessible on Spray Buffer Area Page:
WZ1408480—UN—06JUN25

![[WZ1408480.png]]
**Figure WZ1408480**

Width
Width— is the side-to-side distance of additional coverage.
NOTE: When a See & Spray target is detected, a minimum of two nozzles are activated. More nozzles may be activated depending on selected spray tip, boom height, or if the target is located between two or more nozzles.
WZ1408481—UN—06JUN25

![[WZ1408481.png]]
**Figure WZ1408481**

Length
Length— is the front-to-back distance of additional coverage.
NOTE: Large setting is recommended when operating at max boom height. The spray length may require adjustment if the machine speed or wind speed change.
