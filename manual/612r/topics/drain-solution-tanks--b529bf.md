# Drain Solution Tanks

CAUTION: The solution tanks can contain hazardous chemicals that can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Drain the solution tanks in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated. Do not drain solution onto the ground. Drain solution into a suitable container and dispose of properly.

NOTE: The drain ports are located on the bottom side of the machine underneath the solution tank. Underframe shields must be lowered to access the drain ports.
1. Open the lock levers and remove the cap from the drain port (A or B) for the tank to be drained.
2. Place a suitable container below the drain port or connect a nurse tank hose to the port.
3. Press menu button (C or D) on the keypad for the tank system being drained.

4. Press drain tank button (A) on the micro display.
5. Verify that fluid drains from the tank and into the designated container. Monitor the fluid level on the micro display. When the desired fluid level is reached, press cancel button (B) to close the drain valve.
6. Install the cap on the drain port and close the lock levers.
