# Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold

Using Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold allows the boom sections on both sides to fold or unfold sequentially, stopping after each section is folded/unfolded. You must release the button(s) after a section is folded/unfolded and press the button(s) again to continue the procedure.
The sequential boom fold/unfold procedure is the same as the One Side Fold/Unfold procedure, except both boom tilt switches are pressed for this procedure rather than only one.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the folding and unfolding area can result in injury or death.
IMPORTANT: Avoid damage; only unfold the booms where there is enough space.
NOTE: To unfold or fold the booms, the machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the Transport Lock must be disengaged.
