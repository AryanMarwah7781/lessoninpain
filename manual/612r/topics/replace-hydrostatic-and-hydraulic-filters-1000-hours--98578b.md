# Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours

N155093—UN—30MAR21

![[N155093.png]]
**Figure N155093** — A: Hydrostatic Filter; B: Hydraulic Filter (2 used)

1. Remove hydrostatic filter (A) and hydraulic filters (B).
2. Lubricate the new filter packing with John Deere Torq-Gard Supreme 10W-30 or equivalent.
3. Install and hand-tighten the filter elements.
4. Check the oil level and add as required. (See Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily in this section.) Do not install the reservoir cap at this time.

5. Install a 6894 kPa (68.9 bar) (1000 psi) pressure gauge to the charge pressure receptacle (A).
6. Disconnect fuel injector harness (B) on the cylinder head.
NOTE: The following steps could require two people.
7. Turn the ignition key for 15 seconds at 1-minute intervals. Monitor the charge pressure while starting the engine. Repeat until the charge pressure consistently reaches the specified pressure.
Specification
Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 207—345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi)
8. Turn the ignition key for three additional 15-second cycles.
9. Check the oil level and add as required.
10. Reconnect the fuel injector harness.
CAUTION: Avoid injury or death to others. Keep away all bystanders while performing the start-up procedure. Machine could move unexpectedly.
11. Start the engine and run at a low idle for 5 minutes.
12. Check the charge pressure and verify that it is within specification.
Specification
Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 2344—2827 kPa (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi)
13. Check the oil level and add as required.
14. Operate the engine at a high idle.
15. Verify that the machine drive system operates properly. Move the machine in forward and reverse directions.
16. Check for leaks.
17. Check the oil level and add as required. Install the reservoir cap.
