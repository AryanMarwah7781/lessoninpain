# Boom Calibration | See & Spray Camera Calibration | Procedure

Procedure Requirements:
Engine State | High idle, in park
Boom State | Unfolded
Wheel Speed | 0 km/h (0 mph)
Operating State | Not applying product
Camera State | One or more in communication;
oriented correctly
Lighting Conditions | Consistent
BoomTrac State | Ready
Ground Conditions | Firm, dry, and level
Procedure Overview:
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.
IMPORTANT: Booms can contact and may damage the exhaust stack and/or walkway handrail when using manual mode to fold and unfold booms. Do not allow booms to go higher than the top of the cradle support when operating boom in manual mode.
IMPORTANT: This procedure requires fully unfolding the boom. Ensure that adequate space is available.
