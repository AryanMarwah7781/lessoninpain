# Replace Crankshaft Vibration Damper— 5000 Hours or Five Years

IMPORTANT: The crankshaft vibration damper assembly is not repairable and should be replaced every 5 years or 5000 hours, whichever occurs first.
Have a John Deere dealer or other service provider replace the crankshaft vibration damper.
