# One Side Fold/Unfold

Using One Side Fold/Unfold allows you fold or unfold one boom at a time. The booms are designed so that only one boom can be unfolded/folded if desired.
The procedure for folding/unfolding one side is the same as the Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold procedure, except only one of the two boom tilt switches are pressed for this procedure rather than both. You must release the button(s) after a section is folded/unfolded and press the button(s) again to continue the procedure.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the folding and unfolding area can result in injury or death.
IMPORTANT: Avoid damage; only unfold the boom where there is enough space.
NOTE: To unfold or fold the boom, the machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the Transport Lock must be disengaged.
