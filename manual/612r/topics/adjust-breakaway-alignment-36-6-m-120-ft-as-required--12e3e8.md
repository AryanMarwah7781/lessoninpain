# Adjust Breakaway Alignment—36.6 m (120 ft) (As Required)

Fold Linkage Alignment
NOTE: Procedure for the left-hand boom is shown. The right-hand boom is similar.
1. Unfold the boom and lower to a comfortable working level.
2. When necessary, clean the machine of pesticides.
(See Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides in the Safety section.)
N126675—UN—18OCT16

![[N126675.png]]
**Figure N126675** — A: Centerline of Outer Boom Section; B: Fold Linkage; C: 90°

3. Visually inspect the alignment of parts (A and B). With the fold cylinder charged to normal operating pressure, the centerline of the outer boom (A) must be 90° (C) from fold linkage (B).
N153415—UN—13NOV20

![[N153415.png]]
**Figure N153415** — A: Fold Stop (2 Used); B: Shim

4. Loosen fold stops (A) and add or remove shims (B) as needed to achieve proper alignment.
IMPORTANT: Verify that the fold stops are properly aligned before tightening. Unevenly aligned bumpers can lead to uneven load distribution, resulting in failure of the fold stop hardware. Trimming the shims for each fold stop individually may be necessary to achieve optimal performance.
5. Tighten the fold stops to specification.
Specification
Fold Stops—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 25 N·m (18.5 lb·ft)
Breakaway Locking Pin Alignment
N153416—UN—13NOV20

![[N153416.png]]
**Figure N153416** — A: Hole in Lower Bracket; B: Hole in Curved Bracket; C: Pin

N126212—UN—17OCT16

![[N126212.png]]

1. Visually inspect the alignment of parts (A—C). With the breakaway tension spring properly adjusted, pin (C) must be free to push through the hole in the curved bracket (B) and fully seat in the hole in the lower bracket (A).

![[N136018.png]]

N155459—UN—05MAY21

NOTE: If visual inspection is not possible, remove parts (A—D) and manually press the pin. If removed, tighten the plunger (A) to specification.
Specification
Solenoid Plunger—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 62 N·m (46 lb·ft)
2. Adjust fold stop (E) as needed to achieve proper operation.
3. Tighten the lock nut on the fold stop when finished.
Breakaway Alignment
N136445—UN—19MAR18

![[N136445.png]]
**Figure N136445** — A: Outer Boom; B: Breakaway; C: Fore/Aft Travel; D: Fold Stop; E: Fold Stop

N153418—UN—13NOV20

![[N153418.png]]
**Figure N153418** — A: Outer Boom; B: Breakaway; C: Fore/Aft Travel; D: Fold Stop; E: Fold Stop

1. Visually inspect the alignment of parts (A and B). With the breakaway tension spring properly adjusted, the centerline of the breakaway is aligned to the centerline of the outer boom.
2. Adjust fold stop (D) as needed to achieve proper alignment.
3. Adjust fold stop (E) until initial contact with the breakaway is made.
4. Tighten the lock nuts on the fold stops.
