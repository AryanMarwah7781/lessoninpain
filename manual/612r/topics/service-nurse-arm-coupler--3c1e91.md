# Service Nurse Arm Coupler

Service the nurse arm coupler as needed and to remove debris.
CAUTION: Solution could remain in the nurse arm coupler. Wear protective equipment to ensure that excess solution does not come into contact with you or others.
IMPORTANT: Rinse the nurse arm plumbing before servicing.
Valve between the nurse tank and arm must be closed before servicing.
N137842—UN—08JUN18

![[N137842.png]]
**Figure N137842** — A: O-ring (2 Used)

1. Drain nurse arm plumbing before servicing nurse arm coupler.
2. Disassemble nurse arm coupler.
3. Replace O-rings (A) as needed.
4. Reassemble nurse arm coupler.
