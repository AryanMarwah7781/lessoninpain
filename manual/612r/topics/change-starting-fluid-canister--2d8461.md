# Change Starting Fluid Canister

CAUTION: Do not use starting fluid near fire, sparks, or flames. Read caution information on the container. Protect container against damage. Do not carry starting fluid canisters inside cab.
RXA0167141—UN—20MAR19

![[RXA0167141.png]]
**Figure RXA0167141**

Starting Fluid Canister
1. Open hood to access the canister.
2. Remove safety cap and plastic spray nozzle from new canister.
IMPORTANT: To avoid drawing dust into engine, always keep a starting fluid can in position, or clean the bottom of canister and install it bottom side up.
3. Loosen canister and remove old can.
4. Install new can and tighten the canister.
