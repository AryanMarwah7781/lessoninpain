# Park the Machine

N166477—UN—08MAR24

![[N166477.png]]
**Figure N166477** — A: Multi-Function Lever; B: Low Idle Switch

1. To slow and stop the machine, slowly return the multi-function lever (A) to the PARK position. Press down on the brake pedal if a quicker stop is necessary.
NOTE: Secondary brake switch is not to be engaged when parking the machine. Park brakes are automatically applied when the multi-function lever is in the PARK position.
Park brake switch may be engaged to prevent the machine from moving if the multi-function lever is bumped. Park brake switch must be disengaged before moving the machine.
2. Press low idle switch (B) to reduce engine speed to low idle.
3. Turn off all lights and accessories.
IMPORTANT: Prevent engine damage. Before stopping an engine that has been operating at working load, idle the engine for at least 2 minutes to cool the hot engine parts.
On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is OFF. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF), immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.
4. Turn the key switch to the OFF position and remove the key.
5. Lower the ladder.
