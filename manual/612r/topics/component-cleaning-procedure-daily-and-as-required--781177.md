# Component Cleaning Procedure—Daily and as Required

T6642EJ—UN—18OCT88

![[T6642EJ.png]]
**Figure T6642EJ**

IMPORTANT: Avoid damage to the cameras, wiring harnesses, and electrical components. Use low pressure when using a pressure washer and avoid spraying directly at the harness connectors, cameras, and other electrical components.
Camera Lenses
NX1436539—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1436539.png]]
**Figure NX1436539** — A: Camera Lens

IMPORTANT: Avoid damage to the camera lenses (A). Do not use chemical cleaners, high-pressure water, or high-pressure air when cleaning the lenses.
Clean lenses daily or as required using a spray bottle with water, a damp cloth with light pressure, or low-pressure air. A mild detergent may be used if there is grease on the lens.
Vision Processing Unit
NX1436515—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1436515.png]]
**Figure NX1436515** — A: Vision Processing Unit

Ensure that all vision processing units (VPU) (A) are clean. Remove any dry grass or other easily combustible material. Make sure all connectors are secure and that caps are present on all unused ports.
