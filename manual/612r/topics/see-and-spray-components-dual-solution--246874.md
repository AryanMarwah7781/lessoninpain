- Cameras (A) are located across the booms and near the front of the machine. Number of cameras and locations are dependent on the boom.
- Each camera is connected to a vision processing unit (VPU) (B). Vision processing units are located on the center frame.
- Each VPU is connected to Ethernet switch (C). The Ethernet switch is located on the center frame.
- Fuse boxes (D, E, and F) are located on the left-hand side of the center frame.
- A domain control unit (G) is located on the left-hand side of the center frame.
- A vision extender (VEX) (H) is located in two locations on each inner wing and behind the left-hand storage box.
