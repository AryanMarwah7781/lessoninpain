# Spreader | Spinner Calibration

This procedure calibrates the spinner to ensure that the speed reported is accurate. Accurate spinner speed ensures that the desired spread pattern and width is achieved.
Perform Calibration When:
- Spinner drive motors are replaced or repaired.
- Spinner control valve is replaced.
- Hydraulic pump is replaced on the machine.
- Hydraulic valves are replaced on the spinner.
