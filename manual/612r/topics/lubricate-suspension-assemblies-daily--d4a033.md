![[NX1432685.png]]
**Figure NX1432685** — A: Grease Bank; B: Grease Fitting (4 used)

600R, 612R, and 616R Grease Fitting Locations
Lubricate the front and rear suspension assemblies with ONLY John Deere SD polyurea grease. Use five pumps of grease at each fitting (A).
IMPORTANT: Use of other greases in the suspension system is not recommended. Use of other greases can result in premature wear of the suspension components.
NOTE: Four grease fittings are at one location on each wheel for 400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R models.
