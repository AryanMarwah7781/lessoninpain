# Operate Front Fill Air Purge

Machines with a Manual Air Purge Valve
CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals. Exposure to hazardous materials could cause injury and be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440202—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440202.png]]
**Figure NX1440202** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Air Purge Valve

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that can poison, causing serious injury or death. Before purging, make sure that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open air purge valve (B) and wait ten seconds.
3. Close the front fill air purge valve.
Machines without a Maunual Air Purge Valve
CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals. Exposure to hazardous materials could cause injury and be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440210—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440210.png]]
**Figure NX1440210** — A: Quik-Fill Valve

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that can poison, causing serious injury or death. Before purging, make sure that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
NX1440205—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440205.png]]
**Figure NX1440205** — A: Air Purge Button

2. Press air purge button (A) on the micro display.
NX1440206—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440206.png]]
**Figure NX1440206** — A: Select Button

3. Press select button (A) to begin the air purge procedure.
NX1440209—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440209.png]]
**Figure NX1440209** — A: Cancel Button

4. During the procedure, press cancel button (A) to end and exit the air purge procedure if necessary.
