# Fill Fuel Tank

N155102—UN—21APR21

![[N155102.png]]
**Figure N155102** — A: Fuel Fill Ladder; B: Fuel Tank Fill Cap

CAUTION: Shut off the engine before filling the fuel tank.
Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank.
NOTE: 400R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R, and 616R fuel tank capacity is 566 L (149 gal).
408R fuel tank capacity is 485 L (128 gal).
1. Lower fuel fill ladder (A).
2. Remove fuel tank fill cap (B). Fill the tank with the proper grade of diesel fuel to the correct specification. (See the Lubrication And Maintenance section.)
