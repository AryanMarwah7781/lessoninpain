# Change Engine Oil and Filter—250 Hours (6.8 L Engine)

IMPORTANT: Sulfur content should not exceed 0.10%. Sulfur content of less than 0.10% is preferred. Refer to Fuel, Lubricants, and Coolants section for more information on oil change intervals.
N103034—UN—10APR13

![[N103034.png]]
**Figure N103034** — A: Crankcase Drain Plug

1. Run engine to warm oil. Stop engine. Remove crankcase drain plug (A). Reinstall plug after oil drains out.
N154900—UN—29MAR21

![[N154900.png]]
**Figure N154900** — A: Filter Element

2. Remove filter element (A). Apply a thin film of oil to seal of the replacement filter and install. Hand-tighten filter element. Do not overtighten.
3. Fill crankcase with seasonal viscosity grade oil. (See Diesel Engine Oil—Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)4 5
Specification
Crankcase with Filter—Capacity. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 30 L (31.7 qt)
4. Start engine and check for leaks.
5. Stop engine. Check oil level.
