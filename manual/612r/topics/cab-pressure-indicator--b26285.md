Cab air pressure is indicated in the status screens of machine display (A). If cab pressure is low, the cab pressure icon (B) will appear in the machine status bar.
Air pressure indicator icon assists in identifying air flow through the fresh air filter and potential leaks in cab seals. If the cap pressure icon appears in the status bar while the door and windows are closed and the blower is ON, refer to Troubleshooting section of this manual.
