# Full Boom Breakaway

N136441—UN—19MAR18

![[N136441.png]]
**Figure N136441** — A: 28° Degrees

CAUTION: Serious injury or death to you or others can occur from standing in front of boom when it is returning to its original position. Full boom breakaway system is under hydraulic pressure. If the boom must be lifted or aided over an object or an obstruction, stand behind the boom to avoid injury.
The full boom breakaway system provides boom damage protection to the inner booms when the outer boom section is folded in or the inner section strikes an object. The system allows the boom to breakaway 28° degrees (A).
Once the operator backs away or clears the struck object, the boom returns to its original position.
