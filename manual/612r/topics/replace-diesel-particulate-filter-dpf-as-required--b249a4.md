# Replace Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF)—As Required

When exhaust filter and warning light indicators are alerted on display, you have 250 hours to replace your diesel particulate filter (DPF).
Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider to replace your diesel particulate filter.
