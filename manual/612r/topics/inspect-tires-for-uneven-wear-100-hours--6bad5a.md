# Inspect Tires For Uneven Wear—100 Hours

NOTE: Rephasing steering system is required before road transport. (See rephasing instructions in Check Front Axle Toe-In—Annually in Wheels and Tires section.)
Inspect tires for uneven wear every 100 hours. If uneven wear is found, rotate the tires and check for component wear that may be causing the wear.
