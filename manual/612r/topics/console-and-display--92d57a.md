Armrest Control Console
Armrest Control Console— loosen knobs and slide the armrest control console up/forward or down/back in slots on outer plate. Tighten knobs to lock the armrest into position.

Display

USB Charging
USB Charging— allows you to charge mobile devices. These ports are located inside the console storage compartment. They are not connected to the radio or the display.

Auxillary Outlet
Auxillary Outlet— used for powering devices (12 V). Located inside the console storage compartment.
