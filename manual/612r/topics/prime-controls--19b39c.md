# Prime Controls

Prime Controls allow you to load the conveyor with the product while stationary. Loading the conveyor before application reduces the time necessary to achieve the desired target rate.
Perform When:
Product bin has been emptied.
Items Accessible on Prime Controls Page:
NOTE: Some items below are only displayed if machine is equipped with the associated option.

CAUTION: Belt(s) begin movement on button press. Product discharge may occur if the spinners are still enabled.
Spinners cannot be shielded due to their function. Keep away from the spinners while they are turning.
