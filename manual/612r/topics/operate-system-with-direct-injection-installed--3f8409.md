# Operate System with Direct Injection Installed

**Figure N129728** — A: System Pressure; B: System Flow; C: Standard Flow with Combined A + B Mode; D: High Flow with Combined A + B Mode; E: Direct Injection Trapped Pressure; F: Trapped Pressure Range

Direct Injection Trapped Pressure
ExactApply can be used in combination with a Direct Injection (DI) system. For high flow usage with DI, the high-pressure relief kit BKK11360 should be installed to prevent trapped pressure. The system can trap pressure above the 1034 kPa (10.34 bar) (150 psi) working range of the nozzles. In this condition, the nozzle solenoids may not open. System pressure can be relieved using the strainer drain valves on the boom or by opening a fence row nozzle momentarily (if equipped). Conditions for this scenario are indicated by the shaded area (F) of the graph.

Machines Equipped with High-Pressure Relief
When a flow rate or pressure in the indicated range is required and pressures are being trapped, the check valve (A) must be removed or bypassed when direct injection is not being used.
To perform a rinse procedure on machines equipped with Direct Injection and ExactApply high- pressure relief:
1. Unfold the boom and lower it to the lowest position.
NOTE: 40 psi or lower is the recommended pump pressure for the rinse procedure.
2. Ensure that the solution pump is ON at standby pressure.
