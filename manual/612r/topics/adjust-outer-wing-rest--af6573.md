# Adjust Outer Wing Rest

N116946—UN—02APR15

![[N116946.png]]
**Figure N116946** — A: Outer Wing Rest; B: Cap Screws; C: Inner Wing

IMPORTANT: Do not adjust outer wing rest (A) before outer fold cylinder is correctly adjusted. (See Adjust Outer Fold Cylinder in this section.)
1. Loosen cap screws (B).
2. Adjust outer wing rest to obtain contact with inner wing (C).
3. Tighten cap screws.
