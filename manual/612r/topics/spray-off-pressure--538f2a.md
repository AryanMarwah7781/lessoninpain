# Spray Off Pressure

Spray off pressure is the pressure the pump maintains when not spraying. Spray off pressure is primarily intended to increase agitation when not spraying. It should not be used as a means for boom charge compensation.
NOTE: Pressure Recirculation setting will take priority over Spray Off Pressure setting if Pressure Recirculatlon is enabled.
Increase Spray Off Pressure When:
- Applying dry flowable, heavy, or thick liquid chemicals.
- You desire increased agitation pressure.
Decrease Spray Off Pressure When:
- Using easily foaming chemicals.
- High agitation rates are not necessary to keep solution mixed.
