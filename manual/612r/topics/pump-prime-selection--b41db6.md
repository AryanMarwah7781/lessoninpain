# Pump Prime Selection

Prime Pump Selection allows you to select which direct injection pumps to prime and at which rate to prime them. You can also choose to prime only the injection line or the injection line and boom plumbing. Select the proper settings to avoid skips when beginning application.
Modify When:
- Changing application rate.
- Changing pumps in use.
- The entire boom does not need to be primed.
