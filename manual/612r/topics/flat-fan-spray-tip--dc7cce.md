Flat Fan spray tips are an ideal choice for broadcast spraying when it is desirable to have a uniform amount of chemical applied across the entire width of the boom.
Flat Fan spray tips apply solution in an elliptical pattern.
To achieve uniform application, it is necessary to overlap these spray tips. Usually this is done with spray tips spaced 508 mm (20 in) apart and positioned 457— 559 mm (18—22 in) above the target surface. Most flat fan spray tips are designed to operate in the 207—414 kPa (2—4 bar) (30—60 psi) range.
