# Replace Roof Worklights

NX695660—UN—05DEC25

![[NX695660.png]]
**Figure NX695660** — A: Slot

1. Use a flat head screwdriver to gently pry out and remove the light assembly at slot (A).
2. Disconnect the harness connectors.
3. Remove and replace the LED assembly.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.
