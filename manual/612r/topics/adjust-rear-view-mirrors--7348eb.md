# Adjust Rear View Mirrors

CAUTION: Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank.
N155107—UN—21APR21

![[N155107.png]]
**Figure N155107** — A: Right-Hand Mirror; B: Left-Hand Mirror

- Use a commercial ladder to access the right-hand mirror (A), and adjust for desired rear visibility by pivoting the mirror head.
- Use the landing on the left-hand side of the machine to access the left-hand mirror (B), and adjust for desired rear visibility by pivoting the mirror head.
N152999—UN—21OCT20

![[N152999.png]]
**Figure N152999** — A: Undercarriage Mirror

Adjust right-hand undercarriage mirror (A) to desired rear visibility by pivoting the mirror head.
