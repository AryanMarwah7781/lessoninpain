# Remove and Install Service Brake Caliper

1. Park machine on a flat, level surface.
N102418—UN—01MAR13

![[N102418.png]]
**Figure N102418** — A: Cap Screw (4 used); B: Fender; C: Hydraulic Line; D: Cap Screw (2 used); E: Brake Caliper

2. Remove cap screws (A) and fender (B).
3. Disconnect hydraulic line (C), then remove cap screws (D) and brake caliper (E).
4. Install in reverse order, using the following special instructions:
- Tighten brake caliper mounting cap screws to specification.
Specification
Brake Caliper Mounting Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 271 N·m (200 lb·ft)
- Lubricate fender mount-to-drive mount housing cap screws with clean engine oil or 10W30 oil used for the hydrostatic system and install. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Fender Mount-to-Drive Mount Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 268 N·m (198 lb·ft)
- Perform service brake bleeding procedure. (See Bleed Service Brakes in this section.)
