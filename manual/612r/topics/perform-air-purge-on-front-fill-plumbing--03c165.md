# Perform Air Purge on Front Fill Plumbing

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals. Exposure to hazardous materials could cause injury and be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440687—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440687.png]]
**Figure NX1440687** — A: Quik-Fill Valve (main); B: Quik-Fill Valve (dedicated)

Quik-Fill Valves (front load station)
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valves (A and B) can contain hazardous materials that can poison, causing serious injury or death. Before purging, make sure that the Quik-Fill valves are closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valves (A and B) are closed and the caps are installed on the Quik-Fill adapters.
NX1440837—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440837.png]]
**Figure NX1440837** — A: Cancel Button

2. While filling a solution tank from the front load station, allow the fill procedure to reach completion or press cancel button (A) on the micro display showing the tank solution level.

![[NX1440839.png]]
**Figure NX1440839** — A: Select Button

3. Press select button (A) to beign the air purge procedure.

![[NX1440840.png]]
**Figure NX1440840** — A: Cancel Button

4. During the procedure, press cancel button (A) to end and exit the procedure and return to the menu screen.
