# Boom Calibration | See & Spray Camera

This calibration verifies that the See & Spray cameras will actuate the appropriate nozzles.
Perform Calibration When:
- Camera installation verification is required.
- Cameras are replaced.
- Boom maintenance has been performed.
- Preparing for the first operation of the season.
- Camera or camera mounting brackets hit an obstacle and are approximately returned to the original position.
NOTE: Ensure that the machine is sitting on level ground, the surrounding area has even lighting, and the camera lenses are clean. To calibrate the entire boom length, two calibration mats must be placed.
NOTE: Completion of Boom Tilt Valve Calibration is required before running this calibration.
