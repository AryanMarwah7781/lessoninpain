# Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually

CAUTION: Decontaminate all spraying equipment before servicing. Decontamination should be done in a safe area by washing with water, neutralization, or by means recommended by the manufacturer of chemical last used.
Spray solutions or vapors may be extremely dangerous. Treat all spray chemicals, solutions, or solution residues with great caution. Do not take chances. When in doubt, proceed as though contamination is present.
Keep spray material from contacting skin. If spray material contacts skin, wash off immediately with clean water and detergent or follow instructions of manufacturer of chemical last used.
1. Check manufacturer's label of the last used chemical to see if special cleaning procedures are necessary.
2. Safely dispose of any chemical solution remaining in the tank. Follow chemical manufacturer's instructions for disposal of chemical solution.
3. Flush the tank and pump thoroughly with clean water and allow the water to drain out.
4. Mix household detergent with 95 L (25 gal) of water or use Incide-Out Spray Tank Cleaner (PM434QT) and circulate through the system for 30 minutes, then drain. Discharge detergent solution through spray nozzles until the tank is empty.
5. Fill tank with water. Add 0.95 L (1 qt) of household ammonia for every 95 L (25 gal) of water. Operate pump to circulate ammonia solution through the spray system for 15—20 minutes. Let stand overnight.
6. Flush the ammonia solution out of sprayer. Discharge the solution through the spray nozzles until tank is empty.
7. Fill tank with clean water and flush system by discharging the water through the open nozzle bodies.
8. Clean the area around the solution pump and motor.

9. Loosen clamps (A) and disconnect hose (B) and elbow fitting (C) from the solution pump.
10. Disconnect harness connector (D).
CAUTION: Approximate weight of the solution pump and motor assembly is 40 kg (88 lb). Use two people to remove pump and motor assembly.
NOTE: Support the motor with a suitable stand before removing it from the pump.

11. Remove mounting nuts (A), pump, and motor.

NOTE: The solution pump wet seal fluid capacity is approximately 23 oz.
12. Position the pump so that one of the plugs (A) is at the twelve o'clock position and remove plug if installed.
13. While the plug is held in the twelve o’clock position, fill the pump with the supplied seal chamber fluid. Fill the pump until it is level with the bottom threads and seeps from hole.
14. Apply thread lock and sealer (medium strength) to the plug.
15. Install the plug and tighten to specification.
Specification
Fill Plug—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 13 N·m (119 lb·in)
16. Install pump in reverse order. Tighten pump mounting nuts to specification.
Specification
High Flow Pump Mounting Nuts—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 315 N·m (232 lb·ft)
17. Check the reservoir oil level and add as necessary. (See Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily in this section.)
18. Flush the system with SprayMaster Winterizer Fluid (N305634) if the machine is to be stored in a climate colder than 0°C (32°F).
