# Replace Courtesy Service Light Strip

NOTE: There are two courtesy service light strips located above each cab pillar on the outside of the cab roof. A courtesy service light strip is also under the storage box on the left-hand side of the machine. Each light strip is secured by 2 TORX head screws.

Courtesy Service Light Strip
1. Disconnect harness connector (A).
2. Remove TORX head screws (B).
3. Remove the courtesy service light strip (C).
4. Replace light strip and install in reverse order of removal.
