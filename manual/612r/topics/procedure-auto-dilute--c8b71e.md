# Procedure | Auto Dilute

This procedure allows you to dilute the remaining solution in the tank at the end of the job before it can be sprayed out or drained on the ground. Procedure uses clean water from the rinse tank to dilute the solution in the solution tank.
Spray Dilution Factor dilutes the liquid in the first cycle so that the diluted liquid can be sprayed out.
Drain Dilution Factor dilutes the liquid over multiple cycles so that the tank can be drained at the end of the last cycle.
NOTE: Rinse tanks refills may be required during the dilution process.
Perform Procedure When:
- You need to dilute the product remaining in the tank before draining on the ground.
- You need to dilute the product before spraying the remaining product in the tank.
