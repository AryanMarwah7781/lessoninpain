# Direct Injection Pump Calibration Procedure

Procedure Requirements:
Pump | OFF
Carrier Solution Pump | OFF
Carrier Pressure | Less than 12 psi
Inlet Vacuum | Less than 5.5 psi
Injection Pressure | Less than 20 psi
Machine | Park
Faults | None Active
Procedure Overview:
NOTE: For dual Tank configurations, tank 1 shall be referred.
