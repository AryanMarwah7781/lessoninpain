# Boom Calibration | Center Frame Valve

This process calibrates the boom center frame valves.
Perform Calibration When:
- BoomTrac is not performing as expected.
- Center frame response is too slow or too fast.
- A valve cartridge has been replaced.
- Center frame sensor has been calibrated.
