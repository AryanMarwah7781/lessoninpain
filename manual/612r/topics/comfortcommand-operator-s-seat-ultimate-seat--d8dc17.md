# ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Ultimate Seat)

N152622—UN—17SEP20

![[N152622.png]]
**Figure N152622**

ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Ultimate Seat)
ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Ultimate Seat) describes the controls for the ultimate leather seat.
N152684—UN—25NOV20

![[N152684.png]]
**Figure N152684**

Seat Bottom Depth/Angle Adjustment Control
Seat Bottom Depth/Angle Adjustment— push the control forward or backward to slide seat forward or rearward for the best working position. Push the front of the control upward or downward to raise or lower the angle of the seat bottom.
N152685—UN—25NOV20

![[N152685.png]]
**Figure N152685**

Backrest Angle Adjustment Control
Backrest Angle Adjustment Control— adjusts the seat back angle. Press the top of the control forward to raise the angle of the seat back. Press the top of the control backward to lower the angle of the seat back.
N152686—UN—25NOV20

![[N152686.png]]
**Figure N152686**

Lumbar Adjustment Controls
Lumbar Adjustment Controls— press forward or back to increase or decrease lumbar support. Press up or down to raise or lower lumbar position.
N152681—UN—25NOV20

![[N152681.png]]
**Figure N152681**

Seat Swivel Handle
Seat Swivel Handle— lift the handle to allow the seat to swivel. Push down on the handle to lock seat in position.
N152680—UN—25NOV20

![[N152680.png]]
**Figure N152680**

Weight/Height Adjustment Switch
Weight/Height Adjustment Switch— press and hold the top or bottom of the switch to increase or decrease seat height.
N152689—UN—25NOV20

![[N152689.png]]
**Figure N152689**

Ventilated/Heated Seat Control
Ventilated/Heated Seat Control— push the control left or right to select ventilated or heated seat.
N152688—UN—25NOV20

![[N152688.png]]
**Figure N152688**

Ventilated/Heated Intensity Control
Ventilated/Heated Intensity Control— select off, low, or high intensity.
N152690—UN—25NOV20

![[N152690.png]]
**Figure N152690**

Bolster Adjustment Control
Bolster Adjustment Control— push plus (+) or minus (-) on the control to adjust the amount of support from the backrest bolsters.
N152691—UN—25NOV20

![[N152691.png]]
**Figure N152691**

Cushion Length Adjustment Control
Cushion Length Adjustment Control— push the top or bottom of the control to increase or decrease the seat cushion length.
N152692—UN—25NOV20

![[N152692.png]]
**Figure N152692**

Massage Pattern Control
Massage Pattern Control— push the bottom or top of the control to choose between massage pattern one or two. Return the control to the center position to end the massage pattern. The massage pattern will end after about ten minutes.
N152693—UN—25NOV20

![[N152693.png]]
**Figure N152693**

Massage Intensity Control
Massage Intensity Control— push the bottom or top of the control to choose between low or high massage intensity. Choose a massage pattern before choosing a massage intensity.
H127122—UN—26SEP19

![[H127122.png]]
**Figure H127122**

Armrest Angle Adjustment Knob
Armrest Angle Adjustment Knob— adjusts the left-hand armrest angle up or down. Rotate knob clockwise to decrease angle and counterclockwise to increase angle.
N152683—UN—25NOV20

![[N152683.png]]
**Figure N152683**

Left-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment
Left-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment— loosen the nut to adjust the left-hand armrest. Tighten the nut to lock the height into the desired position.
H117047—UN—22JUL21

![[H117047.png]]
**Figure H117047**

Right-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment
Right-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment— loosen knob and slide the armrest control console up/forward or down/back in slots on outer plate. Tighten knob.
H127308—UN—26SEP19

![[H127308.png]]
**Figure H127308**

Ride Firmness Control
Ride Firmness Control— select one of three levels of ride firmness:
- Most firm (+)
- Medium firmness (center position)
- Least firm (-)
N152687—UN—25NOV20

![[N152687.png]]
**Figure N152687**

Attenuation Lockout Handle
Attenuation Lockout Handle— limits the amount of fore/aft or side-to-side movement of the seat.
