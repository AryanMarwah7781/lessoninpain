# Fill Solution Tanks Simultaneously—Front Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent the exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1441243—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441243.png]]
**Figure NX1441243** — A: Quik-Fill Valve, Tank 1; B: Quik-Fill Valve, Tank 2; C: Cap, Tank 1; D: Cap, Tank 2; E: Cover

Front Fill Station
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valves (A and B) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill caps (C and D), verify that Quik-Fill valves are closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valves are closed.
2. Open the lock levers and remove the caps.
3. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill valve for tank 1 and close the lock levers. Repeat for tank 2.
4. Open the cover (C) for access to the micro display.
NX1440866—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440866.png]]
**Figure NX1440866** — A: Fill Button

5. Select fill button (A) to open the dual product fill menu.
NX1440867—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440867.png]]
**Figure NX1440867** — A: Tank 1 Button

6. Select tank 1 button (A) to open the tank 1 fill menu.
NX1440868—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440868.png]]
**Figure NX1440868** — A: Push-On Fill Button

7. Select push-on fill button (A) to open the fill menu for the tank 1 port.
NX1440871—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440871.png]]
**Figure NX1440871** — A: Tank Fill Button

8. Select tank fill button (A) to begin the filling procedure for tank 1.
9. Open the Quik-Fill valve for tank 1.
10. Open the nurse tank valve and fill tank 1 from the nurse tank.
NX1441246—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441246.png]]
**Figure NX1441246** — A: Tank 2 Button

11. During the fill procedure for tank 1, select tank 2 button (A) to open the fill menu for tank 2.
NX1441251—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441251.png]]
**Figure NX1441251** — A: Push-On Fill Button; B: Pull-On Fill Button

12. Select push-on fill button (A) to open the push-on fill menu for tank 2.
NOTE: Tank 2 fill can also be done using the solution pump by selecting pull-on fill button (B).
NX1441252—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441252.png]]
**Figure NX1441252** — A: Tank Fill Button

13. Select tank fill button (A) to begin the filling procedure for tank 2.
14. Open the Quik-Fill valve for tank 2.
15. Open the nurse tank valve and fill tank 2 from the nurse tank.
NX1441253—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441253.png]]
**Figure NX1441253** — A: Tank 1 Cancel Fill Button; B: Tank 2 Cancel Fill Button; C: Tank 1 and Tank 2 Cancel Fill Button

NOTE: The operator must monitor the micro display to prevent overfilling the solution tank. When using an external pump source, the display shows only approximate volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor the tank fill accurately.
16. Monitor the solution level in tanks as shown on the micro display. When the tanks contain the desired amount of solution, select tank 1 cancel fill button (A) or tank 2 cancel fill button (B) to end the filling procedure for either tank 1 or tank 2. Select tank 1 and tank 2 cancel fill button (C) to end the filling procedure for both tanks at the same time.
17. Close the nurse tank valves.
18. Close the Quik-Fill valves at the front load station.
19. Disconnect the nurse tank hoses from the Quik-Fill valves.
20. Install the caps on the Quik-Fill valves and close the lock levers.
21. If necessary, purge residual solution from the front fill plumbing using the onboard air supply. (See Perform Air purge on Front Fill plumbing in the section.)
