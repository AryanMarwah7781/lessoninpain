# Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures

Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures describes how to fold and unfold the boom or booms for spraying or transport. There are three ways to fold or unfold the boom:
N152955C—UN—26JUN25

![[N152955C.png]]
**Figure N152955C**

Auto Boom Fold/Unfold
- Auto Boom Fold/Unfold—allows you to fold/unfold both booms simultaneously.
N152956C—UN—26JUN25

![[N152956C.png]]
**Figure N152956C**

Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold
- Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold—allows you to fold/ unfold both booms simultaneously but only folding/ unfolding one section of each boom at a time.
N152957—UN—25NOV20

![[N152957.png]]
**Figure N152957**

One Side Fold/Unfold
- One Side Fold/Unfold—allows you to fold/unfold one section of one boom at a time.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the folding and unfolding area can result in injury or death.
IMPORTANT: Avoid damage; only unfold the booms where there is enough space.
NOTE: To unfold or fold the booms, the machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the Transport Lock must be disengaged.
