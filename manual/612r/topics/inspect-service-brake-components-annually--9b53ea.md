# Inspect Service Brake Components— Annually

CAUTION: Service brake system operates at high pressure. To avoid the risk of injury to yourself or bystanders, DO NOT crack lines in this system if a brake bleeding is necessary (See Bleed Service Brakes in Chassis section of this manual). The hydraulic brake components in this system should be serviced by a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
N102347—UN—11FEB13

![[N102347.png]]
**Figure N102347** — A: Spring Pins; B: Caliper Pins; C: Brake Pads

1. Remove spring pins (A) and caliper pins (B).
2. Remove brake pads (C) from between rotor and caliper.
N60507—UN—03JUL02

![[N60507.png]]
**Figure N60507** — A: Pad Material

3. Inspect brake pad material (A) for deep grooves, uneven wear, and minimum thickness.
Specification
Brake Pad Material—Minimum Thickness On Each Pad. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 1.5 mm (0.059 in)
N136022—UN—05MAR18

![[N136022.png]]
**Figure N136022** — A: Rotor Face; B: Rotor Thickness

4. Inspect rotor face (A) for deep grooves and uneven wear.
5. Inspect rotor for minimum thickness (B).
Specification
Rotor—Thickness. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 11 mm (0.440 in)
6. Reassemble brakes in reverse order of removal. Replacement components can be obtained via regular spare part channels.
