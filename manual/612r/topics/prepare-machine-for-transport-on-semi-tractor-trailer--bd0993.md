# Prepare Machine for Transport on Semi- Tractor Trailer

CAUTION: Suspension linkages can cause injury to you or others from movement when inflating or deflating air springs. Keep hands and body away from suspension linkage.
1. Empty solution tank and rinse tank.
N154885—UN—11MAR21

![[N154885.png]]
**Figure N154885** — A: Unload Valve

2. Deflate front and rear suspension air springs using unload valve (A).
3. Set wheels at 3048 mm (120 in) tread setting.
IMPORTANT: Do NOT tie boom wings to transport trailer.
Do not tie wire around solution plumbing.
NOTE: The right-hand side boom is tied down the same as the left-hand side.
N154888—UN—11MAR21

![[N154888.png]]
**Figure N154888** — A: Wire; B: Wire Location; C: Wire; D: Wire Location

N154886—UN—25MAR21

![[N154886.png]]
**Figure N154886** — A: Wire; B: Wire Location; C: Wire; D: Wire Location

N154887—UN—11MAR21

![[N154887.png]]
**Figure N154887** — A: Wire; B: Wire Location; C: Wire; D: Wire Location

4. Tie left-hand breakaway boom section to inner boom section using wire (A) at location (B).
5. Tie left-hand inner boom section to boom cradle using wire (C) at location (D).
6. Tie right-hand inner boom section to boom cradle using wire (C) at location (D).
NOTE: The left-hand side aspirator is removed the same as the right-hand side.
N136006—UN—05MAR18

![[N136006.png]]
**Figure N136006** — A: Clamp; B: Right-Hand Aspirator/Nozzle Body Assembly

7. Loosen clamp (A).
8. Remove and retain right-hand aspirator/nozzle body assembly (B).
NOTE: Store aspirator/nozzle body assembly in left-hand storage compartment.
Verify that assembly is installed to boom before machine returns to operation.
N136007—UN—05MAR18

![[N136007.png]]
**Figure N136007** — A: Cap

9. Install cap (A) and tighten the clamp.
10. Drive machine onto trailer or truck.
N154889—UN—11MAR21

![[N154889.png]]
**Figure N154889** — A: Ladder; B: Hook Strap

11. Get down from machine and raise lower portion of ladder (A) into transport position. Retain with the hook strap (B) as shown.
