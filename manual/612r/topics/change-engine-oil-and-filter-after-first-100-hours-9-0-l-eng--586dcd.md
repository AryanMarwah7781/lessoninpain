# Change Engine Oil and Filter—After First 100 Hours (9.0 L Engine)

NOTE: The initial break-in service interval of a new or rebuilt wet sleeve engine with Break-In Plus must go at least 100 hours to ensure that the surface mating of the rings and liners has had an opportunity to occur. The 100-hour minimum applies to all new or rebuilt engines. The maximum service interval is the same as the service interval recommendations listed in Engine Oil and Filter Service Intervals—Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V Engines located in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.
1. Operate engine to warm oil. Stop engine.
NOTE: Engine crankcase holds 31 L (32.8 qt).

![[N103034.png]]
**Figure N103034** — A: Crankcase Drain Plug

2. Place suitable container below the drain plug (A) to catch oil. Remove crankcase drain plug.
3. Reinstall plug after oil has drained.
1 If certain conditions are met, the oil and filter service interval can be extended to 500 hours. See Engine Oil and Filter Service Intervals— Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V Engines in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.. 2 If operating at altitudes above 1675 m (5500 ft), oil and filter service intervals are shortened. See Diesel Engine Oil Service Interval for Operation at High Altitude in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.
N154901—UN—29MAR21

![[N154901.png]]
**Figure N154901** — A: Oil Filter Cover; B: Plug

4. Using a 32-mm wrench, unscrew oil filter cover (A) and lift, allowing oil to drain into crankcase.
NOTE: Do not remove the plug (B) on the base of the oil filter housing. Oil automatically drains back into crankcase when filter is removed.
N136011—UN—05MAR18

![[N136011.png]]
**Figure N136011** — A: Filter

Remove Filter from Cover
5. While holding cover, strike filter (A) against solid surface to remove. Discard used filter.
6. Remove old O-ring and replace with new O-ring provided with new filter element.
7. Press new filter into cover until it snaps into place.
8. Insert cover and filter into the oil filter housing, then tighten cover to specification.
Specification
Oil Filter Cover—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 40 N·m 30 lb·ft
9. Fill crankcase with seasonal viscosity grade oil. (See Diesel Engine Oil—Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)3 2
3 If certain conditions are met, the oil and filter service interval can be extended to 500 hours. See Engine Oil and Filter Service Intervals— Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V Engines in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.
Specification
Crankcase with Filter—Capacity. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 31 L (32.8 qt)
10. Start engine and check for leaks.
11. Stop engine. Check oil level.
