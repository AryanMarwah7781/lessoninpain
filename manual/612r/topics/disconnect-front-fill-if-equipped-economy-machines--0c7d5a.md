# Disconnect Front Fill—If Equipped (Economy Machines)

NOTE: Clean the area around connections before removing hoses to prevent contamination.
Catch any fluids in a suitable container.
Verify that gaskets are retained when removing clamps.
NX1452822—UN—26MAR26

![[NX1452822.png]]
**Figure NX1452822** — A: Clamp (2 used); B: Fitting, Elbow; C: Fitting, Coupling; D: Fitting, Elbow

1. Remove and retain clamps (A) and the assembly of fittings (B—D).
NX1453558—UN—30MAR26

![[NX1453558.png]]
**Figure NX1453558** — A: Clamp; B: P-Clamp (2 used); C: Fitting, Hose Barb (2 used); D: Fitting, Elbow (2 used); E: Fitting, Coupling; F: Hose, Front Fill

NOTE: Retain all necessary hardware when removing P-clamps.
2. Remove and retain clamp (A) and P-clamps (B).
3. Remove and retain the assembly of fittings (C—E) and hose (F).
4. Install caps to the strainer and front fill pipe to avoid contamination.
