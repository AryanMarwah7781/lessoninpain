# Repair Nozzle Half Clamp

CAUTION: Keep spare nozzle tips for in-field replacement. DO NOT clean nozzle tips by placing in mouth and blowing. You could swallow or inhale hazardous chemicals, which will poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others.
Relieve trapped pressure in spray boom before servicing nozzle body.
Erase is a trademark of Precision Laboratories, LLC | NyoGel is a trademark of Nye Lubricants Inc.
1. Perform a pressure relief procedure. (See Pressure Relief in the Operating Nozzle Control section.)
2. Remove nozzle body.
N159617—UN—24NOV21

![[N159617.png]]
**Figure N159617** — A: Pin; B: Solenoid

3. Support nozzle body with blocking. Support under the pin (A) with a suitable tool. Make certain the solenoid (B) is not resting on blocking.
4. Using a small pin punch, remove the pin.
N156504—UN—23JUL21

![[N156504.png]]
**Figure N156504** — A: Pin; B: Half Clamp; C: Screw; D: Nut; E: Housing Body Assembly

5. Inspect parts for wear or damage, and replace as needed.
6. Assemble in reverse order using a C-clamp or channel-lock pliers to install the pin.
7. Install nozzle body. (See Remove and Install Nozzle Body in this section.)
