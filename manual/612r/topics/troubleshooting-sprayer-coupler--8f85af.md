# Troubleshooting Sprayer Coupler

Symptom | Problem | Solution
Coupler does not lock.
Air system leak. | Check air lines, air seals, and valves.
Proximity sensors do not sense the nurse coupler.
Clean proximity sensors and check function.
Debris on poppet or coupler prevent full engagement of couplers.
Remove debris.
Inadequate air capacity to inflate air seal at start-up due to other system demand such as the auto air suspension.
Wait 1—2 minutes for air systems to reach full capacity.
Arming switch not activated. | Activate arming switch on the front
coupler.
Debris on proximity sensors. | Clean proximity sensors.
Coupler poppet cylinder does not stay extended during service.
Shutoff valve open. | Fully close the valve.
Poppet leaks.
Debris caught in the poppet. | Remove debris.
O-ring not seated or damaged. | Repair O-ring.
Coupler poppet cylinder does not retract.
Shutoff valve is partially closed. | Fully open the valve.
