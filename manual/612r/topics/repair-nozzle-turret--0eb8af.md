# Repair Nozzle Turret

CAUTION: Keep spare nozzle tips for in-field replacement. DO NOT clean nozzle tips by placing in mouth and blowing. You could swallow or inhale hazardous chemicals, which will poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others.
Relieve trapped pressure in the spray boom before servicing nozzle body.
IMPORTANT: Do not allow a solvent or diesel fuel to contact rubber parts as it causes deterioration.
1. Perform a pressure relief procedure. (See Pressure Relief in the Operating Nozzle Control section.)
NOTE: It is not necessary to remove the nozzle body when removing the nozzle turret.
2. Remove nozzle body from the spray boom. (See Remove and Install Nozzle Body—As Required in this section.)

3. Remove O-ring (A).
4. Remove nozzle cap (B).
5. Remove the seal (C). Repeat steps for remaining nozzle caps.

6. Remove the clip (A) and housing (B).
7. Remove O-rings (C).
IMPORTANT: Always use new O-rings. Damaged or used O-rings leak.
NOTE: Lubricate turret O-rings and seals with PMH3503 Parker Super O-Lube or equivalent lubricant during reassembly.
8. Inspect parts for wear or damage. Replace parts as needed.
9. Assemble in reverse order.
10. Install the nozzle body. (See Remove and Install Nozzle Body—As Required in this section.)
