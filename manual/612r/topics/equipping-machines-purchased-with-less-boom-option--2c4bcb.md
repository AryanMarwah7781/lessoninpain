IMPORTANT: The 408R, 410R, 412R, 612R, and 616R Sprayers are optimized for up to a 36.6 m (120 ft) steel or 40.2 m (132 ft) carbon fiber spray boom. Installation of a longer spray boom may result in diminished spraying performance. To avoid sprayer vehicle damage and loss of warranty when using non-standard spray booms:
- DO NOT modify a factory spray boom for adding length.
- DO NOT install an aftermarket spray boom (including plumbing and optional equipment) with a dry weight exceeding 1597.8 kg (3522 lb).
- DO NOT install an aftermarket spray boom that has a center of gravity (B) further from the boom pivot (A) than a distance (C) of 6719 mm (265 in).
Modifying a factory-installed spray boom or installing an aftermarket spray boom that does not meet each one of the previous limitations is an alteration or modification of the sprayer in a way that is not approved of by John Deere.
