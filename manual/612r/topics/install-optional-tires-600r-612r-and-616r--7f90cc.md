# Install Optional Tires—600R, 612R, and 616R

SSL212136—UN—23NOV16

![[SSL212136.png]]
**Figure SSL212136**

Decal A—Jacking Point Decal
CAUTION: Sprayer with boom weighs approximately 16 059 kg (36 410 lb). To avoid injury or death to you or others, lift and support an empty machine only. Perform the procedure using the recommended equipment available via regular special tool order channels, including the approved jack bracket, spindle spacers, and 6804 kg (7.5 t[US]) jack. Support the machine using JDG10393 Shop Stand, which is also available via regular special tool order channels. If recommended equipment is not available, equivalent equipment can be used. Locate the jacking point decal (A) for the proper jacking location on the axle. Lift only one wheel at a time. Before jacking a wheel, turn it fully outward.
IMPORTANT: To eliminate slippage and damage to the axle, a protective plate can be used. Retain fender mounting hardware for installation of the jack bracket.

NX1432712—UN—25NOV25

![[NX1432712.png]]
**Figure NX1432712** — A: Location (2 used); B: Jack Bracket; C: Cap Screw (4 used); D: Location

CAUTION: Air spring can explode if over-inflated, causing serious injury or death to you or others. When adjusting suspension assemblies, do not exceed 689 kPa (6.89 bar) (100 psi). Keep hands and body away from suspension linkage.
NOTE: Spindle Spacers (JDG1781) and jack bracket kit can be obtained via regular spare part channels.
2. Inflate the air spring and install a JDG1781 Spindle Spacer on each spindle in the locations (A) shown.
3. Deflate the air spring until the machine is resting on the spacers.
4. Position jack bracket (B) and install cap screws (C).
5. Raise the machine using a bottle jack at location (D).
IMPORTANT: Support machine using JDG10393 Jackstand, which has a capacity of 6804 kg (15 000 lb). Do not use JT07365 Jackstand for supporting the machine.
6. Support the machine using suitable stands.
IMPORTANT: Maximum weight of a row crop wheel and tire assembly is 495 kg (1091 lb). Maximum weight of a float wheel and tire assembly is 715 kg (1577 lb).
7. Attach a suitable lifting device to the outer wheel and tire assembly.
N155090—UN—26MAR21

![[N155090.png]]
**Figure N155090** — A: Nut (12 used); B: Washer (12 used); C: Clamp Ring Section (6 used)

8. Remove nuts (A), washers (B), clamp ring sections (C), and the outer wheel and tire assembly.
IMPORTANT: Damage to the planetary hub and wheel can occur when the correct mounting nut torque is not maintained. Tighten nuts in a crisscross pattern to specification. Tighten the wheel mounting nuts after the first 1 hour of use and every 10 hours thereafter until the correct torque is maintained. Check the torque on wheel nuts after every 100 hours of use.
9. Install the outer wheel and tire assembly, clamp ring sections, washers, and mounting nuts.
10. Raise the machine and remove the jackstand.
11. Lower the machine and remove the jack bracket.
12. Inflate the air spring and remove JDG1781 Spindle Spacers.
13. Tighten wheel mounting nuts (A) in a crisscross pattern to specification.
Specification
Outer Wheel Mounting Nuts—Torque (dry). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 531 N·m (392 lb·ft)
14. Install the fender in reverse order, using the following special instructions:
- Lubricate fender bracket cap screws with engine oil and install. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Fender Bracket-to-Drive Housing Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 268 N·m (198 lb·ft)
15. Repeat the procedure for the remaining tires.
