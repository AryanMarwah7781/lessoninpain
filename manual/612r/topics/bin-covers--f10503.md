# Bin Covers

Bin Covers application opens and closes the roll tarp on bins 1 and 2. Covering the product bins prevents product from getting wet or blowing out during transport or field operation.
Operate Bin Covers When:
- Loading product bins.
- Transporting.
- Operating in the field.
- Precipitation is falling.
