# Replace Fan Belt—9.0 L Engine

NOTE: Fan drive belt is equipped with an automatic tensioner that does not require adjustment.
1. Raise hood.
N153304—UN—08MAR21

![[N153304.png]]
**Figure N153304** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Left-Hand Engine Side Shield

2. Remove cap screws (A) and left-hand engine side shield (B).
N135793—UN—26FEB18

![[N135793.png]]
**Figure N135793** — A: Cap Screw (2 used); B: Fan Shield

3. Remove cap screws (A) and fan shield (B).
N103030—UN—10APR13

![[N103030.png]]
**Figure N103030** — A: Tensioner; B: Belt

4. Install 13 mm (1/2 in) breaker bar in tensioner (A).
5. Rotate tensioner clockwise to release belt tension.
6. Remove belt (B) from pulleys and carefully release the tensioner.
N103027—UN—08APR13

![[N103027.png]]
**Figure N103027** — A: Alternator Pulley; B: Idler Pulley (2 used); C: Fan Pulley; D: Drive Pulley; E: Tensioner Pulley

Fan Belt Routing—9.0 L Engine
7. Replace belt as shown.
8. Install fan shield and left-hand engine side shield.
