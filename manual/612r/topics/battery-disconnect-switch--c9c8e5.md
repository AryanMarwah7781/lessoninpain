# Battery Disconnect Switch

N153287—UN—19FEB21

![[N153287.png]]
**Figure N153287** — A: Switch; B: Light Emitting Diode (LED)

IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until light emitting diode (LED) (B) has turned off. This indicates that the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete.
Rotate battery disconnect switch (A) clockwise, as shown, to turn machine electrical system on. Rotate switch counterclockwise to turn system off.
