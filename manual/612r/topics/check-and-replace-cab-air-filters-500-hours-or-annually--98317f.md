# Check and Replace Cab Air Filters—500 Hours or Annually

NOTE: Decal is located on the rear window on the left-hand side of cab.
N154906—UN—29MAR21

![[N154906.png]]
**Figure N154906** — A: Decal

Cab air filters MUST be replaced every 500 hours or every year, whichever comes first. Check filters regularly. Replace filters sooner if they become restricted or if manufacturer recommends. When replaced, record date and engine hours on decal (A).
For checking and replacing procedure, see Check and Replace Cab Air Filters in Cab and Air Conditioning section.
