Direct Injection Trapped Pressure

Individual Nozzle Control (INC) Pro)
Individual Nozzle Control (INC) Pro can be used in combination with a Direct Injection (DI) system. For high flow usage with DI, the high-pressure relief kit should be installed to prevent trapped pressure. The system can trap pressure above the 1034 kPa (10.34 bar) (150 psi) working range of the nozzles. In this condition, the nozzle solenoids may not open. System pressure can be relieved using the strainer drain valves on the boom or by opening a fence row nozzle momentarily (if equipped). The shaded area (F) of the graph indicates the conditions for this scenario.

Machines Equipped with High-Pressure Relief
When flow rate or pressure in the indicated range is required and pressures are trapped, the check valve (A) must be removed or bypassed when direct injection is not being used.
Perform a rinse procedure on machines equipped with Direct Injection and INC Pro high-pressure relief using the following procedure:
1. Unfold the boom and lower it to the lowest position.
NOTE: 2.75 bar (275 kPa ) (40 psi) or lower is the recommended pump pressure for the rinse procedure.
2. Ensure that the solution pump is ON at the standby pressure.
