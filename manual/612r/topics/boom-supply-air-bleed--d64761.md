# Boom Supply Air Bleed

NOTE: The boom supply air bleed feature is not present on machines equipped with the pressure recirculation system.
N101653—UN—28JAN13

![[N101653.png]]
**Figure N101653** — A: Solenoid Valve; B: Line; C: Boom Supply Line; D: Solution Tank

The boom supply air bleed system consists of the solenoid valve (A) and line (B). They are attached to the boom supply line (C) just before the boom isolation valve and to the top of the solution tank (D). The system allows air trapped in the boom supply line to be released to the solution tank before entering the boom. The system activates automatically when the solution pump is engaged. The solenoid opens until the first time the master ON spray function is activated. The solenoid valve then closes. This process is activated every time the solution pump switch is cycled ON and OFF.
