# Air Flow Mode

Air Flow Mode allows you to adjust the distribution of air flow inside the cab or enable windshield defrost.
NOTE: Some items below are only displayed if machine is equipped with the associated option.
Available Air Flow Modes:
N145867C—UN—19JUN25

![[N145867C.png]]
**Figure N145867C**

Air Flow Modes
Select the desired air flow mode on the toggle bar.
N145870C—UN—19JUN25

![[N145870C.png]]
**Figure N145870C**

Defrost, Operator, and Floor
Defrost, Operator, and Floor— distribute air flow evenly throughout the cab and defrost the windshield.
NOTE: Air conditioning turns ON when Defrost air flow mode is selected.
N145871C—UN—19JUN25

![[N145871C.png]]
**Figure N145871C**

Operator and Floor
Operator and Floor— direct air flow evenly throughout the cab.
CommandARM Air Flow Controls:
H117877C—UN—25JUN25

![[H117877C.png]]
**Figure H117877C**

Air Flow Control
CommandARM Air Flow Control— press the air flow mode button on the armrest to toggle between modes.
