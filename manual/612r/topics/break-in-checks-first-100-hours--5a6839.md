# Break-In Checks—First 100 Hours

Engine is ready for normal operation.
Tighten the wheel bolts daily during the first week of operation.
Check for leaks.
Operate the engine at heavy loads without sustained maximum load.
Avoid idling the engine longer than 5 minutes.
Closely observe the coolant temperature during operation.
Check the air intake system hoses and clamps.
Daily or Every 10 Hours
IMPORTANT: John Deere Break-In Plus oil must be added if oil level is below the ADD mark on the dipstick. DO NOT fill above the crosshatch pattern or the FULL mark.
- Check the engine oil level frequently.
- Check the coolant level frequently.
- Check the hydraulic oil level.
- Drain water and sediment from the water separator.
- Inspect the tires for damage and correct inflation.
- Check the air springs for proper inflation.
At 50 Hours
- Change the planetary hub oil. (See Planetary Hub Oil in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)
At 100 Hours
IMPORTANT: Change the oil and filter between a minimum of 100 hours and a maximum of 500 hours if certain conditions are met during the initial operation of a new engine. (See Engine Oil and Filter Service Intervals — Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V Engines in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)
- Change the engine oil and filter.
- Check the tread adjust side gap and shim gap. (See Check Shim Gap on Axle Tread Adjust—After First 100 Hours in the Lubrication and Maintenance section.)
- Inspect the machine for loose hardware.
NOTE: Reset the service hours to zero after service. (See Service Intervals in the CommandCenter Display Information section.)
