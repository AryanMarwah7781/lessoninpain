# Auto Boom Fold/Unfold

Auto Boom Fold/Unfold procedure allows you to fold or unfold both booms at the same time.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the folding and unfolding area can result in injury or death.
IMPORTANT: Avoid damage; only unfold the booms where there is enough space.
NOTE: To unfold or fold the booms, the machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the Transport Lock must be disengaged.
Procedure to Modify:
Unfold for Spraying:
WZ1409986—UN—18JUN25

![[WZ1409986.png]]
**Figure WZ1409986**

Boom Fold/Unfold Foot Switch
1.Push and hold the Boom Fold/Unfold Foot Switch.

Center Boom Raise/Lower Switch
2.With the foot switch still pressed, press and hold the bottom of the Center Boom Switch (lower) until both sides of the boom have completely unfolded.
3.Release the foot switch and center boom switch.
Fold for Transport:
WZ1409986—UN—18JUN25
Boom Fold/Unfold Foot Switch
1.Push and hold the Boom Fold/Unfold Foot Switch.
N152641C—UN—20JUN25
Center Boom Raise/Lower Switch
2.With the foot switch still pressed, press and hold the top of the Center Boom Switch (raise) until both sides of the boom have completely folded in against the machine and are lowered into the cradles.
3.Release the foot switch and center boom switch.
