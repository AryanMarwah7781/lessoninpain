# Hydraulic System

Symptom | Problem | Solution
All hydraulic system fails to function.
Bad fuse. | Replace fuse.
Low oil supply. | Fill oil to correct fill specification. (See
Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily in
Lubrication and Maintenance section.)
Engine speed set too low. | Run engine at high idle.
Clogged suction line. | Clean line.
Damaged pump drive coupling. | Replace coupling.
Pump faulty. | Replace pump.
System overheating in cold weather.
When operating machine in low ambient temperatures below -7°C (20° F), the hydraulic oil may overheat due to a restriction at the hydraulic oil cooler.
Install winter screens to hydraulic coolers to maintain correct temperature.
Oil escapes at breather in the reservoir cap.
Reservoir over-filled. | Reduce oil to correct fill specification.
(See Check Hydraulic Oil Level—
Daily in Lubrication and Maintenance
section.)
Excessive speed on rough terrain. | Select slower target speed.
Driving on steep slopes. | Stay off slopes to steep for operation.
Booms level or fold too fast.
Incorrect orifice, or none in the work port.
Use correct orifices, properly installed. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
Lift arms drop too fast.
Incorrect orifice, or none in the boom valve.
Use correct orifices, properly installed. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
Booms fold too slow.
Plugged orifice. | Remove orifice, clean, and reinstall.
Symptom | Problem | Solution
Incorrect orifice. | Install correct orifice. Contact a John
Deere dealer or other service provider.
Some functions work erratically or not at all.
Low oil. | Fill tank to the proper level.
Loose or disconnected wires at switch or solenoid.
Check and repair connections.
Failed solenoid coil. | Replace coil.
Erratic steering.
Hydraulic oil overheated. | Fill tank to the proper level.
Clean oil cooler and screens.
Pump faulty. | Replace pump.
AutoTrac slow to acquire line or sluggish response.
Clean AutoTrac strainer. (See Clean AutoTrac Strainer—As Required in Lubrication and Maintenance section.)
All hydraulic functions but one do not work or are slow.
Check valve stuck open in the load sense line of function that works properly.
Clean or replace check valve. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
Sluggish or slow rate control, cannot meet speed or pressure requirements, must drive at too low of speed.
Plugged hydraulic compensator strainer.
Remove, inspect, and clean strainer. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
One hydraulic function fails to work properly.
Bad fuse. | Replace.
Bad switch. | Repair or replace.
Check valve sticks shut in the load sense line of the hydraulic valve.
Clean or replace check valve.
Solenoid or hydraulic function faulty. Repair or replace.
Hydraulic spool not shifting properly. Clean or replace hydraulic spool. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
