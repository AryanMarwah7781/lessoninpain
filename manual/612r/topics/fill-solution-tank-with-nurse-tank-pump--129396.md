# Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump

CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the solution tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1435337—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435337.png]]
**Figure NX1435337** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

Side Quik-Fill
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect nurse tank fill hose to Quik-Fill adapter, close lock levers, and open nurse tank valve.
NX1435327—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435327.png]]
**Figure NX1435327** — A: Push-On Fill Button

4. Press the push-on fill button (A).
NX1440374—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440374.png]]
**Figure NX1440374** — A: Tank Fill Button

5. Select tank fill button (A) on the micro display.
NOTE: Automatic fill is not active when filling with a nurse pump. Operator must monitor solution tank content volume to prevent overfilling solution tank. When using an external pump source, the sight gauge can give erratic volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor tank fill accurately.
6. Open Quik-Fill valve and fill using nurse pump.
7. After solution tank has filled to the desired level, shut down the nurse tank pump and close nurse tank valve.
8. Close Quik-Fill valve and press push-on fill button to close solution tank sump valve.
9. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter and replace cap.
