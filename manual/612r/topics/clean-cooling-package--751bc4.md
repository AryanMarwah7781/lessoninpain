# Clean Cooling Package

1. Stop the engine and remove the key.
N153310—UN—09MAR21

![[N153310.png]]
**Figure N153310** — A: Latch Handle

2. Pull the latch handle (A) to raise the hood.
N153311—UN—09MAR21

![[N153311.png]]
**Figure N153311** — A: Cap Screw (3 used); B: Cap Screw and Nut; C: Side Shield (2 used)

Left-Hand Side Shield
N153303—UN—08MAR21

![[N153303.png]]
**Figure N153303** — A: Cap Screw (3 used); B: Cap Screw and Nut; C: Side Shield (2 used)

Right-Hand Side Shield
3. Remove and retain cap screws (A), cap screw and nut (B), and side shields (C).
N153312—UN—09MAR21

![[N153312.png]]
**Figure N153312** — A: Air Conditioner Condenser Latch (2 used); B: Handle; C: Air Conditioner Condenser

4. Release the air conditioner condenser latches (A).
CAUTION: Reduce compressed air to 210 kPa (2 bar) (30 psi) or less when using for cleaning purposes. Clear area of bystanders, guard against flying debris, and wear personnel protection equipment, including eye protection. A filter-type respirator is also advised to avoid inhalation of harmful particles.
NOTE: Using an air hose, clean the radiator and cooler units by blowing out any foreign material from back to front.
5. Pull out on the handle (B) to position the air conditioner condenser (C) and coolers for cleaning.
6. Clean the air conditioner condenser, coolers, and radiator using compressed air or water.
7. Return all parts to original positions after cleaning and retain them properly.
