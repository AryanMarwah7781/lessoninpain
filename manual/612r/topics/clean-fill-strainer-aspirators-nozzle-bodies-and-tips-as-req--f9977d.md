# Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required

Clean Fill Strainer
CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Drain solution and clean strainer in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials, which can poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), make sure that Quik-Fill valve is turned off.
N99947—UN—11JUL13

![[N99947.png]]
**Figure N99947** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove cap.
CAUTION: Do not drain solution onto the ground. Drain into a container. Drain solution in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
3. Open Quik-Fill valve and drain solution into a suitable container.
4. After solution has drained, close Quik-Fill valve and replace cap.
N105932—UN—10SEP13

![[N105932.png]]
**Figure N105932** — A: End Cap; B: Strainer Body; C: Ring

5. Remove end cap (A) from end of strainer body (B) and drain solution into a suitable container.
NOTE: Do NOT remove ring (C) from strainer body. Strainer can be removed through end cap opening.
N135934—UN—05MAR18

![[N135934.png]]
**Figure N135934** — A: Strainer

6. Pull strainer (A) out of strainer body.
NOTE: Soak strainer in cleaning solution to dissolve chemical residue if needed.
7. Empty strainer and rinse with clean water.
8. Insert strainer into strainer body.
NOTE: Verify that strainer is properly seated in the strainer body before cap is installed. If the strainer is not fully seated, cap and O-ring may not seat properly allowing leaks to occur.
9. Install end cap on strainer body.
10. Tighten end cap and check for leaks.
Clean Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips
CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Drain solution and clean strainer in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
Do not drain solution onto the ground. Drain into a container. Drain solution in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
1. Relieve boom pressure. (See Pressure Relief in Spray System section.)
N118272—UN—23JUN15

![[N118272.png]]
**Figure N118272** — A: Aspirator; B: Nozzle Body

2. Remove aspirator (A), nozzle body (B), or tip. Catch solution in a suitable container.
NOTE: Soak components in cleaning solution to dissolve chemical residue if needed.
3. Clean components of chemical deposits.
4. Install all previously removed components and check for leaks.
