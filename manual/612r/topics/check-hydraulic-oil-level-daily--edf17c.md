# Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily

N153557—UN—25NOV20

![[N153557.png]]
**Figure N153557** — A: Site Tube; B: Filler Cap; C: Decal

NOTE: Hydrostatic system and hydraulic system use the same reservoir.
Observe oil level in sight tube (A) with machine on level surface, tread adjusted to narrow position, booms in cradle, and engine stopped. Verify that oil is at the proper level for the current oil temperature as shown on fill level decal (C).
If oil level is below the lower mark for the current temperature range, remove filler cap (B) and add hydraulic oil. (See Hydrostatic/Hydraulic Oil in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)
