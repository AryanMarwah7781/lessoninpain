9. Use the page down softkey (A) to scroll through the boom sections (B).
10. Set up the boom sections. The recommended setup for nozzles per section on 40 m (132 ft) boom is as follows:
Recommended Nozzle Count per Sections for 40 m (132 ft) boom - 11 Sections
L5 | L4 | L3 | L2 | L1 | C | R1 | R2 | R3 | R4 | R5
508 mm (20 in) Nozzle Spacing
6 | 6 | 6 | 9 | 9 | 8 | 9 | 9 | 6 | 6 | 6
381 mm (15 in) Nozzle Spacing
8 | 8 | 8 | 12 | 13 | 9 | 13 | 12 | 8 | 8 | 8
11. Select the Save softkey (C).
12. Verify that the Total Boom Width (D) is 40.6 m (133.33 ft) for 508 mm (20 in) nozzle spacing and 40.8 m (133.75 ft) for 381 mm (15 in) nozzle spacing.
