# See & Spray Calibration Procedure

NOTE: Boom tilt valve calibration must be completed before performing the See & Spray camera calibration procedure.
Verify BoomTrac Sensor Settings
Verify lateral and vertical sensor placement. See Edit Sensor Placement in the CommandCenter Display Application section of the machine Operator’s Manual.
Prepare to Calibrate See & Spray System
Calibration of the See & Spray system is required when removing the sprayer from storage or after replacement of one of the cameras.
IMPORTANT: Calibration must be performed on flat, smooth ground. The calibration will fail if attempted on uneven ground, such as gravel or grass.
IMPORTANT: Do not scratch or damage the face of the calibration boards.
NOTE: For best results, calibration should be performed in sunny or light overcast conditions. Calibration should not be performed during rain. There should be no shadows directly on the calibration mat.
NOTE: Handling of calibration mat is difficult in windy conditions. Avoid performing calibration in windy conditions or find a protected area to perform calibration.
NOTE: A new calibration will be required if a camera bracket has been bent or if a spray camera has been removed or replaced.
The calibration process captures the proper region of interest within the field of view of the camera, with respect to the measured boom height above the ground. As the boom rises, the region of interest would decrease to keep from identifying targets outside of the physical bounds of the respective nozzle bodies of a camera.
1. Move the machine to a shop floor or a field with bare ground where the booms can be completely unfolded and have room to fully rise and lower.
NOTE: For proper calibration, the terrain and calibration mats must be flat. There should be no wrinkles in the calibration mat.
2. Set the park brake.
CAUTION: Avoid possible injury or death from boom movement during the calibration process. Booms will move automatically several times during the process. Make sure that bystanders are clear of the machine during the process.
3. Unfold, level, and lower the booms to approximately 500 mm (20 in) above the ground.
Prepare for Full Boom and Front Camera Calibration
NOTE: Observe how calibration mats are stored when delivered so they can be properly stored when the procedure is completed.

Start Calibration Procedure
IMPORTANT: Environmental conditions can affect the calibration procedure. Do not perform calibration during rain or winds over 16 km/h (10 mph).
1. Start the engine and stay in the operator’s seat.
2. Verify that the park brake is engaged.
3. Verify that there are no faults associated with any of the cameras or processors. Go to See & Spray - Image Processor/Camera in the See & Spray
Application section for the procedure to access and view each processor group to verify proper operation and that no faults are present.

Yaw Adjustment
NOTE: Some cameras are mounted on other styles of mounting brackets. If shims cannot be used to adjust the camera as needed, use the tolerance in the mounting holes to adjust the yaw, pitch, or roll.
Do not use more than two shims at one mounting screw. If more than two shims are needed to correct the camera position, the camera bracket needs to be repaired or replaced.
One 2.5 mm shim adjusts the yaw position roughly 2.8°.
- To add yaw, loosen the flange lock nut (A) and install up to two shims (B) as needed between the camera assembly and bracket. Retighten the flange lock nut.
- To subtract yaw, loosen the flange lock nut (C) and install up to two shims as needed between the camera assembly and bracket. Retighten the flange lock nut.
7.

Pitch Adjustment
NOTE: One 4 mm shim adjusts the pitch position roughly 2°.
- To add pitch, loosen the flange cap screws (A) and install up to two shims (B) as needed between the mounting bracket and boom. Retighten the flange cap screws.
- To subtract pitch, loosen the flange cap screws (C) and install up to two shims as needed between the mounting bracket and boom. Retighten the flange cap screws.
8.
N163219—UN—06JAN23

![[N163219.png]]
**Figure N163219** — A: Flange Lock Nut (2 used); B: Flange Cap Screw (2 used); C: Shim, 4 mm (as needed); D: Flange Cap Screw (2 used)

Roll Adjustment
NOTE: The hole tolerance between the camera assembly and bracket adjusts the roll position roughly 2°.
- To add roll, loosen the flange lock nuts (A), turn the camera assembly clockwise, and retighten the flange lock nuts.
- If further adjustment is needed, loosen flange cap screws (B) and install up to two shims (C) as needed between the mounting bracket and boom.
- To subtract roll, loosen the flange lock nuts (A), turn the camera assembly counterclockwise, and retighten the flange lock nuts.
- If further adjustment is needed, loosen flange cap screws (D) and install up to two shims (C) as needed between the mounting bracket and boom.
NOTE: If two shims have already been used at any single mounting screw and further adjustment is still needed, then the mounting bracket needs repaired or replaced.
9. Run the calibration procedure again and repeat adjustment steps as needed until all cameras are positioned within tolerance and all other camera faults are cleared.
10. Store the calibration mats in the same manner as they were delivered, ensuring they are clean, dry, and not wrinkled. Store the mats in supplied bags indoors to avoid prolonged sunlight.
