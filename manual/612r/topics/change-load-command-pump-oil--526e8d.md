# Change Load Command Pump Oil

IMPORTANT: Change Load Command pump oil at end of every use season and/or before storage.
NOTE: Presence of moisture or milky oil can be identified as normal. Drain moisture and fill to proper oil level as needed. If oil level is excessively overfull, internal seal failure may be present. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
N122829—UN—26APR16

![[N122829.png]]
**Figure N122829** — A: Plug; B: Plug; C: Plug

Pump oil is full when it reaches plug (B). Remove plug (B) to check oil level.
1. Remove plug (A) and drain oil completely. Replace plug.
2. Remove plug (B or C) and fill pump with 1 L (0.26 gal) of GL5 80W-90 gear lube. Replace plug.
