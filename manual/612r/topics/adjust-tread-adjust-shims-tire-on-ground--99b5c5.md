# Adjust Tread Adjust Shims—Tire on Ground

NOTE: If sticking occurs, remove debris from axle knees and spray with a John Deere TEFLON based lubricant. Activate tread width adjust in and out until free.
IMPORTANT: Tread adjust shims must be adjusted with machine empty and spray booms folded.
Axles should be extended to 3454 mm (136 in) to adjust the shim gap properly.
1. Adjust tread width to 3454 mm (136 in) so that all shim hardware can be accessed.
N100106—UN—17SEP12

![[N100106.png]]
**Figure N100106** — A: Lock Nut (3 used); B: Cap Screw (3 used)

2. Loosen lock nuts (A).
3. Remove cap screws (B) and coat with anti-seize compound.
4. Install and tighten cap screws (B) to specification. This forces inner axle tube to opposite side of outer axle tube.
Specification
Cap Screw—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 271 N·m (200 lb·ft)
5. Repeat tightening until the proper torque is maintained on all three cap screws.
6. Loosen cap screws (B) 1/8-1/4 of a turn.
7. Tighten lock nuts (A) to specification.
Specification
Lock Nut—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 475 N·m (350 lb·ft)
