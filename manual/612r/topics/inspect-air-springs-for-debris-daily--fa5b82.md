Machine without Air Spring Cover

![[NX1432680.png]]
**Figure NX1432680** — A: Air Spring; B: Inspection Area; C: Cover

Machine with Air Spring Cover
IMPORTANT: Dirt and debris collection around air spring (A) can damage the air spring.
Inspect the area around the air spring for dirt and debris collection. Clean the area of any dirt or debris.
Verify that no dirt or debris is trapped at inspection area (B), between the rubber and piston on the air spring.
NOTE: Machines can be equipped with cover (C) which provides additional protection to the air spring.
