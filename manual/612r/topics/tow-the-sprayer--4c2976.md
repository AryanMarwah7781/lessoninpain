# Tow The Sprayer

IMPORTANT: To tow or pull the sprayer, engine MUST be running and hydraulic system MUST have correct hydraulic pressure or damage WILL occur to brakes and wheel hubs.
If engine is not running or proper hydraulic system pressure cannot be maintained, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider and consult the sprayer Technical Manual for proper brake and wheel hub disengagement procedure.
The machine should only be towed if stuck, WITH the engine running, correct hydraulic pressure, and the secondary brake released. Only pull the machine a short distance and at slow speed of 0—5 km/h (0—3 mph).
When towing from the front:
1. Remove tread sensors and brackets.
IMPORTANT: Do not tow at tie down rings. Severe damage to suspension will occur.
NOTE: Operator needs to remove front fill bracket (if equipped) or mirror if they are contacted by the chain or strap.
2. Wrap strap or chain around both the left end and the right end of the front axle and pull the sprayer directly forward. Place the strap or chain between the last two tread bolts on the end of the axle.
When towing from the rear:
1. Remove tread sensors and brackets.
IMPORTANT: Do not tow at tie down rings. Severe damage to suspension will occur.
NOTE: Operator needs to remove brake light and brackets, backup alarm, and direct injection pump components (if equipped) that will be contacted by the chain or strap.
2. Wrap strap or chain around both the left end and the right end of the rear axle and pull the sprayer directly rearward. Place the strap or chain between the last two tread bolts on the end of the axle.
