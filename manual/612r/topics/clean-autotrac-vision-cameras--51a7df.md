CAUTION: To avoid serious injury or death, shut OFF the engine, set the parking brake, and remove the key prior to cleaning the vision cameras.
IMPORTANT: Do not pressure wash the vision cameras. Use normal water pressure less than 3.4 bar (345 kPa) (50 psi).
Do not use an abrasive, oily, or dirty cloth to clean the vision cameras.
Clean AutoTrac Vision cameras (A and B) of dirt and other contaminants using water and a clean soft cloth as required. A mild detergent may be used if there is grease on the lens.
Verify that all hardware is tight and is not missing.
