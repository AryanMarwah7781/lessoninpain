# Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (9.0 L Engine)

TS1343—UN—18MAR92
CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system can perform repairs. Before disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with high-pressure common rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.
1. Thoroughly clean the exterior of the filter elements and filter mounting areas.
N153308—UN—09MAR21

![[N153308.png]]
**Figure N153308** — A: Drain Valve; B: Primary Fuel Filter

CAUTION: Fuel in filters can be under pressure. Open valve (A) on bottom of the water separator bowl to relieve pressure before removing filter.
NOTE: Use a suitable container to catch fuel when draining or removing fuel filters.
Fuel/water separator is part of fuel filter assembly.
2. Open drain valve (A) to remove water and contaminates from the primary fuel filter (B). Catch contents in a suitable container.
IMPORTANT: Always replace both filters at the same time.
NOTE: Additional fuel drains from filter housings.
N153309—UN—09MAR21

![[N153309.png]]
**Figure N153309** — A: Primary Fuel Filter; B: Secondary Fuel Filter

3. Rotate primary fuel filter (A) counterclockwise to remove it from housing.
4. Rotate secondary fuel filter (B) counterclockwise to remove it from housing.
IMPORTANT: DO NOT pre-fill either fuel filter with fuel.
5. Lube filter seals with diesel fuel and install filters.
6. Bleed fuel system. (See Bleed Fuel System in this section.)
