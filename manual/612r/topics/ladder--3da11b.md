# Ladder

Ladder automatically raises when the park brake is disengaged and multi-function lever is moved forward. When multi-function lever is moved to reverse, a 1- second delay exists. The machine must be moving at speeds greater than 1.6 km/h (1 mph).
The ladder automatically lowers when the machine is shut off or when the machine is in park and the operator is out of seat.
