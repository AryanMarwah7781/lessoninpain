# Determining Nozzle Location

When a nozzle or individual nozzle control (INC) unit fault is detected, the operator is notified on the CommandCenter display. Each nozzle is numbered based on its position on the spray boom. Nozzles are counted starting on the left-hand tip of the boom and ending at the right-hand boom tip.
There is a maximum of nine nozzles operated by each INC unit. Consecutive nozzles with a fault may indicate that the fault is at the INC unit.
The following tables indicate the last nozzle number on the left-hand wing, allowing for quicker location of the faulted nozzle.
