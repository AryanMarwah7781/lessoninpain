# Repair Nozzle Half Clamp

Single Plumbing
CAUTION: Keep spare nozzle tips for field replacement. DO NOT clean nozzle tips by placing in mouth and blowing. You could swallow or inhale hazardous chemicals which will poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others.
Relieve trapped pressure in spray boom before servicing nozzle body.
1. Perform pressure relief procedure. (See Pressure Relief in the Operating Nozzle Control section.)
2. Remove nozzle body.
N120765—UN—14DEC15

![[N120765.png]]
**Figure N120765** — A: Blocking; B: 3/8 in x 8 mm Deep Socket; C: Solenoid; D: Pin

3. Support nozzle body with blocking (A) and 3/8 in x 8 mm deep socket (B). Make certain solenoid (C) is not resting on blocking.
4. Using a small pin punch, tap the pin (D) into socket.
N120766—UN—14DEC15

![[N120766.png]]
**Figure N120766** — A: Pin; B: Half Clamp; C: Screw; D: Nut; E: Housing Body Assembly

5. Inspect parts for wear or damage, replace as needed.
6. Assemble in reverse order using a C-clamp or channel-lock pliers to install pin.
7. Install nozzle body. (See Remove and Install Nozzle Body in this section.)
Dual Plumbing
CAUTION: Keep spare nozzle tips for field replacement. DO NOT clean nozzle tips by placing in mouth and blowing. You could swallow or inhale hazardous chemicals, which will poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others.
Relieve trapped pressure in spray boom before servicing nozzle body.
1. Perform the pressure relief procedure. (See Pressure Relief in the Operating Nozzle Control section.)
2. Remove nozzle body.
