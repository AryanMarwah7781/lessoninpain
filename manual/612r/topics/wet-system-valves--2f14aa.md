# Wet System Valves

Solution Tank Sump Valve
N94211—UN—15JAN14

![[N94211.png]]
**Figure N94211** — A: Solution Tank Sump Valve

With user input, the solution tank sump valve (A) controls different loading methods when using either the solution pump or a nurse tank pump. The solution tank sump valve is also used to close off the solution tank from the rest of the wet system in case repairs are required in the system.
Opening and closing of the solution tank valve is controlled electronically by the automated wet system.
Pressure Valves
Multiple pressure valves are controlled electronically by the automated wet system and direct the flow of solution once it exits the pressure side of the solution pump.
N98843—UN—17SEP12

![[N98843.png]]
**Figure N98843** — A: Bypass Pressure Valve; B: Eductor/Tank Rinse Pressure Valve

The bypass pressure valve (A) directs flow to the solution tank when using the solution pump to load from a nurse tank.
The eductor/tank rinse pressure valve (B) directs solution to a venturi at the solution tank. This venturi creates the vacuum required to draw solution from the eductor hopper into the solution tank. The eductor/tank rinse pressure valve also directs water from the rinse tank to nozzles inside the solution tank when the rinse function is selected.
N99941—UN—17SEP12

![[N99941.png]]
**Figure N99941** — C: Agitation Pressure Valve

The agitation pressure valve (C) regulates the amount of agitation inside the solution tank.
N99942—UN—17SEP12

![[N99942.png]]
**Figure N99942** — D: Boom Isolation Valve

The boom isolation valve (D) is closed to allow for cleaning strainers and for boom air purge use (if equipped).
Side Quik-Fill Valve
NX1435340—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435340.png]]
**Figure NX1435340** — A: Valve Handle

Side Quik-Fill valve completes the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer.
Once the nurse tank hose has been connected to the machine, rotate valve handle (A) until parallel with plumbing to open the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer.
After filling procedure is completed, rotate handle back to the closed position as shown before disconnecting nurse tank hose from the sprayer.
Front Fill Isolation Valve
NX1439788—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1439788.png]]
**Figure NX1439788** — A: Front Fill Isolation Valve

Front fill isolation valve (A) completes the connection between the front Quik-Fill valve and the solution tank and is electrically controlled by the solution system control unit.
Front Quik-Fill Valve
NX1439789—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1439789.png]]
**Figure NX1439789** — A: Cap; B: Valve Handle

Front Quik-Fill valve completes the connection between the nurse tank and the front fill isolation valve.
Once cap (A) has been removed and the nurse tank hose has been connected to the machine, rotate valve handle (B) until parallel with the plumbing to open the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer.
After the filling procedure is completed, rotate the valve handle back to the closed position as shown.

Strainer/Flowmeter Shutoff Valve
NX1432687—UN—25NOV25

![[NX1432687.png]]
**Figure NX1432687** — A: Strainer/Flowmeter Shutoff Valve

Flowmeter Shutoff Valve
Strainer/Flowmeter shutoff valve (A) allows the operator to close the pressure line when cleaning the pressure strainers or flowmeters. Closing the valve limits the amount of solution that drains and reduces the amount of air introduced to the system.
To open, rotate handle parallel to solution hose as shown.
To close, rotate handle perpendicular to the solution hose.
NOTE: Close strainer/flowmeter shutoff valve when filling sprayer using nurse system pump to avoid air being forced into boom.
