# Check Engine Speeds—750 Hours

With no load, slow idle speed should be 900±50 rpm for machines with 6.8 L (415 in3) and 9.0 L (549 in3) engines.
Fast idle speed should be:
- 2140±50 rpm for machines with 6.8 L (415 in3) engines.
- 2240±50 rpm for machines with 9.0 L (549 in3) engines.
If speeds are not correct, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
