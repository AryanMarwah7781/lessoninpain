# Connect Front Fill—If Equipped (Economy Machines)

1.
NX1453560—UN—30MAR26

![[NX1453560.png]]
**Figure NX1453560** — A: Fitting, Hose Barb (2 used); B: Fitting, Elbow (2 used); C: Fitting, Coupling; D: Hose, Front Fill; E: Clamp; F: P-Clamp (2 used)

Remove any installed caps from the strainer and front fill pipe.
NOTE: Gaskets and hardware retained during front fill disconnection are used when installing clamps and P-clamps.
2. Connect the assembly of fittings (A—C) and hose (D) to the front fill pipe using clamp (E) in the position shown.
3. Retain the hose to the support brackets using P-clamps (F).
