# Inspect Fan Belt Tensioner—6.8 L Engine

1. Raise the hood.
N153303—UN—08MAR21

![[N153303.png]]
**Figure N153303** — A: Cap Screw; B: Cap Screw and Nut; C: Right-Hand Engine Side Shield

2. Remove cap screw (A) and cap screw and nut (B), then lift right-hand engine side shield (C) off alignment studs.
N135792—UN—26FEB18

![[N135792.png]]
**Figure N135792** — A: Breaker Bar; B: Tensioner; C: Alternator Pulley

3. Release tension on belt using a 13 mm (1/2 in) breaker bar (A).
4. Remove belt from alternator pulley (C).
5. Carefully release the tensioner (B).
6. Remove belt from remaining pulleys.
RXA0068400—UN—10SEP03

![[RXA0068400.png]]
**Figure RXA0068400** — A: Torque Wrench; B: Mark on Tension Arm; C: Mark on Mounting Bracket

N76712—UN—14JUN07

![[N76712.png]]
**Figure N76712** — A: Torque Wrench; B: Mark on Tension Arm; C: Mark on Mounting Bracket

N76713—UN—14JUN07

![[N76713.png]]
**Figure N76713** — A: Torque Wrench; B: Mark on Tension Arm; C: Mark on Mounting Bracket

7. Place mark on the tensioner arm at (B).
8. Measure 21 mm (13/16 in) from mark (B) and put a mark (C) on mounting bracket.
9. Rotate tension arm with torque wrench (A) until marks (B and C) align. If torque wrench measurement is not within specification, replace tensioner mechanism. See regular spare part channels to order.
Specification
Tension Arm—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 18—23 N·m (159—204 lb·in)
10. Install belt and side shield.
11. Lower the hood.
