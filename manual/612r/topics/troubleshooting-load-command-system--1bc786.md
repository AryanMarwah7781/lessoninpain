# Troubleshooting Load Command System

Symptom | Problem | Solution
Tank foams too much.
During first 265 L (70 gal) of filling, reduction in pump speed is not triggered.
Check 265 L (70 gal) tank level sensor function.
The 76 mm (3 in) drop line inside tank is directing the flow incorrectly into the tank.
Properly position 76 mm (3 in) drop line on rod guide so that the outlet of hose is submersed before the 265 L (70 gal) mark.
Chemical used is susceptible to foaming.
Use anti-foaming agent.
Air in system. | Check for vacuum leaks in plumbing
upstream of pump.
Foam generated during auto evacuation cycle.
Evacuate only when required.
Pump running at full speed with slow fill time.
Air entering suction line. | Check for air leaks in plumbing on the
nurse vehicle and sprayer.
Symptom | Problem | Solution
Load Command system does not start.
Machine not started or not in park. | Verify that machine is on and in park.
Load Command system believes that the tank is already full.
Check 4542 L (1200 gal) for the 612R tank level sensor function, and check 6056 L (1200 gal) for the 616R tank level sensor function.
Nurse coupler not properly connected to sprayer coupler.
Verify that coupler is fully seated.
Arming switch has not been activated. Activate arming switch on the front coupler.
Proximity sensors covered with dirt/ debris or failed.
Clean sensors with mild detergent and damp cloth.
Cannot evacuate chemicals out of system.
Evacuation incorrectly selected on display.
Verify that evacuation is correctly selected on display.
Fewer than 284 L (75 gal) in the solution tank.
Verify that at least 284 L (75 gal) are in the solution tank.
Eductor valve not opening, venturi blocked.
Verify that eductor valve opens and closes properly.
Verify that venturi at the top of tank is free of obstruction.
Eductor hopper valves are open. | Verify that eductor hopper valves are
closed.
System does not shut off after 4542 L (1200 gal).
Tank level sensors blocked or malfunctioning.
Verify that tank level sensors are in place and moving freely.
Machine not parked on a level surface while loading solution.
Load machine on a level surface.
