# Left-Hand Cab Items

Left-Hand Cab Items describes items located on the left-hand side of the cab near the door and instructional seat.
N152950—UN—25NOV20

![[N152950.png]]
**Figure N152950**

Emergency Exit
Emergency Exit— allows you to exit the machine in case of emergency.
- Pull down to remove the emergency exit device.
- Use the hammer to break the window and exit cab if doors do not open.
- Use the knife in the handle to cut seat belts if they do not unbuckle.
N152952—UN—25NOV20

![[N152952.png]]
**Figure N152952**

Auxiliary Outlet
Auxiliary Outlet— used when connecting accessories (12 V).
N152951—UN—25NOV20

![[N152951.png]]
**Figure N152951**

120V Outlet
120V Outlet (If Equipped)— used when connecting accessories (120 V).
N152953—UN—25NOV20

![[N152953.png]]
**Figure N152953**

Phone Tray
Phone Tray— place your phone or other small items in this tray.
N152954—UN—25NOV20

![[N152954.png]]
**Figure N152954**

Refrigerator (if equipped)
Refrigerator (if equipped)— provides a space to keep food or beverages cool throughout the day.
NOTE: Refrigerator operates only when the key switch is in the Run position and MUST remain plugged into the auxiliary outlet to keep food or beverages cold.

![[N152976.png]]

Storage Area
Storage Area— use to store items. Located below the instructional seat.
N152977—UN—25NOV20

![[N152977.png]]
**Figure N152977**

Book Storage
Book Storage— use to store books or papers. Located between the operator's seat and the instructional seat.

![[N152978.png]]

Storage Tray
Storage Tray— use to store items. Located on the floor between the operator's seat and the instructional seat.

![[N152979.png]]
**Figure N152979**

Cab Recirculation Air Filter
Cab Recirculation Air Filter— filters the air recirculated by the HVAC system. For more information on how to replace the filter and the frequency of replacement, refer to your machine's Operator's Manual.
