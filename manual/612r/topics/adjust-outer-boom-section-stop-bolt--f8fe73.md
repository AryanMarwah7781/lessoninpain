# Adjust Outer Boom Section Stop Bolt

N106867—UN—25SEP13

![[N106867.png]]
**Figure N106867** — A: Lock Nut; B: Stop Bolt; C: Outer Section

1. Loosen lock nut (A).
2. Adjust outer boom section stop bolt (B) in or out so that outer section (C) is in line with the rest of boom.
3. Tighten lock nut.
