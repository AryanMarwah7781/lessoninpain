# Variable Rate

Variable Rate lets the operator set thresholds for the amount of green objects, called biomass, that the sensors detect. The operator assigns an application rate for everything below the low threshold, called the smaller the biomass limit, and an application rate for everything above the high threshold, called the larger biomass limit. The green objects between the smaller and larger biomass limits are sprayed using a variable rate between those two rates.

Increase Rate
Radio Button
Increase Rate— select to spray targeted plants detected at the smaller biomass limit with the minimum application rate. As the detected targets increase in biomass, the application rate increases along with the increase in detected biomass. At the larger biomass limit, the maximum application rate is sprayed.
Select the Radio button next to Increase Rate to choose this rate pattern.

Decrease Rate— select to spray targeted plants detected at the smaller biomass limit with the maximum application rate. Select the Radio button next to Decrease Rate to choose this rate pattern.

Behavior Below Spray Maximum (Decrease Pattern)
Behavior Below— allows you to choose the spray behavior that will be used when the amount of detected targets fall below the smaller biomass limit. Select the Radio button next to the desired option.
Select Do Not Spray to stop spraying when the detected biomass amount falls below the smaller biomass limit. Do Not Spray will appear as a greyed-out segment on the Summary graph.
Select Spray Min Rate or Spray Max Rate to continue spraying at the minimum/maximum application rate when the detected biomass amount falls below the smaller biomass limit. The selected rate pattern effects whether the option to spray at the minimum rate or spray at the maximum rate are available for Behavior Below. Spray Min/Max Rate will appear as a blue segment on the Summary graph.

Live Biomass
Live Biomass— shows the current biomass percentage for each See & Spray control unit on the machine. The percentages are an average of the represented area on the boom. Each nozzle is applying product based on the biomass percentage measured by the camera.
The portion of the boom with a square around it indicates the general location of the camera live feed being viewed.
