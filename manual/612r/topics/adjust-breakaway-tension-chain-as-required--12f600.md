# Adjust Breakaway Tension Chain (As Required)

36.6 m (120 ft) Boom
NOTE: There are two adjustment procedures that follow. Fine adjustment is done through access in the fold joint with no disassembly needed. Coarse adjustment has some disassembly necessary to access the cross pin on the rod end.
Procedure for the left-hand boom is shown. The right-hand boom is similar.
1. Unfold the boom and lower onto suitable jackstands placed at intermediate joints and the ends of the boom.
2. When necessary, clean the machine of pesticides. (See Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides in the Safety section.)
Fine Adjustment
1.
N153399—UN—13NOV20

![[N153399.png]]
**Figure N153399** — A: Lock Nut; B: Flange Nut; C: Rocker Arm; D: Operating Range

WZ290909461—UN—31MAR17

![[WZ290909461.png]]
**Figure WZ290909461** — A: Lock Nut; B: Flange Nut; C: Rocker Arm; D: Operating Range

Loosen lock nut (A).
2. Adjust flange nut (B) so that rocker arm (C) is inside of operating range (D).
3. Tighten the lock nut.
NOTE: If rocker arm is not inside operating range or trip force cannot be achieved, repeat the adjustment procedure as needed.
If fine adjustment travel is not enough to achieve trip force, back off the adjustment and proceed to coarse adjustment.

1.

2. Fold the rocker arm to the outward position.

4. Inspect the pin for wear or damage and replace as needed.
5. Fold breakaway boom (C) forward and pull bracket (D) out of the breakaway boom.

Lift up on bracket (A) and disengage from cross pin (B).
7. Rotate the bracket in the direction shown and remove from the rod end.
NOTE:
N153414—UN—13NOV20

**Figure N153414** — A: Lock Nut; B: Rod End; C: Cross Pin

Approximately four full turns of the cross pin are necessary to reset the bracket used with fine adjustment.
8. Loosen lock nut (A) to allow adjustment of rod end (B) and cross pin (C).
9. Reassemble in reverse order using the following special instructions.
- Tighten the lock nut to specification
Specification
Cross Pin-to-Rod End Lock Nut—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 92 N·m (68 lb·ft)
- Adjust the spring eyebolt nuts to achieve specified breakaway trip force.
Specification
Breakaway Trip Force—Force. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 245 ± 20 N (55 ± 4 lb)

If the rocker arm is not inside operating range, repeat the fine adjustment procedure as necessary.
- Verify that rocker arm (A) is within operating range (B).
