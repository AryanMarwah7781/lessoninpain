# Boom Air Purge | Status Indicator

Status Indicator displays the status of the boom air purge procedure and a message about that status.
Available Status Indicators:
N118420—UN—02NOV16

![[N118420.png]]
**Figure N118420**

Green
Boom Air Purge Ready (green)— turn the Spray System Master switch on to start.
N118021—UN—13JAN17

![[N118021.png]]
**Figure N118021**

Blue
Boom Air Purge in Progress (blue)— turn the Spray System Master switch off to stop.
N118020—UN—22OCT15

![[N118020.png]]
**Figure N118020**

Amber
Boom Air Purge Paused (amber)— turn the Spray System Master switch on to resume.
N118022—UN—13JAN17

![[N118022.png]]
**Figure N118022**

Red
Boom Air Purge Paused (red)— an error has occurred, causing the procedure to be paused. A message about the error is displayed. To resume the procedure, correct the error and press the Spray System Master switch.
