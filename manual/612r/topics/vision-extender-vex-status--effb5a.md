# Vision Extender (VEX) Status

NX1435676—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435676.png]]
**Figure NX1435676** — A: LED Indicator

NOTE: Each video extender (VEX) has a built-in LED indicator (A) to check status for each controller and aid in troubleshooting.
Solid green—VEX is powered on and all linked to VPU and cameras ready.
Solid white—VEX is powered on. Status of all VPU and camera links unknown/undetermined.
Solid blue—VEX is powered on. Status of all VPU and camera links unknown/undetermined.
Solid red—VEX is powered on and has a determined fault with VPU link or camera.
No color no pattern—Vex has no power from VPU.
