N155110—UN—21APR21

**Figure N155110** — A: Engine Speed; B: Engine Speed Increase Button

Allowing the engine to idle uses fuel inefficiently and can cause a buildup of carbon in the engine.
View the engine speed (A) on the corner post display. Push the engine speed increase button (B) until the desired speed is displayed.
N147164—UN—21OCT19

![[N147164.png]]
**Figure N147164**

Auto Idle
NOTE: Auto Idle is only available when the transmission is in Auto mode and no other machine functions require the engine speed to be higher than 900 rpm.
While operating in Auto mode, the Auto Idle feature will automatically lower the engine rpm to minimum engine speed after a period of inactivity from the operator. The time length of the inactivity period can be adjusted by the operator between 1—30 seconds.
