# Spreader | Spinner Calibration | Procedure

Procedure Requirements:
High Oil Temperature | Above 60°C (140°F)
Hydraulic Enable Switch | Engaged
Spreader State | Stopped
Operating State | Machine parked
Engine State | Running at high idle
Procedure Overview:
CAUTION: The spinners reach maximum speed during the process. Be sure to calibrate in an open, safe area. Ensure that there are no bystanders in the area of the spreaders.
NOTE: If spinners are currently enabled, the system automatically stops the spinners to proceed with calibration.
You must remain seated during calibration.
