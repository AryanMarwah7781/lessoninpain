# Operate Boom Isolation Valve (Single Solution)

N125204—UN—24AUG16

![[N125204.png]]
**Figure N125204** — A: Boom Isolation Valve

Boom isolation valve (A) stops the flow of solution from the tank and pump to the boom when closed. The valve is closed unless the pump is ON and the booms are unfolded. The valve also opens when the spray system on/off switch is pressed while the boom is folded.
