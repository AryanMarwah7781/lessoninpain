# Inspect Seat Belts—Annually

N154909—UN—29MAR21

![[N154909.png]]
**Figure N154909** — A: Seat Belt

Inspect seat belt (A) and mounting hardware on your operator's seat and instructional seat (if equipped) at least once a year. If the seat belt system, including the mounting hardware, buckle, belt, or retractor, shows any sign of damage, such as cuts, fraying, extreme or unusual wear, discoloration, or abrasion, the entire seat belt system should be replaced immediately. Replace the belt system only with replacement parts approved for your machine. (See a John Deere dealer or other service provider.)
