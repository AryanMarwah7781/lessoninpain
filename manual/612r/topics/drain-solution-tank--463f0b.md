# Drain Solution Tank

CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Drain solution tank in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
NX1435337—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435337.png]]
**Figure NX1435337** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
Do not drain solution onto the ground. Drain into a container.
1. Verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect nurse tank hose to Quik-Fill or place suitable container under Quik-Fill to catch solution.
NX1441522—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441522.png]]
**Figure NX1441522** — A: Menu Button

4. Press menu button (A) on the keypad.
NX1441515—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441515.png]]
**Figure NX1441515** — A: Drain Tank Button; B: Cancel Button

NX1441520—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441520.png]]
**Figure NX1441520** — A: Drain Tank Button; B: Cancel Button

5. Press drain tank button (A) on the micro display.
6. Verify that fluid drains from the tank and into the designated container (if used). Monitor the fluid level on the micro display. When the desired fluid level is reached, press cancel button (B) to close the drain valve.
7. Remove the nurse tank hose (if used), close the Quik-Fill valve and replace cap.
