# Fill Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Tank (Final Tier 4/Stage V)

CAUTION: Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank.
Avoid contact with eyes. If contacted, immediately flush eyes with large amounts of water for a minimum of 15 minutes. Reference Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information.
Do not ingest DEF. In the event DEF is ingested, contact a physician immediately. Reference Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information.
IMPORTANT: Never put DEF in the diesel fuel tank, or diesel fuel in the DEF tank.
NOTE: Spilled DEF, if left to dry or if only wiped away with a cloth, leaves a white residue. Aside from cosmetic issues, an improperly cleaned DEF spill can interfere with diagnosis of selective catalytic reduction (SCR) system leakage problems.
DEF tank capacity is 29.5 L (7.8 gal).
NX1396832—UN—17MAR25

![[NX1396832.png]]
**Figure NX1396832** — A: Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Level Indicator

Fill the tank every time the machine is refueled. If this cannot be done, monitor diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) level indicator (A) on the corner post display and refill as necessary. To avoid drastic changes in machine performance, always keep the DEF level above the topmost red mark on the level indicator. (See Selective Catalytic Reduction [SCR] System [Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines] in the Operate the Machine section.)
To fill the DEF tank:
1.Before using containers, funnels, and so on, to dispense DEF, wash and rinse items thoroughly with distilled water to remove contaminants.
N153002—UN—22OCT20

![[N153002.png]]
**Figure N153002** — A: DEF Tank Cap

2.Wipe DEF tank cap (A) and areas around the filler neck to reduce chance of contaminating DEF.
3.Remove the cap.
4.Using a funnel, carefully pour DEF into the tank, watching the level through the filler neck.
5.Securely tighten the DEF tank cap.
6.Carefully clean any spills using distilled water only.
If an unapproved fluid, such as diesel fuel or engine coolant, is added to the vehicle DEF tank, drain and rinse the tank. (See Drain and Flush Diesel Exhaust Fluid [DEF] Tank in the Engine and Drive Train section.)
