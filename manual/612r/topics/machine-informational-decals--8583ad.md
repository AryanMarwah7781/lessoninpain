# Machine Informational Decals

Tire Decals
N99103—UN—17SEP12

![[N99103.png]]
**Figure N99103** — A: Tire Pressure; B: Wheel Hardware Torque

Decal A:
IMPORTANT: Refer to Operator’s Manual for proper inflation pressure. (See Inspect Tires—Daily in the Wheels and Tires section.)
Decal B:
IMPORTANT: Torque each nut to 531 N·m (392 lb·ft). Retorque after 1 hour and daily until torque is maintained. Repeat operation each time nuts are loosened. Check torque every 100 hours of operation thereafter.
Remote Terminal Decal
N155106—UN—21APR21

![[N155106.png]]
**Figure N155106** — A: Decal

Remote terminals are for auxiliary electrical power. Do not exceed 30 amps.

Eductor Operation Decal
N155108—UN—21APR21

![[N155108.png]]
**Figure N155108** — A: Eductor Operation Decal

Eductor Operation
1. Turn eductor switch to on.
2. Open eductor hopper valve.
After Rinsing Eductor Bowl
1. Turn eductor switch to off.
2. Close eductor hopper valve.

N122826—UN—26APR16

![[N122826.png]]
**Figure N122826** — A: Jack Bracket Decal

Decal A:

NOTE: Jack bracket kit is available via regular spare part channels.

Fold/Unfold Decal
N153007—UN—22OCT20

![[N153007.png]]
**Figure N153007** — A: Decal

Unfolding for Spraying:
1. Auto Boom Unfold—Push boom fold/unfold floor switch and lower switch until boom is unfolded.
2. Sequential Boom Unfold:
a. Push tilt up switches to raise boom out of front cradle.
b. Push boom fold/unfold pedal and tilt down switches to unfold inner sections.
c. To unfold outer sections, release and push boom fold/unfold pedal and tilt down switches again.
3. One-Side Unfold—Use sequential boom unfold operation for left-hand and right-hand side boom to unfold.
Fold for Transport:
1. Auto Boom Fold—Push boom fold/unfold floor switch and raise switch until boom is folded.
2. Sequential Boom Fold:
a. Push boom fold/unfold pedal and tilt up switches to fold outer boom sections.
b. To fold inner sections, release and push boom fold/unfold pedal and tilt up switches again.
c. Push tilt down switches to lower boom into front cradle.
3. One-Side Fold—Use sequential boom fold operation for left-hand and right-hand side boom to fold.
