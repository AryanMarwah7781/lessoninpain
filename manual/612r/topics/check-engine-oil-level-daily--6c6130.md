# Check Engine Oil Level—Daily

1. Park machine on level ground. Stop engine and remove key.
N153905—UN—27JAN21

![[N153905.png]]
**Figure N153905** — A: Fill Cap; B: Dipstick; C: ADD Hole; D: FULL Hole

N110836—UN—19MAR14

![[N110836.png]]
**Figure N110836** — A: Fill Cap; B: Dipstick; C: ADD Hole; D: FULL Hole

NOTE: Dipstick (B) is attached to the fill cap.
2. Remove fill cap (A) and clean oil from dipstick.
IMPORTANT: Fill cap must be fully tightened to provide an accurate oil level reading.
3. Install fill cap, then remove and check oil level on dipstick.
4. Oil level is adequate if level is between the ADD hole (C) and FULL hole (D).
5. If oil level is below the ADD hole, then add engine oil and recheck until level is adequate. (See Diesel Engine Oil—Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB,
Stage IV, and Stage V in Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.)
IMPORTANT: Do not operate engine with the oil level below the “ADD” mark on dipstick.
Do not overfill oil level.
Acceptable oil usage is 1 gallon of oil added per 400 gallons of diesel fuel consumed. If usage is more, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
After long storage period
Check whether dirt or other foreign bodies have collected under the hood or cab. If so, remove them.
