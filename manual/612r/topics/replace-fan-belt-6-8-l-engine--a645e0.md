# Replace Fan Belt—6.8 L Engine

NOTE: Fan drive belt is equipped with an automatic tensioner that does not require adjustment.
N153303—UN—08MAR21

![[N153303.png]]
**Figure N153303** — A: Cap Screw; B: Cap Screw and Nut; C: Right-Hand Engine Side Shield

1. Raise hood.
2. Remove cap screw (A) and cap screw and nut (B), then lift right-hand engine side shield (C) off alignment studs.
N135792—UN—26FEB18

![[N135792.png]]
**Figure N135792** — A: Breaker Bar; B: Tensioner; C: Alternator Pulley

3. Release tension on belt using a 13 mm (1/2 in) breaker bar (A).
4. Remove belt from alternator pulley (C).
5. Carefully release the tensioner (B).
6. Remove belt from remaining pulleys.
N97486—UN—18JUL12

![[N97486.png]]
**Figure N97486** — A: Alternator Pulley; B: Idler Pulley; C: Fan Pulley; D: Drive Pulley; E: Tensioner Pulley; F: Water Pump Pulley

7. Replace belt as shown.
8. Install side shield and lower the hood.
