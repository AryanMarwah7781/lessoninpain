# Locating Faulted Nozzles

When a nozzle, or individual nozzle control (INC) unit fault is detected the operator is notified on display. Each nozzle is numbered based on its position on the spray boom. Nozzles are counted starting on the left-hand tip of the boom and ending at the right-hand boom tip.
N156561—UN—23JUL21

![[N156561.png]]
**Figure N156561** — A: Nozzle 1; B: Nozzle 97

Example: 36 m (120 ft) Boom with 38 cm (15 in) Spacing
There is a maximum of five nozzles operated by each INC unit. Four or five consecutive nozzles with a fault indicate that the fault is at the INC unit.
N157815—UN—15SEP21

![[N157815.png]]
**Figure N157815** — A: Last Nozzle on Left-Hand Boom
