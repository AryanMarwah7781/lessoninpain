# BoomTrac Settings | Center Frame

NX1458316—UN—21APR26

![[NX1458316.png]]
**Figure NX1458316** — A: Center Frame Control Toggle

Center Frame Control
Select Center Frame Control toggle (A) to change from ON to OFF.
Center Frame Control Settings (CF) ON/OFF
- When and Why to Use Each Setting
Center Frame OFF What happens:
- The center frame boom height sensor is used initially to establish the initial center frame height.
- After that, the center frame control is disabled, and only the wings adjust automatically.
When to turn Center Frame Control to Off:
- If the center frame height sensor is not well aligned with the crop row spacing (sensor should be positioned in the middle of the row for best performance).
- If tire size or spacing causes the machine to run down the canopy rows, interfering with sensor accuracy.
- If the canopy under the center frame is too noisy (e.g., uneven crop or tire tracks), leading to incorrect readings.
- Excessive dust conditions – where dust from the tires may impact the Center Frame sensor readings.
Center Frame ON What happens:
- The Center Frame Height Sensor continuously takes measurements to actively adjust and maintain proper Center Frame height while the sprayer traverses through the field.
When to use:
- When operating in dense canopy conditions to prevent wings from running uphill or downhill due to an incorrect center frame height.
- When center frame boom height sensor is positioned correctly between rows to provide consistent height measurements to the ground/canopy.
- Operating in fallow ground.
