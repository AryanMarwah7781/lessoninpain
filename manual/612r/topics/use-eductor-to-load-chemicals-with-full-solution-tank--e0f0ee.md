# Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank

CAUTION: Read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels carefully before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the solution tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1435340—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435340.png]]
**Figure NX1435340** — A: Quik-Fill Valve

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
NOTE: Solution tank is full (but has room for the volume of the chemical to be added) and engine is running.
Eductor performance can be diminished somewhat with a full tank.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
N135425—UN—22FEB18

![[N135425.png]]
**Figure N135425** — A: Handle

2. Slightly raise the eductor and pull handle (A) to unlatch.
3. Lower the eductor and raise the lid.
NX1435325—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435325.png]]
**Figure NX1435325** — A: Eductor On/Off

4. Press eductor on/off (A) on the keypad.
NX1441266—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441266.png]]
**Figure NX1441266** — A: Pump Speed Increse Button; B: Pump Speed Decrease Button

5. Adjust the eductor pressure if desired using pump speed increase button (A) or pump speed decrease button (B) on the micro display.
N135935—UN—05MAR18

![[N135935.png]]
**Figure N135935** — A: Eductor Sump Valve; B: Eductor Rinse Valve

6. Open eductor sump valve (A) (handle parallel with the solution hose) to allow chemicals to be drawn from the eductor hopper.
7. Open eductor rinse valve (B) (turn clockwise) to rinse the eductor hopper.
N155780—UN—21MAY21

![[N155780.png]]
**Figure N155780** — A: Level Gauge

NOTE: The poly eductor is equipped with a level gauge (A) to aide in filling.
8. Pour chemicals into the eductor.
N135936—UN—05MAR18

![[N135936.png]]
**Figure N135936** — A: Rinse Nozzle

9. Place the chemical container over rinse nozzle (A) in the tank and push down on the container to rinse.
10. Verify that the eductor is empty.
11. Close the eductor rinse valve and eductor sump valve.
12. Close the hopper lid and lift the eductor back to its original position.
NX1435325—UN—10DEC25
13. Press eductor on/off (A) to stop.
