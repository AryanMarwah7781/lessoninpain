# Speed Sensors Calibration Procedure

Procedure Requirements
Engine State | Running
Wheel Speed (calibrate by distance)
16 km/h (10 mph)
Wheel Speed (calibrate by Global Positioning System [GPS] signal)
32 km/h (20 mph)
Operating State | Not applying product
Ground Conditions | Firm, dry, and level
Methods of Calibration Available:
- Calibrate by GPS Signal— drive the machine at approximately 32 km/h (20 mph) in a straight line for 10 seconds and follow messages on the display.
- Calibrate by Distance— measure and mark a straight 122 m (400 ft) course. Drive course at approximately 16 km/h (10 mph) and follow messages on the display.
Calibration Overview:
NOTE: This procedure requires driving the machine on a flat surface. Ensure that adequate space is available.
