# Boom Calibration | Roll Damper Valve

This process calibrates the boom roll damper valves.
Perform Calibration When:
- BoomTrac is not performing as expected.
- Boom tilt response is too slow or too fast.
- A valve cartridge has been replaced.
- Autofold is not performing as expected.
