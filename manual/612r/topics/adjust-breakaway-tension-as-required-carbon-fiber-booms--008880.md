1. Adjust spring eyebolt nuts (D) to achieve specified breakaway tip force (C) (measured at the tip of breakaway).
Specification
Breakaway Tip Force—Force. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 245 ± 20 N (55 ± 4 lb)
NOTE: Correct rocker arm position is necessary to achieve specified tip force. If specified tip force is not obtainable, adjusting breakaway tension chain may be necessary before adjusting breakaway tension. (See Adjust Breakaway Tension Chain [As Required] in this section.)
