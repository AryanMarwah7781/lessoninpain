# Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor

CAUTION: Read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels carefully before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.

![[NX1435337.png]]
**Figure NX1435337** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

Side Quik-Fill
NX1440283—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440283.png]]
**Figure NX1440283** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

Front Quik-Fill
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running the pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
- Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
- If the solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. (See Operate Rinse System in this section.)
- Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
- The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during the entire fill procedure.
4. Open the nurse tank valve.

![[NX1435136.png]]
**Figure NX1435136** — A: Pull-On Fill Button

5. Press pull-on fill button (A) on the keypad, then open the Quik-Fill valve.

**Figure NX1440372** — A: Fill Menu Button

6. Select fill menu button (A) on the front micro display.
NX1440373—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440373.png]]
**Figure NX1440373** — A: Pull-On Fill Button

7. Select pull-on fill button (A).
NX1440374—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440374.png]]
**Figure NX1440374** — A: Tank Auto Fill Button

8. Select tank auto fill button (A) to begin filling the tank.
N98892—UN—11SEP13

![[N98892.png]]
**Figure N98892** — A: Handle

9. Slightly raise the eductor and pull handle (A) to unlatch.
10. Lower the eductor and raise the lid.
NOTE: Do not start the eductor before the solution tank is half full to prevent foaming when chemical is added. Tank level can be viewed using the micro display at filling station and the sight gauge.
Operator can choose different default eductor settings (1—3) using the CommandCenter display in the cab.
Setting 1 is the lowest pressure setting and 3 is the highest pressure setting
NX1435325—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435325.png]]
**Figure NX1435325** — A: Eductor On/Off

11. Press eductor on/off button (A) on the keypad.
NX1441266—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441266.png]]
**Figure NX1441266** — A: Pump Speed Increse Button; B: Pump Speed Decrease Button

12. Adjust the eductor pressure if desired using pump speed increase button (A) or pump speed decrease button (B) on the micro display.
N135935—UN—05MAR18

![[N135935.png]]
**Figure N135935** — A: Eductor Sump Valve; B: Eductor Rinse Valve

13. Open eductor sump valve (A) (handle parallel with the solution hose) to allow chemicals to be drawn from the eductor hopper.
14. Open eductor rinse valve (B) (turn clockwise) to rinse the eductor hopper.
N155780—UN—21MAY21

![[N155780.png]]
**Figure N155780** — A: Level Gauge

NOTE: The poly eductor is equipped with a level gauge (A) to aide in filling.
15. Pour chemicals into the eductor.
N135936—UN—05MAR18

![[N135936.png]]
**Figure N135936** — A: Rinse Nozzle

16. Place the chemical container over rinse nozzle (A) in the tank and push down on the container to rinse.
17. Verify that the eductor is empty.
18. Close the eductor rinse valve and the eductor sump valve.
19. Close the hopper lid and lift the eductor back to original position.
NX1435325—UN—10DEC25
20. Press eductor on/off (A) to stop the eductor.
NOTE: Auto fill shuts down when the upper tank level switches are reached.
21. After the solution tank has filled, close the nurse tank and Quik-Fill valves.
22. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter and replace the cap.
