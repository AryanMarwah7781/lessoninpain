# Visually Inspect Service Brake Components—100 Hours

N102222—UN—11FEB13

![[N102222.png]]
**Figure N102222** — A: Brake Pads; B: Rotor

Visually inspect brake pads (A) and rotors (B) on both front wheels for deep grooves, signs of overheating, or premature wear. If any of these conditions exists, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
