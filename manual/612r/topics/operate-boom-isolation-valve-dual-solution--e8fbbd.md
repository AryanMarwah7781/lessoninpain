# Operate Boom Isolation Valve (Dual Solution)

N163316—UN—01DEC22

![[N163316.png]]
**Figure N163316** — A: Boom Isolation Valve (2 used)

Boom isolation valves (A) stop the flow of solution from the tank and pump to the boom when closed. The valve is closed unless the pump is on and the booms are unfolded. The valve also opens when the spray system master switch is pressed while the boom is folded.
