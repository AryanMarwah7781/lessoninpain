# Transport Machine

CAUTION: DO NOT exceed maximum transport speed of the machine or loss of control can occur, causing serious injury or death.
Transport the machine at the narrowest tread adjust setting only.
NOTE: It is recommended to remove the StarFire receiver when transporting the machine. Follow all local laws to meet height requirements.
1. Be sure that a slow-moving vehicle (SMV) emblem is installed on the boom (or rear of machine).
2. Start the engine. (See Start the Engine in this section.)
N153023—UN—11NOV20

![[N153023.png]]
**Figure N153023** — A: Road Light Button; B: Hazard Light Button

3. Press the road light button (A).
4. Press the hazard light button (B).
5. Check that the amber warning lights are flashing and that all lights are clean and visible.
6. Adjust tread width to the narrowest position. (See the Chassis System Application section for tread adjust procedures.)
IMPORTANT: Machine can be damaged if transported with the booms not lowered onto the boom cradles.
Boom Return to Height will remain active after the boom is folded. When lowering boom onto cradles, boom may stop at set point if it is above support rest height. Boom can be lowered beyond set point by pressing the boom lower switch again.
7. Fold the booms into transport position.
8. If nozzles are dripping, relieve boom pressure. (See Pressure Relief in Spray System Application section.)
9. Push the transport lock switch to put the machine in transport mode.
10. Rephase the steering cylinders. (See rephasing instructions in the Check Front Axle Toe-In— Annually in Wheels and Tires section.)
N137031—UN—27APR18

![[N137031.png]]
**Figure N137031**

High Idle Switch
11. Push the high idle switch to achieve maximum engine speed.
12. Select appropriate target speed range. When the machine is started, the F1 range is automatically selected with a target speed of 16 km/h (10 mph).
IMPORTANT: Do not exceed tire weight load ratings printed in this manual. Weigh the machine and verify maximum transport weight and speed from this manual before loading and do not exceed published weights.
Refer to Wheels and Tires section of this manual for proper tire inflation recommendations. (See Safety Signs section in the manual for guidelines on transporting the machine.)
N137032—UN—27APR18

![[N137032.png]]
**Figure N137032**

Park Brake Switch
13. Press the park brake switch to release the park brakes.
CAUTION: When transporting machine downhill at transport speeds, machine tries to hold the target speed until engine begins to overspeed. At this time, the vehicle speed could increase beyond target speed. Before starting down a hill, select a speed range that is appropriate for the load on the machine and the steepness and length of the slope.
14. Push the multi-function lever slowly forward to start forward travel. Continue pushing the multi-function lever forward to increase ground speed. When engine reaches maximum rpm, rotate the speed range adjustment wheel to increase speed, if desired.
15. To reverse direction of travel, move the multi-function lever to neutral and press the brake pedal as required to bring the machine to a complete stop, then move the multi-function lever to the right and rear to back up the machine. Reverse speed available is proportional to the distance the multi-function lever is moved.
