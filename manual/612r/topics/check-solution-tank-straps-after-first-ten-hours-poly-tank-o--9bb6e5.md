# Check Solution Tank Straps—After First Ten Hours (Poly Tank Only)

1. Fill solution tank.
2. Check solution tank straps for tightness.
3. If loose:
N98930—UN—25JUN13

![[N98930.png]]
**Figure N98930** — A: Lock Nut; B: Nut

a. Loosen lock nuts (A).
b. Tighten until straps are snug and then turn nuts (B) an additional two turns.
c. Tighten lock nuts.
