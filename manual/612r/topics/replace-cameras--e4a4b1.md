# Replace Cameras

NX1436540—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1436540.png]]
**Figure NX1436540** — A: Harness Connector; B: Cap Screw (2 used); C: Nut (2 used)

NX1436534—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1436534.png]]
**Figure NX1436534** — A: Harness Connector; B: Cap Screw (2 used); C: Nut (2 used)

IMPORTANT: Avoid harness connector (A) damage. Never use force or tools on connector. Avoid camera damage. Do not remove the cap screws or brackets from the camera body. Cap screws retaining the camera body to the brackets have a specific torque. Always remove the camera brackets from the boom brackets to avoid damage to the camera.
1. Disconnect the harness connector from the camera.
2. Remove and retain cap screws (B) and nuts (C).
3. Remove the camera and bracket assembly.
4. Align the replacement camera and bracket assembly with the boom bracket and install the previously removed cap screws and nuts.
5. Connect the harness connector to the camera.
NOTE: See & Spray calibration must be performed after replacing a camera.
