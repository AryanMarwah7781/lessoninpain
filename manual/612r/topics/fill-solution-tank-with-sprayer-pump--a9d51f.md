# Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump

CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals or you can be exposed to hazardous materials that could poison and injure you.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1435337—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435337.png]]
**Figure NX1435337** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

Side Quik-Fill
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect nurse tank fill hose to Quik-Fill adapter, close lock levers.
IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
- Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
- If solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse System in this section.
- Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
- The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during entire fill procedure.
4. Open nurse tank valve.
NX1435136—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435136.png]]
**Figure NX1435136** — A: Pull-On Fill Button

5. Open Quik-Fill valve, press pull-on button (A).
NOTE: Auto fill shuts down when upper tank level switches are reached.
6. After solution tank has filled to the desired level, close nurse tank and Quik-Fill valves.
7. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter and replace cap.
