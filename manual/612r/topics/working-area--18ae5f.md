# Working Area

The working area is defined and required as follows:
- an area of 1 meter around the machine for preparation of the spraying liquid (filling work) and cleaning of the spraying apparatus
- the seat on the machine from where the crop protection work is carried out Danger zones are taken to mean:
- the working area of the operator in which preparation of the spraying liquid/dry application and cleaning of the sprayer takes place
- the “maneuvering zone” of the components of the sprayer, and in particular a 1 meter zone around the machine as a whole and the area required to fold out the spray booms or spread pattern area
