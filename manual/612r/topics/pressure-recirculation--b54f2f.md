# Pressure Recirculation

Pressure Recirculation allows you to make adjustments to the pressure setting that is maintained during solution recirculation. Pressure recirculation allows you to keep product circulating between the tank and the nozzle bodies. This allows the product to remain in suspension and prime the boom when not spraying.
NOTE: On machines equipped with dual tanks, each tank system utilizes a separate pressure recirculation system. Each system can be toggled on or off independently by navigating to the Spray System application and selecting the corresponding tank system tab.
Perform Procedure When:
- Between fields.
- Priming the boom.
