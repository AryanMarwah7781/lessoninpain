# Drain Moisture From Load Command Pump Oil—250 Hours

NOTE: Presence of moisture or milky oil can be identified as normal. Drain moisture and fill to proper oil level every 250 hours.
N122829—UN—26APR16

![[N122829.png]]
**Figure N122829** — A: Plug; B: Plug; C: Plug

1. Remove plug (A) and drain moisture collected in tube. Replace plug.
2. Remove plugs (B and C).
3. Refill pump through plug (C) to fill level (B) using GL5 80W-90 gear lube. Replace plugs.
