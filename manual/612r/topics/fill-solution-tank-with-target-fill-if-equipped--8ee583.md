# Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped

CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1435337—UN—12DEC25

![[NX1435337.png]]
**Figure NX1435337** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
NOTE: Side Quik-Fill procedure shown front fill procedure similar.
Target fill will not function when filling tank with the nurse pump (push on).
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect nurse tank fill hose to Quik-Fill adapter, close lock levers, and open nurse tank valve.
IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
- Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
- If solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse System in this section.
- Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
- The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during entire fill procedure.
NX1435136—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435136.png]]
**Figure NX1435136** — A: Pull-On Fill Button

4. Open the Quik-Fill valve and press pull-on fill button (A).
NX1440130—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440130.png]]
**Figure NX1440130** — A: Target Fill Button

5. Press target fill button (A).
NX1440131—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440131.png]]
**Figure NX1440131** — A: Enter Button; B: Clear Button; C: Target Fill Volume; D: Back Button

NOTE: If target fill volume is set using the CommandCenter display or is the same as the last loaded volume, then press enter button (A) to confirm fill volume and begin fill procedure.
6. If necessary, press clear button (B) to reset the volume and set the desired target fill volume (C) using the keypad.
7. Press the enter button to confirm and begin fill procedure.
NOTE: Press back button (D) to return to the previous screen view.
NX1440138—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440138.png]]
**Figure NX1440138** — A: Pump Speed Button; B: Cancel Button

8. During the fill procedure, press pump speed button (A) to change the speed of the solution pump.
9. Press cancel button (B) to end and exit the target fill procedure if desired.
10. After the solution tank has filled to preset level, close nurse tank and Quik-Fill valves.
11. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter and replace cap.
