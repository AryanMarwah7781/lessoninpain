# Fill Solution Tank Without Using Quik-Fill

CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals or you can be exposed to hazardous materials that could poison and injure you.
Do not enter the tank for cleaning or repair!
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
Instructions on the manufacturer's container label, regarding mixing proportions, should be read and strictly followed. Do not pour concentrate into the empty tank.
Use following procedure to fill tank through the solution tank lid:
N99948—UN—17SEP12

![[N99948.png]]
**Figure N99948** — A: Solution Tank Lid

1. Open the solution tank lid (A).
2. Fill the tank about half full with clean, clear water, or other base liquid.
3. Add the chemical concentrate.
4. Finish filling the tank with water or base liquid.
Keep spray material away from skin. If spray material comes in contact with the body, wash it off immediately with clean water and detergent.
