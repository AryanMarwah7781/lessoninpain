# Install Optional Tires—400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R

SSL212136—UN—23NOV16

![[SSL212136.png]]
**Figure SSL212136**

Decal A—Jacking Point Decal
CAUTION: Sprayer with boom weighs approximately 14 865 kg (32 777 lb). To avoid injury or death to you or others, lift and support an empty machine only. Perform the procedure using the recommended equipment available via regular special tool order channels, including approved jack bracket, spindle spacers, and 6804 kg (7.5 t[US]) jack. Support machine using JDG10393 Shop Stand, which is also available via regular special tool order channels. If recommended equipment is not available, equivalent equipment can be used. Locate the jacking point decal (A) for proper jacking location on the axle. Lift only one wheel at a time. Before jacking a wheel, turn it fully outward.
IMPORTANT: To eliminate slippage and damage to the axle, a protective plate can be used. Retain fender mounting hardware for installation of the jack bracket.

NX1432713—UN—25NOV25

![[NX1432713.png]]
**Figure NX1432713** — A: Location (2 used); B: Jack Bracket; C: Cap Screw (3 used); D: Location

NOTE: Spindle Spacers (JDG1781) and jack bracket kit are available from your John Deere dealer.
3. Inflate the air spring and install a JDG1781 Spindle Spacer on each spindle in locations (A) shown.
4. Deflate the air spring until the machine is resting on the spacers.
5. Position jack bracket (B) and install cap screws (C).
6. Raise the machine using a bottle jack at location (D).
IMPORTANT: Support machine using JDG10393 Jackstand, which has a capacity of 6804 kg (15 000 lb). Do not use JT07365 Jackstand for supporting the machine.
7. Support the machine using suitable stands.
IMPORTANT: Maximum weight of a row crop wheel and tire assembly is 380 kg (838 lb). Maximum weight of a float wheel and tire assembly is 515 kg (1136 lb).
8. Attach a suitable lifting device to the wheel and tire assembly.
N153564—UN—20JAN21

![[N153564.png]]
**Figure N153564** — A: Nut (12 used)

9. Remove nuts (A), spacers, and wheel and tire assembly.
IMPORTANT: Damage to the planetary hub and wheel can occur if the correct mounting nut torque is not maintained. Tighten nuts in a crisscross pattern to specification. Tighten wheel mounting nuts after first 1 hour of use and every 10 hours thereafter until correct torque is maintained. Check wheel nuts torque after every 100 hours of use.
10. Install wheel and tire assembly, spacers, and mounting nuts.
11. Raise the machine and remove the jackstand.
12. Lower the machine and remove the jack bracket.
13. Inflate the air spring and remove JDG1781 Spindle Spacers.
14. Tighten wheel mounting nuts (A) in a crisscross pattern to specification.
Specification
Wheel Mounting Nuts—Torque (dry). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 531 N·m (392 lb·ft)
15. Install the fender in reverse order using the following special instructions:
- Lubricate fender bracket cap screws with engine oil and install. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Fender Bracket-to-Drive Housing Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 268 N·m (198 lb·ft)
16. Repeat the procedure for the remaining tires.
