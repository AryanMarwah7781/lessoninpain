# Nozzle Boost Buttons

Nozzle Boost Buttons allow you to turn ON/OFF nozzle boost for individual nozzles. To edit nozzle boost settings, select the Edit Setup button below the nozzle boost buttons.
Available Button States:
WZ1408751—UN—09JUN25

![[WZ1408751.png]]
**Figure WZ1408751**

Nozzle Boost Inactive
Nozzle Boost Inactive— nozzle boost is turned OFF for the nozzle, but is ready to be activated. When the nozzle is inactive, press the button to turn ON nozzle boost.
WZ1408752—UN—09JUN25

![[WZ1408752.png]]
**Figure WZ1408752**

Nozzle Boost Activated
Nozzle Boost Activated— nozzle boost is turned ON for the selected nozzle. When the nozzle is activated, press the button to turn OFF nozzle boost.
WZ1408753—UN—09JUN25

![[WZ1408753.png]]
**Figure WZ1408753**

Nozzle Boost Unavailable
Nozzle Boost Unavailable— nozzle cannot be activated. The nozzle boost button appears greyed out.
