# Boom Alignment Specification Overview

NOTE: Procedure for the right-hand boom is shown. The left-hand boom is similar.
Procedure for the 40.2 m (132 ft) boom is shown. Machines with 36.6 m (120 ft) booms are similar.
For carbon fiber boom terminology, see Carbon Fiber Boom Terminology in this section.

Top View—Fore/Aft Alignment
1. Over a level surface, unfold the boom and lower to a position where the boom is accessible.
2. Verify that the boom is vertically aligned per specification with all adjustment cap screws and nuts tight.
Specification
Vertical Alignment—Distance. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 0 mm ± 50 mm (0 in ± 2.0 in)
3. Verify that the boom is horizontally aligned per specification with all adjustment cap screws and nuts tight.
Specification
Horizontal Alignment—Distance. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 0 mm ± 40 mm (0 in ± 1.6 in)
4. Verify that the breakaway boom has specified breakaway trip force (measured at the tip of breakaway).
Specification
Breakaway Trip Force—Force. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 245 ± 20 N (55 ± 4 lb)
5. Verify that the balance of the spray boom is within specification using the following special instructions:
- Unlock the spray boom suspension (lock cylinder must be disengaged completely).
- Set the boom tilt in the center position (no bias) with the multi-function lever.
- Push down on the end of the breakaway boom with enough force to bottom out the roll suspension. Boom should return within specified distance.
Specification
Vertical Roll Return—Distance. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 0 mm ± 150 mm (0 in ± 6.0 in)
