# Fill Solution Tank Without Using Quik-Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals or you can be exposed to hazardous materials that could poison and injure you.
Do not enter the tank for cleaning or repair!
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
Instructions on the manufacturer's container label, regarding mixing proportions, must be read and strictly followed. Do not pour concentrate into the empty tank.
Use the following procedure to fill the tank through the solution tank lid:
N161075—UN—03MAR22

![[N161075.png]]
**Figure N161075** — A: Solution Tank Lid (tank 1); B: Solution Tank Lid (tank 2)

1. Open the solution tank lid (A or B) for the desired tank.
IMPORTANT: Prevent damage to internal tank components and agitation nozzles. Prevent backflow from the solution tank into the filling equipment. Always keep the filling hose above the liquid in the solution tank and DO NOT insert the fill hose into the tank far enough to contact the agitation nozzles.
2. Fill the tank about half full with clean, clear water or other base liquid.
3. Add the chemical concentrate.
4. Finish filling the tank with water or base liquid.
Keep spray material away from skin. If spray material comes in contact with the body, wash it off immediately with clean water and detergent.
