# Check Torque on Fixed Tread Adjust Hardware—After First 100 Hours

N100105—UN—17SEP12

![[N100105.png]]
**Figure N100105** — A: Cap Screw

1. Check torque on cap screws (A).
Specification
Tread Adjust Fixed Hardware—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 250 N·m 184 ft.-lb.
2. If torque was not maintained cap screws must be replaced using the following procedure:
a. Remove and discard cap screws.
b. Coat new cap screws with anti-seize compound.
c. Install cap screws and tighten to specification.
d. Repeat tightening until torque is maintained.
