# Clean Flowmeter—As Required

NOTE: Standard flow pump systems have a single flowmeter. High flow systems have two flowmeters.
NX1432688—UN—25NOV25

![[NX1432688.png]]
**Figure NX1432688** — A: Flowmeter Insert; B: Flowmeter Shutoff Valve

Clean flowmeter insert (A) as needed with John Deere Brake and Parts Cleaner if the following condition exists:
- Application rates fluctuate.
1. Shut off solution pump.
CAUTION: Do not close the flowmeter shutoff valve (B) with the solution pump running. This can trap pressure in the plumbing that can be hazardous when removing flowmeters for cleaning.
2. Close flowmeter shutoff valve (B).
CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Clean flowmeter in an area where people, animals, vegetation, and water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
3. Unscrew flowmeter inserts and remove.
N136012—UN—05MAR18

![[N136012.png]]
**Figure N136012** — A: Turbine

4. Clean insert with John Deere Brake and Parts Cleaner (Part No. TY26101) by spraying cleaner through turbine (A) and bearing area.
NOTE: Clean the flowmeter thoroughly with water to ensure none of the cleaner sits in the flow meter.
5. Install insert in flowmeter.
6. Open flowmeter shutoff valve.
NOTE: If the flowmeter issue is not resolved with the brake cleaner, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider for flowmeter reconditioning.
Do not allow solution to remain in flowmeters for extended periods of time as chemicals can solidify and cause performance issues.
