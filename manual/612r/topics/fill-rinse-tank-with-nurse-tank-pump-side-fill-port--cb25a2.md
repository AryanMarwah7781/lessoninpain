# Fill Rinse Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill Port

NX1440843—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1440843.png]]
**Figure NX1440843** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open the lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
4. Open the Quik-Fill valve and nurse tank valve.
5. Open cover (C) to access the keypad and micro display.
NX1441235—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441235.png]]
**Figure NX1441235** — A: Menu Button (tank system 1); B: Menu Button (tank system 2)

6. Press menu button (A) on the keypad for the desired tank system.
NX1441236—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441236.png]]
**Figure NX1441236** — A: Rinse Tank Fill Button

7. Select rinse tank fill button (A) on the micro display to access the fill menu.
NX1441237—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441237.png]]
**Figure NX1441237** — A: Push-On Fill Button

8. Select push-on fill button (A) to begin the filling procedure.
NX1441239—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1441239.png]]
**Figure NX1441239** — A: Cancel Button

9. During the procedure, press cancel button (A) to end and exit the filling procedure if desired.
10. Monitor the liquid level in the rinse tank on the micro display. When the rinse tank contains the desired amount of liquid, shut off the nurse tank pump.
11. Close the nurse tank valve.
12. Close the Quik-Fill valve.
13. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter.
14. Install the cap on the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
