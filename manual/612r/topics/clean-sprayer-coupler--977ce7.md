# Clean Sprayer Coupler

NX1440296—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440296.png]]
**Figure NX1440296** — A: Air Seal; B: Proximity Sensor (2 used); C: Poppet

IMPORTANT: Air seal (A) and proximity sensors (B) may not function properly when covered with dirt or debris and can lead to equipment malfunction.
IMPORTANT: Do not pressure wash sprayer coupler components. Pressure washing can lead to equipment malfunction.
NOTE: Use a clean, damp cloth to clean sprayer coupler components. Air seal can be cleaned using mild detergent.
Clean air seal, proximity sensors, and poppet (C) free of debris and other loose material.
