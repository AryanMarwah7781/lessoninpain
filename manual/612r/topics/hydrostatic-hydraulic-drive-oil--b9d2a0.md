# Hydrostatic/Hydraulic Drive Oil

N150985—UN—03JUN20

![[N150985.png]]
**Figure N150985** — A: Torq-Gard 10W-30; B: SAE 10W-30; C: Plus-50 II 10W-30

Use oil viscosity based on the expected air temperature range during the period between oil changes.
The following oil is preferred:
John Deere -Torq-Gard 10W-30 or Plus-50 II 10W-30
Also acceptable:
SAE 10W-30 with API of CJ-4 or higher
