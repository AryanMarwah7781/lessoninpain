# Adjusting Steering Wheel

H127043—UN—26SEP19

![[H127043.png]]
**Figure H127043**

Tilt/Telescope
Tilt/Telescope— pull lever away from the steering column to allow adjustments. Tilt and telescope the upper steering column to the desired position. Push lever toward the steering column to lock the position.
N152696—UN—25NOV20

![[N152696.png]]
**Figure N152696**

Memory
Memory— grasp the steering wheel with both hands and push down on the foot pedal to permit free movement of the full steering column. Release the foot pedal to lock the column into the current position.
NOTE: For easy entry or exit, push down on the foot pedal and allow the column to raise completely.
