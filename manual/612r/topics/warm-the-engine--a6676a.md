# Warm the Engine

NX1396826—UN—17MAR25

![[NX1396826.png]]
**Figure NX1396826** — A: Coolant Temperature

Do not place the engine under full load until it is properly warmed. View the engine coolant temperature (A) on the primary display unit.
IMPORTANT: To provide proper lubrication after the engine starts, operate the engine at approximately 900 rpm with no load for 1—2 minutes. At temperatures below freezing point, this period must be extended to 2—4 minutes.
As the engine and hydraulic fluid warm up, cycle the steering wheel back and forth to circulate warm hydraulic fluid. Continue until steering turns freely. This allows warm hydraulic fluid to circulate more efficiently.
