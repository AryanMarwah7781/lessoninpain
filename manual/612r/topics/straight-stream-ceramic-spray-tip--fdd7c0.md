# Straight Stream Ceramic Spray Tip

N135288—UN—28FEB18

![[N135288.png]]

N135289—UN—28FEB18

![[N135289.png]]
**Figure N135289** — A: Coverage Percent; B: Distribution Profile

The straight stream ceramic tip is ideal for applying fertilizer into solid seeded crops. It creates six individual streams that distribute the fertilizer on the soil while getting less on the crop. The ceramic metering orifice and unique low pressure distribution chamber keep the streams stable to reduce atomization and prevent leaf burn and scorching. Spacing and spray height is similar to 110° broadcast tips.
