# Replace Cab Dome Light

RXA0172763—UN—03DEC19

![[RXA0172763.png]]
**Figure RXA0172763** — A: Screw (2 used); B: Harness Connector; C: Screw (3 used)

RXA0172764—UN—05DEC19

![[RXA0172764.png]]
**Figure RXA0172764** — A: Screw (2 used); B: Harness Connector; C: Screw (3 used)

1. Remove screws (A).
2. Disconnect the harness connector (B).
3. Remove screws (C) that retain the light assembly.
4. Remove and replace with a new light assembly.
5. Reassemble in the reverse order.
