# Wet System Valves

Solution Tank Sump Valve
N94211—UN—15JAN14

![[N94211.png]]
**Figure N94211** — A: Solution Tank Sump Valve

With user input, the solution tank sump valve (A) controls different loading methods when using either the solution pump or a nurse tank pump. The solution tank sump valve is also used to close off the solution tank from the rest of the wet system in case repairs are required in the system.
Opening and closing of the solution tank valve is controlled electronically by the automated wet system.
Pressure Valves
Multiple pressure valves are controlled electronically by the automated wet system and direct the flow of solution once it exits the pressure side of the solution pump.
N98843—UN—17SEP12

![[N98843.png]]
**Figure N98843** — A: Bypass Pressure Valve; B: Eductor/Tank Rinse Pressure Valve

The bypass pressure valve (A) directs flow to the solution tank when using the solution pump to load from a nurse tank.
The eductor/tank rinse pressure valve (B) directs solution to a venturi at the solution tank. This venturi creates the vacuum required to draw solution from the eductor hopper into the solution tank. The eductor/tank rinse pressure valve also directs water from the rinse tank to nozzles inside the solution tank when the rinse function is selected.
N99941—UN—17SEP12

![[N99941.png]]
**Figure N99941** — C: Agitation Pressure Valve

The agitation pressure valve (C) regulates the amount of agitation inside the solution tank.
N161074—UN—02MAR22

![[N161074.png]]
**Figure N161074** — D: Boom Isolation Valve (system 1, nozzles B); E: Boom Isolation Valve (system 2, nozzles A)

Boom Isolation Valves
The boom isolation valves (D and E) are closed to allow for cleaning strainers and for boom air purge use (if equipped).
Side Quik-Fill Valves
N161076—UN—07MAR22

![[N161076.png]]
**Figure N161076** — A: Main Quik-Fill Valve; B: Dedicated Quik-Fill Valve

Side Quik-Fill Valves
The side load station provides two Quik-Fill valves. These valves are used to connect the nurse tank hose to the sprayer. The main Quik-Fill valve can be used to fill tank 1, tank 2, or the rinse tank. The dedicated Quik-Fill valve can only be used to fill the rinse tank.
Once the nurse tank hose has been connected to the Quik-Fill valve, rotate the valve handle 90 degrees to open the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer.
After the filling procedure is completed, rotate the handle back to the closed position as shown before disconnecting the nurse tank hose.

Eductor Sump and Rinse Valves
N161079—UN—07MAR22

![[N161079.png]]
**Figure N161079** — A: Sump Valve (system 1); B: Sump Valve (system 2)

Eductor Sump Valves
Eductor Sump Valve
Eductor sump valves (A and B) allow chemical to be drawn from the bottom of the eductor hopper to the top of the associated solution tank.
N161080—UN—07MAR22

![[N161080.png]]
**Figure N161080** — A: Rinse Valve (system 1); B: Rinse Valve (system 2)

Eductor Rinse Valves
Eductor Rinse Valve
Eductor rinse valves (A and B) allow the operator to direct fluid from the rinse tank or the solution tank to the desired eductor hopper. The fluid is used to rinse the hopper and to rinse empty chemical containers.
Strainer/Flowmeter Shutoff Valve
N161081—UN—07MAR22

![[N161081.png]]
**Figure N161081** — A: Strainer/Flowmeter Shutoff Valve (system 1); B: Strainer/Flowmeter Shutoff Valve (system 2)

Shutoff Valves
The strainer/flowmeter shutoff valves (A and B) allow the operator to close the pressure lines when cleaning the pressure strainers or flowmeters. Closing the valve limits the amount of solution that drains and reduces the amount of air introduced to the system.
To open, rotate handle parallel to solution hose as shown.
To close, rotate handle perpendicular to the solution hose.
NOTE: Close strainer/flowmeter shutoff valve when filling sprayer using nurse system pump to avoid air being forced into boom.
