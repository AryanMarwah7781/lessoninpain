# Remove Liquid System

NOTE: This procedure contains steps pertaining to the removal of optional equipment (such as a foam marker) for this machine. Ignore steps that involve equipment not installed on your machine.
1. Clean the vehicle, empty the solution tank, and rinse the solution system. Drain the rinse tank and direct injection tanks (if equipped). (See Service Spray Equipment Safely in this section.)
2. Raise the center frame to the highest position.
3. Unfold the inner booms to the half-open position.
4. Adjust the tilt cylinders to set the boom to horizontal.
5. Shut off engine and remove ignition key.

6. Install lift cylinder stops (A) to the lift cylinders (B) as shown. Retain with pins (C).
NOTE: Left-hand side is shown. The right-hand side is similar.
Leave safety chains installed during the storage period to prevent movement due to a hydraulic leak down.
Protection hose may have to be shortened when using the safety chains on carbon fiber booms.

10. Install bracket (D) to the boom storage bracket using cap screw (E) and lock nut (F). Install in hole (G) for machines equipped with a carbon fiber boom or in hole (H) for machines equipped with steel booms.

20. Tighten the strap with the ratchet.

a. Disconnect fitting (B) from the strainer.
b. Secure the fittings and hose (C) to the solution system plumbing.
c. Install a plug into the strainer.
NOTE: Caps and plugs are supplied in the dry spreader bundle. If the dry spreader bundle is not ordered, then the needed caps and plugs must be acquired.
24. Install caps over both openings left by the fill hose removal.
IMPORTANT: Label all hydraulic hoses before disconnecting to ensure that they are connected to the correct port during installation of the wet system.
Case drain hose, located near the frame rail, is a low-pressure hose. Load sense hose, located in the valve block, is a high-pressure hose.
Damage to the hydraulic system can occur when hoses are not installed correctly.

NOTE: Retain the hydraulic hoses in a safe location to prevent damage during removal.
CAUTION: The onboard air system is charged at 689—896 kPa (8.9 bar) (100—130 psi). To avoid injury to yourself or others, wear safety goggles and gloves and be sure that others are clear of the area before relieving the onboard air pressure system.

30. Disconnect the air bag supply line elbow fitting (B) from T-fitting (C).
31. Disconnect T-fitting (C) from fitting (D).

34. Use tie bands to secure the line to the solution system where it will not be damaged.

36. Install the caps on the electrical connectors.

37. Remove and retain the mid-body light bracket assembly (A) and fill station light (B).
CAUTION: Crane or hoist must have a rated lifting capacity of at least 4536 kg (10 000 lb) to remove the liquid system from the machine. Verify that any additional lifting or connecting devices, such as anchor shackles or clevises, are properly rated for the job.
Faulty tank strap lift points or lift straps may allow the liquid system to fall during removal and cause severe injury or death to you or others. Inspect the tank strap lifting points and lift straps for wear before attaching and lifting.
NOTE: In order to remove the liquid system from the machine, the crane or hoist must be able to lift the liquid system at least 76.2 cm (30 in). Verify that there is sufficient clearance above the liquid system.
ONLY use a John Deere lift bar approved for this machine.
To maintain proper control of the lift bar and load balance, lifting straps or chains MUST be approximately 102 cm (40 in) long in the front and 97 cm (38 in) long in the back (poly tank only).
Verify that the lifting straps or chains are NOT twisted during installation.
