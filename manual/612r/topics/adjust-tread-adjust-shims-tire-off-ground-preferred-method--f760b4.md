# Adjust Tread Adjust Shims—Tire off Ground (Preferred Method)

IMPORTANT: Tread adjust shims must be adjusted with the machine empty and spray booms folded.
Axles must be extended to 3454 mm (136 in) to adjust the shim gap properly.
NOTE: If sticking occurs, remove debris from the axle knees and spray with a John Deere TEFLON Based Lubricant. Activate tread width adjust in and out until free.
1. Adjust tread width to 3454 mm (136 in) so that all shim hardware can be accessed.
CAUTION: Sprayer with boom weighs approximately 14 865 kg (32 777 lb) for the 412R and 16 059 kg (35 410 lb) for the 612R. To avoid injury or death to you or others, lift and support an empty machine only. Perform the procedure using recommended equipment available via regular special tool order channels, including an approved jack bracket, spindle spacers, and 6804 kg (7.5 ton) jack. Support the machine using JDG10393 Shop Stand, which is also available via regular special tool order channels. If recommended equipment is not available, equivalent equipment can be used. Locate the jacking point decal for the proper jacking location on the axle. Lift only one wheel at a time. Before jacking a wheel, turn it fully outward.
IMPORTANT: To eliminate slippage and damage to the axle, a protective plate can be used. Retain fender mounting hardware for installation of the jack bracket.

2. Remove cap screws (A), cap screws (B), fender mount bracket (C), and fender.
CAUTION: Air spring can explode if overinflated, causing serious injury or death to you or others. When adjusting suspension assemblies, do not exceed 689 kPa (6.89 bar) (100 psi). Keep hands and body away from suspension linkage.
NOTE: JDG1781 Spindle Spacers can be obtained via regular spare part channels.

3. Inflate the air spring and install a JDG1781 Spindle Spacer on each spindle in the locations (A) shown.
4. Deflate the air spring until the machine is resting on the spacers.
5. Position jack bracket (B) and install cap screws (C).
6. Raise the machine using a bottle jack at location (D).
IMPORTANT: Support the machine using JDG10393 Jackstand, which has a capacity of 6804 kg (15 000 lb). Do not use JT07365 Jackstand for supporting the the machine.
There must be no load on the wheel or knee before beginning the adjustment procedure.
7. Position a suitable jackstand under the axle on the frame.
8. Lower the machine onto the jackstand.

9. Remove cap screws (A).
10. Coat the cap screws with anti-seize compound and install loosely in the previous location.
11. Loosen lock nuts (B).
12. Remove dog point cap screws (C) and coat with anti-seize compound.
13. Install and tighten the dog point cap screws to specification. This forces the inner axle tube to the opposite side of the outer axle tube.
Specification
Dog Point Cap Screw—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 136 N·m (100 lb·ft)
14. Repeat tightening until the proper torque is maintained on all three dog point cap screws.
15. Tighten cap screws (A) to specification.
Specification
Cap Screw—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 250 N·m (184 lb·ft)
16. Loosen dog point cap screws 1/8—1/4 of a turn.
17. Tighten lock nuts (B) to specification.
Specification
Lock Nut—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 475 N·m (350 lb·ft)
18. Raise the machine and remove the jackstand.
19. Lower the machine and remove the jack bracket.
20. Inflate the air spring and remove JDG1781 Spindle Spacers.
21. Install the fender in reverse order, using the following special instructions:
- Lubricate fender bracket cap screws and install. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Fender Bracket-to-Drive Housing Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 268 N·m (198 lb·ft)
22. Repeat the procedure for the remaining axles.
