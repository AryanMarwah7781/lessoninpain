Boom Pressure Strainers
Strainers are used to help keep dirt and debris from entering and damaging the solution pump, nozzles, and other components. To maintain the proper solution flow, remove and clean the strainer screens as necessary. (See Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required and Clean Pressure Strainers— Daily in the Wet System section.)
