# Check Shim Gap on Axle Tread Adjust— After First 100 Hours

N100281—UN—25SEP12

![[N100281.png]]
**Figure N100281** — A: Gap; B: Axle Knee; C: Lower Shim Pad

Measure gap (A) between lower machined surface of axle knee (B) and upper surface of lower shim pad (C). If gap exceeds 1.5 mm (0.060 in.) at outside end of pad, adjust shim gap. (See Adjusting Shim Gap On Axle Tread Adjust in Chassis section.)
N101663—UN—28JAN13

![[N101663.png]]
**Figure N101663** — A: Gap

Measure gap (A) between axle knee and side of axle frame. Gap must be 3 mm (0.118) minimum on each side of axle knee. If gap is less than required plastic wear pads must be replaced.
