# Replace Front Grill LED Light Bulb

NX695658—UN—05DEC25

![[NX695658.png]]
**Figure NX695658** — A: LED light Bulb; B: Harness Connector

Right-Hand Side Shown
1. Raise hood.
2. Rotate LED light bulb (A) counterclockwise 1/4 turn and remove.
3. Disconnect wiring harness connector (B) by lifting retaining tab.
4. Replace LED light bulb (A).
5. Install new LED light bulb in reverse order of removal.
