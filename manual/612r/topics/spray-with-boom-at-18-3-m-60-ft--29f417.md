# Spray with Boom at 18.3 m (60 ft)

NOTE: 27.4 and 30.5 m (90 and 100 ft) boom can be folded to 18.3 m (60 ft) if desired.
1. Fold boom to 18.3 m (60 ft). See Boom Folding— 27.4, 30.5 and 36.6 m (90, 100 and 120 ft) Booms for folding instructions.
2. Disable the outer and breakaway sections using the display. (See Boom Sections Custom in CommandCenter Display Applications section.)
NOTE: Spray rate control automatically adjusts for spraying at 18.3 m (60 ft).
3. To spray with the entire boom, enable all boom sections, and unfold boom.
