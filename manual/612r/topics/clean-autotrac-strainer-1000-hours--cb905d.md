# Clean AutoTrac Strainer—1000 Hours

N136657—UN—11APR18

![[N136657.png]]
**Figure N136657** — A: Hose Fitting; B: Strainer Fitting

1. Remove hose fitting (A) from port P on the AutoTrac valve.
2. Remove strainer fitting (B).
3. Clean contaminants from the strainer.
4. Install the stainer fitting in port P of the AutoTrac valve. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Strainer Fitting—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 100 N·m 74 lb·ft
5. Install the hose fitting to the strainer fitting. Tighten to specification.
Specification
Hose Fitting—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 61 N·m 45 lb·ft
6. Verify proper hydraulic oil level in the reservoir. Add oil as necessary.
