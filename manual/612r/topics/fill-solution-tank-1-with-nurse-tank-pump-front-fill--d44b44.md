# Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent the exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440859—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1440859.png]]
**Figure NX1440859** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

Front Load Station
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the smaller front Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed.
2. Open the lock levers and remove cap (B).
3. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill valve and close the lock levers.
4. Open cover (C) for access to the micro display.
NX1440866—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440866.png]]
**Figure NX1440866** — A: Fill Button

5. Select fill button (A) to open the dual product fill menu.
NX1440867—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440867.png]]
**Figure NX1440867** — A: Tank 1 Button

6. Select tank 1 button (A) to open the tank 1 fill menu.
NX1440868—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440868.png]]
**Figure NX1440868** — A: Push-On Fill Button

7. Select push-on fill button (A) to open the fill menu for the tank 1 port.
NX1440871—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440871.png]]
**Figure NX1440871** — A: Tank Fill Button

8. Select tank fill button (A) to begin the filling procedure.
9. Open the Quik-Fill valve.
10. Open the nurse tank valve and fill tank 1 from the nurse tank.
NX1440872—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440872.png]]
**Figure NX1440872** — A: Cancel Button

NOTE: The operator must monitor the micro display to prevent overfilling the solution tank. When using an external pump source, the display shows only approximate volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor the tank fill accurately.
11. Monitor the solution level in the tank as shown on the micro display. When the tank contains the desired amount of solution, select cancel button (A) to shut off the solution tank pump.
12. Shut off the the nurse tank pump.
13. Close the nurse tank valve.
14. Close the Quik-Fill valve at the front load station.
15. Disconnect the nurse tank hose from the Quik-Fill valve.
16. Install the cap on the Quik-Fill valve and close the lock levers.
17. If necessary, purge residual solution from the front fill plumbing using the onboard air supply. (See
Perform Air Purge on Front Fill Plumbing in this section.)
