# Adjust Breakaway Section Springs--Steel Booms

IMPORTANT: Perform a breakaway stop adjustment before adjusting the breakaway spring. Stop position can affect the spring setting.
Always verify the spring setting after any adjustment to the breakaway stop.
N147285—UN—30OCT19

![[N147285.png]]
**Figure N147285** — A: Lock Nut; B: Rod Clevis; C: Adjustment Nut; D: Gap; E: Pivot Casting; F: Chain Link; G: End of Breakaway Section; H: Lock Nut; I: Eyebolt; J: Adjustment Nut

1. Loosen lock nut (A).
NOTE: Do not turn the rod clevis (B) more than 90° while adjusting the chain.
When using a heavier boom, adjust the gap smaller for optimal performance.
2. Adjust the adjustment nut (C) to obtain a gap (D) no larger than 7 mm (0.28 in) between the pivot casting (E) and chain link (F).
3. Tighten the lock nut.
4. Attach a spring scale at the outer end of the breakaway section (G).
5. Pull the breakaway section rearward using the scale. Record the force required to trip the breakaway and verify that it is within specification.
Specification
Breakaway Trip—Force. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 330-347 N (74-78 lb)
6. If the force is less than 330 N (74 lb), perform the following steps:
a. Loosen lock nut (H).
NOTE: Do not turn eyebolt (I) more than 90° while moving the adjustment nut (J).
The gap between the pivot casting and chain link must be checked every time an adjustment is made to the adjustment nut.
b. Tighten the adjustment nut.
c. Repeat step 5 and adjust if necessary.
7. If the force is greater than 347 N (78 lb), perform the following steps:
a. Loosen lock nut (H).
NOTE: Do not turn the eyebolt (I) more than 90° while moving the adjustment nut (J).
The gap between the pivot casting and chain link must be checked every time an adjustment is made to the adjustment nut.
b. Loosen the adjustment nut.
c. Repeat step 5 and adjust if necessary.
8. Once the desired force has been achieved, tighten the lock nut to specification.
Specification
Spring Adjustment Lock Nut—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 88 N·m (65 lb·ft)
