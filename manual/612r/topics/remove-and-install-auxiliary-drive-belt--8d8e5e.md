# Remove and Install Auxiliary Drive Belt

1. Park machine on a level surface, shut off engine, and remove key.
N105939—UN—05SEP13

![[N105939.png]]
**Figure N105939** — A: Cap Screw (4 used); B: Cap Screw (2 used); C: Drive Shaft Shield

2. Remove cap screws (A).
3. Loosen cap screws (B) and remove driveshaft shield (C).
IMPORTANT: Do not start machine after the driveshaft has been removed. Damage to multiple machine parts will occur.
N110647—UN—04MAR14

![[N110647.png]]
**Figure N110647** — A: Cap Screw; B: Drive Shaft

4. Remove and retain cap screws (A) from driveshaft (B). Position driveshaft to allow access to the belt.
N99112—UN—17SEP12

![[N99112.png]]
**Figure N99112** — A: Breaker Bar, 1/2 in; B: Tensioning Arm; C: Belt

5. Insert a 1/2 in breaker bar (A) into the tensioning arm (B) and rotate counterclockwise to relieve belt tension. Remove belt (C).
N97487—UN—27NOV12

![[N97487.png]]
**Figure N97487** — A: Air Compressor Pulley; B: Air Conditioning Compressor Pulley; C: Hydro Pumps Pulley; D: Tensioner Pulley

Auxiliary Belt Routing
6. Install new belt as shown.
7. Verify that belt is properly seated in all pulleys.
IMPORTANT: Apply PM37418 Thread Lock and Sealer (Medium Strength) to threads of the driveshaft cap screws and tighten to specification.
8. Install previously removed cap screws to driveshaft and tighten to specification.
Specification
Drive Shaft Cap Screw—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 108 N·m (80 lb·ft)
9. Install previously removed cap screws, nuts, and bracket.
10. Install previously removed cap screws, nuts, and hose supports.
11. Install previously removed driveshaft shield and tighten retaining hardware.
