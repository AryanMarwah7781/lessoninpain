# Install Liquid System

IMPORTANT: Mid-body light bracket assembly must be removed to avoid interference when installing the liquid system.

1. If installed, remove and retain the mid-body light bracket assembly (A) and fill station light (B).

N102740—UN—19JUL13

![[N102740.png]]
**Figure N102740** — A: Lift Bar; B: Rear Support Bar; C: Front Lift Points; D: Lifting Straps

Lifting Points at Front of Liquid System (stainless)
NOTE: This procedure contains steps pertaining to the installation of optional equipment for this machine. Ignore steps that involve equipment that is not being installed on your machine.
CAUTION: Crane or hoist must have a rated lifting capacity of at least 4536 kg (10 000 lbs) to install the liquid system to the machine. Verify that any additional lifting or connecting devices, such as anchor shackles or clevises, are properly rated for the job.
Faulty tank strap lift points or lift straps may allow the liquid system to fall during installation and cause severe injury or death to you or others. Inspect the tank strap lifting points and lift straps for wear before attaching and lifting.
NOTE: In order to install the liquid system on the machine, the crane or hoist must be able to lift the liquid system at least 76.2 cm (30 in). Verify that there is sufficient clearance above the liquid system.
ONLY use a John Deere lift bar approved for this machine.
To maintain proper control of the lift bar and load balance, lifting straps or chains MUST be approximately 102 cm (40 in) long in the front and 97 cm (38 in) long in the back (poly tank only).
Verify that lifting straps or chains are NOT twisted during installation.
2. Attach lift bar (A) to rear support bar (B) and front lift points (C) using properly rated lifting straps or chains (D).
3. Carefully lift and set the liquid system on the machine.
IMPORTANT: Do not reuse hardware. Always use new hardware when installing the liquid system.
To prevent axle guide plate deformation, DO NOT TIGHTEN any hardware until all hardware and shims are installed.
NOTE: Right side of the machine is shown.

**Figure N100990** — A: Front Mounting Cap Screw (2 used); B: Middle Mounting Cap Screw (6 used); C: Rear Mounting Cap Screw (4 used)

4. Loosely install retaining hardware (A and B) on both sides of the machine.
5. Loosely install rear mounting hardware (C) at the top of the rear axle only.
6. Install the provided shims, utilizing the “hook” portion of the shims to fill gaps between the axle guide plates and skid. The shims minimize guide plate deformation when hardware is tightened.
7. Once gaps have been filled with shims, install the remaining lower mounting hardware (C) at the bottom of the rear axle.
8. Starting from the rear of machine, tighten retaining hardware (A—C) on both sides of the machine to specification.
Specification
Mounting Cap Screws—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 425 N·m (315 lb·ft)

![[N100924.png]]

9. Remove caps and plugs from hoses and fittings.
IMPORTANT: Ensure that hose labels are installed to corresponding ports on the machine.
Damage to the hydraulic system can occur if hoses are not installed correctly.
Case drain hose, located near the frame rail, is a low pressure hose. Load sense hose, located in the valve block, is a high pressure hose.
10. Install four hydraulic hoses (A) to the hydraulic ports on the underside of the sprayer.

15. Install the mid-body light bracket assembly (A) and fill station light (B).
16. Remove caps from the electrical connectors.
N100881—UN—30OCT12

![[N100881.png]]
**Figure N100881** — A: 30-Pin Connector; B: 9-Pin Connector; C: Electrical Connector; D: Electrical Connector

17. Connect electrical connectors (A—C). Install CAN termination resistor to connector (D).
N116113—UN—25FEB15

![[N116113.png]]
**Figure N116113** — A: Boom Air Purge Line; B: Elbow Fitting; C: T-Fitting; D: Fitting

18. Locate where the boom air purge supply line (A) with T-fitting (C) is tie banded to the solution system.
19. Disconnect elbow fitting (B) from fitting (D).
20. Connect T-fitting (C) to fitting (D).
21. Connect elbow fitting (B) to T-fitting (C).
22. Tie band boom air purge line (A) as necessary.
CAUTION: On board air system is charged at 689—896 kPa (8.9 bar) (100—130 psi). To avoid injury to yourself or others, wear safety goggles and gloves and be sure that others are clear of the area before relieving the on board air system pressure.
23.

![[N122835.png]]

For machines without product reclaim:
a. Relieve the air system pressure by opening drain valve (A).
b. Remove fitting (B) from the air tank and connect the air line (if equipped).
24.

a. Relieve the air system pressure by opening drain valve (A).
b. Disconnect the front fill air line (if equipped) from T-fitting (B).
c. Remove and retain the T-fitting from the air tank.

d. Route the reclaim hoses (A) and fittings to the air tank bracket as shown.
e. Secure the fittings to the air tank bracket using tie bands (B).
N155479—UN—11MAY21

![[N155479.png]]

f. Connect fitting (A) to the air tank.
g. Install previously retained T-fitting (B) to the fitting assembly as shown.
h. Connect the front fill and other air lines (if equipped) to the previously installed T-fitting.
N155471—UN—11MAY21

![[N155471.png]]
**Figure N155471** — A: Wiring Harness; B: Pressure Sensor

i. Route the wiring harness (A), as shown, and connect to pressure sensor (B).
IMPORTANT: Tie band hoses as necessary to prevent damage.
25. Disconnect all chains and lifting straps from the liquid system.
26. Raise the center frame to the highest position.
27. Remove lift cylinder stops from the lift cylinders.
N122094—UN—21MAR16

![[N122094.png]]
**Figure N122094** — A: Strap; B: Ratchet

28. Loosen ratchet (B) and remove the strap (A).
29. Remove the strap assemblies from the booms.
30. Unfold the inner booms to the half open position.
31. Remove the boom storage bracket from the solution skid cross-member on each side of the machine.
32. Fold the inner wings to the previous position.
33. Position boom sections to relieve tension on the safety chains. Remove the safety chains.
34. Fold the boom to the transport position.
35. Add water to the solution tank and flush the system.
