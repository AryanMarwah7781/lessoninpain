# Troubleshooting Load Command Pump

Symptom | Problem | Solution
Pump does not achieve full rate.
Restriction on nurse vehicle plumbing. Verify that nurse tank vent is open and adequate.
Pump running at reduced speed for entire fill due to lack of 265 L (70 gal) tank level signal.
Check 265 L (70 gal) tank level sensor function.
Pump running at reduced speed for entire fill due to wrong signal from 4353 L (1150 gal) for the 612R tank level sensor, and 5867 L (1550 gal) for the 616R tank level sensor.
Check 4353 L (1150 gal) for the 612R tank level sensor, and check 5867 L (1550 gal) for the 616R tank level sensor.
Symptom | Problem | Solution
Improper Load Command pump calibration.
Perform pump calibration.
Pump does not achieve full rate and makes noise during start of fill.
Pump is cavitating, not priming. | Verify that pump is getting adequate
solution flow.
Excessive continued knocking of the pump.
Knock sensor not functioning correctly.
Check knock sensor connection. (Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.)
Pump locks up or has start-up difficulties due to swelling lobes.
Incompatible chemical with the EPDM pump lobes.
Use correct grease per Operator's Manual.
Properly rinse system after each chemical use.
Do not let pump sit idle with the chemical in it.
Replace pump lobes with Viton or replacement EPDM lobes from service parts.
Pump running but not loading.
Valve to the nurse tank closed. | Check nurse-side valves.
Shutoff valve for the poppet cylinder is closed.
Fully open the valve.
Pump lost prime (most likely to occur after major servicing).
Add 3.8 L (1 gal) of water to priming port on the pump outlet.
