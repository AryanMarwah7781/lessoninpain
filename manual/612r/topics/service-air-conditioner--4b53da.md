# Service Air Conditioner

X9811—UN—23AUG88

![[X9811.png]]
**Figure X9811**

CAUTION: Escaping refrigerant under pressure can penetrate eyes and skin causing serious injury to you or others. Do not attempt to repair or service refrigerant system. (Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.)
IMPORTANT: R-134a refrigerant must be used. This requires special equipment and procedures. (Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.)
NOTE: Some oil seepage from the compressor shaft seal on the lower front is normal.
Check the following if air conditioner will not cool or cooling is intermittent:
If air conditioner clutch slips after machine has been in storage, compressor may be stuck. Stop engine and turn key switch to OFF position. Remove clutch cover. Rotate clutch hub back and forth to free compressor.
Run engine at 2000 rpm. Turn on the air conditioner and set the fan to high speed. (See HVAC section.)
If cooling is intermittent, clean grille, side screens, radiator, and condenser. If the problem persists, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider to have the evaporator core cleaned.
Inspect cab body filters for restriction. Replace filters if any damage or blockage is detected. (See Checking And Replacing Cab Air Filters in this section.) If the problem is not solved, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
