# Adjust Front Axle Toe-In

N90859—UN—07JAN11

![[N90859.png]]
**Figure N90859** — A: Clamp; B: Cylinder Rod

1. Loosen clamp (A).
2. Adjust cylinder length by turning cylinder rod (B).
3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 for the opposite side of the machine.
N135938—UN—07MAR18

![[N135938.png]]
**Figure N135938** — A: Dimension; B: Dimension

4. Move left and right tires evenly until difference between (A and B) are within specified range.
Specification
Front Wheels—Toe-In. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 0—10 mm (0—0.375 in)
NOTE: With cylinders at half stroke, dimension (B) should be 0—10 mm (0—0.375 in) less than dimension (A).
5. Tighten clamp on each side of machine.
6. Perform Check Front Axle Toe-In procedure to verify adjustments.
