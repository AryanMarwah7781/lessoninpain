# Start the Engine

TS220—UN—15APR13
CAUTION: Prevent asphyxiation. Engine exhaust fumes can cause sickness or death to you or others.
If you must operate engine in a building, be positive there is adequate ventilation. Either use an exhaust pipe extension to remove the exhaust fumes or open doors and windows to bring enough outside air into the area.
Avoid the possibility of personal injury or death:
- Be sure that everyone is clear of the machine.
- Start engine only from the operator’s seat.
-
TS177—UN—11JAN89
Do not start engine by shorting across starter terminals. Machine can start in gear if the normal circuitry is bypassed.
- Engine starting with the multi-function lever out of the “Park” position indicates a malfunction of the starting circuit. Repairs should be made immediately by a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
NOTE: Onboard electronics can require slightly longer starting times during the engine start-up.
N155095—UN—30MAR21

![[N155095.png]]
**Figure N155095** — A: Multi-Function Lever

1. Make sure that the multi-function lever (A) is in the Park position.
2. Sound the horn.
3. Turn the key switch to the Accessory/Run position.
IMPORTANT: Do not operate the starter more than 30 seconds at a time or starter can be damaged. If engine does not start, wait at least 2 minutes before trying again. If engine does not start in four attempts, refer to Troubleshooting section.
To assure adequate lubrication after engine starts, idle engine at approximately 900 rpm with no load for 1—2 minutes. Extend this period when operating at temperatures below freezing.
When starting the machine during cold temperatures, a starting aid may be necessary. (See Cold-Weather Starting—9.0 L Engine with Starting Aid in this section.)
4. Turn the key switch clockwise to the Start position, engaging the starter. Release key switch when engine starts. If the key switch is released before the engine starts, wait until the starter and engine stop turning before trying again.
5. When the engine has started, stop and caution indicator lights go off when the key switch is released.
NX1396826—UN—17MAR25

![[NX1396826.png]]
**Figure NX1396826** — A: Coolant Temperature

6. View the engine coolant temperature (A) on the corner post display.
IMPORTANT: Protect turbocharger during start-up by not accelerating above 1000 rpm until normal engine idle speed is established (900 rpm).
If the engine stalls while operating under load, restart it immediately to prevent overheating and possible turbocharger damage.
