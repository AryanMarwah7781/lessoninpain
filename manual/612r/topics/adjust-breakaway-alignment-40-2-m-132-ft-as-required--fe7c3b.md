# Adjust Breakaway Alignment—40.2 m (132 ft) (As Required)

NOTE: Procedure for the left-hand boom is shown. The right-hand boom is similar.
When necessary, clean the machine of pesticides. (See Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides in the Safety section.)
Breakaway Locking Pin Alignment
1. Unfold the boom and lower to a comfortable working level.

2. Visually inspect the alignment of parts (A—C). With the breakaway tension spring properly adjusted, pin (C) must be free to push through the hole in the curved bracket (B) and fully seat in the hole in the lower bracket (A).
NOTE: If visual inspection is not possible, remove parts (E—H) and manually press the pin. If removed, tighten the plunger (E) to specification.
Specification
Solenoid Plunger—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 62 N·m (46 lb·ft)
3. Adjust fold stop (D) as needed to achieve proper operation.
4. Tighten the lock nut on the fold stop when finished.

1. Visually inspect alignment of parts (A and B). With the breakaway tension spring properly adjusted, the centerline of the breakaway is aligned to the centerline of the outer 2 boom.
2. Adjust fold stop (D) as needed to achieve proper alignment.
3. Adjust fold stop (E) until initial contact with the breakaway is made.
4. Tighten the lock nuts on the fold stops.
5. Loosen lock nut (F) and adjust stop bolt (G) until initial contact with the fold link is made.
6. Tighten the lock nut.
