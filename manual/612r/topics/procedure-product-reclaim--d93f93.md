# Procedure | Product Reclaim

Product Reclaim allows you to clear the majority of the remaining solution from the boom back to the tank.
NOTE: Pump reclaim only clears solution from the pump and the spray line to the boom. Other circuits, such as agitation, are not reclaimed as part of the procedure.
Use Product Reclaim:
- At the end of each day.
- When changing product.
- Before starting a rinse cycle.
- To recover product remaining in the boom sections.
- To clear boom sections of product after spraying.
