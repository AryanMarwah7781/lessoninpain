# Bluetooth Compliance Information

N130203—UN—04MAY17

![[N130203.png]]
**Figure N130203** — A: Compliance Information

The following outlines the necessary compliance information regarding the remote control and receiver and the use of Bluetooth technology on the ExactApply system. Compliance information (A) is located on the Bluetooth receiver on the rear of the machine. Compliance information is listed by country.
