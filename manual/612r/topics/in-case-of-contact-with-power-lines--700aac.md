# In Case of Contact with Power Lines

CAUTION: Risk of Electric Shock
Contact with overhead or underground power lines or electrical arcing can cause serious injury or death. If power lines touch the ground, dangerous voltage differences can exist on the ground (step voltage).
If the machine contacts a power line or electrical arcing occurs:
- If circumstances allow, stay inside the cab.
- Alert local emergency services.
- If the machine can be moved safely, attempt to drive it away from the power line; otherwise, wait for the power line to be deenergized.
- Warn others (for instance bystanders) to stay at least 10 m (33 ft) away from the machine and the surrounding area.
Leave the cab ONLY if absolutely necessary, for example if the machine catches fire.
- If you must leave the cab, do not touch the machine and the ground at the same time.
- Jump clear of the machine without touching it. Land on the ground with feet together.
- Carefully walk at least 10 m (33 ft) away from the machine and any power lines on the ground with small steps by paying attention that feet touch one another to reduce the risk of step voltage.
