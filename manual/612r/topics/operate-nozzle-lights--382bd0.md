# Operate Nozzle Lights

1. Set up the nozzle lights. (See Nozzle Body Lighting in the Boom and Nozzles Application section.)
N154924—UN—11MAR21

![[N154924.png]]
**Figure N154924** — A: Spray System On/Off Switch

2. Press spray system On/Off switch (A) to begin application. Nozzle lights activate in the previously selected mode.
NOTE: Nozzle lights turn off when the nozzles are not spraying.
Nozzle lights are also used to indicate nozzle bodies with faults present. Fault indication occurs when the spray system On/Off switch is ON or the operator selects the fault display button on the display. Nozzle lights flash at a slower rate than the strobe setting to indicate a hardware fault. Software faults are not indicated.
Nozzle lights indicate the fault until disabled by the operator or until 2 minutes have elapsed since the fault occurred.
Fault indication is enabled for all nozzles at all times regardless of section control, IBS, or CommandARM section switch status.
NOTE: Nozzle light and lens are not replaceable. The complete nozzle body must be replaced if the light burns out.
