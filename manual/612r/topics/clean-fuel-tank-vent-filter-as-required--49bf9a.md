# Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required

NOTE: Use a commercial ladder to access fuel tank vent filter.
N153545—UN—18NOV20

![[N153545.png]]
**Figure N153545** — A: Cap Screw (2 used)

1. Remove cap screws (A).
N153546—UN—18NOV20

![[N153546.png]]
**Figure N153546** — A: P-Clamp; B: Fuel Tank Vent Filter

2. Lift cover, then remove and retain P-clamp (A).
3. Remove fuel tank vent filter (B) and clean in soapy detergent solution.
4. Blow dry with compressed air and install.
5. Retain fuel tank vent filter with previously removed P-clamp.
6. Install cover.
