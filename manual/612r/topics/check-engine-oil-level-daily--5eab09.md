# Check Engine Oil Level—Daily

1. Park machine on level ground. Stop the engine and remove key.
N153905—UN—27JAN21

![[N153905.png]]
**Figure N153905** — A: Fill Cap; B: Dipstick; C: ADD Hole; D: FULL Hole

N110836—UN—19MAR14

![[N110836.png]]
**Figure N110836** — A: Fill Cap; B: Dipstick; C: ADD Hole; D: FULL Hole

NOTE: Dipstick (B) is attached to the fill cap.
2. Remove fill cap (A) and clean oil from dipstick.
IMPORTANT: Fill cap must be fully tightened to provide an accurate oil level reading.
3. Install fill cap, then remove and check the oil level on the dipstick.
4. Oil level is adequate if the level is between the ADD hole (C) and FULL hole (D).
5. If the oil level is below the ADD hole, then add engine oil and recheck until the level is adequate. (See Diesel Engine Oil—Interim Tier 4, Final Tier 4, Stage IIIB, Stage IV, and Stage V in Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section of this manual.)
IMPORTANT: Do not operate the engine with the oil level below the “ADD” mark on dipstick.
Do not overfill oil level.
Acceptable oil usage is 1 gallon of oil added per 400 gallons of diesel fuel consumed. If usage is more, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
After a long storage period:
Check whether dirt or other foreign bodies have collected under the hood or cab. If so, remove them.
