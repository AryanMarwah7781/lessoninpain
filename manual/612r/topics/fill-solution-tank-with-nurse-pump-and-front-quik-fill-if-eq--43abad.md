# Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped

CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440283—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440283.png]]
**Figure NX1440283** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap.
3. Connect nurse tank fill hose to Quik-Fill adapter, close lock levers, and open nurse tank valve.
NX1440372—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440372.png]]
**Figure NX1440372** — A: Fill Menu Button

4. Select fill menu button (A) on the front micro display.
NX1440377—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440377.png]]
**Figure NX1440377** — A: Push-On Fill Button

5. Select pull-on fill button (A).
NX1440374—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440374.png]]
**Figure NX1440374** — A: Tank Auto Fill Button

6. Select tank auto fill button (A) to begin filling the tank.
NX1440382—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440382.png]]
**Figure NX1440382** — A: Cancel Button

7. During the procedure, select cancel button (A) to end and exit the filling procedure.
NOTE: Automatic fill is not active when filling with a nurse pump. The Operator must monitor the micro display. When using an external pump source, the sight gauge gives only approximate volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor the tank fill accurately.
8. Fill to the desired level using nurse pump.
9. Shut down nurse pump and close nurse tank valve.
10. Close Quik-Fill valve and move switch to OFF position.
