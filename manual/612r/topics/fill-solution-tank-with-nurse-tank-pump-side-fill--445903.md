# Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the solution tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440843—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1440843.png]]
**Figure NX1440843** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
1. Verify that Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed.
2. Open the lock levers and remove cap (B).
3. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
4. Open the Quik-Fill valve.
5. Lift cover (C) for access to the system keypads and micro displays.
NX1434997—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1434997.png]]
**Figure NX1434997** — A: System 1 Keypad; B: System 2 Keypad; C: Push-On Button (tank system 1); D: Push-On Button (tank system 2)

6. To fill tank 1, press push-on button (C) on system 1 keypad (A).
7. To fill tank 2, press push-on button (D) on system 2 keypad (B).
NX1440374—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440374.png]]
**Figure NX1440374** — A: Tank Fill Button

8. Press tank fill button (A) on the micro display for the selected tank system to begin the filling procedure.
NOTE: Operator must monitor the solution tank content volume to prevent overfilling solution tank. When using an external pump source, the sight gauge can give erratic volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor tank fill accurately.
9. Open the nurse tank valve and start the nurse tank pump.
10. Monitor the solution level in the tank on the micro display and fill to the desired level, then shut off the nurse tank pump.
11. Close the nurse tank valve.
12. Close the Quik-Fill valve.
NX1440858—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440858.png]]
**Figure NX1440858** — A: Cancel Button

13. Close the tank fill selection valve by pressing cancel button (A) on the micro display.
14. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter.
15. Install the cap on the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
