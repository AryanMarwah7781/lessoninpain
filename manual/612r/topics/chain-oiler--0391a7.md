# Chain Oiler

Chain Oiler lubricates the conveyor drive chain. Conveyor drive chain must be properly lubricated to prevent premature wear and failure.
Perform Procedure:
- Daily.
- At the beginning of the season.
Items Accessible on Chain Oiler Page:

Advanced Settings

Chain Oiling Procedure:
CAUTION: The belt begins movement as soon as the button is pressed. Product discharge may occur if the spinners are still enabled.
If Equipped with Spinners: Spinners cannot be shielded due to their function. Keep away from the spinners while they are turning.
NOTE: An oiling cycle is approximately two conveyor revolutions. Conveyor must be running to perform oiling cycle.
