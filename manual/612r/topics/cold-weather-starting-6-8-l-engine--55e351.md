# Cold-Weather Starting—6.8 L Engine

CAUTION: Do not use ether or starting fluids to start engine with a cold-weather starting device. Ether is highly flammable and could ignite on engines with cold-weather starting device.
IMPORTANT: Never use ether starting fluid to start engine. Damage to engine results.
1. Turn off switches, including the radio and dome light.
2. Place the multi-function lever in the Park position.
3. Set the engine speed to low idle.
4. Turn the key switch to the Accessory/Run position.
NX1396830—UN—17MAR25

![[NX1396830.png]]
**Figure NX1396830** — A: Glow Plug Indicator

5. Glow plug indicator (A) illuminates if the fuel temperature is -5°C (23°F) or below. (WAIT TO START THE ENGINE; PREHEATING IS ACTIVE.)
IMPORTANT: Avoid starter damage. Do not operate the starter for more than 30 seconds at a time. If engine does not start, wait at least 2 minutes before trying again.
6. When the glow plug indicator goes out, turn the key switch to the Start position to engage the starter. Release the key switch when the engine starts.
