Water Separator—9.0 L Engine
IMPORTANT: When draining water from fuel filters, the drain valve must be unthreaded completely and the valve must drop down approximately 12 mm (0.5 in) to properly drain water from the filter.
Drain water and contaminates from the water separator bowl by opening the drain valve (A) on the primary filter (C).
IMPORTANT: Replace fuel filters if an excessive amount of water or sediment is present. (See Replacing Fuel Filters in the Engine and Drivetrain section.)
