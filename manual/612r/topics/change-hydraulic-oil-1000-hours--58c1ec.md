# Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours

NOTE: Approximately 98 L (26 gal) of John Deere Torq-Gard Supreme 10W30 oil is required to fill the hydraulic reservoir.
1. Place the booms in the boom rests and retract all the hydraulic cylinders, including the tread adjust cylinders.
2. Park machine on a flat, level surface.
N153556—UN—25NOV20

![[N153556.png]]
**Figure N153556** — A: Plug; B: Shutoff Valve

3. Remove the plug (A) from the reservoir drain hose.
4. Drain fluid by opening the shutoff valve (B).
5. Close the shutoff valve.
6. Install the plug into the drain hose.
N153557—UN—25NOV20

![[N153557.png]]
**Figure N153557** — A: Sight Gage; B: Fill Cap; C: Decal

IMPORTANT: Fill cap (B) contains the hydraulic reservoir breather filter and must be replaced when the hydraulic oil is changed.
7. Remove the fill cap and fill the hydraulic reservoir with the specified hydraulic oil. Verify that the oil showing in the sight gauge (A) is at the proper level for the current oil temperature as shown on the fill level decal (C). Do not install the reservoir cap at this time.
N121582—UN—10FEB16

![[N121582.png]]
**Figure N121582** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle

8. Install a 6894 kPa (68.9 bar) (1000 psi) pressure gauge to the charge pressure receptacle (A).
N135939—UN—05MAR18

![[N135939.png]]
**Figure N135939** — A: Fuel Injector Harness

6.8 L Engine
N135940—UN—05MAR18

![[N135940.png]]
**Figure N135940** — A: Fuel Injector Harness

9.0 L Engine
9. Disconnect fuel injector harness (A) on the cylinder head.
NOTE: The following steps require two people.
10. Turn the ignition key for 15 seconds at 1-minute intervals. Monitor the charge pressure while starting the engine. Repeat until the charge pressure consistently reaches the specified pressure.
Specification
Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 207— 345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi)
11. Turn the ignition key for three additional 15-second cycles.
12. Check the oil level and add as required.
13. Reconnect the fuel injector harness.
CAUTION: Avoid injury or death to others. Keep away all bystanders while performing the start-up procedure. Machine could move unexpectedly.
14. Start the engine and run at a low idle for 5 minutes.
15. Check the charge pressure and verify that it is within specification.
Specification
Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 2344—2827 kPa (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi)
16. Check the oil level and add as required.
17. Operate the engine at a high idle.
18. Verify that the machine drive system operates properly. Move the machine in forward and reverse directions.
19. Check for leaks.
20. Check the oil level and add as required. Install the reservoir cap.
