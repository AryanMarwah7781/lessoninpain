# Adjust Inner Fold Cylinder

Scenario 1
1. Level and raise boom to highest position and fold in against boom stops.
N99932—UN—17SEP12

![[N99932.png]]
**Figure N99932** — A: Lock Nut; B: Cylinder Rod

2. Loosen lock nut (A) and turn the cylinder rod (B) (in or out as needed) until boom is directly above boom rest and will not bind when lowered into the transport position.
3. Tighten lock nut to specification.
Specification
Inner Fold Cylinder Lock Nut—Torque. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 508 N·m (375 lb·ft)
Scenario 2
NOTE: Operator hears banging noise when moving forward from a stop position or when operating in the field. The suspended center frame has limited clearance from the fixed center frame and can swing if not properly aligned vertically.
N120724—UN—29FEB16

![[N120724.png]]
**Figure N120724** — A: Suspended Center Frame; B: Wear Pad

1. Check suspended center frame (A) for signs of an impact with the fixed center frame at the bottom of the boom.
NOTE: For proper vertical alignment, the suspended center frame should contact the wear pads (B) at the top of the fixed center frame.
2. If not, lengthen the inner fold cylinders to maintain the center frame center of gravity.
3. Extend the inner fold cylinder evenly on both sides until light contact is maintained with wear pads in both the folded and unfolded positions.
