# Check & Adjust Rear Axle Toe-In— (Annually or As Needed)

1. Before beginning, check the tire pressure in all tires, ensure the tire pressure is the same for each.
NOTE: Check toe-in with the machine at tread spacing most often used when operating sprayer.
NX1439536—UN—09JAN26

![[NX1439536.png]]
**Figure NX1439536** — A: Prime Rear Steer System

2. Use the Prime Rear Steer System (A) routine to perform an air purge. Navigate to the routine from the display: (Menu>System>Diagnostics Center>Controllers>AutoTrac Controller (ATX) >Calibrations>1101 Prime Rear Steer System).
NX1439537—UN—09JAN26

![[NX1439537.png]]
**Figure NX1439537** — A: Rear Steer Calibration

3. If measuring/adjusting toe to diagnose performance issues or following a replacement of the steering valve, steering solenoid(s), wheel angle sensor(s), wheel angle sensor linkage, or steering cylinder(s), perform the Rear Steer Calibration (A) routine prior to taking measurements. Navigate to the routine from the display: (Menu>System>Diagnostics Center>Controllers > AutoTrac Controller (ATX) >Calibrations> 1102 Rear Steer Calibration).
4. Disengage four-wheel steer and position wheels for straight ahead travel. Drive forward 6 m (20 ft) and park on a flat surface.
NX1441228—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1441228.png]]
**Figure NX1441228** — A: Distance; B: Front Measurement; C: Rear Measurement

NX1440658—UN—19JAN26

![[NX1440658.png]]
**Figure NX1440658** — A: Distance; B: Front Measurement; C: Rear Measurement

IMPORTANT: Measurements must be taken from the flat area of the rim and not the edge of the bead or the side of the tire.
5. Using a tape measure, measure the distance (A) from the ground to the center of the hub.
6. Using that measurement, mark the front (B) and rear (C) centerline of the rim on the flat area of the rim using tape.
NOTE: A laser measuring device can be used to increase accuracy.
NX1439539—UN—09JAN26

![[NX1439539.png]]
**Figure NX1439539** — A: Diagonal Measurement; B: Diagonal Measurement

7. Measure from mark-to-mark diagonally, as shown with lines (A and B), to verify that wheels are straight ahead. Measurement lengths should be equal within 6.3 mm (0.25 in) of each other.
Measurement (A) - Measurement (B) = 6.3 mm (0.25 in) or less
NX1439540—UN—09JAN26

![[NX1439540.png]]
**Figure NX1439540** — C: Front Measurement; D: Rear Measurement

8. If measurements are not within specification perform the measurements for left front to right front and left rear to right rear across from mark-to-mark on the wheel rims as shown by lines C and D.
9. Record the measurements for each:
Line | Measurement
A
B
C
D

![[NX1439765.png]]
**Figure NX1439765** — A: Diagonal measurement; B: Diagonal measurement; C: Front Measurement; D: Rear Measurement

NOTE: If both wheels are out of alignment, adjust one wheel at a time. First, center the wheel that is believed to be more out of alignment.
10. Determine which of these scenarios matches what was measured.
NX1439538—UN—09JAN26

![[NX1439538.png]]
**Figure NX1439538** — A: Rear Left Wheel Alignment Calibration; B: Rear Right Wheel Alignment Calibration

11. Once you have determined which wheel angle needs adjusted. Navigate to the Rear Left and Right Wheel Alignment Calibration. (Menu>System>Diagnostics Center>Controllers > AutoTrac Controller (ATX)>Calibrations).
NX1439539—UN—09JAN26
12. Use the calibration routine to adjust the left or right rear wheel angle as needed. Once complete, repeat the diagonal measurements to ensure the wheels are now straight.
- If the right wheel is toed out, click on the 1104 calibration, holding the autotrac resume button, steer left to bring the right wheel back to center.
- If the left wheel is toed in, click on the 1103 calibration, holding the autotrac resume button, steer left to bring the left wheel back to center.
Specification
Rear Wheels—Diagonal Measurement—Length. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 6.3 mm (0.25 in)
NX1439540—UN—09JAN26
13. With the rear wheels confirmed to be straight, perform the measurements for left front to right front and left rear to right rear across from mark-to-mark on the wheel rims as shown by lines C and D.
IMPORTANT: For toe-in measurements, always take the rear measurement minus the front measurement. If the rear measurement (D) minus the front measurement (C) produces a negative number, this would indicate a toe-out condition.
14. Subtract front spacing measurement from rear spacing measurement to obtain toe-in dimension. Dimension (C) must be the specified amount less than dimension (D). Record the difference in dimensions.
Rear Spacing - Front Spacing = Toe
15. Without adjusting the steering, start the machine and drive forward until the wheels have rotated 180 degrees. The tape marks previously applied to the wheel should be parallel with the floor and can be checked using the floor to hub measurement taken in earlier in the procedure.
16. Repeat the parallel measurements from the rear of the left wheel to the rear of the right wheel (D) and front left to front right (C).
17. Again, Subtract the front measurement from the rear measurement and record the results.
[Toe + Toe]/2= Average Toe
18. Once completed, use the above equation to average the toe measured for step 14 and step 16. Verify the average toe measurement is within specification.
Specification
Rear Wheels—Parallel Measurement—Length. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 10 mm (0.375 in)
19. If dimensions are not within specified range, Navigate to the Rear Left and Right Wheel Alignment Calibration. Adjust both the rear left and rear right equally until the toe is within specification.
20. Repeat measurements as needed to ensure adjustments correctly set the toe within specification.
