# Attach Jack Bracket

CAUTION: Sprayer with boom weighs approximately 16 400 kg (36 080 lb). To avoid injury or death to you or others, lift and support an empty machine only. Perform the procedure using recommended equipment available via regular special tool order channels, including an approved jack bracket, spindle spacers, and 6804 kg (7.5 ton) jack. Support machine using JDG10393 Shop Stand, which is also available via regular special tool order channels. Please, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. Lift only one wheel at a time. Before jacking a wheel, turn it fully outward.
IMPORTANT: To eliminate slippage and damage to the axle, use a protective plate.
1. Empty the solution tank (if necessary).
NOTE: Retain fender mounting hardware for installation of the jack bracket.

2. Remove cap screws (A), cap screws (B), fender mount bracket (C), and the fender.
CAUTION: Air spring can explode if over inflated, causing serious injury or death to you or others. When adjusting suspension assemblies, do not exceed 689 kPa (6.89 bar) (100 psi). Keep hands and body away from suspension linkage.
NOTE: JDG1781 Spindle Spacers can be obtained via regular spare part channels.

3. Inflate the air spring and install a JDG1781 Spindle Spacer on each spindle in the locations (A) shown.
4. Deflate the air spring until the machine is resting on the spacers.
NOTE: Four cap screws are used on the 600R, 612R, and 616R.
5. Position jack bracket (B) and install cap screws (C).
6. When finished using the jack bracket, remove the bracket and hardware.
7. Install the fender in reverse order, using the following special instructions:
- Lubricate the fender bracket cap screws and install. Tighten to specification.
