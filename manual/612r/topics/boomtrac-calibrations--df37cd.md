# BoomTrac Calibrations

Boom Calibration
Accurate calibrations are critical for proper BoomTrac performance. Anytime a component has been replaced, adjusted, or changed in any way, a new calibration must be performed. This ensures accurate sensor readings and optimal system response.
When to Perform Calibration:
- After replacing or adjusting any sensor (position, tilt, roll, or height).
- After replacing or adjusting hydraulic components (valve cartridges, actuators).
- After structural changes or repairs to the boom or center frame.
- When BoomTrac performance is poor or response is inconsistent.
Calibrations that can impact BoomTrac performance:
1. Center Frame Position Calibration.
Required when:
- Center Frame Position Sensor has been replaced or adjusted.
Purpose:
- Ensures the system correctly identifies the center frame position for accurate boom leveling.
2. Tilt Sensor Calibration
Required when:
- Boom Tilt Sensor(s) have been replaced or adjusted.
Purpose:
- Aligns tilt sensor readings for proper boom tilt control.
3. Center Frame Valve (See & Spray Only)
Required when:
- BoomTrac performance is poor.
- Center frame response is too slow or too fast.
- A valve cartridge or center frame position sensor has been replaced.
Purpose:
- Calibrations the valves and position sensor that control the boom center frame movement.
4. Tilt Valve Calibration
Required when:
- BoomTrac performance is poor (tilt response too slow or too fast).
- Hydraulic components on the tilt valve (such as valve cartridges) have been replaced or adjusted.
Purpose:
- Optimizes hydraulic response for smooth and accurate boom tilt operation.
5. Boom Level Calibration
Required when:
- Boom roll sensor has been replaced or adjusted.
- BoomTrac performance indicates incorrect leveling.
Purpose:
- Ensures accurate boom leveling across the entire width.
6. Roll Damper Valve (Dual Product Machines Only)
Required when:
- BoomTrac performance is poor.
- Boom tilt response is too slow or too fast.
- Valve cartridge has been replaced.
- Autofold is not performing as expected.
Purpose:
- Optimizes boom roll damper control and response.
General Calibration Guidelines
- Park the machine on a level surface before starting calibration.
- Ensure ground surface consists of concrete, gravel, or short grass for height sensor calibrations.
- Follow on-screen prompts in the display for each calibration procedure.
- Verify calibration results by performing a functional check after completion.
