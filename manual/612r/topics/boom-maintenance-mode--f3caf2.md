# Boom Maintenance Mode

This procedure can be used to manually control the individual movement of boom sections and the center frame. Operating in boom maintenance mode moves the boom at a slower speed than normal folding operation.
Use When:
- Repair or service is necessary.
- Unfolding or folding boom outside of the normal sequence.
- Unfolding or folding boom in tight areas.
- Diagnosing boom folding problems.
Items Accessible on Boom Maintenance Mode Page:
A113808—UN—15OCT21

![[A113808.png]]
**Figure A113808**

Begin Procedure
Begin Procedure— operate boom in maintenance mode.
Boom Maintenance Mode Procedure:
CAUTION: Folding spray booms while in maintenance mode is not the same as folding in normal operation. Maintain awareness of surroundings and keep bystanders out of folding and unfolding area.
IMPORTANT: Booms can contact and may damage the exhaust stack and/or walkway handrail when using maintenance mode to fold and unfold booms. Do not allow booms to go higher than the top of the cradle support when operating boom in maintenance mode.
IMPORTANT: This procedure enables fully folding/ unfolding the boom and raising tilts to highest positions. Ensure that adequate space is available.
