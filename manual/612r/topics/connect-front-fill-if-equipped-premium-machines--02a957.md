# Connect Front Fill—If Equipped (Premium Machines)

1.
NX1453541—UN—30MAR26

![[NX1453541.png]]
**Figure NX1453541** — A: Fitting, Elbow; B: Fitting, Coupler; C: T-Fitting; D: Pipe, Front Fill; E: Clamp (2 used); F: P-Clamp (2 used)

Remove any installed caps from the strainer, pipes, and fittings.
NOTE: Gaskets and hardware retained during front fill disconnection are used when installing clamps and P-clamps.
2. Connect the assembly of fittings (A—C) and pipe (D) to the hose fitting and coupler using clamps (E) in the position shown.
3. Retain the hose to the support brackets using P-clamps (E).
NX1453544—UN—30MAR26

![[NX1453544.png]]
**Figure NX1453544** — A: Fitting, Hose Barb (2 used); B: Hose, Front Fill; C: Clamp (2 used); D: P-Clamp

4. Connect the assembly of fittings (A) and hose (B) to the elbow fitting and T-fitting using clamps (C) as shown.
5. Secure the hose to the support bracket using P-clamp (D).

![[NX1453547.png]]
**Figure NX1453547** — A: Fitting, Elbow (3 used); B: Fitting, Hose Barb (2 used); C: Hose, Front Fill; D: Clamp (2 used)

6. Connect the assembly of fittings (A and B) and hose (C) to the electric valve and front fill pipe using clamps (D) as shown.
