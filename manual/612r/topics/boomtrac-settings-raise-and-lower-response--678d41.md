**Figure NX1458317**

Lower/Raise Response
Lower/Raise Response allows you to set the response speed of the boom raise and lower functions.
Select + (A) or - (B) to change the lower / raise responce.
When to increase or decrease Lower/Raise rate:
- Increase the response when the spray boom does not rise fast enough to avoid contact with the ground/ crop or the boom does not lower fast enough to avoid spray drift or skips in coverage.
- Decrease the raise response when the spray boom motion is not smooth or continuously adjusting height when minimal terrain height difference is present.
