# Hydraulic Start-Up Procedure

Perform the following procedure after any hydraulic repairs if the hydraulic reservoir was drained during repair. Also perform this procedure when changing the hydraulic or hydrostatic filters and oil.
1. Check the oil level and add as required (see Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily in the Lubrication and Maintenance section). Do not install reservoir cap at this time.
N121582—UN—10FEB16

![[N121582.png]]
**Figure N121582** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle

2. Install a 6894 kPa (68.9 bar) (1000 psi) pressure gauge to the charge pressure receptacle (A).
N153314—UN—11MAR21

![[N153314.png]]
**Figure N153314** — A: Fuel Injector Harness

6.8 L Engine
N153313—UN—11MAR21

![[N153313.png]]
**Figure N153313** — A: Fuel Injector Harness

9.0 L Engine
3. Disconnect fuel injector harness (A) on cylinder head.
NOTE: The following steps may require two people.
4. Crank the engine for 15 seconds at 1 minute intervals. Monitor charge pressure while cranking engine. Repeat until charge pressure consistently reaches the specified pressure.
Specification
Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . . . . 3102—3861 kPa (31.02—38.61 bar) (450—560 psi)
5. Crank engine three additional 15 second cycles.
6. Check the oil level and add as required.
7. Reconnect the fuel injector harness.
CAUTION: Avoid injury or death to others. Keep away all bystanders while performing start-up procedure. The machine could move unexpectedly.
8. Start engine and run at low idle for 5 minutes.
9. Check the oil level and add as required.
10. Operate the engine at high idle.
11. Verify that machine drive system operates properly. Move machine in forward and reverse directions.
12. Check for leaks.
13. Check the oil level and add as required. Install reservoir cap.
