# ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Premium Seat)

N152676—UN—25NOV20

![[N152676.png]]
**Figure N152676**

ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Premium Seat)
ComfortCommand Operator’s Seat (Premium Seat) describes the controls for the premium cloth seat.
N152684—UN—25NOV20

![[N152684.png]]
**Figure N152684**

Seat Bottom Depth/Angle Adjustment Control
Seat Bottom Depth/Angle Adjustment— push the control forward or backward to slide seat forward or rearward for the best working position. Push the front of the control upward or downward to raise or lower the angle of the seat bottom.
N152685—UN—25NOV20

![[N152685.png]]
**Figure N152685**

Backrest Angle Adjustment Control
Backrest Angle Adjustment Control— adjusts the seat back angle. Press the top of the control forward to raise the angle of the seat back. Press the top of the control backward to lower the angle of the seat back.
N152681—UN—25NOV20

![[N152681.png]]
**Figure N152681**

Seat Swivel Handle
Seat Swivel Handle— lift the handle to allow the seat to swivel. Push down on the handle to lock seat in position.
N152680—UN—25NOV20

![[N152680.png]]
**Figure N152680**

Weight/Height Adjustment Switch
Weight/Height Adjustment Switch— press and hold the top or bottom of the switch to increase or decrease seat height.
H128690—UN—03MAR20

![[H128690.png]]
**Figure H128690**

Lumbar In/Out Adjustment Control
Lumbar In/Out Adjustment Control— push plus (+) or minus (-) on the control to increase or decrease lumbar support.
H128692—UN—03MAR20

![[H128692.png]]
**Figure H128692**

Lumbar Up/Down Adjustment Control
Lumbar Up/Down Adjustment Control— push up or down on the control to raise or lower lumbar position.
H128691—UN—03MAR20

![[H128691.png]]
**Figure H128691**

Cushion Length Adjustment Control
Cushion Length Adjustment Control— push the top or bottom of the control to increase or decrease the seat cushion length.
H127122—UN—26SEP19

![[H127122.png]]
**Figure H127122**

Armrest Angle Adjustment Knob
Armrest Angle Adjustment Knob— adjusts the left-hand armrest angle up or down. Rotate knob clockwise to decrease angle and counterclockwise to increase angle.
N152683—UN—25NOV20

![[N152683.png]]
**Figure N152683**

Left-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment
Left-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment— loosen the nut to adjust the left-hand armrest. Tighten the nut to lock the height into the desired position.
H117047—UN—22JUL21

![[H117047.png]]
**Figure H117047**

Right-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment
Right-Hand Armrest Height Adjustment— loosen knob and slide the armrest control console up/forward or down/back in slots on outer plate. Tighten knob.
N152694—UN—25NOV20

![[N152694.png]]
**Figure N152694**

Lateral Isolation Adjustment Handle
Lateral Isolation Adjustment Handle— push the handle forward to release side-to-side movement. Pull the handle rearward to lock.
H127097—UN—26SEP19

![[H127097.png]]
**Figure H127097**

Vertical Shock Dampener Control
Vertical Shock Dampener Control— limits amount of upward motion the seat suspension provides. Push control forward for a soft ride or pull handle rearward for a firm ride. Between these two positions is medium firmness.
H127098—UN—26SEP19

![[H127098.png]]
**Figure H127098**

Fore/Aft Isolation Adjustment Handle
Fore/Aft Isolation Adjustment Handle— push control forward to lock forward or rearward movements. Push control rearward to allow forward or rearward movements.
