These displays provide real-time view from two outward-facing cameras, mounted at the tip of the left and right boom wings, side-mounted cameras on both sides of the machine, and the backup camera.
Benefits of SmartView:
- Operators can monitor traffic and surroundings while moving between fields..
- The camera system provides a wider field of view, reducing the number of blind spots.
- Operators can make more informed decisions when transitioning from the field to the road.
