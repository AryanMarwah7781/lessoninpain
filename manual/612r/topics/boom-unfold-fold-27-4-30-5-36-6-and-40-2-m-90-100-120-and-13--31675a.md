# Boom Unfold/Fold—27.4, 30.5, 36.6, and 40.2 m (90, 100, 120, and 132 ft) Booms

Full Boom Auto Unfold/Fold—27.4, 30.5, 36.6, and 40.2 m (90, 100, 120, and 132 ft) Booms
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.
NOTE: The machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the transport lock must be disengaged to unfold or fold the boom.

Unfold the boom using the following steps:
a. Press and hold boom fold/unfold floor switch (A).
b. Press and hold the bottom of center boom switch (B). All boom sections on both sides now unfold completely. Once the booms have completely unfolded, release the switch.
c. Press the bottom of the center boom switch until the boom is lowered to the desired height. Release the switch.
d. Press the bottom of left boom switch (C) to lower the left boom to the desired height. Press the bottom of right boom switch (D) to lower the right boom to the desired height.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.

b. Press and hold the top of the center boom raise switch (B). The booms now raise completely and fold in towards the machine. Once the booms are completely folded in against the machine and lowered into the cradles, release the switch.
Full Boom Sequential Unfold/Fold—27.4, 30.5, 36.6, and 40.2 m (90, 100, 120, and 132 ft) Booms
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.
NOTE: The machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the transport lock must be disengaged to unfold or fold the boom.
Corresponding boom sections on either side will unfold sequentially, stopping after each section is unfolded. The operator must release the unfold button and press again to continue the unfold procedure.
The booms are designed so that only one boom can be unfolded/folded if desired.

Unfold the boom using the following steps:
a. Press center boom raise switch (A) to raise the boom to the highest position.
b. Press the top of right and left boom switches (B and C) to raise the booms out of the cradles.
c. Press and hold boom fold/unfold floor switch (D) and the bottom of the right and left boom switches until the inner wings are unfolded.
d. Release the right and left boom switches and press again until the outer/breakaway sections are unfolded. Release the switches.
e. Press center boom lower switch (E) to position the nozzles at desired height.
f. Press the bottom of the left boom switch to lower the left boom to the desired height. Press the bottom of the right boom switch to lower the right boom to the desired height.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.

e. Press and hold center boom lower switch (E) to lower the booms into the cradles. Release the switch.

Unfold one side of the boom using the following steps:
a. Press center boom raise switch (A) to raise the boom to the highest position.
b. Push and hold boom fold/unfold floor switch (B) and the bottom of left or right boom switch (C or D) until the inner section of the corresponding boom is unfolded.
c. Release the switch and press again until the corresponding outer/breakaway section is unfolded.
NOTE: Both sides may be unfolded simultaneously by pressing the left and right boom switches simultaneously if desired.
d. Release the switch.
e. Press and hold center boom lower switch (E) to lower the boom to the desired height.
CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the area can result in injury or death.

Fold one side of the boom using the following steps:
a. Push and hold boom fold/unfold floor switch (A) and the top of the left or right boom switch (B or C) until the breakaway/outer wing on the corresponding side is folded.
b. Release the switch and press again until the corresponding inner section is folded. The center section raises as the inner section folds.
NOTE: Both sides may be folded simultaneously by pressing the left and right boom switches simultaneously if desired.
c. Release the switches.
d. Press and hold center boom lower switch (D) to lower the booms into the cradles. Release the switch.
