# Avoid High-Pressure Fluids

X9811—UN—23AUG88

![[X9811.png]]
**Figure X9811**

Inspect hydraulic hoses and steel piping periodically–at least once per year–for leakage, kinking, cuts, cracks, abrasion, blisters, corrosion, exposed wire braid, or any other signs of wear or damage.
Replace worn or damaged hose assemblies immediately with John Deere approved replacement parts.
Escaping fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin causing serious injury.
Avoid the hazard by relieving pressure before disconnecting hydraulic or other lines. Tighten all connections before applying pressure.
Search for leaks with a piece of cardboard. Protect hands and body from high-pressure fluids.
In case of accidental skin injection, seek immediate surgical treatment.
