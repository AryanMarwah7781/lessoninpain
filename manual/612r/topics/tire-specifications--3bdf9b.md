CAUTION: Installing tires that do not meet original equipment tire specifications may cause machine malfunction. Consult a John Deere dealer or other service provider for guidance. Use of substandard tires or larger than recommended tires voids the warranty and decreases stability, affects steering, results in premature tire failure, or causes other durability or safety issues.
