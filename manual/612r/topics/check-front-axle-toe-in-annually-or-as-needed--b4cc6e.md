# Check Front Axle Toe-In—(Annually or as Needed)

1. Before beginning, check the tire pressure in all tires, ensure the tire pressure is the same for each.
NOTE: Check toe-in with the machine at tread spacing most often used when operating sprayer.
Rephasing steering system is required before road transport.
IMPORTANT: Failure to rephase steering system before setting toe-in can result in insufficient ball joint thread engagement on cylinder barrel.
2. Rephase steering system:
a. Turn wheels full left, then turn steering wheel three additional revolutions while slowly moving forward at around 0.8 km/h (0.5 mph).
b. Turn wheels full right, then turn steering wheel three additional revolutions while slowly moving forward at around 0.8 km/h (0.5 mph).
c. Repeat these steps 1—3 more times.
3. Position wheels for straight ahead travel. Utilizing an AutoTrac A-B line helps ensure the machine is traveling as straight as possible.
4. Once the wheels are straight, drive forward 6 m (20 ft) and park on a flat surface.

5. Using a tape measure, measure the distance (A) from the ground to the center of the hub.
IMPORTANT: Measurements must be taken from the flat area of the rim and not the edge of the bead or the side of the tire.
6. Using that measurement, mark the front (B) and rear (C) centerline of the rim on the flat area of the rim using tape.
NOTE: A laser measuring device can be used to increase accuracy.

7. Measure from mark-to-mark diagonally, as shown with lines (A and B), to verify that wheels are straight ahead. Measurement lengths should be equal within 6.3 mm (0.25 in) of each other.
Specification
Measurement (A) - Measurement (B). . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 6.3 mm (0.25 in) or less
IMPORTANT: Toe-in is measured at the wheel rim, not at outside of tire.

![[NX1460007.png]]
**Figure NX1460007** — C: Rear Spacing Dimension; D: Front Spacing Dimension

8. Measure from mark-to-mark on the rear of each wheel rim. Record measurement for rear spacing dimension (C).
9. Measure from mark-to-mark on the front of each wheel rim. Record measurement for front spacing dimension (D).
10. Subtract front spacing (D) from rear spacing (C) to obtain toe-in dimension. Dimension (D) must be the specified amount less than dimension (C). If measurement produces a negative number, this would indicate a toe-out condition. Record dimension.
Specification
Front Wheels—Toe-In. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 0—10 mm (0—0.375 in)
11. Without making any adjustment or turning the steering wheel, start the machine and drive forward until the wheels have rotated 180 degrees. The tape marks previously applied to the wheel should be parallel with the floor and can be checked using the the floor to hub measurement previously taken.

12. Repeat the parallel measurements from rear of left wheel to rear of right wheel (C) and front left to front right (D).
13. Subtract front spacing from rear spacing to obtain toe-in dimension. Record dimension.
14. If the toe measurements recorded are greater than 12. 5 mm (0.5 in) different, continue driving forward 180° and taking measurements until consistent measurements are obtained.

15. Once completed, average the toe measured for each step. The average toe measurement should be between 0—10 mm (0—0.375 in)
16. If dimensions are not within specified range, see Adjust Front Axle Toe-In in this section.
