Pressure Strainers
Strainers are used to help keep dirt and debris from entering and damaging the solution pump, nozzles, and other components. Strainer screens should be removed and cleaned to maintain solution flow.
See Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required and Clean Pressure Strainers— Daily in Wet System section.
NOTE: For machines equipped with the ExactApply nozzle control system, 80 mesh strainers must be used in locations C and D; anything smaller can cause performance issues.
