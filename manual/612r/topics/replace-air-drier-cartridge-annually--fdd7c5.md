# Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually

CAUTION: The onboard air system is charged at 689—896 kPa (6.89—8.96 bar) (100—130 psi). To avoid injury to yourself or others, wear safety goggles and gloves and be sure that others are clear of area before relieving the onboard air system pressure.
N153550—UN—23NOV20

![[N153550.png]]
**Figure N153550** — A: Drain Valve

1. Relieve air system pressure by opening valve (A).
N136021—UN—05MAR18

![[N136021.png]]
**Figure N136021** — A: Cartridge; B: O-Ring

2. Using a strap wrench, turn the desiccant cartridge (A) counterclockwise, then remove and discard.
NOTE: If there is excessive oil present in the desiccant filter, compressor may require servicing.
3. Remove and discard O-ring (B) from the adapter plate stud.
4. Clean top surface of the adapter plate and threaded stud.
5. Apply a light coat of grease (supplied with the new cartridge) to the new O-ring. Install O-ring on the adapter plate.
6. Apply a generous coat of grease to the surface of the new desiccant cartridge gasket.
7. Thread new cartridge into adapter plate by turning clockwise until gasket contacts adapter plate. Tighten cartridge with an additional 1/2 turn. DO NOT overtighten.
