# Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent the exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
Filling the rinse tank from this location pushes the fluid through the shared plumbing with the main solution system, including the fill pump that could be contaminated.
Ensure that the shared plumbing is clean before filling the rinse tank to avoid contamination.
1. Set the target fill amount in the display in the tank system application for system 1 or system 2. The rinse tank cannot target fill.
NX1440873—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1440873.png]]
**Figure NX1440873** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

Front Fill Station
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed.
2. Verify that the larger Quik-Fill valve (A) at the front fill station is closed.
3. Open the lock levers and remove the cap (B).
4. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running the pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
- Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
- If the solution tank is empty, perform a low-volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse System in this section. Stop the rinse cycle after the pump is primed.
- The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during the entire fill procedure.
5. Open the nurse tank valve and the Quik-Fill valve.
6. Open cover (C) for access to the micro display.
NX1440866—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440866.png]]
**Figure NX1440866** — A: Fill Button

7. Select fill button (A) to access the fill menu.
NX1440867—UN—20JAN26

![[NX1440867.png]]
**Figure NX1440867** — A: Tank 1 Fill Button

NOTE: The following steps describe filling tank 1. The procedure is similar for the other tanks.
8. Select tank 1 fill button (A) to access the tank 1 fill menu.
NX1442023—UN—26JAN26

![[NX1442023.png]]
**Figure NX1442023** — A: Pull-On Fill Button

9. Select pull-on fill button (A).
NX1442026—UN—26JAN26

![[NX1442026.png]]
**Figure NX1442026** — A: Target Fill Button

10. Press target fill button (A).
NX1442029—UN—26JAN26

![[NX1442029.png]]
**Figure NX1442029** — A: Enter Button; B: Selection Button; C: Selection Button

NOTE: If target fill volume is set using the CommandCenter display or is the same as the last loaded volume, then press enter button (A) to confirm fill volume and begin fill procedure.
11. If necessary, change the target fill volume.
a. Press selection button (B) to change the active digit to be changed.
b. Press selection button (C) to increase the value of the active digit.
IMPORTANT: Prevent pump seal damage. When pulling solution onto the machine, the water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during the entire fill procedure.
12. Press the enter button to confirm and begin fill procedure.
NX1442030—UN—26JAN26

![[NX1442030.png]]
**Figure NX1442030** — A: Pump Speed Button; B: Cancel Button

13. During the fill procedure, press pump speed button (A) to change the speed of the solution pump.
14. When the level in the solution tank reaches the target level, the solution pump automatically shuts off. To stop filling before reaching the target level, press cancel button (B).
15. After the solution tank has filled, close the Quik-Fill valve.
16. Close the nurse tank valve.
17. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill valve.
18. Install the cap on the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
