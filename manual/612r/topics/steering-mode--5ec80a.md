# Steering Mode

Steering Mode allows you to select how the wheels can be steered.
Available Steering Modes:
- Two-Wheel Steer— recommended for use during spraying. Reduces yaw in the machine and boom, which improves stability and reduces swaying of the boom.
- Four-Wheel Steer— recommended for use on headlands as it improves maneuverability of the machine and reduces crop damage due to the front and rear wheels following similar tracks. Four-wheel steer can also be used during road transport to maneuver around tight turns.
- Crab Steer— useful in certain circumstances such as avoiding soil compaction by offsetting the front and rear axle and driving in different tracks. Crab steer can also assist in circumstances when the operator needs to avoid an obstacle while maintaining the machine heading.
