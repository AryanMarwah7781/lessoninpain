# Quick Stop Switch

N152634—UN—25NOV20

![[N152634.png]]
**Figure N152634**

Quick Stop Switch
Quick Stop Switch allows you to shut OFF application system in the case of an emergency.
CAUTION: When pressing the quick stop switch, you are responsible for pulling back on the multi-function lever to stop the machine.
It is always recommended to wear your seat belt to avoid serious injury.
The following systems stop when Quick Stop Switch is pressed:
- Solution pump
- Spray system on/off
- Spread system master (if equipped)
- Spinners (if equipped)
- Wet system valves
