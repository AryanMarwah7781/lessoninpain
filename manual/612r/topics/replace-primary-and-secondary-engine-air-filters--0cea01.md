# Replace Primary and Secondary Engine Air Filters

NX1396258—UN—10MAR25

![[NX1396258.png]]
**Figure NX1396258** — A: Latch; B: Filter Cover

NX1396259—UN—10MAR25

![[NX1396259.png]]
**Figure NX1396259** — A: Latch; B: Filter Cover

1. Set the tread width to the widest setting to allow space for filter removal.
2. Open latch (A).
3. Open filter cover (B) approximately 45°. Slide the filter cover down to remove.

![[NX1396261.png]]
**Figure NX1396261** — A: Primary Filter; B: Lip

NX1396264—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396264.png]]
**Figure NX1396264** — A: Primary Filter; B: Lip

4. Slide primary filter (A) forward away from lip (B) of the filter housing.
5. Remove the primary filter.
N153570—UN—27JAN21

![[N153570.png]]
**Figure N153570** — A: Primary Filter; B: Secondary Filter

IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to clean filters.
Replace secondary filter (B) every second primary filter change.
Install new secondary filter immediately to prevent dust from entering the air intake system.
6. Replace primary filter (A) and the secondary filter.
7. Clean dirt from inside the cover and canister.
NX1396269—UN—07MAR25

![[NX1396269.png]]
**Figure NX1396269** — A: Filter Cover; B: Ridge

8. With filter cover (A) at a 45° angle, slide the filter cover onto the filter housing.
9. Close the filter cover, verifying that ridge (B) of the filter housing is positioned in the notch of the filter cover as shown.

![[NX1396280.png]]
**Figure NX1396280** — A: Latch; B: Rod

10. Close latch (A), verifying that the latch is centered on rod (B) as shown.
