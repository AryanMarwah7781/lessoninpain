# Operate Rinse System (Rinse Solution Pump, Boom, and Flowmeter Only)

Perform the following procedure to rinse the pump, boom, and flowmeter. Spray rinse water at 620—690 kPa (6.2—6.9 bar) (90—100 psi) to assist in chemical removal.
CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Use the rinse system in an area where people, animals, vegetation, and water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
NOTE: During a boom rinse cycle, the pump bleed and air bleed lines add some fresh water to the solution tank. This could result in the product being diluted and/or extra product in the tank.
Boom rinse procedures can be operated from the cab using the display. (See the Rinse System Application section of this manual.)
1. Unfold the boom.
NOTE: Engine speed can increase when a rinse cycle is initiated.
NX1435331—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1435331.png]]
**Figure NX1435331** — A: Rinse Button

2. Press rinse button (A).
NX1440572—UN—15JAN26

![[NX1440572.png]]
**Figure NX1440572** — A: Boom Rinse Button

3. Press boom rinse button (A).
4. Rinse procedure begins automatically and rinses the following:
- Boom Plumbing
- Flowmeters
- Pressure Strainers
5. Allow the boom to rinse with clean water.
NX1440579—UN—15JAN26

![[NX1440579.png]]
**Figure NX1440579** — A: Cancel Button

6. Press cancel button (A) to stop the rinse procedure.
NOTE: Chemical buildup in the aspirators can occur if not cleaned out thoroughly and regularly.
7. | After the boom has been rinsed, close all nozzle
bodies not on an aspirator.
8. Set the aspirator to “Open” position.
9. Perform a second boom rinse procedure using the following directions:
NOTE: Rinsing all sections at once may not produce enough system pressure to flush the boom plumbing properly.
Active boom sections must be controlled from the cab using section control switches.
- Flush one boom section at a time for 5 to 10 seconds until all sections are flushed.
NOTE: Engine speed reverts to the previous level when the rinse procedure is turned off if the throttle position has not been adjusted.
10. Press cancel button (A) to stop rinse procedure.
11. Return the nozzle bodies to previous positions.
