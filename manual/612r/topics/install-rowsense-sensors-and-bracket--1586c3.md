# Install RowSense Sensors and Bracket

NOTE: 410R is shown. 408R, 412R, 612R, and 616R are similar.
The right-hand side is shown. The left-hand side is similar.
1. Set the machine to the widest tread setting.

![[N155454.png]]
**Figure N155454** — A: Crop Shield; B: Cap Screw and Nut (12 used); C: Nut (2 used); D: U-Bolt and Plate; E: Crop Shield Frame; F: Cap Screw and Bushing (3 used)

2.
For machines equipped with crop shields:
a. Remove and retain crop shield (A), cap screws and nuts (B), nuts (C), and U-bolt and plate (D).
b. Support crop shield frame (E) using an appropriate lifting device.
NOTE: Four cap screws and bushings are used on 612R and 616R.
c. Remove and retain cap screws and bushings (F).
N155455—UN—04MAY21

![[N155455.png]]
**Figure N155455** — A: Fender Frame; B: Cap Screw and Bushing (3 used)

3.
For machines equipped with fenders only:
a. Support fender frame (A) using an appropriate lifting device.
NOTE: Four cap screws and bushings are used on 612R and 616R.
b. Remove and retain cap screws and bushings (B).
N155456—UN—04MAY21

![[N155456.png]]
**Figure N155456** — A: Cap Screw (5 used); B: Wheel Motor Cover

4. Remove and retain cap screws (A) and wheel motor cover (B).
NOTE: 408R, 410R, and 412R Only—Machines equipped with fenders and crop shields use M16 x 110 cap screws. Machines with fenders only use M16 x 80 cap screws.
612R and 616R Only—Four M16 x 80 cap screws are used on all machine configurations.
N155460—UN—05MAY21

![[N155460.png]]
**Figure N155460** — A: Sensor Bracket Assembly; B: Cap Screw and Bushing (3 used)

Machine with Fenders Only Shown
5. Install sensor bracket assembly (A) between the wheel motor housing and fender frame using cap screws and bushings (B) as shown.
IMPORTANT: Apply dielectric compound to all electrical connections.
N155461—UN—05MAY21

![[N155461.png]]
**Figure N155461** — A: RowSense Harness; B: Sensor Harness; C: P-Clamp (2 used); D: Cap Screw and Nut (2 used); E: Tie Band

N155462—UN—05MAY21

![[N155462.png]]
**Figure N155462** — A: RowSense Harness; B: Sensor Harness; C: P-Clamp (2 used); D: Cap Screw and Nut (2 used); E: Tie Band

6. Cut any necessary tie bands and uncoil RowSense harness (A).
7. Connect the RowSense harness to sensor harness (B).
8. Secure the RowSense harness to the RowSense bracket using P-clamps (C), cap screws and nuts (D), and tie band (E) as shown.
N155464—UN—10MAY21

![[N155464.png]]
**Figure N155464** — A: Sensor Position, Lower; B: Sensor Position, Middle; C: Sensor Position, Upper

NOTE: The RowSense sensor has three adjustment positions. The lower position is typically used. Varying crop and field conditions require the sensor to be raised into position (B) or (C).
9. Adjust the RowSense sensor in the lower adjustment position (A).
NOTE: Some flexing of the wheel motor cover is required to align bolt holes.
10. Install the wheel motor cover with the previously removed hardware.
11. Repeat the procedure for the remaining sensor and bracket.
