# Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Side Fill

CAUTION: Carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals or you can be exposed to hazardous materials that could poison and injure you.
IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
NX1440843—UN—22JAN26

![[NX1440843.png]]
**Figure NX1440843** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

Side Fill Station
CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
NOTE: The target fill amount can also be set using the micro display at the side load station.
1. Set the target fill amount in the tank system application display.
2. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed.
3. Open lock levers and remove cap (B).
4. Connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running the pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
- Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
- If solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse System in this section. End the rinse cycle after the pump is primed.
- The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during the entire fill procedure.
5. Open the nurse tank valve.
6. Open the Quik-Fill valve.
7. Lift the cover (C) for access to the system keypads and micro displays.
NX1434994—UN—10DEC25

![[NX1434994.png]]
**Figure NX1434994** — A: System 1 Keypad; B: System 2 Keypad; C: Pull-On Button (tank 1); D: Pull-On Button (tank 2)

8. To fill tank 1, press the pull-on button (C) on the system 1 keypad (A).
9. To fill tank 2, press the pull-on button (D) on the system 2 keypad (B).
NX1440131—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440131.png]]
**Figure NX1440131** — A: Enter Button; B: Clear Button; C: Target Fill Volume; D: Back Button

NOTE: If target fill volume is set using the CommandCenter display or is the same as the last loaded volume, then press enter button (A) to confirm fill volume and begin fill procedure.
10. If necessary, press clear button (B) to reset the volume and set the desired target fill volume (C) using the keypad.
IMPORTANT: When pulling solution on the machine with the solution pump, the water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during entire fill procedure or pump seal damage can occur.
11. Press the enter button to confirm and begin fill procedure.
NOTE: Press back button (D) to return to the previous screen view.
NX1440138—UN—14JAN26

![[NX1440138.png]]
**Figure NX1440138** — A: Pump Speed Button; B: Cancel Button

12. During the fill procedure, press pump speed button (A) to change the speed of the solution pump.
13. When the level in the solution tank reaches the target level, the solution pump automatically shuts off. To stop filling before reaching the target level, press cancel button (B).
14. Close the Quik-Fill valve.
15. Close the nurse tank valve.
16. Remove the fill the hose from the Quik-Fill adapter.
17. Install the cap on the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers.
