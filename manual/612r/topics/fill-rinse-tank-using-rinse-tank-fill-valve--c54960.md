# Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve

IMPORTANT: Damage to rinse tank and surrounding structure can occur when filling at a high rate if tank lid is not removed. Remove tank lid when filling at high rates to avoid damage.
N99946—UN—17SEP12

![[N99946.png]]
**Figure N99946** — A: Filler Cap; B: Valve

1. Remove fill cap (A) and connect fill hose.
2. Open valve (B) and fill tank to desired level.
3. After tank is filled, close valve.
4. Remove fill hose and reattach fill cap.
