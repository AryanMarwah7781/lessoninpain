## Symptoms

### Nozzles do not automatically shut off after the boom or master valve is turned off. Nozzles continue to drip or leak out several seconds after shut off.

1. **Air leaks in the boom sections.** — Pressurize the boom plumbing to check for air leaks on the hoses and fittings. Fix leaks. [[troubleshooting-nozzles-that-drip-leak-or-seep-after-valve-i--948232|Troubleshooting Nozzles That Drip, Leak, Or Seep After Valve is Shut Off]] (p. 55-21)  
   Fix / related: [[relieve-spray-pressure]]
2. **Issues in the nozzle bodies.** — Inspect nozzle bodies. Remove debris and replace damaged components. [[troubleshooting-nozzles-that-drip-leak-or-seep-after-valve-i--948232|Troubleshooting Nozzles That Drip, Leak, Or Seep After Valve is Shut Off]] (p. 55-21)  
   Fix / related: [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req]] · [[clean-spraymaster-5-position-nozzle-bodies-standard-flow--fea20a|Clean SprayMaster 5-Position Nozzle Bodies—Standard Flow]] · [[clean-spraymaster-5-position-nozzle-bodies-high-flow--a04e41|Clean SprayMaster 5-Position Nozzle Bodies—High Flow]] · [[nozzle]]
3. **Solution hose damage.** — Verify that hoses are in good condition. Replace as necessary. [[troubleshooting-nozzles-that-drip-leak-or-seep-after-valve-i--948232|Troubleshooting Nozzles That Drip, Leak, Or Seep After Valve is Shut Off]] (p. 55-21)  
   Fix / related: [[relieve-spray-pressure]]
