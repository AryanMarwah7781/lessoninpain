# Hydrostatic Drive troubleshooting

This page follows the manual's Hydrostatic Drive troubleshooting table (p. 160-6 to 160-9) [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]. Each
symptom heading is the symptom as printed, and the causes are in the printed order. The manual does not
rank causes by cost or likelihood. The hydrostatic and hydraulic systems use the same reservoir
[[check-hydraulic-oil-level-daily--99fd63|Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily]].

> [!inference] Rows continued across pages
> The table has rows at the top of p. 160-7 and p. 160-9 with an empty Symptom column. They are listed
> here under the last symptom printed on the page before, which is how the printed table reads. The
> manual does not print "continued" on these rows [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]].

## Cold weather: read this first

The table carries this footnote on three symptoms. It is printed two ways:

- "When operating in temperatures below -12°C (10°F), run sprayer for 10—15 min to warm up hydrostatic
  drive oil." — footnote 1, p. 160-6, on *Sprayer will not travel forward or reverse with
  multi-function lever in forward or reverse* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]].
- "When operating in temperatures below -12 degrees C (10 degrees F), run sprayer for 10-15 min to warm
  up hydrostatic drive oil." — footnotes 2 and 3, p. 160-7 and p. 160-8, on *Sprayer not responding to
  target speed switch* and *Lack of power or loss of power* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]].

**Example: a morning colder than 10°F and the sprayer won't move forward or reverse.** That is below
the footnote's threshold, so the first printed step is to run the sprayer for 10—15 min to warm the hydrostatic drive
oil [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]. The manual gives the threshold only as "-12°C (10°F)"; it prints no other temperature for this
symptom, and none is converted here. If the machine still will not travel after warm-up, work through
the causes under that symptom below, starting with the oil level. Check the level against the decal
mark for the current oil temperature: "Verify that oil is at the proper level for the current oil
temperature as shown on the fill level decal (C)."
[[check-hydraulic-oil-level-daily--99fd63|Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily]].

The engine warm-up applies too: "At temperatures below freezing point, this period must be extended to
2—4 minutes," and "As the engine and hydraulic fluid warm up, cycle the steering wheel back and forth to
circulate warm hydraulic fluid." [[warm-the-engine--a6676a|Warm the Engine]]. For all of the manual's
cold-weather values in one place — fuel, starting aids, DEF, winter screens — see the settings page
[[cold-weather-and-storage]].

> [!inference] Oil choice in the cold
> The manual says to "Use oil viscosity based on the expected air temperature range during the period
> between oil changes" [[hydrostatic-hydraulic-drive-oil--b9d2a0|Hydrostatic/Hydraulic Drive Oil]], but
> it prints no temperature range for the preferred 10W-30 oils. The viscosity to use in very cold
> weather is not covered in this manual; ask your dealer.

## System overheating.
*p. 160-6* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **Fan belt slipping or broken.** → Replace belt or tensioner.
  See [[inspect-and-replace-fan-belt-as-required--16e0c6|Inspect and Replace Fan Belt—As Required]] and [[engine]].
- **Side screens plugged.** → Clean screens.
  See [[clean-screens-and-cooling-package-as-required--cd4253|Clean Screens and Cooling Package—As Required]].
- **Low on oil.** → Check for leak and correct.
- **Oil cooler core plugged.** → Clean oil cooler.
  See [[clean-screens-and-cooling-package-as-required--cd4253|Clean Screens and Cooling Package—As Required]].
- **Exceeding relief valve pressure.** → Reduce speed.
- **Plugged oil cooler bypass valve.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Excessive high-pressure leakage.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Charge flush is inadequate.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Loop flush is inadequate.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## System overheating in cold weather.
*p. 160-6* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **When operating machine in low ambient temperatures below -7°C (20° F), the hydraulic oil may
  overheat due to a restriction at the hydraulic oil cooler.** → Install winter screens to hydraulic
  coolers to maintain correct temperature.

The same row is printed in the Hydraulic System table [[hydraulic-system--47f651|Hydraulic System]].

## Sprayer will not travel forward or reverse with multi-function lever in forward or reverse.
*p. 160-6, continued on p. 160-7* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

Footnote 1, as printed: "When operating in temperatures below -12°C (10°F), run sprayer for 10—15 min
to warm up hydrostatic drive oil." [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **Low on oil.** → Check for leak and correct. Fill hydraulic reservoir with oil to proper level for
  current oil temperature. See [[check-hydraulic-oil-level-daily--99fd63|Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily]].
- **Plugged hydrostatic filter(s).** → Change filter element(s).
  See [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours]].
- *(p. 160-7)* **Exceeding limiter pressure.** → Shift to low speed.
- **Broken driveshaft from engine to pump.** → Repair as needed.
- **Air leak in system.** → Tighten connections.
- **Drive system inoperative.** → Check pump and motor. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service
  provider.

The park brake must also be released before the machine will move: "Park brake switch must be
disengaged before moving the machine." [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]]. See [[brake]].

## Ground travel speed erratic.
*p. 160-7* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **Radar picking up signals from tall crops.** → Disconnect radar and use wheel speed sensor.
- **Wheel speed sensor faulty.** → Repair or replace.
- **Low on oil.** → Check for leaks and correct. Fill hydraulic reservoir with oil to proper level for
  current oil temperature.
- **Plugged hydrostatic filter.** → Change filter element.
- **Exceeding limiter pressure.** → Reduce speed.
- **Plugged shift orifice.** → Could be something wrong inside motor so swash plate angle is not changing.
- **Drive system unable to maintain charge pressure.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Sprayer not responding to target speed switch.
*p. 160-7* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

Footnote 2, as printed: "When operating in temperatures below -12 degrees C (10 degrees F), run sprayer
for 10-15 min to warm up hydrostatic drive oil." [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **Bad fuse.** → Replace fuse. See [[replace-fuses--9cfc9e|Replace Fuses]].
- **Low on hydrostatic oil.** → Check for oil leaks and correct. Fill hydraulic reservoir with oil to
  proper level for current oil temperature.
- **Defective solenoids or switches.** → Check and repair as needed.
- **Plugged hydrostatic filter.** → Change filter element.
- **Air in system.** → Tighten connections, hose clamps, and check for broken hydraulic lines.
- **Drive system unable to build charge pressure.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Faulty hydrostatic motor.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Wheel hubs/motors will not engage.
*p. 160-8* [[hydrostatic-drive--ef26b5|Hydrostatic Drive]]

- **Hydrostatic system out of oil.** → Check oil level for leaks and correct. Fill hydraulic reservoir with
  oil to proper level for current oil temperature.
- **Drive hubs disengaged.** → Engage drive hubs.
- **Park brake not releasing.** → Check park brake solenoid.
- **Hoses incorrectly connected.** → Correctly connect hoses. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Damaged or faulty wheel motors.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Damaged or faulty drive hubs.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Faulty wheel motor/hub coupling.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

See [[wheel]] and [[brake]].
