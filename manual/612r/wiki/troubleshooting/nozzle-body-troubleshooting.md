# Nozzle Body troubleshooting

**This page applies only to machines with electronically controlled nozzle bodies: ExactApply or
Individual Nozzle Control (INC).** The manual prints a separate Nozzle Body table for each option:
ExactApply at p. 125-34 to 125-35 [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]], and INC at p. 130-31 to 130-32 [[nozzle-body-troubleshooting--48cd75|Nozzle Body Troubleshooting]]. Use the table for your system.
Symptoms are as printed and causes are in the printed order. See the component pages [[nozzle]] and
[[spray-tip]].

> [!inference] Rows continued across pages
> In the ExactApply table the row "Valve seat over-tightened." is at the top of p. 125-35 with an empty
> Symptom column. It is listed under *Nozzle drips or does not shut off.*, the last symptom on p. 125-34
> [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]].

## ExactApply Nozzle Body table (if equipped with ExactApply)

### Valve is lodged open.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Debris in the valve.** → Clean debris from effected valve.
- **Pressure strainer damaged.** → Check pressure strainers for holes or other damage. See
  [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily]].

### Boosted nozzles won't spray.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Debris in nozzle tip.** → Clean around nozzle and nozzle tips.

### Valve is lodged closed.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Debris in the valve.** → Clean debris from effected valve.

### Coil circuit is open.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Defective nozzle body.** → Test nozzle body. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
  Replace nozzle body.
- **Coil is defective.** → Test coil resistance (6.0—7.0 ohm).

### Coil circuit is closed.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Defective nozzle body.** → Test nozzle body. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
  Replace nozzle body.
- **Coil is defective.** → Test coil resistance (6.0—7.0 ohm).

### System cannot maintain constant pressure.
*p. 125-34* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Worn poppet valves.** → Inspect and replace poppet valves.
- **Sticky poppet valves.** → Inspect and clean poppet valves.
- **Incorrect spray tip installed.** → Install correctly sized spray tips.
- **Incorrect specific gravity entered.** → Enter the correct specific gravity in the display. See
  [[specific-gravity--90a332|Specific Gravity]].

> [!inference] Strainers as a related check
> The daily pressure-strainer procedure lists "Pump fails to maintain dead head pressure" and
> "Application rates fluctuate" as reasons to clean the boom supply line strainers
> [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]. That is not the same symptom as
> this row, and the Nozzle Body table does not list strainers here [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]. It is offered as a related check,
> not a printed cause.

### Nozzle drips or does not shut off.
*p. 125-34, continued on p. 125-35* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Plunger is lodged with debris.** → Remove and clean the plunger. See [[inspect-cartridge-components-annually--80cd8a|Inspect Cartridge Components—Annually]].
- **Plunger is worn.** → Inspect and replace plunger.
- **An O-ring is damaged.** → Replace O-ring.
- **Cartridge tube bent.** → Roll tube on flat surface to check straightness, replace as necessary.
- **Cartridge tube out of round.** → Inspect for damage due to improper tool usage. Verify plunge moves
  freely in tube. Replace as necessary.
- *(p. 125-35)* **Valve seat over-tightened.** → Check for damaged or deformed threads. Replace as necessary.

### Nozzle sprays erratically.
*p. 125-35* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Plunger is worn.** → Inspect and replace plunger. See [[inspect-cartridge-components-annually--80cd8a|Inspect Cartridge Components—Annually]].

### Solution leaks from other nozzles and around turret.
*p. 125-35* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Turret seals failure.** → Replace turret seals as needed. See [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]].
- **Debris on turret seal.** → Clean and lubricate turret seal.

### Solution leaking around solenoid coil.
*p. 125-35* [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]]

- **Damaged or missing valve seat O-rings.** → Inspect and replace as necessary.

### Warnings for the ExactApply fixes

> [!warning] CAUTION — nozzle tips and trapped pressure · [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]
> CAUTION: Keep spare nozzle tips for field replacement. DO NOT clean nozzle tips by placing in mouth and blowing. You could swallow or inhale hazardous chemicals which will poison, causing serious injury or death to you or others.
> Relieve trapped pressure in the spray boom before servicing nozzle body.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-26 [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]
> *Applies to:* ExactApply

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — rubber parts · [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]

>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-26 [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]
> *Applies to:* ExactApply

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — O-rings · [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]
> IMPORTANT: Always use new O-rings. Damaged or used O-rings leak.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-27 [[repair-nozzle-turret--8bf974|Repair Nozzle Turret]]

> IMPORTANT: Ensure that the outer stem surface is dry before installing the solenoid.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-24 [[inspect-cartridge-components-annually--80cd8a|Inspect Cartridge Components (Annually)]]
> *Applies to:* ExactApply

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — daily rinse · [[rinse-nozzle-bodies-daily--422740|Rinse Nozzle Bodies—Daily]]

>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-20 [[rinse-nozzle-bodies-daily--422740|Rinse Nozzle Bodies—Daily]]
> *Applies to:* ExactApply

## Differences between the two tables

- ExactApply lists *Boosted nozzles won't spray.*, *Coil circuit is closed.* and *System cannot maintain
  constant pressure.*; the INC table does not [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]] [[nozzle-body-troubleshooting--48cd75|Nozzle Body Troubleshooting]].
- For *Coil circuit is open.*, ExactApply gives "Defective nozzle body" (test the nozzle body), and INC
  gives "Defective wiring" (repair wiring) [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]] [[nozzle-body-troubleshooting--48cd75|Nozzle Body Troubleshooting]].
- Both give the same coil resistance range, printed as "6.0—7.0 ohm" (ExactApply) and "6.0—7.0 Ω" (INC)
  [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]] [[nozzle-body-troubleshooting--48cd75|Nozzle Body Troubleshooting]].

## What the manual does not specify

- How to test the nozzle body in the ExactApply table. The table says "Test nozzle body. Contact a John
  Deere dealer or other service provider." and gives no procedure [[nozzle-body--325070|Nozzle Body]].
- Which specific gravity value to enter for a given product
  [[specific-gravity--90a332|Specific Gravity]].
