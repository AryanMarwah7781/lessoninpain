# Nurse Arm — troubleshooting

**Option: Load Command (if equipped).** This table is printed in the Load Command section (p. 120-12)
and applies only to machines with the Load Command nurse arm [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13). It is reproduced in its printed
order; the manual gives no cost or check order, so none is implied.

The same section has separate tables for the sprayer coupler, the Load Command pump and the Load
Command system: [[troubleshooting-sprayer-coupler--8f85af|Troubleshooting Sprayer Coupler]],
[[troubleshooting-load-command-pump--4d3750|Troubleshooting Load Command Pump]], [[troubleshooting-load-command-system--1bc786|Troubleshooting Load Command System]].

Fix topics: [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]] (service the nurse arm coupler as needed and to remove debris), [[rinse-nurse-arm--522bea|Rinse Nurse Arm]], [[clean-sprayer-coupler--977ce7|Clean Sprayer Coupler]],
[[load-command-poppet-maintenance--adcdcf|Load Command Poppet Maintenance]] (extends the Load Command poppet for maintenance and cleaning).

## Warnings that apply to these fixes

> [!warning] CAUTION (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> CAUTION: Solution could remain in the nurse arm coupler. Wear protective equipment to ensure that excess solution does not come into contact with you or others. [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]] (p. 120-16, 120-17)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Rinse the nurse arm plumbing before servicing.
> Valve between the nurse tank and arm must be closed before servicing.
> [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]] (p. 120-16, 120-17)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Do not pressurize nurse arm plumbing beyond pressure capacity of hose and components. [[rinse-nurse-arm--522bea|Rinse Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-14)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Air seal (A) and proximity sensors (B) may not function properly when covered with dirt or debris and can lead to equipment malfunction. [[clean-sprayer-coupler--977ce7|Clean Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-6)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Do not pressure wash sprayer coupler components. Pressure washing can lead to equipment malfunction. [[clean-sprayer-coupler--977ce7|Clean Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-6)

> [!warning] CAUTION (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> CAUTION: Do not rely on load command nurse arm to hold solution in nurse tank. Close tank valve (A) on nurse vehicle before filling tank, before transporting, and when not in use. [[prepare-nurse-arm-for-transport--39e6d5|Prepare Nurse Arm for Transport]] (p. 120-9)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Use ONLY John Deere SD Polyurea grease (TY6341). [[lubricate-nurse-arm-every-season--e38a02|Lubricate Nurse Arm—Every Season]] (p. 120-9, 120-10)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Load Command)
> IMPORTANT: Lubricate nurse arm grease points at the beginning and end of every season. [[lubricate-nurse-arm-every-season--e38a02|Lubricate Nurse Arm—Every Season]] (p. 120-9, 120-10)

## Symptoms

### Nurse arm does not fall away.

1. **Binding between couplers.** — Verify air relief valve and vent coupler is clear. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
2. **Too large of approach angle.** — Adjust approach angle as needed. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
3. **Debris between coupler joints.** — Clean coupler inlet and suction side of valve area. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]]
4. **Air seal not deflating.** — Check valve and hoses. Verify seal is venting properly. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
### Solution does not pump.

1. **Suction-side valve is closed or mechanical problem.** — Open suction-side valve. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
2. **Debris caught in coupler.** — Remove debris from coupler or line. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]]
### Coupler does not lock.

1. **Debris on poppet or coupler prevent full engagement of couplers.** — Remove debris. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]] · [[load-command-poppet-maintenance--adcdcf|Load Command Poppet Maintenance]]
2. **Air seal does not inflate.** — Check air supply to seal. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
### Poppet does not close.

1. **Debris build up around poppet.** — Clean poppet area. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[load-command-poppet-maintenance--adcdcf|Load Command Poppet Maintenance]]
2. **Poppet return spring not functioning.** — Check spring function. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
3. **Nurse vehicle pump running with suction open to nurse coupler line causing poppet to open.** — Close valve from coupler to nurse vehicle pump. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)
### Poppet leaks.

1. **O-ring damaged, missing, or not seated properly.** — Check O-ring. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[service-nurse-arm-coupler--3c1e91|Service Nurse Arm Coupler]]
2. **Debris caught in poppet.** — Remove debris. [[troubleshooting-nurse-arm--064225|Troubleshooting Nurse Arm]] (p. 120-12, 120-13)  
   Fix / related: [[load-command-poppet-maintenance--adcdcf|Load Command Poppet Maintenance]]
