## All four tread adjusts do not function.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Outside of tread adjust speed range. | Travel between 1 km/h (0.6 mph) and 8 km/h (5 mph). |
| Fuse blown. | Replace fuse. |
| Faulty switch. | Replace switch. |
| Load sense jamming valve not working. | Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |
| Engine rpm's set too low. | Increase engine speed to high idle. |
| Low or no hydraulic oil. | Fill hydraulic reservoir to correct level. |
| Plugged suction strainer in hydraulic reservoir. | Inspect and clean strainer. |
| Faulty tread adjust valve. | Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |
| Faulty hydraulic pump. | See Troubleshooting Of Hydraulic Systems in this section. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |

## One individual tread adjust does not function, but others do.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Damaged or corroded electrical connection. | Repair, replace, or clean electrical connection. |
| Damaged electrical harness. | Repair harness. |
| Faulty solenoid valve. | Switch coil with good coil, if function operates, replace coil. |
| Tread adjust shim pads gap adjustment incorrectly set. | Adjust tread adjust shim pads gap correctly. (See Chassis section.) |
| Knee stuck. | Lubricate with TY25733 TEFLON based lubricant. |
| Load sense jam check valve leaking. | Clean, repair, or replace check valve. |
| Check valve stuck shut in load sense line. | Clean, repair, or replace check valve. |
| Broken, damaged, or worn shim pads. | Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |
| Plugged orifice in combo valve tread adjust ports. | Replace orifice. See regular spare part channels to order. |
| Faulty tread adjust diode. | Replace diode. |
| Faulty tread adjust valve. | Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |
| Faulty tread adjust cylinder. | Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. |

## One individual tread adjust does not stop when switch is released.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Sticking or faulty switch. | Replace switch. |
| Sticking hydraulic spool. | Clean repair or replace. |
| Faulty solenoid valve. | Repair or replace solenoid valve. |
