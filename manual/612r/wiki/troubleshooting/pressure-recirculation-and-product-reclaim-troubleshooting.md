# Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim troubleshooting

**Options: pressure recirculation and product reclaim, printed in the ExactApply section.** This
table is in the ExactApply Nozzle Control System (If Equipped) section, pages 125-39 and 125-40
[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]],
and the operating topics for both functions are in the same section
[[operate-pressure-recirculation--0995f2|Operate Pressure Recirculation]]
[[operate-product-reclaim--fc981d|Operate Product Reclaim]]. If your machine does not have these
functions, this page does not apply. Symptoms and rows are in the manual's printed order (the table
does not rank causes); the row split was checked against the rendered page 125-40.

## Warnings for the fixes

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]] (p. 55-26, 55-27, 115-16). Applies to: cleaning the flowmeter
> CAUTION: Do not close the flowmeter shutoff valve (B) with the solution pump running. This can trap pressure in the plumbing that can be hazardous when removing flowmeters for cleaning.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]] (p. 55-26, 55-27, 115-16). Applies to: cleaning the flowmeter
> CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Clean flowmeter in an area where people, animals, vegetation, and water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.

Spray and air-system pressure warnings: [[pressurized-systems-safety]]; relieving boom pressure:
[[relieve-spray-pressure]].

## Pressure recirculation not starting.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Solution pump is turned off. | Turn on the solution pump. Pressure recirculation should start immediately. |
| Spray system on/off switch was recently turned off. | Wait 2 minutes until pressure recirculation resumes flow. |

[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]]

## Pressure recirculation not showing flow on the display.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Plugged solution hose. | Check and unplug the hose. |
| Flowmeter plugged or dirty. | Clean the flowmeter. |
| Flowmeter faulty. | Replace flowmeter. |

[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]]
— [[clean-flowmeter-as-required]].

| Problem | Solution |

## Product is not returning to the tank.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Product reclaim was recently ran. | Wait 30 seconds after product reclaim procedure for fluid to begin flowing. |

## Product reclaim not starting.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Air leak in the system. | Check for leak and repair. |
| Remote load is active. | Turn off remote load. |
| Solution pump is turned on. | Turn off the solution pump. |
| Air purge is active. | Deactivate air purge. |
| Active diagnostic trouble code for the product reclaim system. | Remedy any active diagnostic trouble codes. |

[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]]
— [[operate-product-reclaim--fc981d|Operate Product Reclaim]].

## Product reclaim cycle not completing due to timing out.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Air leak in the system. | Check for leak and repair. |
| Pressure sensor faulty. | Replace pressure sensor. |
| 3-way air valve is stuck. | Inspect and ensure that the valve is working properly. |

[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]]

## Unable to build solution system pressure after product reclaim and receiving a pump dry warning.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Solution pump removed of all fluid. | Attempt to enable the solution pump again. |
| Pump bleed valve is closed. | Inspect and ensure that the valve is open. |
| Pump bleed lines are plugged. | Inspect and clear the lines of obstructions. |

[[pressure-recirculation-and-product-reclaim-troubleshooting--039ba3|Pressure Recirculation and Product Reclaim Troubleshooting]]

## Not able to spray after product reclaim cycle but not receiving a pump dry warning.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|

| Boom isolation valve is stuck. | Inspect and ensure that the valve is working properly. |

## Too much solution in the lines after product reclaim (more than 15 L).

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Poor performance due to boom position. | Ensure that the booms are tilted to the highest position during product reclaim for best performance. |
