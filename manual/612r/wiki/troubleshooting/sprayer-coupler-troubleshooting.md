# Sprayer Coupler troubleshooting

**Option: Load Command.** The sprayer coupler is part of the Load Command system, and this table is
printed in the Load Command (If Equipped) section on page 120-13
[[troubleshooting-sprayer-coupler--8f85af|Troubleshooting Sprayer Coupler]]. If your machine does
not have Load Command, this page does not apply. Symptoms and rows are in the manual's printed order
(the table does not rank causes). Related Load Command tables:
[[troubleshooting-load-command-system--1bc786|Troubleshooting Load Command System]].

## Warnings for the fixes

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: servicing the sprayer coupler
> CAUTION: Operator must wear appropriate personal protection equipment when servicing sprayer coupler.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: servicing the sprayer coupler
> CAUTION: Servicing can result in solution loss. Run evacuation and rinse procedures to prevent solution loss. Collect remaining solution.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: servicing the sprayer coupler
> CAUTION: Coupler cylinder is spring-loaded with compression spring and could cause serious injury to you or others.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[clean-sprayer-coupler--977ce7|Clean Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-6). Applies to: cleaning the sprayer coupler
> IMPORTANT: Do not pressure wash sprayer coupler components. Pressure washing can lead to equipment malfunction.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: servicing the sprayer coupler
> IMPORTANT: Clean air seal (A) using mild detergent and damp cloth. Clean proximity sensors (B) using damp cloth. Do NOT pressure wash.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: servicing the sprayer coupler
> IMPORTANT: Poppet (A) should stay extended and not drift in. If drifting occurs, tighten or service shutoff valve as necessary.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[service-sprayer-coupler--26f987|Service Sprayer Coupler]] (p. 120-15, 120-16). Applies to: sprayer coupler shutoff valve
> IMPORTANT: Shutoff valve must be completely opened for Load Command to function.

The coupler uses the onboard air system; air-system warnings are on [[pressurized-systems-safety]].

## Coupler does not lock.

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Air system leak. | Check air lines, air seals, and valves. |
| Proximity sensors do not sense the nurse coupler. | Clean proximity sensors and check function. |
| Debris on poppet or coupler prevent full engagement of couplers. | Remove debris. |
| Inadequate air capacity to inflate air seal at start-up due to other system demand such as the auto air suspension. | Wait 1—2 minutes for air systems to reach full capacity. |
| Arming switch not activated. | Activate arming switch on the front coupler. |
| Debris on proximity sensors. | Clean proximity sensors. |
