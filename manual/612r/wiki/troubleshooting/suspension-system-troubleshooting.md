# Suspension System — troubleshooting

**System:** suspension, base machine (Troubleshooting section, p. 160-20; no option) [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21). The
manual's table is reproduced in its printed order; the manual gives no cost or check order, so none
is implied. Components: [[tire]], [[wheel]], [[steering]]. Park first: [[park-machine-safely]].
Tire, jacking and air-spring warnings in full: [[tire-and-wheel-safety]].

## Warnings that apply to these fixes

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Air spring can explode if over inflated causing serious injury or death to you or others. When adjusting suspension assemblies, do not exceed 689 kPa (6.89 bar) (100 psi). Keep hands and body away from suspension linkage. [[check-air-springs-daily--582452|Check Air Springs—Daily]] (p. 115-12)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION
> Avoid injury from impact with sprayer frame. Chassis moves during inflation and deflation of air bag suspension system. Stand clear of machine when operating system.
> [[auto-air-level-decal--fd02d1|Auto Air Level Decal]] (p. 10-7, 10-8)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Use of other greases in the suspension system is not recommended. Use of other greases can result in premature wear of the suspension components. [[lubricate-suspension-assemblies-daily--d4a033|Lubricate Suspension Assemblies—Daily]] (p. 45-5, 115-11)
> Also printed in: [[suspension-and-steering-grease--f4a9c8|Suspension and Steering Grease]] (p. 110-12)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Explosive separation of a tire and rim parts can cause serious injury or death. Only attempt to mount a tire if you have the proper equipment and experience to perform the job. ## Symptoms

### Suspension system gives a rough ride.

1. **Tire inflation incorrectly set.** — Check tire inflation (see Inspect Tires—Daily in the Wheels and Tires section). [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]] · [[tire]]
2. **Pressure in air springs not correctly set.** — Correctly set pressure in air springs. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[check-air-springs-daily--582452|Check Air Springs—Daily]]
3. **Proper greasing intervals have not been followed or wrong type of lubrication has been used.** — Follow correct lubrication intervals and lubrication procedures. (See Lubrication And Maintenance section). [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[lubricate-suspension-assemblies-daily--d4a033|Lubricate Suspension Assemblies—Daily]]
### Excessive grease leakage on the suspension.

1. **Over greased.** — Lubricate correctly. (See Lubricate Suspension Assemblies—Daily in the Lubrication And Maintenance section.) [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[lubricate-suspension-assemblies-daily--d4a033|Lubricate Suspension Assemblies—Daily]]
2. **Worn or damaged seals.** — Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)
### Excessive play in suspension or steering components.

1. **Main suspension spindles or bushings are worn.** — Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[steering]]
### Excessive movement of the entire suspension assembly relative to the mainframe.

1. **Loose tread adjust shims.** — Properly adjust tread adjust shims. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)  
   Fix / related: [[adjust-tread-adjust-shims-tire-off-ground-preferred-method--f760b4|Adjust Tread Adjust Shims—Tire off Ground (Preferred Method)]] · [[adjust-tread-adjust-shims-tire-on-ground--99b5c5|Adjust Tread Adjust Shims—Tire on Ground]]
### Auto Air Bag valve leaks air continually.

1. **Elbow installed incorrectly pushing valve stem into constant “ON” position.** — Screw the elbow out so that three threads are showing. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)
### One air bag lower for the Auto Air system.

1. **Bracket is not adjusted correctly.** — Adjust bracket to match other air bags. [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]] (p. 160-20, 160-21)
