# Operator's Cab troubleshooting

This page follows the manual's Operator's Cab troubleshooting table (p. 160-23 to 160-25) [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]. Each
symptom heading is the symptom as printed, and the causes are in the printed order. The manual does not
rank causes by cost. It applies to all models. The CAT4 blower symptom applies only if equipped. See the
component page [[cab]] and the cab safety page [[cab-and-operator-station-safety]].

> [!inference] Rows continued across pages
> The rows at the top of p. 160-25 (from "Expansion valve not functioning.") have an empty Symptom
> column. They are listed under *Failure to cool.*, the last symptom on p. 160-24 [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].

## Drafts.
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Poor air distribution.** → Adjust directional air louvers. Set pressurizer blower switch to "MED" or "LOW".

## Inadequate air flow.
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Clogged air filters.** → Replace filters. See [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
- **Clogged air inlet screen.** → Clean screen.
- **Evaporator core air flow restricted.** → Clean evaporator and housing with compressed air.
- **Defective blower fan switch.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Loose wire connection.** → Tighten connection.
- **Faulty blower fan motor.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Inadequate CAT4 blower air flow (if equipped).
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Clogged air filter.** → Replace the filter. The Cat 4 filter has a one year replacement interval
  [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
- **Clogged air inlet screen.** → Clean the inlet screen.
- **Faulty wiring or loose connections.** → Repair or replace wiring.
- **Faulty blower fan motor.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Water leaking or dripping from evaporator core compartment.
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Drain tubes plugged (AC).** → Clean drain tubes.
- **Loose hose clamp.** → Tighten clamp.

## Pressurizer fan, windshield wiper and/or compressor will not run.
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Faulty wiring or loose connections.** → Repair or replace wiring.
- **Defective switch.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Defective motor.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Strange odors inside the operator's cab.
*p. 160-23* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Dirty air filters.** → Replace filters.
- **Drain tubes plugged.** → Clean drain tubes.
- **Evaporator condenser pan dirty.** → Clean pan and outlet with compressed air.
- **Tobacco smoke and tar on evaporator exterior.** → Replace filters and clean the exterior of
  evaporator. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Partial frosting and sweating of lines combined with poor cooling.
*p. 160-24* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Compressor belt slipping.** → Replace belt.
- **Loss of refrigerant.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Restricted or clogged line.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Expansion valve malfunctioning.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Ice flecks blowing from evaporator.
*p. 160-24* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Shut off air conditioner.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Failure to cool.
*p. 160-24, continued on p. 160-25* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Insufficient blower speed.** → Increase blower speed.
- **Dirty air filters.** → Replace filters.
- **Debris on the radiator screen.** → Clean screen. See
  [[clean-screens-and-cooling-package-as-required--cd4253|Clean Screens and Cooling Package—As Required]].
- **Loose compressor drive belt.** → Replace belt.
- **Dirty air screen.** → Clean screen.
- **Lint or dirt on condenser fins.** → Blow out condenser fins with compressed air.
- **Heater hoses installed backwards.** → Install heater hoses correctly. Contact a John Deere dealer or
  other service provider.
- **Condenser is overheating.** → Clean condenser screens, cores, and fins of condenser and radiator.
- **Outside temperature too low. (Below 70°F) (21°C).** → Wait until day gets warmer. If there is a
  malfunction in system, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Loose wiring connector.** → Tighten connection.
- **Compressor clutch not engaging.** → Check wiring. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Refrigerant is lost or extremely low (may have burned thermal fuse or low pressure switch disengages
  system).** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Defective temperature control switch.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Restriction in the refrigerant system. (High pressure switch disengages system).** → Contact a John
  Deere dealer or other service provider.
- *(p. 160-25)* **Expansion valve not functioning.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Burned out clutch field or faulty field.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Severe restriction in the high side.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
- **Clogged line strainer.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

**Checks from Service Air Conditioner.** This maintenance topic is headed "Check the following if air
conditioner will not cool or cooling is intermittent" [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]]:

- If the air conditioner clutch slips after the machine has been in storage, the compressor may be stuck:
  stop engine and turn key switch to OFF position, remove the clutch cover, and rotate the clutch hub back
  and forth to free the compressor [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].
- Run engine at 2000 rpm, turn on the air conditioner and set the fan to high speed [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].
- If cooling is intermittent, clean grille, side screens, radiator, and condenser; if the problem persists,
  contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider to have the evaporator core cleaned [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].
- Inspect cab body filters for restriction and replace them if any damage or blockage is detected [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].

When first starting the machine after storage, run it at no more than low idle for 4 minutes with the air
conditioning on, to prevent compressor seal failure
[[remove-machine-from-storage--234a44|Remove Machine from Storage]].

> [!conflict] Which refrigerant
> Service Air Conditioner says "R-134a refrigerant must be used" [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]]. The Specifications section lists
> the air conditioner refrigerant as "1234yf", with a freon charge of 1.8 kg (4 lbs)
> [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. Neither is a generic module. Do not add
> refrigerant yourself. The manual says not to attempt to repair or service the refrigerant system, and
> to contact your dealer [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].

## Cab pressure indicator reading at the low end of indicator.
*p. 160-25* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Cab blower switch turned off.** → Turn switch on.
- **Door or window not latched.** → Latch door or window.
- **Restricted air filter.** → Replace with a John Deere activated carbon cab air filter or appropriate
  substitute. See [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
- **Damaged door or window seals.** → Replace damaged seals.
- **Loss of the seal around control linkage, lines, wires, etc.** → Seal any openings.
- **Problem cannot be identified and corrected.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

The cab pressure icon appears in the machine status bar when cab pressure is low
[[cab-pressure-indicator--b26285|Cab Pressure Indicator]].

## Cab pressure indicator reading at high end of indicator.
*p. 160-25* [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]]

- **Damaged seal or pleated filter media on the air filter.** → Replace with a John Deere activated carbon
  cab air filter or appropriate substitute.
- **Missing or incorrect air filter.** → Replace with a John Deere activated carbon cab air filter or
  appropriate substitute.
- **Air filter not seated properly.** → Reseat or replace with a John Deere activated carbon cab air filter
  or appropriate substitute.
- **Problem cannot be identified and corrected.** → Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

## Warnings for the fixes

Replacing cab filters:

> [!warning] CAUTION — activated carbon filters · [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> CAUTION: The use of activated carbon filters in the air-ventilation system of the operator’s cab does not protect against damage to your health caused by toxic chemicals. Before working with chemicals, review and comply with the warning and handling instructions of the manufacturer for the chemicals used and take the recommended actions to protect yourself and the environment against damage. Chemicals must be stored and transported only in properly closed containers that are specified for the purpose. Do not store or transport these containers inside the operator’s cab. For detailed instructions, refer to the documentation provided by the manufacturer.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 90-3 [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — activated carbon filter replacement · [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> IMPORTANT: Activated carbon filters must be replaced after 500 operating hours or after one year (whichever comes first). Heavy-duty operation can require shorter service intervals. Therefore, check the condition of the filter elements regularly. Activated carbon filters must NOT be cleaned (for example, with compressed air.) Instead they must be replaced by new filter elements immediately when the filtering effect decreases. (Dispose of used filters in accordance with regional regulations.)
>
> *Printed on:* p. 90-3 [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — recirculation filter · [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> IMPORTANT: DO NOT clean filter. Replace filter at the service interval or when performance indicates.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 90-3 [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]
> *Applies to:* all models
> [!warning] CAUTION — cab is not chemical protection · [[cab-pressure-indicator--b26285|Cab Pressure Indicator]]
> CAUTION: Exposure to chemicals, including pesticides, can cause injury or death. Wear personal protective equipment in accordance with the chemical manufacturer's label.
> DO NOT rely only on this cab, cab pressure indicator, or cab air filters to protect against chemical exposure.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 90-2 [[cab-pressure-indicator--b26285|Cab Pressure Indicator]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — cab is not chemical protection · [[cab-air-filters--63553f|Cab Air Filters]]
> CAUTION: Exposure to chemicals, including pesticides, can cause injury or death. Wear personal protective equipment in accordance with chemical manufacturer's label.

>
> *Printed on:* p. 90-3 [[cab-air-filters--63553f|Cab Air Filters]]

Cleaning the evaporator, condenser pan and condenser fins with compressed air:

> [!warning] CAUTION — compressed air · [[using-compressed-air-for-cleaning--97e20d|Using Compressed Air For Cleaning]]
> CAUTION: Reduce compressed air to 210 kPa (2 bar) (30 psi) or less when using for cleaning purposes. Clear area of bystanders, guard against flying debris, and wear personnel protective equipment, including eye protection.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 05-10 [[using-compressed-air-for-cleaning--97e20d|Using Compressed Air For Cleaning]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — compressed air · [[clean-cooling-package--751bc4|Clean Cooling Package]]
> CAUTION: Reduce compressed air to 210 kPa (2 bar) (30 psi) or less when using for cleaning purposes. Clear area of bystanders, guard against flying debris, and wear personnel protection equipment, including eye protection. A filter-type respirator is also advised to avoid inhalation of harmful particles.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 95-12 [[clean-cooling-package--751bc4|Clean Cooling Package]]
> *Applies to:* all models
