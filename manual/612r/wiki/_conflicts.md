## Detail

### 1. Fast idle speed is stated two different ways — [[engine]]

The Specifications section groups fast idle (no load) by **model** — 2140 rpm for the 400R, 408R,
410R, 412R, 612R and all Stage 3 units, 2240 rpm for the 600R and 616R — and separately states a
rated engine speed of 2100 rpm (no load) for the 400R, 410R, 410R, 412R, 612R and 2200rpm for the
600R, 616R [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].
The 750-hour check groups the same speeds by **engine displacement** — 2140±50 rpm for machines
with 6.8 L engines and 2240±50 rpm for machines with 9.0 L engines
[[check-engine-speeds-750-hours--a056fe|Check Engine Speeds—750 Hours]].
These do not agree for the 400R, 410R, 412R and 612R, which the Specifications section lists with
9.0 L engines but at the lower fast idle. Neither source is generic Deere-wide boilerplate.
Confirm the correct speed for your machine with your John Deere dealer before adjusting anything.

### 2. How far to turn the Quik-Fill handle is described differently in the two sections — [[fill-valve]]

The single solution section says to rotate the valve handle "until parallel with plumbing" to open
and back to the closed position as shown
[[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]], while the dual product section says to rotate the
valve handle 90 degrees to open and back to the closed position as shown
[[wet-system-valves--b2adcf|Wet System Valves]]. Neither is a generic Deere-wide module. The two
sections describe different machine configurations, so use the one that matches your solution
system rather than assuming they are interchangeable.

### 3. Which filters to drain at the daily water separator check — [[fuel-system]]

One Drain Water Separator—Daily topic says to drain water and contaminates from the water
separator bowl by opening the drain valve (A) on the primary filter (C)
[[drain-water-separator-daily--f9a048|Drain Water Separator—Daily (Prestarting Checks)]], while
the Lubrication and Maintenance copy of the same daily check says to open the drain valve (A) on
the primary (C) and secondary (B) filters
[[drain-water-separator-daily--c8c494|Drain Water Separator—Daily (Lubrication and Maintenance)]].
Both are machine-specific topics; neither is a generic Deere-wide module. This page does not
choose between them — ask your dealer which applies to your engine and filter assembly.

### 4. 1600 gal tank stated as 6056 L, 6057 L and 6065 L — [[solution-tank]]

The manual prints three different metric values for the same 1600 gal tank, and none of the three
topics is a generic Deere-wide module:
- The Single Solution Command System topic gives the 616R solution tank capacity as 6056 L
  (1600 gal) [[solution-system--544787|Solution System]].
- The Dual System Command System capacity table gives the entire tank as 6057 L (1600 gal)
  [[solution-system--3fd9a2|Solution System]].
- The Machine Specification table gives 616R Capacity as 6065 L (1600 gal)
  [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].
All three print the same US value, 1600 gal, and three different metric values. The first two
topics describe different tank arrangements, so they may not be the same tank; the specification
table is machine-level. This page does not pick one. Confirm the capacity for your machine with
your John Deere dealer.

### 5. Maximum solution pressure — [[spray-tip]]

The generic Deere-wide chemical-safety module (`generic: true`) says: do not operate solution
delivery system at pressures exceeding 345kPa (3.5 bar) (50 psi)
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
The machine-specific Wet System topic says most flat fan spray tips are designed to operate in the
207—414 kPa (2—4 bar) (30—60 psi) range
[[flat-fan-spray-tip--dc7cce|Flat Fan Spray Tip]]. The two overlap only up to the lower figure, and
this page does not choose between them. Confirm the limit for your machine and your installed tip
with your John Deere dealer, and follow the product label.

### 6. The same tire-substitution text is carried at two different signal levels — [[tire]]

The Specifications section's tire tables print this text at CAUTION level and say to consult a
John Deere dealer or other service provider for guidance
[[tire-specifications--3bdf9b|Tire Specifications]], while the Machine Specification table prints
the same wording at IMPORTANT level and adds "or tire supplier" to the list of people to consult
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. Neither is a generic Deere-wide module.
Both are reproduced verbatim above; treat the requirement at the higher of the two signal levels
and confirm tire choice with your dealer.

### 7. Wheel hardware retightening interval — [[wheel]]

The Wheels and Tires section says to tighten the wheel mounting nuts "after the first 1 hour of
use and every 10 hours thereafter until the correct torque is maintained"
[[tighten-wheel-hardware--d61ec1|Tighten Wheel Hardware]], while the Lubrication and Maintenance
section says to tighten wheel hardware "after the first 10 hours of use and every 100 hours
thereafter until the correct torque is maintained"
[[tighten-wheel-hardware-100-hours--db6073|Tighten Wheel Hardware—100 Hours]] — the same wording
appears in the after-first-10-hours topic
[[tighten-wheel-hardware-after-first-10-hours--5797ef|Tighten Wheel Hardware—After First 10 Hours]].
Both are machine-specific topics (neither is a generic Deere-wide module) and both give the same
torque and the same "check after every 100 hours of use" rule; only the run-in interval differs.
This page does not choose between them. Confirm the interval for your machine with your John Deere
dealer, and if in doubt the shorter interval is the more frequent check.

### 8. The two copies word the cautions differently — [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req]]

The Wet System copy says the spray system "can contain" hazardous material, that the Quik-Fill
valve (A) "can contain" hazardous materials, and that you should make sure the Quik-Fill valve is

The generic Deere-wide chemical-safety module (`generic: true`) says do not operate solution
delivery system at pressures exceeding 345kPa (3.5 bar) (50 psi)
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]] (p. 55-1).
The machine-specific Flat Fan Spray Tip topic says most flat fan spray tips are designed to operate
in the 207—414 kPa (2—4 bar) (30—60 psi) range [[flat-fan-spray-tip--dc7cce|Flat Fan Spray Tip]] (p. 55-6).
The upper part of the tip range is above the generic cap. This page does not choose; confirm the
limit for your machine with your dealer. For wind, the low-pressure / large-droplet direction stays
below both.

### 44. Machine procedures that run above the generic 50 psi cap — [[wind-and-drift]]

The rinse procedure says to set spray pressure at 620—690 kPa (6.2—6.9 bar) (90—100 psi) when
spraying rinse water [[operate-rinse-system--738e0d|Operate Rinse System]] (p. 60-25), and the boom air
bleed says to run the sprayer in manual spray mode at 620.6 kPa (90 psi)
[[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]] (p. 55-19).
Both are machine-specific and both exceed the generic 345kPa (3.5 bar) (50 psi) cap
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]] (p. 55-1).
These are cleaning/bleeding operations, not application settings, but the manual does not say the
cap excludes them. Reported, not resolved.

### 46. 616R tank level sensor volume — [[load-command-troubleshooting]]

The troubleshooting table says to "check 6056 L (1200 gal) for the 616R tank level sensor function" [[troubleshooting-load-command-system--1bc786|Troubleshooting Load Command System]],
but the Load Command operating topic says the 616R system "shuts off at 6056 L (1600 gal)" [[operating-load-command--d37e14|Operating Load Command]]. The
metric figure agrees; the gallon figure does not. Neither is a generic module. The manual does not say
which is correct. The 616R solution tank capacity is itself printed three different ways elsewhere in
the manual; see the conflicts register.

### 47. Water to prime the Load Command pump — [[load-command-troubleshooting]]

The pump troubleshooting table says "Add 3.8 L (1 gal) of water to priming port on the pump outlet" [[troubleshooting-load-command-pump--4d3750|Troubleshooting Load Command Pump]].
The Fill Load Command Pump procedure says "Fill port with minimum of 3.7 L (1 gal) of water"
[[fill-load-command-pump--e83c85|Fill Load Command Pump]]. Both give 1 gal; the metric figure differs.
Neither is a generic module.

### 48. How many nozzles each INC unit operates — [[locating-faulted-nozzles-troubleshooting]]

Locating Faulted Nozzles (p. 130-30) says "a maximum of five nozzles operated by each INC unit" and that
four or five consecutive faulted nozzles "indicate" the fault is at the INC unit [[locating-faulted-nozzles--477984|Locating Faulted Nozzles]]. Determining Nozzle
Location (p. 25-G-4) says "There is a maximum of nine nozzles operated by each INC unit. Consecutive
nozzles with a fault may indicate that the fault is at the INC unit." [[determining-nozzle-location--984404|Determining Nozzle Location]]. Neither is a generic module.
The manual does not say which applies to which configuration. Check the INC unit layout on your boom or
ask your dealer.

### 49. Metric length of the 100 ft boom — [[locating-faulted-nozzles-troubleshooting]]

Locating Faulted Nozzles labels it "30.5 m (100 ft) Boom" [[locating-faulted-nozzles--477984|Locating Faulted Nozzles]]; Determining Nozzle Location labels it
"30.6 m (100 ft) Boom" [[determining-nozzle-location--984404|Determining Nozzle Location]]. The nozzle numbers are the same. Neither is a generic module.

### 50. Which refrigerant — [[operator-s-cab-troubleshooting]]

Service Air Conditioner says "R-134a refrigerant must be used" [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]]. The Specifications section lists
the air conditioner refrigerant as "1234yf", with a freon charge of 1.8 kg (4 lbs)
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. Neither is a generic module. Do not add
refrigerant yourself. The manual says not to attempt to repair or service the refrigerant system, and
to contact your dealer [[service-air-conditioner--4b53da|Service Air Conditioner]].

### 51. Distance for re-pairing — [[remote-nozzle-control-troubleshooting]]

receiver for 20 seconds [[operate-system-with-the-remote-control--30853f|Operate System with the Remote Control]]. Both machine-specific; the ranges overlap but are not the same.
