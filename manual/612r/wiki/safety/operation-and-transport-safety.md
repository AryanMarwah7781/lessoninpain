# Operating and transporting the machine

This page covers the hazards met while driving: preparing for and travelling on public roads, driving
in the field, slopes and parking, and moving the machine by trailer or tow. Every DANGER, CAUTION and
IMPORTANT is copied as printed. When the same warning appears in several places it is given once, with
every page it appears on. For boom movement and overhead lines see [[boom-and-overhead-lines-safety]];
for the cab, seat belts and getting on and off see [[cab-and-operator-station-safety]]. The step-by-step
road procedure is [[transport-machine]].

## Transport speed limits, as printed

| Limit | Condition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Speed Range (Transport) 0—56 km/h (0—35 mph) | all models | [[machine-specification--bfe721\|Machine Specification]] |
| EU/CU countries 0—40 km/h (0—24.8 mph) | EU/CU countries | [[machine-specification--bfe721\|Machine Specification]] |
| Do not transport machine above 48 km/h (30 mph) | **unless application system is empty** | [[axle-load-decal--4b48d5\|Axle Load Decal]] |
| Maximum transport speeds are not available | when the tread width is not adjusted to the narrowest setting | [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8\|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]] |
| F1 range, target speed of 16 km/h (10 mph) | selected automatically when the machine is started | [[transport-machine--4216ec\|Transport Machine]] |
| Speed Range (Field) w/Wet System 0—40 km/h (0—25 mph) | field operation | [[machine-specification--bfe721\|Machine Specification]] |

The axle load decal on the left-hand corner post reads (no signal word is printed on it): "Avoid serious
injury or death resulting from loss of control or collision due to machine overload during transport.
Refer to Operator's Manual for proper tire pressure and maximum loads. Do not transport machine above
48 km/h (30 mph) unless application system is empty. Follow all local laws or regulations for machine
maximum speed limit" [[axle-load-decal--4b48d5|Axle Load Decal]].

So, as printed: with anything in the application system, 48 km/h (30 mph) is the limit; the
0—56 km/h (0—35 mph) transport range applies only with the application system empty, the tread at its
narrowest, and within local law [[axle-load-decal--4b48d5|Axle Load Decal]]
[[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]].

## Task: preparing for and travelling on public roads

> [!warning] CAUTION — maximum transport speed · [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> CAUTION: DO NOT exceed maximum transport speed of the machine or loss of control can occur, causing serious injury or death.
> Transport the machine at the narrowest tread adjust setting only.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-26 [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — tread width on the road · [[tread-adjust--bcebd6|Tread Adjust]]
> CAUTION: Always use the narrowest possible tread width when transporting the machine on a road.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 25-L-1 [[tread-adjust--bcebd6|Tread Adjust]]; p. 25-L-5 [[manual-tread-adjust-procedure--f69e40|Manual Tread Adjust Procedure]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — speed range and narrow tread · [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]]
> IMPORTANT: Machine damage can occur if the speed range adjustment switch is moved from a higher speed range to a lower speed range, causing the engine to exceed 3000 rpm. Target speed can be changed when the machine is moving or stopped.
> Maximum transport speeds are not available when the tread width is not adjusted to the narrowest setting.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-16 [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]]
> *Applies to:* all models; printed in both the Manual Mode and Auto Mode driving procedures

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — speed range and narrow tread · [[drive-the-machine-auto-mode--c2def8|Drive the Machine—Auto Mode]]
> IMPORTANT: Machine damage can occur if the speed range adjustment switch is moved from a higher speed range to a lower speed range, causing the engine to exceed 3000 rpm. The target speed can be changed when the machine is moving or stopped.
> Maximum transport speeds are only available if the tread width is adjusted to narrowest setting.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-18 [[drive-the-machine-auto-mode--c2def8|Drive the Machine—Auto Mode]]
> *Applies to:* all models; printed in both the Manual Mode and Auto Mode driving procedures

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — booms on the cradles · [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> IMPORTANT: Machine can be damaged if transported with the booms not lowered onto the boom cradles.
> Boom Return to Height will remain active after the boom is folded. When lowering boom onto cradles, boom may stop at set point if it is above support rest height. Boom can be lowered beyond set point by pressing the boom lower switch again.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-26 [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — tire load ratings · [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> IMPORTANT: Do not exceed tire weight load ratings printed in this manual. Weigh the machine and verify maximum transport weight and speed from this manual before loading and do not exceed published weights.
> Refer to Wheels and Tires section of this manual for proper tire inflation recommendations. (See Safety Signs section in the manual for guidelines on transporting the machine.)
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-27 [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]
> *Applies to:* all models; tire tables differ by model — see the tire page

> [!warning] CAUTION — warning lights · [[operate-warning-lights--b8e48e|Operate Warning Lights]]
> CAUTION: When transporting the machine on a road or highway at night or during the day, use lights and devices for adequate warning to operators of other vehicles. Check local governmental regulations. Various safety devices are available and can be obtained via regular spare part channels. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider. Keep safety items in good condition. Replace missing or damaged items.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-15 [[operate-warning-lights--b8e48e|Operate Warning Lights]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — nozzle lights · [[nozzle-body-lighting--9d74cb|Nozzle Body Lighting]]
> CAUTION: Do not transport the machine when the nozzle lights are in manual mode. Your vision and the vision of others will be impaired.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 25-G-31 [[nozzle-body-lighting--9d74cb|Nozzle Body Lighting]]
> *Applies to:* machines with nozzle body lighting

> [!warning] CAUTION — AutoTrac on roadways · [[operate-autotrac--5661e4|Operate AutoTrac]]
> CAUTION: While AutoTrac is active, the operator is responsible for steering at the end of the path and collision avoidance.
> The system automatically steers the machine. To avoid injury, verify that no bystanders are in the vicinity before calibration.
> Always turn off (disable) AutoTrac system before entering a roadway. Do not attempt to turn on (activate) AutoTrac system while transporting on a roadway.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 140-3 [[operate-autotrac--5661e4|Operate AutoTrac]]
> *Applies to:* machines with AutoTrac / AutoTrac RowSense

> [!warning] CAUTION — AutoTrac on roadways · [[operate-autotrac--5661e4|Operate AutoTrac]]
> CAUTION: Always turn off (disable) AutoTrac system before entering a roadway. To disable AutoTrac from the display, press the steer on-off button until STEER OFF is displayed.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 140-4 [[operate-autotrac--5661e4|Operate AutoTrac]]
> *Applies to:* machines with AutoTrac / AutoTrac RowSense

> [!warning] CAUTION — AutoTrac on roadways · [[autotrac-rowsense-theory-of-operation--64ef2f|AutoTrac RowSense Theory of Operation]]
> CAUTION: Do not use the AutoTrac RowSense system on roadways. Always turn off (disable) AutoTrac RowSense system before entering a roadway. Do not attempt to turn on (activate) AutoTrac RowSense system while transporting on a roadway.
> When the system is activated, stay alert and pay attention to the surrounding environment. Take control of steering when necessary to avoid field hazards, bystanders, equipment, or other obstacles. To operate and steer the machine safely, stop operation if poor visibility conditions impair steering ability.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 140-11 [[autotrac-rowsense-theory-of-operation--64ef2f|AutoTrac RowSense Theory of Operation]]
> *Applies to:* machines with AutoTrac / AutoTrac RowSense

From the Safety section (no signal word printed): keep SMV emblem and reflectors clean and in place;
do not exceed maximum transport speed specified in the operator manual; reduce speeds for icy, wet,
graveled or soft roadway surfaces; check and follow local regulations for equipment size, lighting and
marking before driving on public roadways [[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]].
Use headlights, flashing warning lights, and turn signals day and night (generic Deere-wide module)
[[use-safety-lights-and-devices--fe3bed|Use Safety Lights and Devices]].

## Task: driving — downhill, braking, and who is around the machine

> [!conflict] Downhill transport: which speed limit the caution states
> The same downhill CAUTION is printed three ways. The Manual Mode driving procedure says to "select a
> speed (less than 35 mph)" [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]]; the
> Auto Mode procedure says "select a speed (less than 56.3 km/h [35 mph])"
> [[drive-the-machine-auto-mode--c2def8|Drive the Machine—Auto Mode]]; and the Transport Machine
> procedure gives no number, only "select a speed range that is appropriate for the load on the machine
> and the steepness and length of the slope" [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]. The
> Specifications section gives the transport range as 0—56 km/h (0—35 mph)
> [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. None of these is a generic module. All three
> versions are carried below; none says what speed is appropriate for a given slope or load.

> [!warning] CAUTION — downhill at transport speed · [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]]
> CAUTION: When transporting the machine downhill at transport speeds, the machine tries to hold the target speed until the engine starts to overspeed. At this time, the vehicle speed could increase beyond the target speed. Before starting down a hill, select a speed (less than 35 mph) that is appropriate for the load on the machine and the steepness and length of the slope.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-17 [[drive-the-machine-manual-mode--3165f8|Drive the Machine—Manual Mode]]
> *Applies to:* all models

At this time, the vehicle speed could increase beyond the target speed. Before starting down a hill, select a speed (less than 56.3 km/h [35 mph]) that is appropriate for the load on the machine and the steepness and length of the slope.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-19 [[drive-the-machine-auto-mode--c2def8|Drive the Machine—Auto Mode]]

## Task: slopes and parking

From the Safety section (no signal word printed): avoid holes, ditches, and obstructions which may cause
sprayer roll-over, especially on hillsides; avoid sharp turns on hills; never drive near the edge of a
gully or steep embankment; stay off slopes that are too steep for operation
[[use-caution-on-hillsides--649b6a|Use Caution On Hillsides]]. DO NOT operate close to a ditch or creek
[[operate-safely--cb2671|Operate Safely]].

Park brakes are automatically applied when the multi-function lever is in the PARK position; the
secondary brake switch is not to be engaged when parking the machine
[[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]]. Park brake engages when engine is turned off regardless
of multi-function lever position; always shut engine off when leaving machine and remove key when
leaving machine unattended [[operate-safely--cb2671|Operate Safely]]. After each secondary brake use,
verify the park brakes: stop fully loaded machine on a 20 degree slope or steepest grade available that
the machine will be parked on, with rear of machine up hill, and press the park brake switch; if the
machine rolls or "creeps", contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider
[[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]]. See
[[park-machine-safely]].

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — park brake switch · [[park-brake--e0c23d|Park Brake]]
> IMPORTANT: Pressing the park brake switch applies the park brakes at vehicle speeds below 0.8 km/ h (0.5 mph).
>
> *Printed on:* p. 30-14 [[park-brake--e0c23d|Park Brake]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — stopping the engine · [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]]
> IMPORTANT: Prevent engine damage. Before stopping an engine that has been operating at working load, idle the engine for at least 2 minutes to cool the hot engine parts.
> On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is OFF. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF), immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-27 [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]]
> *Applies to:* all models; the DEF paragraph applies to Final Tier 4/Stage V engines

> [!warning] CAUTION — nurse tank on a hill · [[operating-load-command--d37e14|Operating Load Command]]
> CAUTION: Swinging nurse arm can cause injury to you and others. DO NOT PARK the nurse tank on a hill where the nurse arm could swing out. ALWAYS park the nurse tank on flat ground.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 120-7 [[operating-load-command--d37e14|Operating Load Command]]
> *Applies to:* machines with Load Command

> [!warning] CAUTION — nurse tank on a hill · [[operating-load-command--d37e14|Operating Load Command]]
> CAUTION: Swinging nurse arm can cause injury to you and others. Always maintain control of the nurse arm when it is unlatched or disconnected.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 120-7 [[operating-load-command--d37e14|Operating Load Command]]
> *Applies to:* machines with Load Command

## What the manual does not specify

- A maximum slope angle for operation, for transport, or for parking. The only angle printed is the
  20 degree slope used to *test* the park brakes
  [[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]].
- A downhill speed for a given load or grade; all three versions of the caution leave it to the operator
  [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].
- A transport speed for a partially filled tank. The decal only distinguishes an empty application
  system from one that is not empty [[axle-load-decal--4b48d5|Axle Load Decal]].
