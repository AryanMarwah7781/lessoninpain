# Servicing the machine safely

Warnings you meet while maintaining the machine, grouped by the hazard of the task, copied
verbatim. Identical text printed in several places appears once with every page listed; each
different wording has its own box. Chemical decontamination is on [[chemical-handling-safety]],
tires, jacking and air springs on [[tire-and-wheel-safety]], batteries, electrical work and welding
on [[electrical-and-welding-safety]].

## 1. Before any service

Stop and secure the machine first: [[park-machine-safely]]. The general rules have no signal word
and come from generic Deere-wide modules (`generic: true`):

- Understand service procedure before doing work. Keep area clean and dry [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
- Never lubricate, service, or adjust machine while it is moving. Keep hands, feet, and clothing away
  from power-driven parts. Disengage all power and operate controls to relieve pressure. Lower
  equipment to the ground. Stop the engine and secure the machine. If equipped, remove the key,
  disconnect the battery, and allow machine to cool [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
- Securely support any machine elements that must be raised for service work [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
- Falling while cleaning or working at height can cause serious injury. Use a ladder or platform to
  easily reach each location. Use sturdy and secure footholds and handholds [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
- Use tools appropriate to the work. Makeshift tools and procedures can create safety hazards [[use-proper-tools--e4ecc4|Use Proper Tools]].
- Engine exhaust fumes can cause sickness or death. If it is necessary to run an engine in an
  enclosed area, remove the exhaust fumes from the area with an exhaust pipe extension [[work-in-ventilated-area--277b10|Work In Ventilated Area]].

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Remove key from the key switch to help prevent accidents. [[operate-the-engine--9bbe7e|Operate the Engine]] (p. 50-15)

> [!warning] CAUTION (applies if equipped: AutoTrac, RowSense)
> CAUTION: To avoid serious injury or death, shut OFF the engine, set the parking brake, and remove the key prior to cleaning the vision cameras. [[clean-autotrac-vision-cameras--51a7df|Clean AutoTrac Vision Cameras]] (p. 145-1)

> [!warning] CAUTION (applies if equipped: AutoTrac, RowSense)
> CAUTION: To avoid serious injury or death, shut OFF the engine, set the parking brake, and remove the key prior to cleaning the row sensor. [[clean-autotrac-rowsense-sensor--e7cd7e|Clean AutoTrac RowSense Sensor]] (p. 145-1)

## 2. Pressurized fluids: hydraulic, fuel, accumulators, solution pump

Applies to: hydraulic lines, accumulators, high-pressure fuel system, solution pump. Related:
[[fuel-system]], [[solution-pump]], [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours]],
[[relieve-spray-pressure]].

Generic rules (no signal word):
- Escaping fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin causing serious injury. Avoid the hazard by
  relieving pressure before disconnecting hydraulic or other lines. Tighten all connections before
  applying pressure. Search for leaks with a piece of cardboard. Protect hands and body from
  high-pressure fluids. In case of accidental skin injection, seek immediate surgical treatment
  [[avoid-high-pressure-fluids--3fc79d|Avoid High-Pressure Fluids]].
- Escaping fluid or gas from pressurized hydraulic accumulator systems can cause serious injury.
  Extreme heat can cause the accumulator to burst, and pressurized lines can be accidentally cut. Do
  not weld or use a torch near a pressurized accumulator or pressurized line. Relieve pressure from
  the hydraulic system before removing accumulator. Never attempt to relieve hydraulic system or
  accumulator pressure by loosening a fitting. Accumulators cannot be repaired [[service-accumulator-systems-safely--6bd0ed|Service Accumulator Systems Safely]].
- High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar
  with this type of system should perform repairs. Do not disconnect or attempt repair of fuel lines,
  sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with
  High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system [[do-not-open-high-pressure-fuel-system--78594e|Do Not Open High-Pressure Fuel System]].

## 3. Cooling system

Applies to: surge tank, radiator, engine coolant.

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Explosive release of fluids from pressurized cooling system can cause serious burns.
> Shut off engine, set park brake, and remove key.
> NEVER add coolant when engine is overheated. Wait for it to cool.
> NEVER remove surge tank cap when coolant or engine is hot. Wait until engine coolant is cool to remove cap.
> SLOWLY loosen cap to relieve pressure before removing completely.
> [[service-cooling-system-safely--04c2de|Service Cooling System Safely]] (p. 05-8)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (generic Deere-wide module)
> IMPORTANT: Water may be used as coolant in emergency situations only.
> Foaming, hot surface aluminum and iron corrosion, scaling, and cavitation occur when water is used as the coolant, even when coolant conditioners are added.
> Drain cooling system and refill with recommended engine coolant as soon as possible.
> [[operating-in-warm-temperature-climates--f16850|Operating in Warm Temperature Climates]] (p. 110-6)

## 5. Exhaust filter: hot surfaces, cleaning cycles and ash

Applies to: exhaust filter, Diesel Particulate Filter, exhaust outlet. Generic rules (no signal
word): during exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine may run at elevated idle and hot
temperatures for an extended period of time. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach
temperatures hot enough to burn people, or ignite or melt common materials. Keep exhaust outlet
away from people and anything that can melt, burn, or explode. Contact with exhaust components
while still hot can result in serious personal injury. Avoid contact with these components until
cooled to safe temperatures [[clean-exhaust-filter-safely--2abccf|Clean Exhaust Filter Safely]].

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: The exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle.
> Servicing machine or attachments during exhaust filter cleaning can result in serious personal injury. Avoid exposure and skin contact with hot exhaust gases and components.
> During automated or manual/stationary exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine runs at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to cause burns to people, or ignite or melt common materials.
> Never perform exhaust filter cleaning procedures in a closed building unless suitable exhaust is provided.
> [[exhaust-filter-system-overview--49a974|Exhaust Filter System Overview]] (p. 25-C-3, 25-C-4)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: When AUTO, PARKED, or REQUESTED cleaning is enabled, the exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle. Disable exhaust filter cleaning system in conditions where it can be unsafe for elevated exhaust temperatures. [[auto-exhaust-filter-cleaning--5f0614|AUTO Exhaust Filter Cleaning]] (p. 25-C-4, 25-C-5)

Only a qualified service provider should remove ash from the exhaust filter. Personal protective equipment and clothing, maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition, should be used when handling and cleaning exhaust filter. See a John Deere dealer or other service provider for exhaust filter handling and disposal. [[exhaust-filter-ash-handling-and-disposal--8bcac6|Exhaust Filter Ash Handling and Disposal]] (p. 05-10)
