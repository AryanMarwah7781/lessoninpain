# Cab, ladder and operator station

This page covers the hazards met getting on and off the machine, in the operator's seat, when starting
the engine, when working with chemicals from the cab, in an emergency exit, and during parked exhaust
filter cleaning. Every DANGER, CAUTION and IMPORTANT is copied as printed. When the same warning
appears in several places it is given once, with every page it appears on. Several cautions are printed
more than once with small differences in wording or capitalisation. Each printed version is carried
separately rather than merged. See also [[cab]], [[operation-and-transport-safety]] and
[[boom-and-overhead-lines-safety]].

## Task: getting on and off the machine

The ladder automatically raises when the park brake is disengaged and the multi-function lever is moved
forward, and automatically lowers when the machine is shut off or when the machine is in park and the
operator is out of seat [[ladder--3da11b|Ladder]].

From the Safety section (generic Deere-wide module; no signal word printed): prevent falls by facing the
machine when getting on and off; maintain 3-point contact with steps, handholds, and handrails; use
extra care when mud, snow, or moisture present slippery conditions; never jump when exiting machine;
never mount or dismount a moving machine
[[use-steps-and-handholds-correctly--d57810|Use Steps and Handholds Correctly]]. Falling while cleaning
or working at height can cause serious injury; use a ladder or platform to easily reach each location
[[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].

The Do Not Ride decal reads: "Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not ride on machine."
[[do-not-ride-decal--63a7ca|Do Not Ride Decal]]

## Task: in the seat — seat belts, riders and the quick stop

From the Safety section (no signal word printed): "Avoid crushing injury or death during rollover."
Fasten the seat belt across the hips and inspect seat belt and mounting hardware at least once a year
[[use-seat-belt-properly--f3594d|Use Seat Belt Properly]]. Only allow the operator on the machine; keep
riders off (generic Deere-wide module) [[keep-riders-off-machine--90b605|Keep Riders Off Machine]].

> [!warning] CAUTION — seat belt · [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]
> CAUTION: To avoid serious injury or death to you or others, always use the seat belt properly.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-25 [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — seat belt inspection · [[seat-belts--15e188|Seat Belts]]
> CAUTION: Inspect seat belts and mounting hardware on your machine at least once a year. If the seat belt system, including mounting hardware, buckle, belt, or retractor, shows any sign of damage such as cuts, fraying, extreme or unusual wear, discoloration or abrasion, the entire seat belt system should be replaced immediately. Replace the belt system only with replacement parts approved for your machine. Replacement parts can be obtained via regular spare part channels.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 30-28 [[seat-belts--15e188|Seat Belts]]
> *Applies to:* all models; operator and instructional seats

> [!warning] CAUTION — instructional seat · [[instructional-seat--a95b28|Instructional Seat]]
> CAUTION: The instructional seat has been provided only for training operators or diagnosing machine problems. Keep all other riders off the machine and equipment. Always wear seat belts.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 30-28 [[instructional-seat--a95b28|Instructional Seat]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — quick stop switch · [[quick-stop-switch--43ba9f|Quick Stop Switch]]
> CAUTION: When pressing the quick stop switch, you are responsible for pulling back on the multi-function lever to stop the machine.
> It is always recommended to wear your seat belt to avoid serious injury.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 30-20 [[quick-stop-switch--43ba9f|Quick Stop Switch]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — operate from the seat with AutoTrac RowSense · [[autotrac-rowsense-theory-of-operation--64ef2f|AutoTrac RowSense Theory of Operation]]
> IMPORTANT: The AutoTrac RowSense system is intended to aid the operator in performing field operations more efficiently. The operator is always responsible for the machine path and must continue to pay attention to the surrounding environment while operating the machine. ## Task: starting the engine

> [!conflict] Lever position for starting: park only, or park or neutral?
> The machine's own Prevent Machine Runaway CAUTION says "Start engine only from the operator's seat,
> with multi-function lever in “park.”" [[prevent-machine-runaway--f285a6|Prevent Machine Runaway]].
> The Bypass Start decal reads "Start only from seat in park or neutral."
> [[bypass-start-decal--cfcea5|Bypass Start Decal]], and the generic Deere-wide Prevent Machine Runaway
> module (`generic: true`) says "with transmission in neutral or park"
> [[prevent-machine-runaway--7c4822|Prevent Machine Runaway]]. The Start the Engine procedure also says
> that the engine starting with the multi-function lever out of the "Park" position indicates a
> malfunction of the starting circuit [[start-the-engine--a12543|Start the Engine]]. The manual does not
> reconcile them; the machine-specific procedures say Park.

> [!warning] DANGER — bypass start decal · [[bypass-start-decal--cfcea5|Bypass Start Decal]]
> DANGER
> Start only from
> seat in park or neutral. Starting in gear kills.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 10-4 [[bypass-start-decal--cfcea5|Bypass Start Decal]]
> *Applies to:* all models; decal on the machine

> [!warning] CAUTION — machine runaway · [[prevent-machine-runaway--f285a6|Prevent Machine Runaway]]
> CAUTION: Runaway machine can cause serious injury or death to you or others.
> Do not start engine by shorting across starter terminals. Machine starts in gear if normal circuitry is bypassed.
> NEVER start engine while standing on ground. Start engine only from the operator's seat, with multi-function lever in “park.”
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-2 [[prevent-machine-runaway--f285a6|Prevent Machine Runaway]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — exhaust fumes at start-up · [[start-the-engine--a12543|Start the Engine]]
> CAUTION: Prevent asphyxiation. Engine exhaust fumes can cause sickness or death to you or others.
> If you must operate engine in a building, be positive there is adequate ventilation. Either use an exhaust pipe extension to remove the exhaust fumes or open doors and windows to bring enough outside air into the area.
> Avoid the possibility of personal injury or death:
> - Be sure that everyone is clear of the machine.
> - Start engine only from the operator’s seat.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 50-5 [[start-the-engine--a12543|Start the Engine]]
> *Applies to:* all models

DO NOT start engine with multi-function lever engaged [[operate-safely--cb2671|Operate Safely]].

## Task: emergency exit

In an emergency, exit the cab through the entrance door when possible; if not, a hammer is fitted in the
cab that can be used to break a window [[emergency-exit--3327a1|Emergency Exit]]. The emergency exit
device also has a knife in the handle to cut seat belts if they do not unbuckle
[[left-hand-cab-items--80aecc|Left-Hand Cab Items]].

> [!warning] CAUTION — emergency exit hammer · [[emergency-exit--3327a1|Emergency Exit]]
> CAUTION: Cover eyes, face and uncovered skin from jagged or broken glass when using break glass hammer.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 05-16 [[emergency-exit--3327a1|Emergency Exit]]
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — emergency exit hammer · [[emergency-exit--3327a1|Emergency Exit]]
> CAUTION: Do not use hammer on front window (windshield). It will not shatter.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 05-16 [[emergency-exit--3327a1|Emergency Exit]]
> *Applies to:* all models

If the machine contacts a power line, the manual says to stay inside the cab if circumstances allow — see
[[boom-and-overhead-lines-safety]] [[in-case-of-contact-with-power-lines--700aac|In Case of Contact with Power Lines]].

## Task: exhaust filter cleaning with the operator at the station

During parked filter cleaning the machine must remain parked; the conditions are stop machine motion,
set engine speed to low idle, engage park brake, stop remote loading, and turn spray or spread system
master OFF [[parked-filter-cleaning--52f4ff|Parked Filter Cleaning]].

> [!warning] CAUTION — hot exhaust during filter cleaning · [[exhaust-filter-system-overview--49a974|Exhaust Filter System Overview]]
> CAUTION: The exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle.
> Servicing machine or attachments during exhaust filter cleaning can result in serious personal injury. Avoid exposure and skin contact with hot exhaust gases and components.
> During automated or manual/stationary exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine runs at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to cause burns to people, or ignite or melt common materials.
> Never perform exhaust filter cleaning procedures in a closed building unless suitable exhaust is provided.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 25-C-3 [[exhaust-filter-system-overview--49a974|Exhaust Filter System Overview]]
> *Applies to:* machines with an exhaust filter (Final Tier 4/Stage V)

> [!warning] CAUTION — hot exhaust during filter cleaning · [[auto-exhaust-filter-cleaning--5f0614|AUTO Exhaust Filter Cleaning]]
> CAUTION: When AUTO, PARKED, or REQUESTED cleaning is enabled, the exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle. Disable exhaust filter cleaning system in conditions where it can be unsafe for elevated exhaust temperatures.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 25-C-4 [[auto-exhaust-filter-cleaning--5f0614|AUTO Exhaust Filter Cleaning]]
> *Applies to:* machines with an exhaust filter (Final Tier 4/Stage V)

> [!warning] CAUTION — hot exhaust during filter cleaning · [[parked-filter-cleaning--52f4ff|Parked Filter Cleaning]]
> CAUTION: When AUTO, PARKED, or REQUESTED cleaning is enabled, the exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle.
> Servicing machine or attachments during exhaust filter cleaning can result in serious personal injury. Avoid exposure and skin contact with hot exhaust gases and components.
> During automated or manual/stationary exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine runs at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to cause burns to people, or ignite or melt common materials.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 25-C-5 [[parked-filter-cleaning--52f4ff|Parked Filter Cleaning]]
> *Applies to:* machines with an exhaust filter (Final Tier 4/Stage V)

> [!warning] CAUTION — exhaust filter ash · [[exhaust-filter-ash-handling-and-disposal--8bcac6|Exhaust Filter Ash Handling and Disposal]]
> CAUTION: Under federal, state and/or local laws or regulations, exhaust filter ash may be classified as a hazardous waste. Hazardous waste must be disposed of in accordance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws or regulations governing hazardous waste disposal. Only a qualified service provider should remove ash from the exhaust filter. Personal protective equipment and clothing, maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition, should be used when handling and cleaning exhaust filter. See a John Deere dealer or other service provider for exhaust filter handling and disposal.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 05-10 [[exhaust-filter-ash-handling-and-disposal--8bcac6|Exhaust Filter Ash Handling and Disposal]]
> *Applies to:* machines with an exhaust filter

From the Safety section (generic Deere-wide module): keep the machine away from people, animals, or
structures which may be susceptible to harm or damage from hot exhaust gases or components, and always
disable movement (neutral), set the parking brake and disconnect power to attachments or tools before
leaving the operator's station [[clean-exhaust-filter-safely--2abccf|Clean Exhaust Filter Safely]].
