# Handling chemicals and pesticides

Every chemical-hazard warning the manual prints for the spray system, grouped by the task in which
you meet it. Each warning is copied verbatim in its own box with the page(s) where it is printed;
identical text printed in several places appears once with every page listed. Where the wording
differs even slightly, each version has its own box. Warnings from Dual Product, ExactApply and
Individual Nozzle Control sections are marked in the box title.

## The product label and the law govern

- Always follow all label directions for effective, safe, and legal use of agricultural chemicals
  [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]]. The same sentence is printed in [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
- Read and follow all instructions in: the manufacturer's label for each chemical applied, the U.S.
  Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Worker Protection Standard for Agricultural Pesticides,
  state or regional guidelines for worker safety and health, and the Operator's Manual for this
  machine; numerous requirements must be met, including but not limited to EPA regulations [[avoid-exposure-to-chemicals--54538c|Avoid Exposure To Chemicals]].
- Observe all national, state, and local laws, regulations, or ordinances governing the handling or
  disposal of waste fluids [[decommissioning-proper-recycling-and-disposal-of-fluids-and--e34318|Decommissioning: Proper Recycling and Disposal of Fluids and Components]].

Where anything on this page and the product label differ, the label and the law govern.
Both [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]] and [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]] are generic Deere-wide modules (`generic: true`).

## 1. Before you start: protective equipment and the cab

Applies to: operator, cab, cab air filters. Related: [[cab]].

- Wear appropriate personal protective equipment as recommended by the manufacturer. In the absence
  of manufacturer's instructions, follow these general guidelines: Chemicals labeled 'Danger': Most
  toxic. Generally require use of goggles, respirator, gloves, and skin protection. Chemicals labeled
  'Warning': Less toxic. Generally require use of goggles, gloves, and skin protections. Chemicals
  labeled 'Caution': Least toxic. Generally require use of gloves and skin protection [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]].
- A Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) provides specific details on chemical products: physical and
  health hazards, safety procedures, and emergency response techniques
  [[handle-chemical-products-safely--05af3d|Handle Chemical Products Safely]] (generic).

## 4. Spraying

Applies to: solution delivery system, nozzles, spray tips. Related: [[wind-and-drift]],

The generic chemical-sprayer module has no signal word; its drift and pressure rules are

- If nozzle clogs or system malfunctions, stop engine and relieve spray pressure from system.
- Do not place nozzle tips or other components to the mouth to clear obstructions. Keep spare tips
  on hand for replacement.

  solution delivery system at pressures exceeding 345kPa (3.5 bar) (50 psi); do not spray when winds

## 7. Storage, disposal and personal hygiene

No signal word on these; they are the manual's rules [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]]:
- Keep chemicals in original containers. Do not transfer chemicals to unmarked containers or to
  containers used for food or drink.
- Store chemicals in a secure, locked area way from human or livestock food. Keep children away.
- Always dispose of containers properly. Triple rinse empty containers and puncture or crush
  containers and dispose of properly.
- Wash hands and face after using chemicals and before eating, drinking, smoking, or urination. Do
  not smoke or eat while applying chemicals. After handling chemicals, always bathe or shower and
  change clothes. Wash clothing before wearing again.

Properly dispose of unused chemicals, flushing solution, and empty chemical containers, and
decontaminate equipment used in mixing, transferring, and applying chemicals after use [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]]. Do not
pour waste fluids onto the ground, down a drain, or into any water source, and minimize exposure to
components which may have residue from agricultural chemicals, such as fertilizers and pesticides
[[decommissioning-proper-recycling-and-disposal-of-fluids-and--e34318|Decommissioning: Proper Recycling and Disposal of Fluids and Components]].

## 8. First aid

- Always have soap, water, and towel available when working with chemicals. If chemical contacts
  skin, hands, or face, wash immediately with soap and water. If chemical gets into eyes, flush
  immediately with water [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]].
- Seek medical attention immediately if illness occurs during or shortly after use of checmials
  [[handle-agricultural-chemicals-safely--28d9d7|Handle Agricultural Chemicals Safely]] (sic — spelling as printed).
- If spray or chemical concentrate contacts skin, hands, or face, wash immediately with soap and
  water. If spray or chemical concentrate gets into eyes, flush immediately with water [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
- The solution-pump CAUTION in section 6 adds: if spray material contacts skin, wash off immediately
  with clean water and detergent or follow manufacturer's instructions for the chemical last used
  [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]].
- Check the MSDS for emergency response techniques
  [[handle-chemical-products-safely--05af3d|Handle Chemical Products Safely]].
