## 1. Inflating, mounting and choosing tires

Applies to: tires, rims. The general rules have no signal word and come from generic Deere-wide
modules (`generic: true`):

- Explosive separation of a tire and rim parts can cause serious injury or death. Do not attempt to
  mount a tire unless you have the proper equipment and experience to perform the job [[service-tires-safely--db4bf1|Service Tires Safely]].
- Always maintain the correct tire pressure. Do not inflate the tires above the recommended
  pressure. Never weld or heat a wheel and tire assembly. The heat can cause an increase in air
  pressure resulting in a tire explosion. Welding can structurally weaken or deform the wheel [[service-tires-safely--db4bf1|Service Tires Safely]].
- When inflating tires, use a clip-on chuck and extension hose long enough to allow you to stand to
  one side and NOT in front of or over the tire assembly. Use a safety cage if available [[service-tires-safely--db4bf1|Service Tires Safely]].
- Wheels and tires are heavy. When handling wheels and tires use a safe lifting device or get an
  assistant to help lift, install, or remove [[service-tires-safely--db4bf1|Service Tires Safely]].
- Use only prescribed tire sizes with correct ratings and inflate to the pressure specified in this
  manual. Use of other than prescribed tires may decrease stability, affect steering, result in
  premature tire failure, or cause other durability or safety issues [[follow-tire-recommendations--fe9e2b|Follow Tire Recommendations]].

## 5. Tread width, transport and towing

Applies to: tread adjust, tires, wheel hubs. Related: [[transport-machine]].

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Always use the narrowest possible tread width when transporting the machine on a road. [[tread-adjust--bcebd6|Tread Adjust]] (p. 25-L-1, 25-L-2, 25-L-3)
> Also printed in: [[manual-tread-adjust-procedure--f69e40|Manual Tread Adjust Procedure]] (p. 25-L-5, 25-L-6)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: DO NOT exceed maximum transport speed of the machine or loss of control can occur, causing serious injury or death.
> Transport the machine at the narrowest tread adjust setting only.
> [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26, 50-27)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not exceed tire weight load ratings printed in this manual. Weigh the machine and verify maximum transport weight and speed from this manual before loading and do not exceed published weights.
> Refer to Wheels and Tires section of this manual for proper tire inflation recommendations. (See Safety Signs section in the manual for guidelines on transporting the machine.)
> [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26, 50-27)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: To tow or pull the sprayer, engine MUST be running and hydraulic system MUST have correct hydraulic pressure or damage WILL occur to brakes and wheel hubs.
> If engine is not running or proper hydraulic system pressure cannot be maintained, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider and consult the sprayer Technical Manual for proper brake and wheel hub disengagement procedure.
> The machine should only be towed if stuck, WITH the engine running, correct hydraulic pressure, and the secondary brake released. Only pull the machine a short distance and at slow speed of 0—5 km/h (0—3 mph).
> [[tow-the-sprayer--4c2976|Tow The Sprayer]] (p. 105-2)
