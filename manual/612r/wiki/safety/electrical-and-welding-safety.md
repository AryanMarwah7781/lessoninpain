# Electrical work and welding

Warnings for power lines, batteries, jump-starting and charging, disconnecting the battery, fuses,
installing electrical accessories, and welding or heating on the machine — copied verbatim and
grouped by task. Identical text printed in several places appears once with every page listed; each
different wording has its own box. Component page: [[electrical-system]]. General service rules are
on [[maintenance-safety]].

## 1. Overhead and underground power lines

Applies whenever the machine moves or the boom is raised or unfolded. Related: [[boom]],
[[transport-machine]].

> [!warning] DANGER
> DANGER
> To avoid injury or death do not contact electric lines when moving or operating this machine.
> [[electric-line-contact-decal--693605|Electric Line Contact Decal]] (p. 10-1)

> [!warning] CAUTION (generic Deere-wide module)
> CAUTION: Risk of Electric Shock
> Contact with overhead or underground power lines or electrical arcing can cause serious injury or death. If power lines touch the ground, dangerous voltage differences can exist on the ground (step voltage).
> [[in-case-of-contact-with-power-lines--700aac|In Case of Contact with Power Lines]] (p. 05-13)

### If your machine has ExactApply (product reclaim)

> [!warning] CAUTION (applies if equipped: ExactApply)
> CAUTION: Keep away from overhead power lines. Serious death to you or others can result if the machine contacts electrical wires. Know the transport height, boom operating height, and boom folding height of the machine.
> Perform a complete assessment of the field before performing any spraying operations to decide the best working method and boom folding/unfolding operations.
> [[operate-product-reclaim--fc981d|Operate Product Reclaim]] (p. 125-38, 125-39)

## 2. Servicing batteries

Applies to: batteries, battery terminals. The generic rules (no signal word) from
[[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]] (`generic: true`):
- Battery gas can explode. Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Use a flashlight to check
  battery electrolyte level. Never check battery charge by placing a metal object across the posts.
  Use a voltmeter or hydrometer. Always remove grounded (-) battery clamp first and replace grounded
  clamp last [[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]].
- If acid is spilled on skin or in eyes: flush skin with water; apply baking soda or lime to help
  neutralize the acid; flush eyes with water for 15—30 minutes; get medical attention immediately.
  If acid is swallowed: do not induce vomiting; drink large amounts of water or milk, but do not
  exceed 2 L (2 qt.); get medical attention immediately [[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]].

> [!warning] WARNING (generic Deere-wide module)
> WARNING: Battery posts, terminals, and related accessories contain lead and lead compounds, chemicals known to the State of California to cause cancer and reproductive harm. Wash hands after handling. [[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]] (p. 05-7, 05-8, 85-2, 85-3)
> Also printed in: [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Batteries can explode causing serious injury or death to you or others. Do not attempt to service batteries unless you have the proper equipment and experience to perform the job. Have it done by a John Deere dealer or other service provider. [[servicing-batteries--e9a1c5|Servicing Batteries]] (p. 85-3)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: BATTERY IS NEGATIVE GROUNDED. Always connect starter cable to positive (+) terminal of battery and battery ground cable to negative (-) terminal of battery. Reversed polarity in battery or alternator can result in permanent damage to electrical system. [[servicing-batteries--e9a1c5|Servicing Batteries]] (p. 85-3)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Battery gas can explode. Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Use a flashlight to check battery electrolyte level.
> Never check battery charge by placing a metal object across the posts. Use a voltmeter or hydrometer.
> Always remove grounded (-) battery clamp first and replace it last.
> [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Sulfuric acid in battery electrolyte is poisonous. It is strong enough to burn skin, eat holes in clothing, and cause blindness if splashed into eyes.
> Avoid the hazard by:
> 1.Filling batteries in a well-ventilated area.
> 2.Wearing eye protection and rubber gloves.
> 3.Avoiding breathing fumes when electrolyte is added.
> 4.Avoiding spilling or dripping electrolyte.
> 5.Use proper jump start procedure.
> If you spill acid on yourself:
> 1.Flush your skin with water.
> 2.Apply baking soda or lime to help neutralize the acid.
> 3.Flush your eyes with water for 15—30 minutes. Get medical attention immediately.
> If acid is swallowed:
> 1.Do not induce vomiting.
> 2.Drink large amounts of water or milk, but do not exceed 2 L (2 quarts).
> 3.Get medical attention immediately.
> [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not add water in freezing weather unless machine operates at least 30 minutes. [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30)

## 3. Jump-starting, booster batteries and chargers

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Gas given off by batteries is explosive. Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Make last connection and first disconnection at a point away from booster batteries. [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Be sure polarity is correct before making connections. Reversed polarity will damage electrical system or possibly cause an explosion of a battery.
> If a booster battery is used, two or more batteries cannot be used in series. They must be parallel. Be sure polarity is correct before making connections. Reversed polarity will damage electrical system or possibly cause an explosion of a battery.
> [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Set battery charger at the nominal 12 volt and no more than 16 volt maximum. [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to jump start machine from these terminals. They are protected with 30-amp fuse. [[use-remote-terminals--32db2b|Use Remote Terminals]] (p. 85-24)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not jump-start engines with arc welding equipment. Currents and voltages are too high and may cause permanent damage. [[welding-near-electronic-control-units--175df5|Welding Near Electronic Control Units]] (p. 85-1)

## 4. Disconnecting the battery (Final Tier 4/Stage V engines: wait for the DEF purge)

Applies to: battery disconnect switch, battery cables. The DEF-purge condition applies to Final Tier
4/Stage V engine equipped machines. Related: [[park-machine-safely]].

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until Light Emitting Diode (LED) on the battery box has turned off. This indicates the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines. [[operate-the-engine--9bbe7e|Operate the Engine]] (p. 50-15)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until Light Emitting Diode (LED) on the battery box has turned off. This indicates the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug lines. In freezing weather, DEF will freeze and possibly burst lines. [[disconnect-battery-final-tier-4-and-stage-v-engines--fc1019|Disconnect Battery (Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines)]] (p. 85-1)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until light emitting diode (LED) (B) has turned off. This indicates that the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete. [[battery-disconnect-switch--c9c8e5|Battery Disconnect Switch]] (p. 85-24)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: After light turns off, turn switch counterclockwise to disconnect battery. [[machine-informational-decals--8583ad|Machine Informational Decals]] (p. 50-2, 50-3, 50-4)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Prevent engine damage. Before stopping an engine that has been operating at working load, idle the engine for at least 2 minutes to cool the hot engine parts.
> On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is OFF. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF), immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.
> [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]] (p. 50-27, 50-28)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is off. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]] (p. 165-5, 165-6, 165-7)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (generic Deere-wide module)
> IMPORTANT: Do not remove battery leads for at least 4 minutes after engine stops. The SCR system automatically purges itself of Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) immediately after the engine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, residual DEF can freeze and possibly damage components of the SCR system during cold-weather exposure. [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-overview--8b2d6a|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System Overview]] (p. 50-12, 50-13)

## 6. Installing radios, antennas and other electrical accessories

- Falling while installing or removing electronic components mounted on equipment can cause serious
  injury. Use a ladder or platform to easily reach each mounting location. Do not install or remove
  components in wet or icy conditions [[handle-electronic-components-and-brackets-safely--7269e1|Handle Electronic Components and Brackets Safely]] (generic).

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Under no circumstances should business band radio antenna be mounted to rear of cab or antenna cable be routed near harness for electrical system controllers or operator controls. Failure to follow these precautions could expose operator to radio frequency energy levels higher than recommended by American National Standards Institute (ANSI) and/or could cause undesirable performance of electronically controlled systems. [[installing-mobile-radio-and-antenna--4e9a21|Installing Mobile Radio and Antenna]] (p. 30-38)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Prevent possible personal injury. Disconnect battery ground cable before any electrical repair. [[installing-mobile-radio-and-antenna--4e9a21|Installing Mobile Radio and Antenna]] (p. 30-38)

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Avoid personal injury. Never mount business band radio antenna to rear of cab. Never route antenna cable near harness for electrical system controllers or operator controls. Failure to follow these precautions could expose operator to radio frequency energy levels higher than recommended by American National Standards Institute (ANSI) and/or could cause undesirable performance of electronically controlled systems.
> Disconnect battery ground cable before any electrical repair.
> [[install-business-band-citizens-band-cb-antenna-and-or-radio--179867|Install Business Band/Citizens Band (CB) Antenna and/or Radio]] (p. 90-5, 90-6)

## 7. Engine block heater cords and starting fluid

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: To avoid electrical shock or fire, use a 3-wire, 14 AWG (14 gauge), heavy-duty electrical cord with 15 A rating, suitable for outdoor use. Always plug electrical cord into 120-volt outlet protected by GFI (ground fault interrupter).
> Before connecting heater to a power source, be sure that element is immersed in coolant. NEVER energize the heater in air. Doing so can cause the element sheath to burst, causing personal injury.
> [[auxiliary-heater-usage--008885|Auxiliary Heater Usage]] (p. 50-7, 50-8)

## 9. Welding and heating on the machine

Before welding, protect the electronic control units — procedure from [[welding-near-electronic-control-units--175df5|Welding Near Electronic Control Units]]:
1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery cable(s).
2. Disconnect the positive (+) battery cable(s).
3. Short the positive and negative vehicle battery cables together. Do not attach to vehicle frame.
4. Clear or move any wiring harness sections away from welding area.
5. Connect welder ground close to welding point and away from control units.
6. After the repair area is complete and all components are back in their proper locations, connect
   positive (+) battery cable(s) first, then connect negative (-) battery cable(s).

On Final Tier 4/Stage V machines, step 1 is subject to the DEF-purge wait in section 4.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT (applies if equipped: Dry Spreader)
> IMPORTANT: DO NOT WELD THE VEHICLE FRAME! Such welding can lead to fatigue cracking.
> Do not distort the machine mounts when tightening the mounting hardware.
> [[install-dry-spreader--3e25a5|Install Dry Spreader]] (p. 75-1, 75-2, 75-3, 75-4, 75-5, 75-6)

Generic rules (no signal word, `generic: true`):
- Hazardous fumes can be generated when paint is heated by welding, soldering, or using a torch.
  Remove paint a minimum of 100 mm (4 in.) from area to be affected by heating. If paint cannot be
  removed, wear an approved respirator before heating or welding [[remove-paint-before-welding-or-heating--2d5644|Remove Paint Before Welding or Heating]].
- If you use solvent or paint stripper, remove stripper with soap and water before welding. Allow
  fumes to disperse at least 15 minutes before welding or heating. Do not use a chlorinated solvent
  in areas where welding will take place [[remove-paint-before-welding-or-heating--2d5644|Remove Paint Before Welding or Heating]].
- Flammable spray can be generated by heating near pressurized fluid lines, resulting in severe burns
  to yourself and bystanders. Do not heat by welding, soldering, or using a torch near pressurized
  fluid lines or other flammable materials [[avoid-heating-near-pressurized-fluid-lines--5bd505|Avoid Heating Near Pressurized Fluid Lines]].
- Do not weld or use a torch near a pressurized accumulator or pressurized line
  [[service-accumulator-systems-safely--6bd0ed|Service Accumulator Systems Safely]].
- Never weld or heat a wheel and tire assembly — see [[tire-and-wheel-safety]]
  [[service-tires-safely--db4bf1|Service Tires Safely]].
