# Booms, folding and overhead lines

The hazards on this page are the ones an operator meets when the boom moves or when the machine is
near electric lines: unfolding and folding, maintenance mode and boom calibrations, breakaway, and
working or travelling near overhead lines. Every DANGER, CAUTION and IMPORTANT is copied as printed.
When the same warning appears in several places it is given once, with every page it appears on.
Warnings printed on the machine as decals are shown the way the decal prints them, without a colon
after the signal word [[electric-line-contact-decal--693605|Electric Line Contact Decal]].

Related pages: [[boom]] · [[boom-height]] · [[operation-and-transport-safety]] ·
[[cab-and-operator-station-safety]].

## Task: working near electric lines

> [!warning] DANGER — electric line contact decal · [[electric-line-contact-decal--693605|Electric Line Contact Decal]]
> DANGER
> To avoid injury or death do not contact electric lines when moving or operating this machine.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 10-1 [[electric-line-contact-decal--693605|Electric Line Contact Decal]]
> *Applies to:* all models; decal on the machine

> [!warning] CAUTION — contact with power lines · [[in-case-of-contact-with-power-lines--700aac|In Case of Contact with Power Lines]]
> CAUTION: Risk of Electric Shock
> Contact with overhead or underground power lines or electrical arcing can cause serious injury or death. If power lines touch the ground, dangerous voltage differences can exist on the ground (step voltage).
>
> *Printed on:* p. 05-13 [[in-case-of-contact-with-power-lines--700aac|In Case of Contact with Power Lines]] (generic Deere-wide module)
> *Applies to:* all models

> [!warning] CAUTION — overhead lines during product reclaim · [[operate-product-reclaim--fc981d|Operate Product Reclaim]]
> CAUTION: Keep away from overhead power lines. Serious death to you or others can result if the machine contacts electrical wires. Know the transport height, boom operating height, and boom folding height of the machine.
> Perform a complete assessment of the field before performing any spraying operations to decide the best working method and boom folding/unfolding operations.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 125-38 [[operate-product-reclaim--fc981d|Operate Product Reclaim]]
> *Applies to:* machines with ExactApply and Product Reclaim (printed in the ExactApply Product Reclaim procedure)

**What to do if the machine contacts a power line** (Safety section; generic Deere-wide module)
[[in-case-of-contact-with-power-lines--700aac|In Case of Contact with Power Lines]]:

- If circumstances allow, stay inside the cab. Alert local emergency services.
- If the machine can be moved safely, attempt to drive it away from the power line; otherwise, wait
  for the power line to be deenergized.
- Warn others (for instance bystanders) to stay at least 10 m (33 ft) away from the machine and the
  surrounding area.
- Leave the cab ONLY if absolutely necessary, for example if the machine catches fire. If you must
  leave the cab, do not touch the machine and the ground at the same time; jump clear of the machine
  without touching it and land on the ground with feet together; carefully walk at least 10 m (33 ft)
  away with small steps, feet touching one another, to reduce the risk of step voltage.

Other printed instructions on this hazard carry no signal word. The Safety section says: keep away
from overhead power lines; serious injury or death to you or others can result should the machine
contact electrical wires; know the transport height, boom operating height and boom folding height of
your machine [[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]]. It also says
"DO NOT fold or unfold boom near overhead wires." [[operate-safely--cb2671|Operate Safely]]

> [!inference] Heights you need to know
> The manual tells you to know the transport height, boom operating height and boom folding height
> [[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]]. The Specifications section gives only
> the boom operating height, 686—2197 mm (27—86.5 in)
> [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. No transport height or folding height is
> printed in these topics, so measure them or ask your dealer.

## Task: folding and unfolding the booms

the boom section and the calibrations, "stay out of the folding and unfolding area" in the operator's
station section). Both are carried below.

## Task: boom breakaway

> [!warning] CAUTION — full boom breakaway · [[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]]
> CAUTION: Serious injury or death to you or others can occur from standing in front of boom when it is returning to its original position. Full boom breakaway system is under hydraulic pressure. If the boom must be lifted or aided over an object or an obstruction, stand behind the boom to avoid injury.
>
> *Printed on:* p. 80-6 [[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]]
> *Applies to:* machines with full boom breakaway

If the boom must be lifted or aided over an object or an obstruction, stand behind the boom [[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]].

## Task: fitting a non-standard boom

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — aftermarket or lengthened booms · [[equipping-machines-purchased-with-less-boom-option--2c4bcb|Equipping Machines Purchased with Less Boom Option]]
> IMPORTANT: The 408R, 410R, 412R, 612R, and 616R Sprayers are optimized for up to a 36.6 m (120 ft) steel or 40.2 m (132 ft) carbon fiber spray boom. Installation of a longer spray boom may result in diminished spraying performance. To avoid sprayer vehicle damage and loss of warranty when using non-standard spray booms:
> - DO NOT modify a factory spray boom for adding length.
> - DO NOT install an aftermarket spray boom (including plumbing and optional equipment) with a dry weight exceeding 1597.8 kg (3522 lb).
> - DO NOT install an aftermarket spray boom that has a center of gravity (B) further from the boom pivot (A) than a distance (C) of 6719 mm (265 in).
>
> *Printed on:* p. 80-1 [[equipping-machines-purchased-with-less-boom-option--2c4bcb|Equipping Machines Purchased with Less Boom Option]]
> *Applies to:* 408R, 410R, 412R, 612R and 616R as printed; the 400R and 600R are not named
