# Fuel, fire and batteries — safety

Warnings met when refueling, servicing the fuel system, starting in cold weather, dealing with a fire,
and working on the batteries. Each warning is copied verbatim, once, with every page it is printed on.
Generic Deere-wide modules (`generic: true`) are marked. Fuel-system pressure warnings are here;
pressure in other systems is on [[pressurized-systems-safety]]. The rule about waiting for the DEF
purge before disconnecting the battery is on [[exhaust-and-def-safety]].

## Task: refueling

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[fill-fuel-tank--966e81|Fill Fuel Tank]] (p. 45-4). Applies to: refueling
> CAUTION: Shut off the engine before filling the fuel tank. Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[handling-and-storing-diesel-fuel--530af5|Handling and Storing Diesel Fuel]] (p. 110-2) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: handling and storing diesel fuel
> CAUTION: Reduce the risk of fire. Handle fuel carefully. DO NOT fill the fuel tank when engine is running. DO NOT smoke while you fill the fuel tank or service the fuel system.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[diesel-fuel--b7117c|Diesel Fuel]] (p. 110-1) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: fuel choice
> CAUTION: Avoid severe injury or death due to the fire and explosion risk from using E-Diesel fuel.

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (no signal word) — [[handle-fuel-safely-avoid-fires--1e08df|Handle Fuel Safely—Avoid Fires]] (p. 05-13). Applies to: refueling, fuel containers
> Handle fuel with care: it is highly flammable. Do not refuel the machine while smoking or when near open flame or sparks. Always stop engine before refueling machine. Fill fuel tank outdoors. Prevent fires by keeping machine clean of accumulated trash, grease, and debris. Always clean up spilled fuel. Use only an approved fuel container for transporting flammable liquids. Never fill fuel container in pickup truck with plastic bed liner. Always place fuel container on ground before refueling. Touch fuel container with fuel dispenser nozzle before removing can lid. Keep fuel dispenser nozzle in contact with fuel container inlet when filling. Do not store fuel container where there is an open flame, spark, or pilot light such as within a water heater or other appliance.

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (no signal word) — [[avoid-static-electricity-risk-when-refueling--d41f03|Avoid Static Electricity Risk When Refueling]] (p. 05-14). Applies to: refueling with ultra-low sulfur diesel
> Static charges can build up in ULSD fuel while it is flowing through fuel delivery systems. Static electricity discharge when combustible vapors are present could result in a fire or explosion. Therefore, it is important to ensure that the entire system used to refuel your machine (fuel supply tank, transfer pump, transfer hose, nozzle, and others) is properly grounded and bonded.

## Task: fuel-system service (lines, filters, water separator)

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (no signal word) — [[do-not-open-high-pressure-fuel-system--78594e|Do Not Open High-Pressure Fuel System]] (p. 05-8). Applies to: engines with High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system
> High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system should perform repairs. Do not disconnect or attempt repair of fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]] (p. 95-6). Applies to: fuel lines
> CAUTION: Escaping diesel fuel under pressure can have sufficient force to penetrate the skin, causing serious injury. Before disconnecting lines, be sure to relieve pressure. Before applying pressure to the system, be sure ALL connections are tight and lines, pipes and hoses are not damaged. Keep hands and body away from pinholes and nozzles which eject fluid under pressure. Use a piece of cardboard or wood, rather than hands, to search for suspected leaks. If ANY fluid is injected into the skin, it must be surgically removed within a few hours by a doctor familiar with this type injury or gangrene may result. Doctors unfamiliar with this type of injury may call the Deere & Company Medical Department in Moline, Illinois, or other knowledgeable medical source.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]] (p. 95-6). Applies to: HPCR fuel system
> CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system should perform repairs. Before disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]] (p. 95-6). Applies to: fuel tank
> IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to pressurize the fuel tank with compressed air or any other means. Fuel tank and surrounding structural damage could result.

Engine must be allowed to sit for at least 15 minutes before beginning work on fuel system; this
allows fuel system to bleed off internal high pressure
[[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]].

The fuel-filter topics print this CAUTION in three slightly different wordings; each is copied as printed.

> CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system can perform repairs. Prior to disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with a high-pressure common rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-9-0-l-engine--3400ab|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (9.0 L Engine)]] (p. 95-9, 95-10); [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]] (p. 115-32, 115-33). Applies to: replacing fuel filters — 6.8 L and 9.0 L engines
> CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system can perform repairs. Before disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with high-pressure common rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-9-0-l-engine--e41111|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (9.0 L Engine)]] (p. 115-33). Applies to: replacing fuel filters — 9.0 L engine
> CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system can perform repairs. Before disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with high pressure common rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--01999c|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]] (p. 95-8, 95-9); [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]] (p. 115-32, 115-33). Applies to: replacing fuel filters — 6.8 L engine
> CAUTION: Fuel in filters can be under pressure. Open valve (A) on bottom of the water separator bowl to relieve pressure before removing the filter.

Applies to: replacing fuel filters — 9.0 L engine
> CAUTION: Fuel in filters can be under pressure. Open valve (A) on bottom of the water separator bowl to relieve pressure before removing filter.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[drain-water-from-fuel-filter-6-8-l-engine-only--6dc9d8|Drain Water from Fuel Filter—6.8 L Engine Only]] (p. 95-8). Applies to: 6.8 L engine only
> CAUTION: Always shut off engine, remove key, and set park brake before performing maintenance work on the fuel filter.

The 6.8 L engine is the 408R; the 400R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R and 616R have the 9.0 L engine
(Final Tier 4) [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

## Task: cold-weather starting

The engine type decides which starting-aid warning applies (6.8 L = 408R; 9.0 L = all other models
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]).

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[cold-weather-starting-6-8-l-engine--55e351|Cold-Weather Starting—6.8 L Engine]] (p. 50-6). Applies to: 6.8 L engine (408R)
> CAUTION: Do not use ether or starting fluids to start engine with a cold-weather starting device. Ether is highly flammable and could ignite on engines with cold-weather starting device.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[cold-weather-starting-6-8-l-engine--55e351|Cold-Weather Starting—6.8 L Engine]] (p. 50-6). Applies to: 6.8 L engine (408R)
> IMPORTANT: Never use ether starting fluid to start engine. Damage to engine results.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[cold-weather-starting-9-0-l-engine-without-starting-aid--ca07ce|Cold Weather Starting—9.0 L Engine without Starting Aid]] (p. 50-6, 50-7). Applies to: 9.0 L engine without starting aid
> CAUTION: Starting fluid is flammable. While using this product, do not smoke and make sure to extinguish all flames. Turn off all pilot lights, stoves, heaters, electrical motors, and other sources of ignition while using this product and/or if vapors are still present. Avoid contact of aerosol with battery terminals, solenoid, or other electrical/electronic components. Do not overuse this product. Keep a cap on the container and store in cool location when not in use.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[cold-weather-starting-9-0-l-engine-with-starting-aid--152c60|Cold Weather Starting——9.0 L Engine with Starting Aid]] (p. 50-7). Applies to: 9.0 L engine with starting aid
> CAUTION: Starting fluid is flammable. While using this product, do not smoke and make sure to extinguish all flames. Turn off all pilot lights, stoves, heaters, electrical motors, and other sources of ignition while using this product and/or if vapors are still present.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[change-starting-fluid-canister--2d8461|Change Starting Fluid Canister]] (p. 50-7). Applies to: starting fluid canister
> CAUTION: Do not use starting fluid near fire, sparks, or flames. Read caution information on the container. Protect container against damage. Do not carry starting fluid canisters inside cab.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[auxiliary-heater-usage--008885|Auxiliary Heater Usage]] (p. 50-7, 50-8). Applies to: auxiliary (coolant) heater
> CAUTION: To avoid electrical shock or fire, use a 3-wire, 14 AWG (14 gauge), heavy-duty electrical cord with 15 A rating, suitable for outdoor use. Always plug electrical cord into 120-volt outlet protected by GFI (ground fault interrupter). Before connecting heater to a power source, be sure that element is immersed in coolant. NEVER energize the heater in air. Doing so can cause the element sheath to burst, causing personal injury.

> [!inference] Ether on the 6.8 L engine vs starting fluid on the 9.0 L engine
> The 6.8 L topic forbids ether or starting fluids with a cold-weather starting device, while the
> 9.0 L topics describe using starting fluid. These are different engines, so they are not treated as
> a conflict; check which engine your machine has before using any starting fluid
> [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

## Task: fire

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[in-case-of-fire--e93d75|In Case of Fire]] (p. 05-2) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: any fire on or near the machine
> CAUTION: Avoid personal injury.

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (continuation of the CAUTION above) — [[in-case-of-fire--e93d75|In Case of Fire]] (p. 05-2)
> Stop machine immediately at the first sign of fire. Fire may be identified by the smell of smoke or sight of flames. Because fire grows and spreads rapidly, get off the machine immediately and move safely away from the fire. Do not return to the machine! The number one priority is safety.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]] (p. 165-5, 165-6, 165-7). Applies to: washing the machine before storage
> CAUTION: DO NOT use gasoline for washing. Gasoline is highly flammable and can ignite if exposed to sparks or open flame, causing a fire that can cause serious injury or death.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]] (p. 55-3, 55-4, 55-5). Applies to: solution pump
> CAUTION: DO NOT pump flammable or explosive fluids such as gasoline, fuel oil, and kerosene. ONLY pump solutions compatible with pump components.

## Task: batteries — inspecting, charging, boosting

> [!warning] WARNING — printed on [[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]] (p. 05-7, 05-8, 85-2, 85-3) (generic Deere-wide module); [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30). Applies to: batteries
> WARNING: Battery posts, terminals, and related accessories contain lead and lead compounds, chemicals known to the State of California to cause cancer and reproductive harm. Wash hands after handling.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[servicing-batteries--e9a1c5|Servicing Batteries]] (p. 85-3). Applies to: servicing batteries
> CAUTION: Batteries can explode causing serious injury or death to you or others. Do not attempt to service batteries unless you have the proper equipment and experience to perform the job. Have it done by a John Deere dealer or other service provider.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30). Applies to: battery service
> CAUTION: Battery gas can explode. Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Use a flashlight to check battery electrolyte level. Never check battery charge by placing a metal object across the posts. Use a voltmeter or hydrometer. Always remove grounded (-) battery clamp first and replace it last.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]] (p. 115-29, 115-30). Applies to: battery electrolyte
> CAUTION: Sulfuric acid in battery electrolyte is poisonous. It is strong enough to burn skin, eat holes in clothing, and cause blindness if splashed into eyes. Avoid the hazard by: 1.Filling batteries in a well-ventilated area. 2.Wearing eye protection and rubber gloves. 3.Avoiding breathing fumes when electrolyte is added. 4.Avoiding spilling or dripping electrolyte. 5.Use proper jump start procedure. If you spill acid on yourself: 1.Flush your skin with water. 2.Apply baking soda or lime to help neutralize the acid. 3.Flush your eyes with water for 15—30 minutes. Get medical attention immediately. If acid is swallowed: 1.Do not induce vomiting. 2.Drink large amounts of water or milk, but do not exceed 2 L (2 quarts). 3.Get medical attention immediately.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[servicing-batteries--e9a1c5|Servicing Batteries]] (p. 85-3). Applies to: battery cables
> IMPORTANT: BATTERY IS NEGATIVE GROUNDED. Always connect starter cable to positive (+) terminal of battery and battery ground cable to negative (-) terminal of battery. Reversed polarity in battery or alternator can result in permanent damage to electrical system.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4). Applies to: booster battery or charger
> CAUTION: Gas given off by batteries is explosive. Keep sparks and flames away from batteries. Make last connection and first disconnection at a point away from booster batteries.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4). Applies to: booster battery
> IMPORTANT: Be sure polarity is correct before making connections. Reversed polarity will damage electrical system or possibly cause an explosion of a battery. If a booster battery is used, two or more batteries cannot be used in series. They must be parallel. Be sure polarity is correct before making connections. Reversed polarity will damage electrical system or possibly cause an explosion of a battery.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[operate-a-booster-battery-or-charger--6af242|Operate a Booster Battery or Charger]] (p. 85-3, 85-4). Applies to: battery charger
> IMPORTANT: Set battery charger at the nominal 12 volt and no more than 16 volt maximum.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[use-remote-terminals--32db2b|Use Remote Terminals]] (p. 85-24). Applies to: remote terminals
> IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to jump start machine from these terminals. They are protected with 30-amp fuse.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[welding-near-electronic-control-units--175df5|Welding Near Electronic Control Units]] (p. 85-1). Applies to: welding near electronic control units
> IMPORTANT: Do not jump-start engines with arc welding equipment. Currents and voltages are too high and may cause permanent damage.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[installing-mobile-radio-and-antenna--4e9a21|Installing Mobile Radio and Antenna]] (p. 30-38). Applies to: installing a mobile radio
> CAUTION: Prevent possible personal injury. Disconnect battery ground cable before any electrical repair.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[battery-box-fuses-and-relay--6ee8f8|Battery Box Fuses and Relay]] (p. 85-22, 85-23). Applies to: battery box fuses
> IMPORTANT: Only attempt to disassemble fuses when instructed by a John Deere dealer or other service provider. Ensure that both the negative (-) and positive (+) battery connections are disconnected from both batteries before fuse inspection or replacement.

The generic battery module also gives first aid for acid: if acid is spilled on skin or in eyes,
flush skin with water, apply baking soda or lime to help neutralize the acid, and flush eyes with
water for 15—30 minutes, then get medical attention immediately; if acid is swallowed, do not induce
vomiting, drink large amounts of water or milk, but do not exceed 2 L (2 qt.), and get medical
attention immediately [[handling-batteries-safely--3a873e|Handling Batteries Safely]] (generic
Deere-wide module). The machine topic prints the same limit as 2 L (2 quarts)
[[service-batteries-250-hours--871de0|Service Batteries—250 Hours]].

**Final Tier 4 / Stage V machines:** do not disconnect the battery until the DEF purge is complete —
the warnings, and a disagreement between them, are on [[exhaust-and-def-safety]].
