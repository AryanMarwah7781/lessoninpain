# Exhaust, aftertreatment and DEF — safety

Warnings met when running the engine in a building, during exhaust filter cleaning, when handling
exhaust filter ash, when filling, storing or servicing diesel exhaust fluid (DEF), and when shutting
down and disconnecting the battery. Each warning is copied verbatim, once, with every page it is
printed on. Generic Deere-wide modules (`generic: true`) are marked.

**Applicability:** the DEF and SCR warnings apply to machines with Final Tier 4 or Stage V engines —
the SCR topic is titled "Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System (Final Tier 4 and Stage V
Engines)" [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-final-tier-4-and-st--c3c982|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System (Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines)]].
The component background is on [[aftertreatment]].

## Task: starting and running the engine indoors

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[start-the-engine--a12543|Start the Engine]] (p. 50-5, 50-6). Applies to: running the engine in a building
> CAUTION: Prevent asphyxiation. Engine exhaust fumes can cause sickness or death to you or others. If you must operate engine in a building, be positive there is adequate ventilation. Either use an exhaust pipe extension to remove the exhaust fumes or open doors and windows to bring enough outside air into the area. Avoid the possibility of personal injury or death: - Be sure that everyone is clear of the machine. - Start engine only from the operator’s seat.

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (no signal word) — [[work-in-ventilated-area--277b10|Work In Ventilated Area]] (p. 05-7)
> Engine exhaust fumes can cause sickness or death. If it is necessary to run an engine in an enclosed area, remove the exhaust fumes from the area with an exhaust pipe extension. If you do not have an exhaust pipe extension, open the doors and get outside air into the area.

## Task: exhaust filter cleaning (AUTO, parked, requested)

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[exhaust-filter-system-overview--49a974|Exhaust Filter System Overview]] (p. 25-C-3, 25-C-4). Applies to: exhaust filter system
> CAUTION: The exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle. Servicing machine or attachments during exhaust filter cleaning can result in serious personal injury. Avoid exposure and skin contact with hot exhaust gases and components. During automated or manual/stationary exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine runs at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to cause burns to people, or ignite or melt common materials. Never perform exhaust filter cleaning procedures in a closed building unless suitable exhaust is provided.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[auto-exhaust-filter-cleaning--5f0614|AUTO Exhaust Filter Cleaning]] (p. 25-C-4, 25-C-5). Applies to: AUTO exhaust filter cleaning
> CAUTION: When AUTO, PARKED, or REQUESTED cleaning is enabled, the exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle. Disable exhaust filter cleaning system in conditions where it can be unsafe for elevated exhaust temperatures.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[parked-filter-cleaning--52f4ff|Parked Filter Cleaning]] (p. 25-C-5, 25-C-6). Applies to: parked filter cleaning
> CAUTION: When AUTO, PARKED, or REQUESTED cleaning is enabled, the exhaust temperature can be high under no load or light load conditions at certain times during the exhaust filter cleaning cycle. Servicing machine or attachments during exhaust filter cleaning can result in serious personal injury. Avoid exposure and skin contact with hot exhaust gases and components. During automated or manual/stationary exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine runs at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to cause burns to people, or ignite or melt common materials.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[auto-exhaust-filter-cleaning--5f0614|AUTO Exhaust Filter Cleaning]] (p. 25-C-4, 25-C-5); [[parked-filter-cleaning--52f4ff|Parked Filter Cleaning]] (p. 25-C-5, 25-C-6). Applies to: exhaust filter cleaning

> [!warning] Generic Deere-wide safety text (no signal word) — [[clean-exhaust-filter-safely--2abccf|Clean Exhaust Filter Safely]] (p. 05-9)
> During exhaust filter cleaning operations, the engine may run at elevated idle and hot temperatures for an extended period of time. Exhaust gases and exhaust filter components reach temperatures hot enough to burn people, or ignite or melt common materials. Keep machine away from people, animals, or structures which may be susceptible to harm or damage from hot exhaust gases or components. Avoid potential fire or explosion hazards from flammable materials and vapors near the exhaust. Keep exhaust outlet away from people and anything that can melt, burn, or explode. Closely monitor machine and surrounding area for smoldering debris during and after exhaust filter cleaning.

## Task: exhaust filter ash and disposal

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[exhaust-filter-ash-handling-and-disposal--8bcac6|Exhaust Filter Ash Handling and Disposal]] (p. 05-10). Applies to: exhaust filter ash
> CAUTION: Under federal, state and/or local laws or regulations, exhaust filter ash may be classified as a hazardous waste. Hazardous waste must be disposed of in accordance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws or regulations governing hazardous waste disposal. Only a qualified service provider should remove ash from the exhaust filter. Personal protective equipment and clothing, maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition, should be used when handling and cleaning exhaust filter. See a John Deere dealer or other service provider for exhaust filter handling and disposal.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[exhaust-filter-diesel-particulate-filter-dpf-ash-handling-an--bafd5d|Exhaust Filter / Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) Ash Handling and Disposal]] (p. 115-19). Applies to: diesel particulate filter ash
> CAUTION: Under federal, state, and/or local laws or regulations, Diesel Particulate Filter ash may be classified as a hazardous waste. Hazardous wastes must be disposed of in accordance with all applicable federal, state and local laws or regulations governing hazardous waste disposal. Only a qualified service provider should remove ash from the DPF. Personal protective equipment and clothing, maintained in a sanitary and reliable condition, should be used when handling and cleaning a DPF. Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider for assistance.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[exhaust-filter-disposal--85a6f8|Exhaust Filter Disposal]] (p. 115-19). Applies to: exhaust filter disposal
> CAUTION: Proper management of an Exhaust Filter that has reached the end of its useful life is required, since the ash or catalyst material in the device may be classified as hazardous waste under federal, state, and/or local laws or regulations. Used Exhaust Filters, which include the Diesel Particulate Filter, may be exchanged at regular spare part channels.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[fill-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-tank-final-tier-4-stage-v--b81914|Fill Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Tank (Final Tier 4/Stage V)]] (p. 45-4, 45-5). Applies to: filling the DEF tank — Final Tier 4/Stage V

> CAUTION: Avoid contact with eyes. In case of contact, immediately flush eyes with large amounts of water for a minimum of 15 minutes. Reference the Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[cleaning-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-tank--4e2bb4|Cleaning Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Tank]] (p. 115-18, 115-19) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: storing and refilling DEF
> CAUTION: Avoid contact with eyes. In case of contact, immediately flush eyes with large amounts of water for a minimum of 15 minutes. Reference the Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[storing-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def--18be20|Storing Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)]] (p. 110-9, 110-10) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: storing DEF
> IMPORTANT: It is unlawful to tamper with or remove any component of the aftertreatment system. Do not use DEF that does not meet the required specifications or operate the engine with no DEF. Never attempt to create DEF by mixing agricultural grade urea with water. Agricultural grade urea does not meet the necessary specifications and can damage the aftertreatment system. Do not add any chemicals or additives to DEF in an effort to prevent freezing. Any chemicals or additives added to DEF can damage the aftertreatment system. Never add water or any other fluid in place of, or in addition to DEF. Operating with a modified DEF or using an unapproved DEF can damage the aftertreatment system.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[refilling-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-tank--8f4e5a|Refilling Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Tank]] (p. 110-10) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: refilling DEF
> IMPORTANT: Use only distilled water to rinse components that are used to deliver DEF. Tap water can contaminate DEF. If distilled water is not available, rinse with clean tap water, then thoroughly rinse with ample amounts of DEF. If DEF is spilled or contacts any surface other than the storage tank, immediately clean the surface with clear water. DEF is corrosive to painted and unpainted metallic surfaces and can distort some plastic and rubber components. If DEF is filled into engine fuel tank or other fluid compartment, do not operate engine until system is properly purged of DEF. Contact your John Deere dealer or other service provider to drain and clean the system.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[testing-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def--4c0b70|Testing Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)]] (p. 110-10, 110-11) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: testing DEF
> IMPORTANT: Using DEF with the correct concentration is critical to engine and aftertreatment system performance. Extended storage and other conditions can adversely alter the DEF concentration.

## Task: stopping the engine and disconnecting the battery (DEF purge)

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]] (p. 50-27, 50-28). Applies to: parking the machine — Final Tier 4/Stage V
> IMPORTANT: Prevent engine damage. Before stopping an engine that has been operating at working load, idle the engine for at least 2 minutes to cool the hot engine parts. On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is OFF. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF), immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[operate-the-engine--9bbe7e|Operate the Engine]] (p. 50-15). Applies to: Final Tier 4/Stage V
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until Light Emitting Diode (LED) on the battery box has turned off. This indicates the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[disconnect-battery-final-tier-4-and-stage-v-engines--fc1019|Disconnect Battery (Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines)]] (p. 85-1). Applies to: Final Tier 4/Stage V
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until Light Emitting Diode (LED) on the battery box has turned off. This indicates the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug lines. In freezing weather, DEF will freeze and possibly burst lines.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[battery-disconnect-switch--c9c8e5|Battery Disconnect Switch]] (p. 85-24). Applies to: Final Tier 4/Stage V
> IMPORTANT: Do not disconnect battery on machines equipped with Final Tier 4/Stage V engines until light emitting diode (LED) (B) has turned off. This indicates that the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system purge is complete.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]] (p. 165-5, 165-6, 165-7). Applies to: preparing the machine for storage — Final Tier 4/Stage V
> IMPORTANT: On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is off. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[machine-informational-decals--8583ad|Machine Informational Decals]] (p. 50-2, 50-3, 50-4). Applies to: battery disconnect switch (decal)
> IMPORTANT: After light turns off, turn switch counterclockwise to disconnect battery.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-overview--8b2d6a|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System Overview]] (p. 50-12, 50-13) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: SCR system

If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, residual DEF can freeze and possibly damage components of the SCR system during cold-weather exposure.

## Task: emissions system — tampering, derates and warnings

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-final-tier-4-and-st--c3c982|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System (Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines)]] (p. 50-13, 50-14). Applies to: Final Tier 4 and Stage V engines
> IMPORTANT: It is unlawful to tamper with or remove any component of the aftertreatment system. It is also unlawful to use diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) that does not meet the specifications provided or to operate the vehicle with no DEF. Using incorrect or unapproved aftertreatment components can damage the vehicle’s aftertreatment system and reduce ability of the aftertreatment system to function correctly. Never interchange aftertreatment components between Interim Tier 4/Stage III B and Final Tier 4/Stage V equipped vehicles.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[aftertreatment-indicators-overview--67561e|Aftertreatment Indicators Overview]] (p. 50-9, 50-10). Applies to: aftertreatment indicators
> IMPORTANT: The operator is informed by the operator warning system when the emission control system does not function correctly and/ or an engine malfunction is detected by the engine control unit. Ignoring the operator warning signals will lead to an emission-related derate, resulting in an effective disablement of non-road mobile machinery operation.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[required-machine-stop-warning--c866aa|Required Machine Stop Warning]] (p. 50-10, 50-11) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: machine stop warning
> IMPORTANT: In some situations, machine engine power may be reduced as described. On notification, immediately place the machine in a safe state and or move it to a safe location. A mandated machine stop can only be removed by a service technician.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[us-epa-qualified-emergency-use-scr-derate-override-option--860795|US EPA Qualified Emergency Use — SCR Derate Override Option]] (p. 50-14, 50-15) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: US EPA emergency SCR derate override (US EPA only)
> IMPORTANT: Operating the engine without emissions related derates could damage the aftertreatment system.

> [!warning] CAUTION — printed on [[emissions-control-system-certification-label--593b4e|Emissions Control System Certification Label]] (p. 170-46, 170-47) (generic Deere-wide module). Applies to: emissions control system
> CAUTION: Statutes providing severe penalties for tampering with emissions controls may apply to the user or dealer.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — printed on [[do-not-modify-fuel-system--9015d3|Do Not Modify Fuel System]] (p. 95-6). Applies to: emissions certified engines
> IMPORTANT: Increasing horsepower, or altering any aspect of fuel and air delivery on emissions certified engines beyond factory rating, will cause emission levels beyond what is approved by United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). Violations of EPA regulations may result in substantial fines to persons or companies committing such violations. John Deere dealers are in violation of their dealer agreement if found to be altering power levels of John Deere equipment. Machine warranty is void if power level is changed from factory specifications. Do not attempt to service injection pump or fuel injectors yourself. Special training and special tools are required.
