# Brake

The machine has three braking systems: primary braking through the hydro system using the
multi-function lever, service braking through the front caliper brakes using the brake pedal, and a
modulated secondary brake that engages the park brake system
[[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]. The park brakes are internal hub brake discs
[[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Service brake system operates at high pressure. To avoid the risk of injury to yourself or bystanders, DO NOT attempt to bleed the brake system at the caliper.
> [[bleed-service-brakes--28b467|Bleed Service Brakes]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Service brake system operates at high pressure. To avoid the risk of injury to yourself or bystanders, DO NOT crack lines in this system if a brake bleeding is necessary (See Bleed Service Brakes in Chassis section of this manual). The hydraulic brake components in this system should be serviced by a John Deere dealer or other service provider.
> [[inspect-service-brake-components-annually--9b53ea|Inspect Service Brake Components— Annually]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: To avoid serious injury or death to you or others, always use the seat belt properly.
> [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: If primary braking is accomplished using the brake pedal instead of the multi-function lever, premature brake wear and damage can occur. Always pull the multi-function lever back before using the brake pedal.
> [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: The secondary brake gradually engages the park brake system. Engaging secondary brake system while machine is moving causes significant wear to the park brake components. Park brakes will need to be tested to verify proper operation after using the secondary brake. (See Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes in this section.)
> [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Pressing the park brake switch applies the park brakes at vehicle speeds below 0.8 km/ h (0.5 mph).
> [[park-brake--e0c23d|Park Brake]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: To tow or pull the sprayer, engine MUST be running and hydraulic system MUST have correct hydraulic pressure or damage WILL occur to brakes and wheel hubs.
> [[tow-the-sprayer--4c2976|Tow The Sprayer]]

## What it is

**Primary (hydro) braking.** To slow the machine, pull back on the multi-function lever for primary
braking [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Braking occurs from the hydro system. The hydro system also provides anti-lock braking to limit wheel locking during deceleration on slippery surfaces. Anti-lock braking is active at all times, unless the system detects a fault.
> [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]

**Service braking.** Press the brake pedal to activate machine service brakes and slow the machine
[[operate-brake-pedal--5cfda7|Operate Brake Pedal]]. For more aggressive (faster) braking, press
down on the brake pedal after the multi-function lever has been pulled back, and be prepared for a
rapid decrease in speed — service braking stops the machine quickly
[[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]. Braking then occurs from the front caliper
brakes and the hydro system [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]. The service brakes
are a full power hydraulic brake system: hydraulic pressure and flow is applied until the desired
pressure setting is reached or the brake pedal is released
[[bleed-service-brakes--28b467|Bleed Service Brakes]].

**Secondary braking.** In the event that the primary (hydro) and service (brake pedal) systems are
not operating correctly, turn the modulated secondary brake switch
[[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]].

**Park brake.** If the park brake switch is engaged, it must be manually disengaged before moving
machine; park brakes will not automatically release when park brake switch has been engaged
[[park-brake--e0c23d|Park Brake]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Park brake switch does not need to be engaged when parking machine. Park brakes are automatically applied when multi-function lever is in park position.
> Park brakes will automatically be disengaged when multi-function lever is moved from the park position if the park brake switch has not been engaged.
> [[park-brake--e0c23d|Park Brake]]

## Related procedures

- **Slow the machine.** Pull back on the multi-function lever for primary braking; press the brake
  pedal after the lever has been pulled back for more aggressive braking; use the modulated
  secondary brake switch only if the primary and service systems are not operating correctly
  [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]].
- **Verify park brake operation after each secondary brake use.** Stop the fully loaded machine on a
  20 degree slope or steepest grade available that the machine will be parked on with rear of
  machine up hill, and press the park brake switch
  [[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]]. If the
  machine rolls or "creeps", contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider for
  servicing/replacement of brake discs; if the machine remains parked, internal hub brake discs are
  acceptable and do not need servicing
  [[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]].
- **Inspect the service brakes visually.** Visually inspect brake pads and rotors on both front
  wheels for deep grooves, signs of overheating, or premature wear; if any of these conditions
  exists, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider
  [[visually-inspect-service-brake-components-100-hours--e5b45f|Visually Inspect Service Brake Components—100 Hours]].
- **Inspect the service brake components (annual).** Remove the spring pins and caliper pins, remove
  the brake pads from between rotor and caliper, inspect brake pad material for deep grooves, uneven
  wear, and minimum thickness, inspect the rotor face for deep grooves and uneven wear, inspect the
  rotor for minimum thickness, then reassemble brakes in reverse order of removal
  [[inspect-service-brake-components-annually--9b53ea|Inspect Service Brake Components— Annually]].
  The specifications given are Brake Pad Material—Minimum Thickness On Each Pad 1.5 mm (0.059 in)
  and Rotor—Thickness 11 mm (0.440 in)
  [[inspect-service-brake-components-annually--9b53ea|Inspect Service Brake Components— Annually]].
- **Bleed the service brakes.** Extend axles to widest position, fully depress the brake pedal 15-20
  times (after releasing the pedal each time wait 2 seconds before fully depressing pedal again),
  then slowly drive machine forward and check service brake operation
  [[bleed-service-brakes--28b467|Bleed Service Brakes]].
- **Remove and install a service brake caliper.** Park machine on a flat, level surface, remove the
  cap screws and fender, disconnect the hydraulic line, then remove the cap screws and brake caliper
  [[remove-and-install-service-brake-caliper--2d86d7|Remove and Install Service Brake Caliper]].
  Install in reverse order, tightening Brake Caliper Mounting Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated) to
  271 N·m (200 lb·ft) and Fender Mount-to-Drive Mount Cap Screws—Torque (lubricated) to
  268 N·m (198 lb·ft), then perform the service brake bleeding procedure
  [[remove-and-install-service-brake-caliper--2d86d7|Remove and Install Service Brake Caliper]].
- **Towing.** The machine should only be towed if stuck, WITH the engine running, correct hydraulic
  pressure, and the secondary brake released; only pull the machine a short distance and at slow
  speed of 0—5 km/h (0—3 mph) [[tow-the-sprayer--4c2976|Tow The Sprayer]]. If engine is not running
  or proper hydraulic system pressure cannot be maintained, contact a John Deere dealer or other
  service provider and consult the sprayer Technical Manual for proper brake and wheel hub
  disengagement procedure [[tow-the-sprayer--4c2976|Tow The Sprayer]].

## Related settings

The brakes have no operator settings in these topics. The only operator choice is which brake to use
and in what order, which is set out in [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Secondary brake switch does not need to be engaged when parking the machine. Park brakes are automatically applied when the multi-function lever is in the PARK position.
> [[reduce-travel-speed--b6b710|Reduce Travel Speed]]

## Related troubleshooting

- **Machine rolls or creeps with the park brake applied.** Contact a John Deere dealer or other
  service provider for servicing/replacement of brake discs
  [[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]].
- **Air in the service brake system.** If the system has any air in it, the air will rise to the
  valve, where it is removed by cycling the brake pedal and pausing
  [[bleed-service-brakes--28b467|Bleed Service Brakes]]. Do not bleed at the caliper
  [[bleed-service-brakes--28b467|Bleed Service Brakes]].
- **Worn pads or scored rotors.** Deep grooves, signs of overheating, or premature wear on the pads
  and rotors are a dealer matter
  [[visually-inspect-service-brake-components-100-hours--e5b45f|Visually Inspect Service Brake Components—100 Hours]];
  the minimum thicknesses to measure against are in the annual inspection
  [[inspect-service-brake-components-annually--9b53ea|Inspect Service Brake Components— Annually]].
- **After using the secondary brake.** Park brakes must be verified for proper operation after each
  secondary brake use
  [[verify-proper-operation-of-park-brakes--461c62|Verify Proper Operation Of Park Brakes]].
