# Boom

The spray boom carries the nozzles across the working width, folds against the machine for
transport, and is controlled from the cab. Everything on this page applies to all models unless a
line says otherwise.

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Spray booms move quickly. Keep everyone out of the folding and unfolding area of the spray booms. Failure to stay out of the folding and unfolding area can result in injury or death.
> [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Serious injury or death to you or others can occur from standing in front of boom when it is returning to its original position. Full boom breakaway system is under hydraulic pressure. If the boom must be lifted or aided over an object or an obstruction, stand behind the boom to avoid injury.
> [[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Folding spray booms while in maintenance mode is not the same as folding in normal operation. Maintain awareness of surroundings and keep bystanders out of folding and unfolding area.
> [[boom-maintenance-mode--f3caf2|Boom Maintenance Mode]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Avoid damage; only unfold the booms where there is enough space.
> [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Booms can contact and may damage the exhaust stack and/or walkway handrail when using maintenance mode to fold and unfold booms. Do not allow booms to go higher than the top of the cradle support when operating boom in maintenance mode.
> [[boom-maintenance-mode--f3caf2|Boom Maintenance Mode]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: This procedure enables fully folding/ unfolding the boom and raising tilts to highest positions. Ensure that adequate space is available.
> [[boom-maintenance-mode--f3caf2|Boom Maintenance Mode]]

## What it is

The illustration and legend below identify terminology used in various places on the carbon fiber
boom, and this terminology is used in the boom procedures in that section
[[carbon-fiber-boom-terminology--898dcd|Carbon Fiber Boom Terminology]]. The parts named there are
the strut, T1 link, T3 link, inner boom section, compression post, T2 link, fold cylinder, outer
boom section, T6/T7 rope and breakaway boom
[[carbon-fiber-boom-terminology--898dcd|Carbon Fiber Boom Terminology]].

The full boom breakaway system provides boom damage protection to the inner booms when the outer
boom section is folded in or the inner section strikes an object
[[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]]. The system allows the boom to breakaway 28°
degrees (A) [[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]]. Once the operator backs away or
clears the struck object, the boom returns to its original position
[[full-boom-breakaway--f070b7|Full Boom Breakaway]].

## Related procedures

There are three ways to fold or unfold the boom
[[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]:

- **Auto Boom Fold/Unfold** — allows you to fold/unfold both booms simultaneously
  [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]. The procedure uses the Boom
  Fold/Unfold Foot Switch together with the Center Boom Raise/Lower Switch
  [[auto-boom-fold-unfold--db954d|Auto Boom Fold/Unfold]].
- **Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold** — allows you to fold/ unfold both booms simultaneously but only
  folding/ unfolding one section of each boom at a time
  [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]. You must release the
  button(s) after a section is folded/unfolded and press the button(s) again to continue the
  procedure [[sequential-boom-fold-unfold--260a45|Sequential Boom Fold/Unfold]].
- **One Side Fold/Unfold** — allows you to fold/unfold one section of one boom at a time
  [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]]. The booms are designed so
  that only one boom can be unfolded/folded if desired
  [[one-side-fold-unfold--0afab8|One Side Fold/Unfold]].

Other boom tasks:

- Boom maintenance mode manually controls the individual movement of boom sections and the center
  frame, and moves the boom at a slower speed than normal folding operation
  [[boom-maintenance-mode--f3caf2|Boom Maintenance Mode]]. Use it when repair or service is
  necessary, when folding outside the normal sequence, when folding in tight areas, or when
  diagnosing boom folding problems [[boom-maintenance-mode--f3caf2|Boom Maintenance Mode]].
- Boom return to height is set up and operated from the multi-function lever switches
  [[operate-boom-return-to-height--e3eb93|Operate Boom Return to Height]]; see [[boom-height]] for
  the height settings themselves.
- 27.4 and 30.5 m (90 and 100 ft) boom can be folded to 18.3 m (60 ft) if desired
  [[spray-with-boom-at-18-3-m-60-ft--29f417|Spray with Boom at 18.3 m (60 ft)]]. After folding,
  disable the outer and breakaway sections using the display; spray rate control automatically
  adjusts for spraying at 18.3 m (60 ft)
  [[spray-with-boom-at-18-3-m-60-ft--29f417|Spray with Boom at 18.3 m (60 ft)]].

## Related settings

- To unfold or fold the booms, the machine must be traveling less than 8 km/h (5 mph) and the
  Transport Lock must be disengaged
  [[boom-fold-unfold-procedures--6a73ff|Boom Fold/Unfold Procedures]].
- The Transport Lock switch prevents hydraulic boom movements (boom folding/unfolding, center frame
  raise/lower, tilt raise/lower), among other functions
  [[transport-lock-switch--1c890e|Transport Lock Switch]]. Press the Transport Lock switch when
  transporting the machine on roadway; the indicator illuminates when selected
  [[transport-lock-switch--1c890e|Transport Lock Switch]].
- Boom height, BoomTrac and return-to-height targets: see [[boom-height]].
- The actual width of the area sprayed by each length boom, at each nozzle spacing, is tabulated in
  [[boom-spray-width--718f68|Boom Spray Width]] — read the values from that table rather than
  assuming the frame length.
