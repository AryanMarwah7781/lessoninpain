## What it is

The standard operator's cab is air conditioned with an air suspension seat, has an AM/FM compact
disc radio with weather band, and uses John Deere Activated Carbon Cab Air Filters
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]]. The air conditioner compressor is made by
Denso, the refrigerant is 1234yf, and the freon charge is 1.8 kg (4 lbs)
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

Cab air filters reduce amounts of dust and contaminants in the cab; make sure that John Deere
activated carbon filters, or appropriate substitutes, are installed at all times
[[cab-air-filters--63553f|Cab Air Filters]].

**The cab is not respiratory protection.** The manual is explicit that you must not rely on the cab,
the cab pressure indicator or the cab air filters to protect against chemical exposure
[[cab-air-filters--63553f|Cab Air Filters]] — see the warnings below.

### Items in the cab

On the left-hand side: auxiliary outlet (12 V), 120V outlet (if equipped), phone tray, refrigerator
(if equipped), storage area below the instructional seat, book storage, storage tray, and the cab
recirculation air filter [[left-hand-cab-items--80aecc|Left-Hand Cab Items]]. The refrigerator
operates only when the key switch is in the Run position and must remain plugged into the auxiliary
outlet to keep food or beverages cold [[left-hand-cab-items--80aecc|Left-Hand Cab Items]].

On the right-hand side: auxiliary outlet (12 V), USB outlet, and an accessory outlet (12 V) used
when connecting auxiliary equipment [[right-hand-cab-items--4c57ff|Right-Hand Cab Items]].

## Related procedures

### Check and replace cab air filters

Cab air filters MUST be replaced every 500 hours or every year, whichever comes first; check filters
regularly and replace them sooner if they become restricted or if the manufacturer recommends
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filters-500-hours-or-annually--98317f|Check and Replace Cab Air Filters—500 Hours or Annually]].
The same interval is given in the as-required entry
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filters-as-required--2b85f7|Check and Replace Cab Air Filters—As Required]].
When filters are replaced, record the date and engine hours on the decal (A)
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filters-500-hours-or-annually--98317f|Check and Replace Cab Air Filters—500 Hours or Annually]].

The replacement procedure
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]:

1. Remove filter cover (A) from rear left-hand side of cab
   [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
2. Remove frame (B) and filter element (C)
   [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
3. Inspect filter for holes, seal damage, and other damage. Replace filter if any damage is
   detected. In dusty conditions, this service can be required more often
   [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].
4. Install filter, frame, and filter cover
   [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].

For Cat 4 filters: remove cover (A), install the replacement Cat 4 filter, replace the cover, and
record the date and engine hours on the chart
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]]. The Cat 4 filter has
a one year replacement interval
[[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].

Use a John Deere activated carbon air filter or appropriate substitute; see a John Deere dealer or
other service provider [[check-and-replace-cab-air-filter--28d137|Check and Replace Cab Air Filter]].

### Clean the cab after working with chemicals

When exposed to hazardous chemicals, clean the exterior and interior of the vehicle daily to keep it
free of the accumulation of visible dirt and contamination
[[clean-vehicle-of-hazardous-chemicals-including-pesticides--37d899|Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides]].
Sweep or vacuum the floor of the cab, clean headliners and inside cowlings of the cab, wash the
entire exterior of the vehicle, and dispose of any wash water with hazardous concentrations of
active or non-active ingredients according to published regulations or directives
[[clean-vehicle-of-hazardous-chemicals-including-pesticides--37d899|Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides]].

### Working in and out of the cab during application

Always close the windows and doors during spraying to generate an overpressure inside the cab,
preventing polluted air from entering the cab
[[avoid-contact-with-agricultural-chemicals-cab-category-1-2-3--1450fb|Avoid Contact with Agricultural Chemicals - Cab Category 1, 2, 3, and 4 According to EN 15695-1]].
Wear personal protective equipment as required by the pesticide use instructions when leaving the
enclosed cab into a treated area, to work with contaminated application equipment such as nozzles,
or to become involved with mixing and loading activities
[[avoid-contact-with-agricultural-chemicals-cab-category-1-2-3--1450fb|Avoid Contact with Agricultural Chemicals - Cab Category 1, 2, 3, and 4 According to EN 15695-1]].
Before reentering the cab, remove protective equipment and store it outside the cab; clean shoes or
boots before entering; and never store chemical containers inside the cab
[[avoid-contact-with-agricultural-chemicals-cab-category-1-2-3--1450fb|Avoid Contact with Agricultural Chemicals - Cab Category 1, 2, 3, and 4 According to EN 15695-1]].

cab categories.

## Related troubleshooting

From the Operator's Cab troubleshooting table
[[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]], cheapest checks first:

- **Cab pressure indicator reading at the low end** — cab blower switch turned off: turn switch on.
  Door or window not latched: latch door or window. Restricted air filter: replace with a John Deere
  activated carbon cab air filter or appropriate substitute. Damaged door or window seals: replace
  damaged seals [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
- **Cab pressure indicator reading at high end** — damaged seal or pleated filter media on the air
  filter, missing or incorrect air filter, or air filter not seated properly: reseat or replace with
  a John Deere activated carbon cab air filter or appropriate substitute
  [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
- **Inadequate air flow** — clogged air filters: replace filters. Clogged air inlet screen: clean
  screen. Evaporator core air flow restricted: clean evaporator and housing with compressed air
  [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
- **Strange odors inside the operator's cab** — dirty air filters: replace filters. Drain tubes
  plugged: clean drain tubes. Evaporator condenser pan dirty: clean pan and outlet with compressed
  air [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
- **Failure to cool** — insufficient blower speed: increase blower speed. Dirty air filters: replace
  filters. Debris on the radiator screen: clean screen. Outside temperature too low
  (Below 70°F) (21°C): wait until day gets warmer
  [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
- **Water leaking or dripping from evaporator core compartment** — drain tubes plugged (AC): clean
  drain tubes. Loose hose clamp: tighten clamp [[operator-s-cab--023d76|Operator's Cab]].
