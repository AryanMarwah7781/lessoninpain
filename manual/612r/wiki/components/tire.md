# Tire

Tire values are **model-specific**. The 408R, the 400R/410R/412R group and the 600R/612R/616R group
each have their own replacement-tire chart and their own inflation-pressure/load tables
[[replacement-tire-specifications--26ff59|Replacement Tire Specifications]]
[[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]]. Never carry a value across from one group to
another.

## What it is

Tire size is read like this — for the example 650/65R38 166D R-1W
[[replacement-tire-specifications--26ff59|Replacement Tire Specifications]]:

- "650" — Tire Section Width in Millimeters
- "65" — Aspect Ratio of Tire - Equal to Tire Section Height/Tire Section Width
- "R" — Radial Construction
- "38" — Nominal Rim Diameter in inches
- "166" — Load Index Rating - Carrying Capacity Code
- "D" — Speed Symbol (determines maximum speed allowed at rated load)
- "R-1 or R-1W" — Regular Tread or Wet Traction Tread

Replacement tires must be of the same size and must have an equivalent load index rating as shown in
the chart [[replacement-tire-specifications--26ff59|Replacement Tire Specifications]].

### Inflation pressure and loads

The full inflation-pressure/load tables (by model, tire size, brand, section width, overall
diameter, speed and tire load) are printed twice in the manual: in the Wheels and Tires section
[[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]] and again in the Specifications section
[[tire-specifications--3bdf9b|Tire Specifications]]. Read the row for **your model, your tire size
and your actual tire load**; do not interpolate.

If tire loads are unknown, inflate tires to the maximum allowable pressure for the respective tire,
as published in the table [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]].

Some rows carry their own speed conditions — for example, one footnote states that load allowance
can be increased by a factor of 1.07 when operating at maximum spray speed of 32 km/h (20 mph), and
another that a tire is B rated 48 km/h (30 mph) but the supplier has approved the above loads for
operation up to 56 km/h (35 mph) [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]]. "Cyclic" is
typically used for normal field operation, referencing the weight reducing as material is applied
[[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]].

The Prestarting Checks entry for inspecting tires simply points at the Wheels and Tires section for
proper inflation pressures, loads, and specifications
[[inspect-tires-daily--8cc0ce|Inspect Tires—Daily]].

## Related procedures

### Inspect tires — daily

Check wheels for missing bolts and nuts, reduced tire pressure, cuts, bubbles, or rim damage
[[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]]. During storage, protect tires from exposure to
petroleum products, chemicals, and direct sunlight; try to avoid rocks and sharp objects and drive
carefully; traveling at high speeds while carrying heavy loads will increase tire wear and reduce
tire life [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]].

### Inspect tires for uneven wear — 100 hours

Inspect tires for uneven wear every 100 hours; if uneven wear is found, rotate the tires and check
for component wear that may be causing the wear
[[inspect-tires-for-uneven-wear-100-hours--6bad5a|Inspect Tires For Uneven Wear—100 Hours]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Rephasing steering system is required before road transport. (See rephasing instructions in
> Check Front Axle Toe-In—Annually in Wheels and Tires section.)
> [[inspect-tires-for-uneven-wear-100-hours--6bad5a|Inspect Tires For Uneven Wear—100 Hours]]

See [[steering]] for the rephasing steps.

### Rotate tires — as required

Contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider for specific recommendations for tire rotation
[[rotate-tires-as-required--616d52|Rotate Tires—As Required]]. The manual gives no rotation pattern
and no rotation interval.

### Install optional tires

The procedure differs by model group. Both groups use JDG1781 Spindle Spacers and a jack bracket
kit obtained via regular spare part channels, raise the machine with a bottle jack at the marked
location, and support it on suitable stands
[[install-optional-tires-600r-612r-and-616r--7f90cc|Install Optional Tires—600R, 612R, and 616R]]
[[install-optional-tires-400r-408r-410r-and-412r--8e2d78|Install Optional Tires—400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R]].

Torques, both model groups
[[install-optional-tires-600r-612r-and-616r--7f90cc|Install Optional Tires—600R, 612R, and 616R]]
[[install-optional-tires-400r-408r-410r-and-412r--8e2d78|Install Optional Tires—400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R]]:

- Wheel mounting nuts — torque (dry): 531 N·m (392 lb·ft)
- Fender bracket-to-drive housing cap screws — torque (lubricated): 268 N·m (198 lb·ft)

Maximum wheel and tire assembly weights differ by model group:

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — maximum assembly weight, 600R/612R/616R
> IMPORTANT: Maximum weight of a row crop wheel and tire assembly is 495 kg (1091 lb). Maximum
> weight of a float wheel and tire assembly is 715 kg (1577 lb).
> [[install-optional-tires-600r-612r-and-616r--7f90cc|Install Optional Tires—600R, 612R, and 616R]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — maximum assembly weight, 400R/408R/410R/412R
> IMPORTANT: Maximum weight of a row crop wheel and tire assembly is 380 kg (838 lb). Maximum weight
> of a float wheel and tire assembly is 515 kg (1136 lb).
> [[install-optional-tires-400r-408r-410r-and-412r--8e2d78|Install Optional Tires—400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R]]

If installing a new type of tire, select the correct tire type in the AutoTrac Steering Optimization
settings located in the AutoTrac Guidance application
[[install-optional-tires-600r-612r-and-616r--7f90cc|Install Optional Tires—600R, 612R, and 616R]],
and see [[autotrac-tuning-with-tire-changes--f75fa8|AutoTrac Tuning with Tire Changes]] for the
Float/Row Crop selection and the extra settings required for the largest tire size width of 800 mm.

### Disposal

It is illegal to burn discarded tires; discarded tires can be disposed of in a permitted landfill
after being processed (shredded or quartered), and may be recycled if a recycling plant is nearby
[[discarded-tires--7cdd24|Discarded Tires]].

## Related troubleshooting

- **Suspension system gives a rough ride** — tire inflation incorrectly set: check tire inflation.
  Pressure in air springs not correctly set: correctly set pressure in air springs
  [[suspension-system--105ebe|Suspension System]].
- **Uneven wear** — rotate the tires and check for component wear that may be causing the wear
  [[inspect-tires-for-uneven-wear-100-hours--6bad5a|Inspect Tires For Uneven Wear—100 Hours]].

## What the manual does not specify

- No tire rotation pattern or interval is given; it refers you to the dealer
  [[rotate-tires-as-required--616d52|Rotate Tires—As Required]].
- No minimum tread depth or wear limit is given for taking a tire out of service
  [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]].
- Inflation pressure is not a single number: it is read from the table for your model, tire size and
  actual tire load, and the manual tells you to weigh the machine to understand exact tire loads and
  inflation pressure [[inspect-tires-daily--f00933|Inspect Tires—Daily]].
