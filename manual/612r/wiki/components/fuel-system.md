# Fuel system

The fuel system runs from the fuel tank through the water separator and fuel filters to a
High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) injection system. The high-pressure side is not an operator
repair.

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Shut off the engine before filling the fuel tank. Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank.
> [[fill-fuel-tank--966e81|Fill Fuel Tank]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Escaping diesel fuel under pressure can have sufficient force to penetrate the skin, causing serious injury. Before disconnecting lines, be sure to relieve pressure. Before applying pressure to the system, be sure ALL connections are tight and lines, pipes and hoses are not damaged. Keep hands and body away from pinholes and nozzles which eject fluid under pressure. Use a piece of cardboard or wood, rather than hands, to search for suspected leaks. If ANY fluid is injected into the skin, it must be surgically removed within a few hours by a doctor familiar with this type injury or gangrene may result. Doctors unfamiliar with this type of injury may call the Deere & Company Medical Department in Moline, Illinois, or other knowledgeable medical source.
> [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury. Only technicians familiar with this type of system should perform repairs. Before disconnecting fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system, wait a minimum of 15 minutes after engine is stopped.
> [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Always shut off engine, remove key, and set park brake before performing maintenance work on the fuel filter.
> [[drain-water-from-fuel-filter-6-8-l-engine-only--6dc9d8|Drain Water from Fuel Filter—6.8 L Engine Only]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Fuel in filters can be under pressure. Open valve (A) on bottom of the water separator bowl to relieve pressure before removing the filter.
> [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to pressurize the fuel tank with compressed air or any other means. Fuel tank and surrounding structural damage could result.
> [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: When draining water from fuel filters, the drain valve must be unthreaded completely and the valve must drop down approximately 12 mm (0.5 in) to properly drain water from the filter.
> [[drain-water-separator-daily--f9a048|Drain Water Separator—Daily]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]]
> IMPORTANT: Always replace both filters at the same time.

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]]
> IMPORTANT: DO NOT pre-fill either fuel filter with fuel.

Handle fuel with care: it is highly flammable [[handle-fuel-safely-avoid-fires--1e08df|Handle Fuel Safely—Avoid Fires]].
Do not refuel the machine while smoking or when near open flame or sparks, and always stop engine
before refueling machine [[handle-fuel-safely-avoid-fires--1e08df|Handle Fuel Safely—Avoid Fires]].

High-pressure fluid remaining in fuel lines can cause serious injury; only technicians familiar
with this type of system should perform repairs
[[do-not-open-high-pressure-fuel-system--78594e|Do Not Open High-Pressure Fuel System]]. Do not
disconnect or attempt repair of fuel lines, sensors, or any other components between the
high-pressure fuel pump and nozzles on engines with High Pressure Common Rail (HPCR) fuel system
[[do-not-open-high-pressure-fuel-system--78594e|Do Not Open High-Pressure Fuel System]].

## What it is

Fuel tank capacity, as printed in the fill procedure: 400R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R, and 616R fuel
tank capacity is 566 L (149 gal); 408R fuel tank capacity is 485 L (128 gal)
[[fill-fuel-tank--966e81|Fill Fuel Tank]].

The Fuel Level Indicator indicates the amount of fuel remaining in the fuel tank, and when the
level reaches 10% of remaining fuel the fuel indicator flashes, an alarm sounds, and a low fuel
message appears [[fuel-level-indicator--2ba352|Fuel Level Indicator]].

Downstream of the tank sit a water separator and fuel filters. The fuel/water separator is part of
the fuel filter assembly [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]].
The manual illustrates the water separator separately for the 6.8 L Engine and the 9.0 L Engine
[[drain-water-separator-daily--f9a048|Drain Water Separator—Daily]].

John Deere brand fuel filters have been designed and produced specifically for John Deere engines,
and to protect the engine from debris and water you should always change engine fuel filters as
specified in this manual [[fuel-filters--121ee5|Fuel Filters]].

## Related procedures

- **Fill the fuel tank.** Lower the fuel fill ladder (A), remove the fuel tank fill cap (B), and
  fill the tank with the proper grade of diesel fuel to the correct specification
  [[fill-fuel-tank--966e81|Fill Fuel Tank]].
- **Relieve fuel system pressure.** The engine must be allowed to sit for at least 15 minutes
  before beginning work on the fuel system; this allows the fuel system to bleed off internal high
  pressure [[relieve-fuel-system-pressure--3d6e3c|Relieve Fuel System Pressure]].
- **Drain water from the fuel filter.** Drain the filter by opening the drain valve (B) on the
  primary fuel filter (A), then bleed the fuel system
  [[drain-water-from-fuel-filter-6-8-l-engine-only--6dc9d8|Drain Water from Fuel Filter—6.8 L Engine Only]].
- **Bleed the fuel system.** Turn the key switch to the Run position to start the electric fuel
  pump and bleed air from the fuel system, allow the pump to run for 60 seconds before starting the
  machine, then start the engine and run at 1200 rpm for 2 minutes
  [[bleed-fuel-system--8e8b25|Bleed Fuel System]].
- **Replace the fuel filters.** Clean the exterior of the filter elements and mounting areas, drain
  water and contaminates into a suitable container, rotate the primary and secondary filters
  counterclockwise to remove them, lube the filter seals with diesel fuel and install, then bleed
  the fuel system [[replace-fuel-filters-500-hours-6-8-l-engine--2b94e9|Replace Fuel Filters—500 Hours (6.8 L Engine)]].
- **Clean the fuel tank vent filter.** Remove the cap screws, lift the cover, remove and retain the
  P-clamp, remove the fuel tank vent filter and clean it in soapy detergent solution, blow dry with
  compressed air and install [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]].
  Use a commercial ladder to access the fuel tank vent filter
  [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]].

> [!conflict] Which filters to drain at the daily water separator check
> One Drain Water Separator—Daily topic says to drain water and contaminates from the water
> separator bowl by opening the drain valve (A) on the primary filter (C)
> [[drain-water-separator-daily--f9a048|Drain Water Separator—Daily (Prestarting Checks)]], while
> the Lubrication and Maintenance copy of the same daily check says to open the drain valve (A) on
> the primary (C) and secondary (B) filters
> [[drain-water-separator-daily--c8c494|Drain Water Separator—Daily (Lubrication and Maintenance)]].
> Both are machine-specific topics; neither is a generic Deere-wide module. This page does not
> choose between them — ask your dealer which applies to your engine and filter assembly.

Do not attempt to create one. Increasing horsepower, or altering
any aspect of fuel and air delivery on emissions certified engines beyond factory rating, will cause
emission levels beyond what is approved by United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)
[[do-not-modify-fuel-system--9015d3|Do Not Modify Fuel System]]. Machine warranty is void if power
level is changed from factory specifications

## Related troubleshooting

- Do not attempt to service the injection pump or fuel injectors yourself — special training and
  special tools are required; see a John Deere dealer
  [[do-not-modify-fuel-system--9015d3|Do Not Modify Fuel System]].
- Never steam clean or spray water on a warm injection pump, as this could cause damage to pump
  parts [[do-not-modify-fuel-system--9015d3|Do Not Modify Fuel System]].
- Replace fuel filters if an excessive amount of water or sediment is present
  [[drain-water-separator-daily--f9a048|Drain Water Separator—Daily]].
- Static charges can build up in Ultra-Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD) fuel while it is flowing through
  fuel delivery systems, and static electricity discharge when combustible vapors are present could
  result in a fire or explosion
  [[avoid-static-electricity-risk-when-refueling--d41f03|Avoid Static Electricity Risk When Refueling]].
  Ensure that the entire system used to refuel your machine is properly grounded and bonded
  [[avoid-static-electricity-risk-when-refueling--d41f03|Avoid Static Electricity Risk When Refueling]].
