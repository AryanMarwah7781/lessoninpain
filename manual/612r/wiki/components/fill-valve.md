# Fill Valve

> The product label and local regulations govern chemical application. The manual tells you to
> carefully read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling
> chemicals, and to follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution
> [[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].

## What it is

"Fill valve" on this machine covers several distinct valves. On a **single solution** machine
[[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]]:

- **Side Quik-Fill valve** — completes the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer
  [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Front Quik-Fill valve** — completes the connection between the nurse tank and the front fill
  isolation valve [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Front fill isolation valve** — completes the connection between the front Quik-Fill valve and
  the solution tank, and is electrically controlled by the solution system control unit
  [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Rinse tank fill valve** — completes the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer
  [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Rinse tank Quik-Fill valve** — the rinse tank can be loaded with clean water through it, for use
  when rinsing the solution system [[solution-system--544787|Solution System]].
- **Solution tank sump valve** — controls different loading methods when using either the solution
  pump or a nurse tank pump, and is also used to close off the solution tank from the rest of the
  wet system in case repairs are required; it is controlled electronically by the automated wet
  system [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Rinse tank sump valve** — allows flow from the rinse tank and is controlled electronically by
  the automated wet system [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].
- **Strainer/flowmeter shutoff valve** — allows the operator to close the pressure line when
  cleaning the pressure strainers or flowmeters; closing the valve limits the amount of solution
  that drains and reduces the amount of air introduced to the system
  [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].

On a **dual product** machine the load stations each provide two Quik-Fill valves. At the side load
station the main Quik-Fill valve can be used to fill tank 1, tank 2, or the rinse tank, and the
dedicated Quik-Fill valve can only be used to fill the rinse tank
[[wet-system-valves--b2adcf|Wet System Valves]]. At the front load station the main Quik-Fill valve
can be used to fill tank 1, tank 2, or the rinse tank, and the dedicated Quik-Fill valve can only be
used to fill tank 1 [[wet-system-valves--b2adcf|Wet System Valves]].

See [[solution-tank]], [[solution-pump]] and [[agitation]] for the rest of the wet system.

## Where it is and how to work the handle

### Side Quik-Fill valve (single solution)

![[NX1435340.png]]
**Figure NX1435340** — A: Valve Handle [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]]

Once the nurse tank hose has been connected to the machine, rotate valve handle (A) until parallel
with plumbing to open the connection between the nurse tank and the sprayer; after the filling
procedure is completed, rotate the handle back to the closed position as shown before disconnecting
the nurse tank hose from the sprayer [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].

## Related procedures

### Fill the rinse tank using the rinse tank fill valve

![[N99946.png]]
**Figure N99946** — A: Filler Cap; B: Valve
[[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]]

1. Remove fill cap (A) and connect fill hose
   [[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]].
2. Open valve (B) and fill tank to desired level
   [[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]].
3. After tank is filled, close valve
   [[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]].
4. Remove fill hose and reattach fill cap
   [[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]].

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — remove the lid when filling at a high rate
> IMPORTANT: Damage to rinse tank and surrounding structure can occur when filling at a high rate if
> tank lid is not removed. Remove tank lid when filling at high rates to avoid damage.
> [[fill-rinse-tank-using-rinse-tank-fill-valve--c54960|Fill Rinse Tank Using Rinse Tank Fill Valve]]

### Fill with a nurse tank pump through a Quik-Fill valve (dual product)

Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed, open the lock levers and remove the cap, connect the
nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers, then open the Quik-Fill
valve and nurse tank valve
[[fill-rinse-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill-port--cb25a2|Fill Rinse Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill Port]].

For the front fill port into tank 1: verify that the smaller front Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed,
open the lock levers and remove cap (B), connect the nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill valve and
close the lock levers, then work through the micro display fill menu before opening the Quik-Fill
valve and the nurse tank valve
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
When finished: shut off the nurse tank pump, close the nurse tank valve, close the Quik-Fill valve
at the front load station, disconnect the nurse tank hose, and install the cap on the Quik-Fill
valve and close the lock levers
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].

### Purge the front fill plumbing

On machines with a manual air purge valve: verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed, open air purge
valve (B) and wait ten seconds, then close the front fill air purge valve
[[operate-front-fill-air-purge--940cbd|Operate Front Fill Air Purge]].

![[NX1440202.png]]
**Figure NX1440202** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Air Purge Valve
[[operate-front-fill-air-purge--940cbd|Operate Front Fill Air Purge]]

On machines without a manual air purge valve: verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed, press the
air purge button (A) on the micro display, then press the select button (A) to begin the air purge
procedure; during the procedure, press the cancel button (A) to end and exit if necessary
[[operate-front-fill-air-purge--940cbd|Operate Front Fill Air Purge]].

## Related troubleshooting

Individual valves can be exercised from the display's valve test pages, which list a DID number, DID
name and DID value for each valve. On a single solution machine the list includes System 1 Front
Fill Isolation Valve Test, System 1 Main Tank Fill Valve Test, Rinse Tank Sump Valve Test, Front
Fill Isolation Valve Test, Tank Fill Selector Valve Test, Powrspray Rinse Fill Valve Test and
Valve Maintenance Test Mode
[[solution-system-1-valve-testing--100b7a|Solution System 1 Valve Testing]]. A corresponding list
exists for dual solution systems
[[dual-solution-system-valve-testing--2801b5|Dual Solution System Valve Testing]].

If the sprayer takes air into the boom while filling from a nurse system pump, check that the
strainer/flowmeter shutoff valve was closed before filling
[[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].

## Applicability

The single solution valve set and handle instructions come from the Single Solution Command System
Operation section [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]]; the two-Quik-Fill-valve load
stations, the separate tank 1 and tank 2 fill paths and the 90-degree handle instruction come from
the Dual System Command System Operation section, which is tagged for the Dual Product option
[[wet-system-valves--b2adcf|Wet System Valves]]. The air purge procedure branches on whether the
machine has a manual air purge valve
[[operate-front-fill-air-purge--940cbd|Operate Front Fill Air Purge]].
