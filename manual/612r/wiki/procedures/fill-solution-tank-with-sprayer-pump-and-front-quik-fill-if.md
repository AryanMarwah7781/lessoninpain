# Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped

Pulling solution from a nurse tank onto the machine through the front Quik-Fill, using the machine's
solution pump. The main steps are the **Single Solution Command System** procedure (base machine,
no option) with a front Quik-Fill fitted
[[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]].
A dual product machine uses a different front-fill procedure — see
[the conditional section](#if-your-machine-has-the-dual-product-solution-system) at the end. Do not
use the dual product steps on a single solution machine.

The product label governs mixing and handling: always follow all label directions for effective,
safe, and legal use of agricultural chemicals
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous.
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed.
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect.
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
> - Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
> - If solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse System in this section.
> - Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
> - The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during entire fill procedure.
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]]

**In an emergency:** the Quick Stop Switch allows you to shut OFF application system in the case of
an emergency, and stops the solution pump, spray system on/off and wet system valves, among others
[[quick-stop-switch--43ba9f|Quick Stop Switch]].

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: When pressing the quick stop switch, you are responsible for pulling back on the multi-function lever to stop the machine.
> [[quick-stop-switch--43ba9f|Quick Stop Switch]]

## Before you start

If the solution tank is empty, prime the pump with a low volume rinse cycle first, and end the rinse
cycle once priming is achieved
[[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if--93b1da|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]].
The rinse procedure is [[operate-rinse-system--738e0d|Operate Rinse System]].

## If your machine has the Dual Product solution system

A dual product machine fills from the front fill station with the POWRSPRAY pump, using a different
micro display sequence with a target fill volume per tank
[[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
In outline, from that topic
[[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]]:

1. Set the target fill amount in the display in the tank system application for system 1 or system
   2; the rinse tank cannot target fill
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
2. Verify that the larger Quik-Fill valve at the front fill station is closed, open the lock levers
   and remove the cap, connect the nurse tank fill hose and close the lock levers, then open the
   nurse tank valve and the Quik-Fill valve
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
3. Open the cover for access to the micro display, select the fill button, the tank fill button for
   the tank being filled, the pull-on fill button and the target fill button; confirm or change the
   target fill volume and press enter to begin
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
4. The pump speed button changes the speed of the solution pump during the fill; the solution pump
   automatically shuts off at the target level, or press cancel to stop early
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
5. Close the Quik-Fill valve, close the nurse tank valve, remove the fill hose, and install the cap
   and close the lock levers
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].

Read that topic in full before using it; it carries its own CAUTION and IMPORTANT blocks, including
a note that filling the rinse tank from this location pushes the fluid through the shared plumbing
with the main solution system
[[fill-solution-tank-with-powrspray-pump-front-fill--cf0c6c|Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump— Front Fill]].
