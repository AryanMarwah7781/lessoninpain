# Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily

This task is written for the base machine (single solution system). It applies to all models
[[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]].

> [!note] Where this is printed
> The manual prints this procedure three times, word for word, under one module ID: in Prestarting
> Checks (p. 45-6 to 45-8), in Wet System (p. 55-24 to 55-26) and in Lubrication and Maintenance
> (p. 115-13 to 115-14) [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]].
> The three copies were compared page by page and the steps, notes and cautions are identical, so
> there is no conflict between them. Each step below gives the page it is printed on in each copy,
> in the order Prestarting Checks · Wet System · Lubrication and Maintenance.

The product label and local regulations govern chemical application and handling; the manual tells
you to treat all spray chemicals, solutions, or solution residues with great caution
[[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]].

## When to clean them

Clean the boom supply line strainers if the pump fails to obtain maximum flow rate, the pump fails
to maintain dead head pressure, application rates fluctuate, or the difference between solution
pump pressure and spray pressure is greater than expected for a given application
[[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-6 · 55-24/55-25 · 115-13).

Strainers help keep dirt and debris from entering and damaging the solution pump, nozzles, and other
components, and strainer screens should be removed and cleaned to maintain solution flow
[[solution-strainers--3be17d|Solution Strainers]]. On the base machine the pressure strainer is
"Pressure Strainer- 50 Mesh (Standard)" and the boom pressure strainers are "80 Mesh (Standard)"
[[solution-strainers--3295d0|Solution Strainers]].

![[N99945.png]]
**Figure N99945** — A: Fill Strainer- 20 Mesh (Standard); B: Pressure Strainer- 50 Mesh (Standard);
C: Pressure Strainer- Outer Boom Sections 80 Mesh (Standard); D: Pressure Strainer- Center Boom
Section 80 Mesh (Standard) [[solution-strainers--3295d0|Solution Strainers]]

The manual says: "For best results, John Deere recommends using an 80 mesh strainer if apporved by
the chemical label. If the chemical label requires a different mesh strainer, follow the chemical
label requirement." (spelling as printed) [[solution-strainers--3be17d|Solution Strainers]].

## Warnings

Each distinct warning is given once. All three printed copies carry the same two cautions
[[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]].

> [!warning] CAUTION — hazardous material (p. 45-6 · 55-24 · 115-13)
> CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Clean filter in an area where people, animals, vegetation, water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated. [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

> [!warning] CAUTION — trapped pressure (p. 45-7 · 55-25 · 115-13)
> CAUTION: Do not close flowmeter shutoff valve (A) with the solution pump running. This can trap pressure in the system that can be hazardous when opening strainers for cleaning. [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]
>
> Do not drain solution onto the ground. Drain into a container. Drain solution in an area where
> people, animals, vegetation water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.

> [!warning] CAUTION — before any service on the spray equipment
> CAUTION: Spray solutions or vapors may be extremely dangerous. Treat all spray chemicals, solutions, or solution residues with great caution. DO NOT take chances. When in doubt, proceed as though contamination is present. [[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]]

> [!conflict] Drain the tank first, or keep solution in it?
> The Safety section says that before performing any service activity you should wear appropriate
> personal protective equipment, clean the vehicle, drain the solution tank, and rinse the wet system
> [[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]]. The daily strainer
> procedure instead assumes solution is still in the system: it closes valves "to prevent excessive
> solution loss" and drains only the strainers into a container
> [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]. Neither is a generic
> Deere-wide module. The manual does not say which applies to the daily strainer check; at minimum
> wear the protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves both sources call for.

## Prerequisites

1. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment
   [[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]].
2. Make sure the solution pump is not running before you close the flowmeter shutoff valve — the
   caution above forbids closing it with the pump running
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-7 · 55-25 · 115-13).
3. Have a container ready with sufficient capacity; machines equipped with an electric valve require
   more solution be drained through the strainer
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-7 · 55-25 · 115-13).

## Steps

1. Close valves to prevent excessive solution loss
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-7 · 55-25 · 115-13):
   1. Close flowmeter shutoff valve (A).

      ![[NX1432687.png]]
      **Figure NX1432687** — A: Flowmeter Shutoff Valve
      [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

   2. Machines equipped with a solution command system: verify push-on fill button (A) is OFF.

      ![[NX1435327.png]]
      **Figure NX1435327** — A: Push-On Fill Button
      [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

2. Remove plug (A) to allow the strainer to drain into a suitable container
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-7 · 55-25 · 115-14).

   ![[N137658.png]]
   **Figure N137658** — A: Plug (80-Mesh Strainer)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

3. Open strainer drain valves (A) and drain solution into a suitable container through the drain
   hoses (B) [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).

   ![[NX1432695.png]]
   **Figure NX1432695** — A: Strainer Drain Valve; B: Drain Hose (50-Mesh Strainer)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

   ![[N137659.png]]
   **Figure N137659** — A: Strainer Drain Valve; B: Drain Hose (80-Mesh Strainer)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

4. Unscrew rings (A) and remove strainer housings (B)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).

   ![[NX1432696.png]]
   **Figure NX1432696** — A: Ring; B: Housing (50-Mesh Strainer)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

   ![[N137660.png]]
   **Figure N137660** — A: Ring; B: Housing (80-Mesh Strainer)
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]]

5. Remove screen and wash with clean water
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).
6. Install screens and housings
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).
7. Install plug
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).
8. Close the strainer drain valves
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).
9. Open boom isolation valve, flowmeter shutoff valve, and solution tank sump valve
   [[clean-pressure-strainers-daily--be6871|Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily]] (p. 45-8 · 55-26 · 115-14).
   To open the flowmeter shutoff valve, rotate the handle parallel to the solution hose
   [[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].

## If your machine has ExactApply

For machines equipped with the ExactApply nozzle control system, 80 mesh strainers must be used in
locations C and D (the outer and center boom section pressure strainers); anything smaller can cause
performance issues [[solution-strainers--3295d0|Solution Strainers]].

## If your machine has Dual Product

A dual product machine has more pressure strainers: a "Pressure Strainer, 50 Mesh" for each of system 1
and system 2, and 80 mesh pressure strainers for nozzles A and nozzles B
[[solution-strainers--fcf0a3|Solution Strainers]]. The manual refers dual product owners to the
same Clean Pressure Strainers—Daily procedure in the Wet System section
[[solution-strainers--fcf0a3|Solution Strainers]]; it does not print separate dual product steps.
