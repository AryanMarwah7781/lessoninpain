# Change Planetary Hub Oil—250 Hours

> [!warning] Check your model before you start
> The 250-hour interval is printed for the **400R, 410R, and 412R** only
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
> The **408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R** have their own topic with a 500-hour interval
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]]
> — see [the section for those models](#if-your-machine-is-a-408r-600r-612r-or-616r-500-hours) below.
> Every model also has a first change After First 50 Hours
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

## Oil and capacity

All hubs are filled with 85W140 Gear Lube from the factory; if oil needs to be added before the
50-hour initial oil change, use 85W140 [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]. Use an oil
viscosity based on the expected air temperature range during the period between oil changes
[[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]. John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic Gear Lube is
preferred for all 400R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R, and 616R hubs; John Deere GL5 85W140 Gear Lube is
preferred for 408R hubs [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]].

Hub capacities [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]: 400R—1.5 L (1.6 qt);
408R—1.2 L (1.3 qt); 410R—1.5 L (1.6 qt); 412R—1.5 L (1.6 qt); 600R—2.5 L (2.6 qt);
612R—2.5 L (2.6 qt); 616R—2.5 L (2.6 qt).

## Prerequisites

The procedure begins with a warm-up drive, then parking on a flat, level surface and letting the
hubs cool
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
Park as described on [[park-machine-safely]] before working at the hubs.

> [!warning] Hot oil
> Allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be handled safely (approximately 30 minutes).
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]]

## Steps — 400R, 410R, and 412R (250 Hours)

1. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
2. Park machine on a flat, level surface
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
3. Allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be handled safely (approximately 30 minutes)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].

   ![[N130090.png]]

   **Figure N130090** — A: Arrow at Two O'Clock Position; B: Drain Plug; C: Fill Plug; D: Fill Level
   Plug; E: Fill Level
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]]

4. Rotate hub until casting arrow is pointed to two o'clock position (A)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].

   > [!warning] NOTE
   > NOTE: Ensure that all oil is drained from the hub. This process can take up to 1 hour when warm, and drained oil should approximately amount to the hub's oil volume capacity that is listed in Planetary Hub Oil in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.
   > [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]]

5. Remove drain plug (B) and drain oil into a suitable container
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
6. Reinstall drain plug and tighten to specification: 400R, 410R, and 412R Drain Plugs—Torque
   20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
7. Remove the fill plug (C)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
8. Remove fill level plug (D)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
9. Fill hub to fill level (E) — refill capacity and recommended oil are under
   [Oil and capacity](#oil-and-capacity)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
10. Install fill level plug and tighten to specification: 400R, 410R, and 412R Fill Level
    Plugs—Torque 8—12 N·m (6—9 lb·ft)
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
11. Install the fill plug and tighten to specification: 400R, 410R, and 412R Fill Plugs—Torque
    20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
12. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
13. Let hub cool for 1 hour and recheck oil level
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
14. Add more oil as required
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].

## If your machine is a 408R, 600R, 612R, or 616R (500 Hours)

This variant has its own page: [[change-planetary-hub-oil-500-hours]]. The first change for all
models is on [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours]].

The steps are the same sequence, with a different interval and — for the 600R, 612R, and 616R —
higher plug torques
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].
612R planetary hub is shown in the figure; 408R, 600R, and 616R hubs are similar
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].

1. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes; park machine on a flat, level
   surface; allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be handled safely (approximately 30
   minutes)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].
2. Rotate hub until casting arrow is pointed to two o'clock position (A); remove drain plug (B) and

3. Reinstall drain plug and tighten: 408R Drain Plugs—Torque 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft); 600R, 612R,

4. Remove the fill plug (C) and fill level plug (D); fill hub to fill level (E)

5. Install fill level plug and tighten: 408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R Fill Level Plugs—Torque

6. Install the fill plug and tighten: 408R Fill Plugs—Torque 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft); 600R, 612R,
   and 616R Fill Plugs—Torque 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].
7. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes; let hub cool for 1 hour and
   recheck oil level; add more oil as required
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].

## First change — After First 50 Hours (all models)

The after-first-50-hours topic uses the same sequence and gives both model groups' torques in one
place: 400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R drain and fill plugs 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft); 600R, 612R, and 616R
drain and fill plugs 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft); fill level plugs on all models 8—12 N·m (6—9 lb·ft)
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
Note that this topic prints the fill level plug model list as "R400R, 408R, 410R, 412R, 600R, 612R,
and 616R" [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

## What the manual does not specify here

- No warm-up alternative is given if the machine cannot be driven at 40 km/h (25 mph) for the
  warm-up [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
- The topics do not say how to dispose of the drained gear oil.
