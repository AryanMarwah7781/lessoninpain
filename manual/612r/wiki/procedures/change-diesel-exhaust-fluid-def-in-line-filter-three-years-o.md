# Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours

**Interval:** as printed, "Three years or 8500 Hours"
[[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]].
**Models:** the topic applies to all models and does not name an engine tier
[[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]].

> [!inference] Which machines have a DEF in-line filter
> The SCR overview lists an Inline DEF Filter "(If Equipped)"
> [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-overview--8b2d6a|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System Overview]],
> and the machine's SCR topic is titled for Final Tier 4 and Stage V engines
> [[selective-catalytic-reduction-scr-system-final-tier-4-and-st--c3c982|Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) System (Final Tier 4 and Stage V Engines)]].
> So this job applies only to a machine with an SCR system that has the in-line filter fitted. The
> filter topic does not state this itself.

See also [[aftertreatment]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Avoid possible personal injury. In case of DEF contact, immediately flush eyes with large amounts of water for a minimum of 15 minutes. Reference the Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information. Do not ingest DEF. In the event DEF is ingested, contact a physician immediately. Reference the Materials Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for additional information.
> [[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Avoid corrosion of vehicle parts or surfaces. If DEF is spilled or contacts any surface other than the storage tank, immediately clean the surface with clear water. DEF is corrosive to painted and unpainted metallic surfaces and can distort some plastic and rubber components. Spilled DEF, if left to dry or if only wiped away with a cloth, will leave a white residue. Improperly cleaned DEF spill may interfere with diagnosis of selective catalytic reduction (SCR) system leakage problems.
> [[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Avoid system and filter damage. Ensure that DEF system is not frozen before changing filter. If system is frozen, operate engine until system has thawed completely.
> [[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]]

  This operator's manual does not show where the filter is on the machine.
- Have a container ready: "NOTE: Container must be DEF compatible and hold at least 300 mL (0.32 qt)." [[change-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-in-line-filter-three-years-o--c872be|Change Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) In-Line Filter—Three years or 8500 Hours]]
- Make sure the DEF system is not frozen (see the IMPORTANT above).
