# Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System

How to find where air is getting in, and how to bleed it out of the boom. Applies to all models
[[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
There are two bleed processes: one for machines **without** pressure recirculation, and one for
machines **with** a pressure recirculation system — use the one that matches your machine
[[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
This process is also called for when removing the machine from storage — see
[[remove-machine-from-storage]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: If performing this process after removing machine from storage, it must be performed after freezing is not an issue or done with winterizing fluid.
> [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]]

> [!conflict] Spray pressure used for the boom air bleed
> The boom air bleed process says to run the sprayer in manual spray mode at 620.6 kPa (90 psi)
> [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
> The generic Deere-wide chemical-safety module (`generic: true`) says do not operate solution
> delivery system at pressures exceeding 345kPa (3.5 bar) (50 psi)
> [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
> The bleed process uses water, but the two statements still disagree on the pressure the solution
> system may be run at. This page does not choose; confirm with your John Deere dealer.

## Find the source of the air first

- Check for a suction leak using soapy water at all of the fitting and hose connections
  [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
- Eliminate air coming from the nurse system by having a valve at the end of the fill hose. Always
  close this valve before disconnecting from the sprayer to ensure that air does not get into the
  system [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].

## Steps — Boom Air Bleed Process (machines without pressure recirculation)

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Machine is equipped with spray pipe aspirators to aid with removing air from boom.
> [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]]

1. Fill solution tank with approximately 473 L (125 gal) of water
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
2. Unfold and lower boom for access to nozzles
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
3. Turn off all nozzle bodies except for the last nozzle body furthest from the machine centerline on
   all pipe assemblies. Remove the spray tip from these nozzle bodies
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
4. Lower the center section and tilt both boom wings all the way up
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
5. Run the sprayer in manual spray mode at 620.6 kPa (90 psi)
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
   (See the conflict callout above.)
6. Cycle sprayer operation through at least three on-off cycles as follows to bleed trapped air from
   the booms
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]]:
   1. Set spray system on/off switch to ON and watch for water discharge from open nozzle bodies
      [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
   2. Set spray system on/off switch to OFF and allow machine to sit for AT LEAST 15 seconds in OFF
      mode to allow air to move to high points
      [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
7. Watch the discharge from each open nozzle body for smooth uniform output. Repeat on-off spray
   cycle until output is uniform. Continue spraying until only 189—283 L (50—75 gal) of water remain
   in the tank
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
8. Rotate all nozzle bodies to spray position. Reinstall spray tips that were previously removed
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: If solution tank is loaded with a solution other than water and purging boom with water is not feasible, operator may tilt up booms all the way and spray for a short distance. This helps remove air from the system.
> [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]]

## If your machine has a pressure recirculation system

1. Fill solution tank with approximately 473 L (125 gal) of water
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
2. Unfold and lower boom
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
3. Turn on pressure recirculation and let it run for a couple of minutes
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
4. Set spray system on/off switch to ON and watch output from nozzles
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
5. When smooth uniform output from nozzles is achieved, set spray system on/off switch to OFF
   [[recommendations-for-eliminating-air-from-solution-system--c98980|Recommendations for Eliminating Air from Solution System]].
