# Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped

This procedure applies only to machines equipped with the **front Quik-Fill** valve; it is written
for the single solution machine
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if-eq--43abad|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]].
Without a front fill, use the side Quik-Fill:
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]]
(p. 60-18 to 60-19). A **Dual Product** machine uses different front-fill procedures — see the
conditional section at the end.

In this procedure the nurse tank's own pump pushes solution into the sprayer; automatic fill is not
active when filling with a nurse pump
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if-eq--43abad|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]].

**The product label and local regulations govern.** Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions
for mixing the spray solution
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if-eq--43abad|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Pump and Front Quik-Fill—If Equipped]].

## If your machine has Dual Product

The front load station on a dual product machine has two Quik-Fill valves, and the procedures differ:

- **Fill any tank** (tank 1, tank 2 or the rinse tank) through the larger front Quik-Fill valve:
  verify it is closed, remove the cap, connect the nurse tank hose, open cover (C) for the micro
  display, select fill, select the tank, select push-on fill, select tank fill, open the Quik-Fill
  valve and the nurse tank valve, and monitor the micro display; when full select cancel, close the
  nurse tank valve and the Quik-Fill valve, disconnect the hose and refit the cap
  [[fill-any-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--4ba26e|Fill Any Tank with Nurse Tank Pump—Front Fill]] (p. 65-15 to 65-17).
- **Fill tank 1 only** through the smaller front Quik-Fill valve (A), using the tank 1 push-on fill
  menu
  [[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]] (p. 65-14).

Both dual product procedures end with: if necessary, purge residual solution from the front fill
plumbing using the onboard air supply
[[fill-any-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--4ba26e|Fill Any Tank with Nurse Tank Pump—Front Fill]].
