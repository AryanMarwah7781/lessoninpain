# Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually

**Interval:** "—1000 Hours or Annually", as printed in the topic title
[[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
**Applies to:** all models; the source topic carries no model, engine or option tag, and its two
figure pairs are not labelled by engine
[[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

The filter-replacement steps are printed twice: in this 1000-hour topic and in Replace Primary and
Secondary Engine Air Filters (Engine and Drivetrain section)
[[replace-primary-and-secondary-engine-air-filters--0cea01|Replace Primary and Secondary Engine Air Filters]],
which is also where Replace Engine Air Filters—As Required points
[[replace-engine-air-filters-as-required--c2b82a|Replace Engine Air Filters—As Required]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Avoid serious injury from falling. Do not stand on the fuel tank when replacing air filters. [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not attempt to clean filters. Replace secondary filter (B) every second primary filter change. Install new secondary filter immediately to prevent dust from entering the air intake system. [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

## Prerequisites

1. Park the machine and stop the engine: [[park-machine-safely]]. Never lubricate, service, or
   adjust machine while it is moving; stop the engine and secure the machine; use a ladder or
   platform to easily reach each location
   [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]] (generic Deere-wide module; the
   air intake topic itself does not state a parking step).

## Part 2 — Replace the air filters

1. Set the tread width to the widest setting to allow space for filter removal
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

   ![[NX1396258.png]]

   **Figure NX1396258** — A: Latch; B: Filter Cover
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

2. Open latch (A)
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
3. Open filter cover (B) approximately 45°. Slide the filter cover down to remove
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

   ![[NX1396261.png]]

   **Figure NX1396261** — A: Primary Filter; B: Lip
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

4. Slide primary filter (A) forward away from lip (B) of the filter housing
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
5. Remove the primary filter
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

   ![[N153570.png]]

   **Figure N153570** — A: Primary Filter; B: Secondary Filter
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

6. Replace primary filter (A) and secondary filter
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
   Do not attempt to clean filters; install a new secondary filter immediately to prevent dust from
   entering the air intake system
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
7. Clean dirt from inside the cover and canister
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

   ![[NX1396269.png]]

   **Figure NX1396269** — A: Filter Cover; B: Clip
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

8. With filter cover (A) at a 45° angle, slide the filter cover onto the filter housing
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
9. Close the filter cover, verifying that clip (B) of the filter housing is positioned in the notch
   of the filter cover as shown
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

   ![[NX1396280.png]]

   **Figure NX1396280** — A: Latch; B: Rod
   [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]]

10. Close latch (A), verifying that the latch is centered on rod (B) as shown
    [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].

> [!conflict] Clip or ridge at the filter cover
> The 1000-hour topic labels callout (B) on Figure NX1396269 "Clip" and says to verify that clip (B)
> of the filter housing is positioned in the notch of the filter cover
> [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
> The Engine and Drivetrain copy of the same step, with the same figure, labels (B) "Ridge" and says
> ridge (B) [[replace-primary-and-secondary-engine-air-filters--0cea01|Replace Primary and Secondary Engine Air Filters]].
> Both are machine-specific. Same figure, different name for the part; check what is on your filter
> housing.

> [!inference] Secondary filter: every change, or every second change?
> Both copies of this procedure print "Replace secondary filter (B) every second primary filter
> change" in the IMPORTANT block and then, in step 6, "Replace primary filter (A) and secondary
> filter"
> [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]],
> [[replace-primary-and-secondary-engine-air-filters--0cea01|Replace Primary and Secondary Engine Air Filters]].
> Read literally, step 6 replaces the secondary filter every time. My reading is that step 6
> applies on the changes where the secondary filter is due, but the manual does not say so. Confirm
> with your dealer.

## What the manual does not specify here

- Air filter part numbers, and whether a restriction indicator governs the "As Required" change —
  the As Required topic only refers back to the procedure
  [[replace-engine-air-filters-as-required--c2b82a|Replace Engine Air Filters—As Required]].
- A torque value for the intake hose clamps; the check is only for damage and tight connection.
- Setting the tread back to working width afterwards is not a printed step.
