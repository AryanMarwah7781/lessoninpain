# Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor

**Applies to:** the base machine — Single Solution Command System (section 60), all models, no
option [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]].
Machines with the **Dual Product** solution system use a different procedure, given only in the
conditional section at the end of this page.

The sprayer's own solution pump draws water from a nurse tank through the Quik-Fill valve while
chemical is loaded through the eductor
[[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]].

**The product label governs.** Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray
solution [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]],
wear personal protective equipment in accordance with the chemical manufacturer's label, and read
and follow the manufacturer's label for each chemical applied, the U.S. Environmental Protection
Agency (EPA) Worker Protection Standard for Agricultural Pesticides, and state or regional
guidelines for worker safety and health
[[avoid-exposure-to-chemicals--54538c|Avoid Exposure To Chemicals]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels carefully before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed. [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect. [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running the pump dry, observe the following guidelines:
> - Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
> - If the solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. (See Operate Rinse System in this section.)
> - Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
> - The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during the entire fill procedure.
>
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]] — priming rinse: [[operate-rinse-system]]

## Related setting — eductor level

Eductor Level setting allows you to change the amount of suction produced in the eductor; the
suction level must be correctly set to ensure proper loading of chemical while minimizing foaming
[[eductor-level--4dff43|Eductor Level]]. Levels: Low (use to reduce chemical foaming during the
loading procedure), Medium (use for easy flowing chemicals), High (use for dry-flowables and heavy or
thick liquid chemicals) [[eductor-level--4dff43|Eductor Level]].

> [!inference] "Settings 1—3" and Low/Medium/High
> The fill procedure speaks of default eductor settings (1—3), Setting 1 lowest and 3 highest
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-sprayer-pump-while-adding-chemicals--f26e48|Fill Solution Tank with Sprayer Pump While Adding Chemicals Through Eductor]];
> the display application names the levels Low, Medium and High
> [[eductor-level--4dff43|Eductor Level]]. I assume these are the same three levels, but the
> manual does not state the mapping.

## If your machine has Dual Product (conditional — not the base procedure)

Dual Product machines add chemicals through two eductors, one per tank system, while filling with
the PowrSpray pump [[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
The same CAUTION and IMPORTANT about the chemical label apply
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
Eductor level is adjusted separately for each solution system [[eductor-level--4dff43|Eductor Level]].

![[N161198.png]]

**Figure N161198** — A: Latch Lever; B: Eductor Handle; C: Eductor On/Off Button (tank system 1);
D: Eductor On/Off Button (tank system 2)

D1. Connect a nurse tank to the Quik-Fill valve at the side load station or at the front load
station, and begin filling the desired solution tank (Fill Solution Tank with PowrSpray Pump—Side
Fill or —Front Fill)
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
D2. Pull on latch lever (A) and unlatch the eductors; pull on handle (B) and rotate the eductors
down into the fill position; raise the lid on the eductor for the desired tank system
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
D3. Press eductor on/off button (C or D) on the load system keypad for the desired tank system —
not before the solution tank is half full
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
D4. Open the eductor sump valve (A or B) for the desired tank system; select the eductor rinse
button and the rinse source (solution tank or rinse tank) on the micro display, select start, and
open the eductor rinse valve (A or B) (clockwise)
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
D5. Pour chemicals into the eductor, rinse the container on the rinse nozzle, and verify that the
eductor is empty
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
D6. Shut off the rinse with the eductor rinse button and the eductor fill with the eductor on/off
button for that tank system; close the eductor rinse valve and the eductor sump valve; close the lid
and lift the eductors back to the original position
[[add-chemicals-using-eductor-while-filling-solution-tank-usin--b8b773|Add Chemicals Using Eductor While Filling Solution Tank Using PowrSpray Pump]].
