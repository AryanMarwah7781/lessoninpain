# Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours

**Interval:** as printed in the topic title, "Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours"
[[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. **Models:** all; step 9
shows a separate figure for the 6.8 L engine and the 9.0 L engine
[[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. The Final Tier 4
displacement is 6.8 L (415 in³) for the 408R and 9.0 L (549 in³) for the 410R, 412R, 400R, 612R,
616R and 600R [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

Approximately 98 L (26 gal) of John Deere Torq-Gard Supreme 10W30 oil is required to fill the
hydraulic reservoir [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
Steps 10 onward need two people
[[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Avoid injury or death to others. Keep away all bystanders while performing the start-up procedure. Machine could move unexpectedly.
> [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Fill cap (B) contains the hydraulic reservoir breather filter and must be replaced when the hydraulic oil is changed.
> [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: The following steps require two people. [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

Generic Deere-wide maintenance rule: never lubricate, service, or adjust machine while it is moving,
and keep hands, feet, and clothing away from power-driven parts
[[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].

## Prerequisites

- Place the booms in the boom rests and retract all the hydraulic cylinders, including the tread
  adjust cylinders (step 1) [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
- Park on a flat, level surface (step 2)
  [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]; see
  [[park-machine-safely]].

> [!inference] Battery and key during this job
> The generic park-safely guidance includes disconnecting the battery ground strap, but steps 10–18
> of this procedure crank and run the engine, so the battery must be connected for those steps
> [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. The topic itself does
> not say when to stop the engine during draining.

## Steps

1. Place the booms in the boom rests and retract all the hydraulic cylinders, including the tread
   adjust cylinders [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
2. Park machine on a flat, level surface
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

   ![[N153556.png]]

   **Figure N153556** — A: Plug; B: Shutoff Valve
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

3. Remove the plug (A) from the reservoir drain hose
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
4. Drain fluid by opening the shutoff valve (B)
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
5. Close the shutoff valve [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
6. Install the plug into the drain hose
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

   ![[N153557.png]]

   **Figure N153557** — A: Sight Gage; B: Fill Cap; C: Decal
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

7. Remove the fill cap and fill the hydraulic reservoir with the specified hydraulic oil. Verify that
   the oil showing in the sight gauge (A) is at the proper level for the current oil temperature as
   shown on the fill level decal (C). Do not install the reservoir cap at this time
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

   ![[N121582.png]]

   **Figure N121582** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]

8. Install a 6894 kPa (68.9 bar) (1000 psi) pressure gauge to the charge pressure receptacle (A)
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
9. Disconnect fuel injector harness (A) on the cylinder head
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

   ![[N135939.png]]

   **Figure N135939** — 6.8 L Engine — A: Fuel Injector Harness
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]] (408R —
   [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]])

   ![[N135940.png]]

   **Figure N135940** — 9.0 L Engine — A: Fuel Injector Harness
   [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]] (all other models —
   [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]])

10. Turn the ignition key for 15 seconds at 1-minute intervals. Monitor the charge pressure while
    starting the engine. Repeat until the charge pressure consistently reaches the specified pressure
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. Specification:
    Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure 207— 345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi)
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
11. Turn the ignition key for three additional 15-second cycles
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
12. Check the oil level and add as required
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
13. Reconnect the fuel injector harness
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
14. Start the engine and run at a low idle for 5 minutes (keep bystanders away — see the CAUTION
    above) [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
15. Check the charge pressure and verify that it is within specification
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. Specification:
    Charge Pump Pressure—Pressure 2344—2827 kPa (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi)
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
16. Check the oil level and add as required
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
17. Operate the engine at a high idle
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
18. Verify that the machine drive system operates properly. Move the machine in forward and reverse
    directions [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
19. Check for leaks [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].
20. Check the oil level and add as required. Install the reservoir cap
    [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]].

> [!inference] Two charge-pressure specifications
> The topic gives two different Charge Pump Pressure values: 207— 345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi)
> in step 10, while cranking with the injector harness disconnected, and 2344—2827 kPa
> (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi) in step 15, with the engine running at low idle
> [[change-hydraulic-oil-1000-hours--58c1ec|Change Hydraulic Oil—1000 Hours]]. They are read at
> different stages, so they are not treated as a conflict here. The manual does not say this in so
> many words.

## What the manual does not specify here

- What to do if charge pressure never reaches specification in step 10 is not stated — contact your
  John Deere dealer.
