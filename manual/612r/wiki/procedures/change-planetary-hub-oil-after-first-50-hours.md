# Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours

**Interval:** "—After First 50 Hours", as printed in the topic title — the initial (break-in) hub oil
change [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
**Applies to:** all models. Torque values differ by model group, and each step below says which
group each value applies to
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

After this first change the manual schedules the hub oil change at 250 Hours for the 400R, 410R and
412R [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]],
and at 500 Hours for the 408R, 600R, 612R and 616R
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].

## Warnings and notes

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Ensure that all oil is drained from the hub. This process can take up to 1 hour when warm, and drained oil should approximately amount to the hub's oil volume capacity that is listed in Planetary Hub Oil in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section.. [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]]

The hub oil is hot after the warm-up drive: allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be
handled safely (approximately 30 minutes) before draining
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

## Oil and capacity

All hubs are filled with 85W140 Gear Lube from the factory, and if oil needs to be added before the
50-hour initial oil change, use 85W140 [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]. For the
refill, use an oil viscosity based on the expected air temperature range during the period between
oil changes [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]:

| Model | Preferred oil | Hub capacity |
|---|---|---|
| 408R | John Deere GL5 85W140 Gear Lube | 408R—1.2 L (1.3 qt) |
| 400R, 410R, 412R | John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic Gear Lube | 1.5 L (1.6 qt) |
| 600R, 612R, 616R | John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic Gear Lube | 2.5 L (2.6 qt) |

Source for every cell: [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]].

## Prerequisite

Step 2 below parks the machine on a flat, level surface; follow [[park-machine-safely]] at that

## Steps

![[N130091.png]]

**Figure N130091** — A: Arrow (at two o'clock position); B: Drain Plug; C: Fill Plug; D: Fill Level
Plug; E: Fill Level. 612R planetary hub is shown; all other planetary hubs are similar
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]]

1. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
2. Park machine on a flat, level surface ([[park-machine-safely]])
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
3. Allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be handled safely (approximately 30 minutes)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
4. Rotate hub until the casting arrow is pointed to the two o'clock position (A)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
5. Remove drain plug (B) and drain oil into a suitable container
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
6. Reinstall drain plug. Tighten to specification
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]]:
   - 400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R Drain Plugs—Torque: 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
   - 600R, 612R, and 616R Drain Plugs—Torque: 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft)
7. Remove the fill plug (C)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
8. Remove fill level plug (D)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
9. Fill hub to the oil fill level (E) — oil and capacity in the table above
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
10. Install fill level plug. Tighten to specification — printed as "R400R, 408R, 410R, 412R, 600R,
    612R, and 616R Fill Level Plugs—Torque": 8—12 N·m (6—9 lb·ft), i.e. the same value for all
    models [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
11. Install the fill plug. Tighten to specification
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]]:
    - 400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R Fill Plugs—Torque: 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
    - 600R, 612R, and 616R Fill Plugs—Torque: 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft)
12. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
13. Let hub cool for 1 hour and recheck the oil level
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
14. Add more oil as required
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

The torques above match those printed in the later 250-hour and 500-hour topics for the same models
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]],
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].
