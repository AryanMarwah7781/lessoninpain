# Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually

**Interval:** "—Annually", as printed in the topic title
[[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
**Applies to:** all models; the source topic carries no model, engine or option tag
[[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: The onboard air system is charged at 689—896 kPa (6.89—8.96 bar) (100—130 psi). To avoid injury to yourself or others, wear safety goggles and gloves and be sure that others are clear of area before relieving the onboard air system pressure. [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]]

## Prerequisites

1. Park the machine and stop the engine: [[park-machine-safely]]. Never lubricate, service, or
   adjust machine while it is moving; stop the engine and secure the machine
   [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]] (generic Deere-wide module; the
   air drier topic itself does not state a parking step).
2. Wear safety goggles and gloves and make sure others are clear of the area before relieving the
   onboard air system pressure
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].

## Steps

![[N153550.png]]

**Figure N153550** — A: Drain Valve
[[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]]

1. Relieve air system pressure by opening valve (A)
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].

![[N136021.png]]

**Figure N136021** — A: Cartridge; B: O-Ring
[[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]]

2. Using a strap wrench, turn the desiccant cartridge (A) counterclockwise, then remove and discard
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].

   > [!warning] NOTE
   > NOTE: If there is excessive oil present in the desiccant filter, compressor may require servicing. [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]]

3. Remove and discard O-ring (B) from the adapter plate stud
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
4. Clean top surface of the adapter plate and threaded stud
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
5. Apply a light coat of grease (supplied with the new cartridge) to the new O-ring. Install O-ring
   on the adapter plate
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
6. Apply a generous coat of grease to the surface of the new desiccant cartridge gasket
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
7. Thread new cartridge into adapter plate by turning clockwise until gasket contacts adapter
   plate. Tighten cartridge with an additional 1/2 turn. DO NOT overtighten
   [[replace-air-drier-cartridge-annually--fdd7c5|Replace Air Drier Cartridge—Annually]].
