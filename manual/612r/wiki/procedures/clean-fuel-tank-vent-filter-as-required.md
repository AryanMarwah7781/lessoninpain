# Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required

This task applies to all models and has no option variants; the interval is "As Required"
[[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]]. The
manual does not say what condition makes it required.

## Warnings

The procedure itself carries no DANGER, WARNING, CAUTION or IMPORTANT text
[[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]]. The
general maintenance and fuel safety rules below come from generic Deere-wide modules
(`generic: true`).

- Falling while cleaning or working at height can cause serious injury. Use a ladder or platform to
  easily reach each location. Use sturdy and secure footholds and handholds
  [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
- Handle fuel with care: it is highly flammable
  [[handle-fuel-safely-avoid-fires--1e08df|Handle Fuel Safely—Avoid Fires]].

## Prerequisites

1. Park the machine safely — see [[park-machine-safely]]. Never lubricate, service, or adjust the
   machine while it is moving; stop the engine and secure the machine, and if equipped remove the key
   and allow the machine to cool [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]].
2. Use a commercial ladder to access the fuel tank vent filter
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-17).

## Steps

1. Remove cap screws (A)
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).

   ![[N153545.png]]
   **Figure N153545** — A: Cap Screw (2 used)
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]]

2. Lift cover, then remove and retain P-clamp (A)
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).

   ![[N153546.png]]
   **Figure N153546** — A: P-Clamp; B: Fuel Tank Vent Filter
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]]

3. Remove fuel tank vent filter (B) and clean in soapy detergent solution
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).
4. Blow dry with compressed air and install
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).
5. Retain fuel tank vent filter with previously removed P-clamp
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).
6. Install cover
   [[clean-fuel-tank-vent-filter-as-required--49bf9a|Clean Fuel Tank Vent Filter—As Required]] (p. 115-18).
