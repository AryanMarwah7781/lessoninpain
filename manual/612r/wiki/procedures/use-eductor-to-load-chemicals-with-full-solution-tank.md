# Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank

This page is written for the base machine (Single Solution Command System)
[[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]].
A machine with the **Dual Product** option uses a different procedure — see the conditional section
at the end; do not mix the two.

**The product label and local regulations govern.** Read the directions printed on the chemical
manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals, and follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions
for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect
[[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION — read the label (p. 60-20)
> CAUTION: Read the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels carefully before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — when to add chemical (p. 60-21)
> IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the solution tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect. [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

> [!warning] CAUTION — Quik-Fill valve (p. 60-21)
> CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

## Prerequisites

1. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment
   [[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]].
2. Starting condition: the solution tank is full (but has room for the volume of the chemical to be
   added) and the engine is running. Eductor performance can be diminished somewhat with a full tank
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).

## Steps

1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).
2. Slightly raise the eductor and pull handle (A) to unlatch
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).

   ![[N135425.png]]
   **Figure N135425** — A: Handle
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

3. Lower the eductor and raise the lid
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).
4. Press eductor on/off (A) on the keypad
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).

   ![[NX1435325.png]]
   **Figure NX1435325** — A: Eductor On/Off
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

5. Adjust the eductor pressure if desired using pump speed increase button (A) or pump speed decrease
   button (B) on the micro display
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-21).

   ![[NX1441266.png]]
   **Figure NX1441266** — A: Pump Speed Increse Button; B: Pump Speed Decrease Button (spelling as
   printed)
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

6. Open eductor sump valve (A) (handle parallel with the solution hose) to allow chemicals to be drawn
   from the eductor hopper
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).
7. Open eductor rinse valve (B) (turn clockwise) to rinse the eductor hopper
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).

   ![[N135935.png]]
   **Figure N135935** — A: Eductor Sump Valve; B: Eductor Rinse Valve
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

8. Pour chemicals into the eductor
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).
   The poly eductor is equipped with a level gauge (A) to aide in filling
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]].

   ![[N155780.png]]
   **Figure N155780** — A: Level Gauge
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

9. Place the chemical container over rinse nozzle (A) in the tank and push down on the container to
   rinse
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).

   ![[N135936.png]]
   **Figure N135936** — A: Rinse Nozzle
   [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]]

10. Verify that the eductor is empty
    [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).
11. Close the eductor rinse valve and eductor sump valve
    [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).
12. Close the hopper lid and lift the eductor back to its original position
    [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).
13. Press eductor on/off (A) to stop
    [[use-eductor-to-load-chemicals-with-full-solution-tank--e0f0ee|Use Eductor to Load Chemicals with Full Solution Tank]] (p. 60-22).

The eductor sump valve allows chemical to be drawn from the bottom of the eductor hopper to the top
of the solution tank, and the eductor rinse valve lets the operator direct solution to rinse the
hopper and chemical containers; both are only operational when the eductor flow rate valve is open
[[wet-system-valves--2f14aa|Wet System Valves]].

## If your machine has Dual Product

Use "Add Chemicals to Full Tank Using Eductor" (p. 65-25 to 65-27) instead
[[add-chemicals-to-full-tank-using-eductor--d5a96a|Add Chemicals to Full Tank Using Eductor]]. It
carries the same label CAUTION and "do not add chemical until just before field use" IMPORTANT, and
differs from the base procedure as follows
[[add-chemicals-to-full-tank-using-eductor--d5a96a|Add Chemicals to Full Tank Using Eductor]]:

1. Pull on the latch lever (A) and unlatch the eductors; pull on handle (B) and rotate the eductors
   down into the fill position; raise the lid on the eductor for the desired tank system.
2. Press the eductor on/off button (C or D) on the load system keypad for the desired tank system.
3. Eductor pressure settings (1—3) can be selected on the CommandCenter display in the cab, or
   adjusted with the pump speed increase/decrease buttons on the micro display.
4. Open the eductor sump valve (A or B) for the desired tank system.
5. Select the eductor to rinse on the load station keypad (A for system 1, B for system 2), then the
   rinse source on the micro display — rinse button (A) rinses from the solution tank, (B) from the
   rinse tank — and select start.
6. Open the eductor rinse valve (A or B) for the tank system being used (turn the handle clockwise to
   open), pour chemicals into the eductor, and rinse the container over the rinse nozzle.
7. Verify that the eductor is empty; shut off the rinse with the eductor rinse button (A or B) and the
   eductor fill with the eductor on/off button (C or D).
8. Close the eductor rinse valve and the eductor sump valve, close the lid and lift the eductors back
   to the original position.

> [!warning] NOTE — dual product only (p. 65-25 to 65-27)
> NOTE: Do not start the eductor before the solution tank is half full to prevent foaming when the chemical is added. [[add-chemicals-to-full-tank-using-eductor--d5a96a|Add Chemicals to Full Tank Using Eductor]]
