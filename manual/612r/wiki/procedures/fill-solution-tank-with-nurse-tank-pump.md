# Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump

**Applies to:** the base machine — Single Solution Command System (section 60), all models, no
option [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]].
Machines with the **Dual Product** solution system use different procedures, given only in the
conditional section at the end of this page.

Here the nurse tank's own pump pushes water in through the Quik-Fill valve; the sprayer's solution
pump does not do the filling. Automatic fill is not active when filling with a nurse pump
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]].

**The product label governs.** Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray
solution [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]],
and read and follow the manufacturer's label for each chemical applied, the U.S. Environmental
Protection Agency (EPA) Worker Protection Standard for Agricultural Pesticides, and state or regional
guidelines for worker safety and health
[[avoid-exposure-to-chemicals--54538c|Avoid Exposure To Chemicals]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed. [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the solution tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect. [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]]

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Automatic fill is not active when filling with a nurse pump. Operator must monitor solution tank content volume to prevent overfilling solution tank. When using an external pump source, the sight gauge can give erratic volume readings. Therefore, a flowmeter is recommended to monitor tank fill accurately. [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]]

## Prerequisites

1. Wear personal protective equipment in accordance with the chemical manufacturer's label
   [[avoid-exposure-to-chemicals--54538c|Avoid Exposure To Chemicals]].
2. Arrange a way to monitor the tank volume — a flowmeter is recommended, because the sight gauge
   can give erratic readings with an external pump
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]].

## If your machine has Dual Product (conditional — not the base procedure)

Dual Product machines have two tank systems and two load stations, and the nurse-pump fill is
printed separately for the side load station and the front load station
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]],
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
The same CAUTIONs and IMPORTANT apply to both
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].

### Dual Product — side fill

![[NX1440843.png]]

**Figure NX1440843** — A: Quik-Fill Valve; B: Cap; C: Cover

![[NX1434997.png]]

**Figure NX1434997** — A: System 1 Keypad; B: System 2 Keypad; C: Push-On Button (tank system 1);
D: Push-On Button (tank system 2)
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]]

S1. Verify that Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed; open the lock levers and remove cap (B); connect the
nurse tank fill hose to the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers; open the Quik-Fill valve
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S2. Lift cover (C) for access to the system keypads and micro displays
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S3. To fill tank 1, press push-on button (C) on system 1 keypad (A); to fill tank 2, press push-on
button (D) on system 2 keypad (B)
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S4. Press tank fill button (A) on the micro display for the selected tank system to begin the
filling procedure
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S5. Open the nurse tank valve and start the nurse tank pump; monitor the solution level on the micro
display, fill to the desired level, then shut off the nurse tank pump
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S6. Close the nurse tank valve and the Quik-Fill valve
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S7. Close the tank fill selection valve by pressing cancel button (A) on the micro display
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].
S8. Remove the fill hose, install the cap on the Quik-Fill adapter and close the lock levers
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump-side-fill--445903|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump— Side Fill]].

### Dual Product — front fill (tank 1)

S1. Verify that the smaller front Quik-Fill valve (A) is closed; open the lock levers and remove cap
(B); connect the nurse tank fill hose and close the lock levers; open cover (C)
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
S2. On the micro display select fill button, then tank 1 button, then push-on fill button, then tank
fill button to begin the filling procedure
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
S3. Open the Quik-Fill valve, then open the nurse tank valve and fill tank 1 from the nurse tank
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
S4. Monitor the level on the micro display; when the tank contains the desired amount, select cancel
button (A), shut off the nurse tank pump, close the nurse tank valve and the Quik-Fill valve
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
S5. Disconnect the hose, install the cap and close the lock levers; if necessary, purge residual
solution from the front fill plumbing using the onboard air supply
[[fill-solution-tank-1-with-nurse-tank-pump-front-fill--d44b44|Fill Solution Tank 1 with Nurse Tank Pump —Front Fill]].
