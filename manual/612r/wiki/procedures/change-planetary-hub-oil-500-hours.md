# Change Planetary Hub Oil—500 Hours

**Interval and models, exactly as printed:** "Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and
616R)—500 Hours"
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].

> [!warning] Check your model before you start
> The 400R, 410R and 412R are **not** on the 500-hour schedule. The manual prints a separate
> procedure for them at a different interval:
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]].
> Every model also gets an initial change:
> [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].

The procedure is illustrated with the 612R hub; the 408R, 600R, and 616R hubs are similar
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).

## Oil and capacity

The oil preferred for all 408R hubs is John Deere GL5 85W140 Gear Lube; for 600R, 612R, and 616R hubs
it is John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic Gear Lube
[[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]. Use an oil viscosity based on the expected air
temperature range during the period between oil changes
[[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]].

Hub capacities [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]:

| Model | Capacity per hub |
|---|---|
| 408R | 1.2 L (1.3 qt) |
| 600R | 2.5 L (2.6 qt) |
| 612R | 2.5 L (2.6 qt) |
| 616R | 2.5 L (2.6 qt) |

The Specifications section gives the same final drive capacities: 1.2 L (1.3 qt) each for the 408R
and 2.5 L (2.6 qt) each for the 600R, 612R, and 616R
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

## Warnings

The procedure carries no DANGER, WARNING or CAUTION text. It does tell you to let the hubs cool
until the oil can be handled safely before draining
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]].

> [!warning] NOTE — drain completely (p. 115-31)
> NOTE: Ensure that all oil is drained from the hub. This process can take up to 1 hour when warm, and drained oil should approximately amount to the hub's oil volume capacity that is listed in Planetary Hub Oil in the Fuels, Lubricants, and Coolants section. [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]]

## Steps

1. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
2. Park machine on a flat, level surface
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
   See [[park-machine-safely]].
3. Allow hubs to cool to a point that the oil can be handled safely (approximately 30 minutes)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
4. Rotate hub until casting arrow is pointed to two o'clock position (A)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
5. Remove drain plug (B) and drain oil into a suitable container
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
6. Reinstall drain plug. Tighten to specification
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31):
   - 408R Drain Plugs—Torque: 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
   - 600R, 612R, and 616R Drain Plugs—Torque: 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft)
7. Remove the fill plug (C)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
8. Remove fill level plug (D)
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-31).
9. Fill hub to fill level (E), using the refill capacity and recommended oil in the table above
   [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32)
   [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]].
10. Install fill level plug. Tighten to specification — 408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R Fill Level
    Plugs—Torque: 8—12 N·m (6—9 lb·ft)
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32).
11. Install the fill plug. Tighten to specification
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32):
    - 408R Fill Plugs—Torque: 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft)
    - 600R, 612R, and 616R Fill Plugs—Torque: 30—40 N·m (22—30 lb·ft)
12. Drive machine at 40 km/h (25 mph) or above for 15 —20 minutes
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32).
13. Let hub cool for 1 hour and recheck oil level
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32).
14. Add more oil as required
    [[change-planetary-hub-oil-408r-600r-612r-and-616r-500-hours--ae1d30|Change Planetary Hub Oil (408R, 600R, 612R, and 616R)—500 Hours]] (p. 115-32).

Repeat for each hub. The manual does not state the number of hubs in this procedure; the
Specifications section gives final drive capacity as "Each"
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

## If your machine is a 400R, 410R or 412R

Use [[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]]
instead. The steps are the same sequence, but the torques are 20—30 N·m (15—22 lb·ft) for both drain
and fill plugs and 8—12 N·m (6—9 lb·ft) for fill level plugs
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-400r-410r-and-412r-250-hours--d48d3d|Change Planetary Hub Oil (400R, 410R, and 412R)—250 Hours]],
the capacity is 1.5 L (1.6 qt) per hub, and the preferred oil is John Deere GL5 80-140 Synthetic
Gear Lube [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]].

## Initial change (all models)

All hubs are filled with 85W140 Gear Lube from the factory; if oil needs to be added before the
initial oil change, use 85W140 [[planetary-hub-oil--a0928b|Planetary Hub Oil]]. The initial change
uses the same steps, with torques split 400R, 408R, 410R, and 412R versus 600R, 612R, and 616R
[[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours--7dc7e5|Change Planetary Hub Oil—After First 50 Hours]].
