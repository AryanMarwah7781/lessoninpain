# Transport Machine

This task applies to all models and has no option variants in the printed procedure
[[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]. Steps 1–10 are printed on p. 50-26 and steps 11–15
on p. 50-27 [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].

## Speed limits

- Speed range (transport): 0—56 km/h (0—35 mph); EU/CU countries 0—40 km/h (0—24.8 mph)
  [[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].
- The axle load decal on the left-hand corner post says: "Do not transport machine above 48 km/h
  (30 mph) unless application system is empty. Follow all local laws or regulations for machine
  maximum speed limit" [[axle-load-decal--4b48d5|Axle Load Decal]].
- When the machine is started, the F1 range is automatically selected with a target speed of
  16 km/h (10 mph) [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].

So the upper end of the transport range applies only with the application system empty; with any
load in the application system the decal's lower limit governs
[[axle-load-decal--4b48d5|Axle Load Decal]]. Local laws and regulations may set lower limits still
[[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION — maximum transport speed (p. 50-26)
> CAUTION: DO NOT exceed maximum transport speed of the machine or loss of control can occur, causing serious injury or death. [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

> [!warning] CAUTION — downhill at transport speed (p. 50-27)
> CAUTION: When transporting machine downhill at transport speeds, machine tries to hold the target speed until engine begins to overspeed. At this time, the vehicle speed could increase beyond target speed. Before starting down a hill, select a speed range that is appropriate for the load on the machine and the steepness and length of the slope. [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — booms on the cradles (p. 50-26)
> IMPORTANT: Machine can be damaged if transported with the booms not lowered onto the boom cradles. [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — tire load ratings (p. 50-27)
> IMPORTANT: Do not exceed tire weight load ratings printed in this manual. Weigh the machine and verify maximum transport weight and speed from this manual before loading and do not exceed published weights. [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

From the Safety section [[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]]:
keep away from overhead power lines — serious injury or death to you or others can result should
the machine contact electrical wires; know the transport height, boom operating height and boom
folding height of your machine; stop slowly to avoid "nose diving"; keep SMV emblem and reflectors
clean and in place; reduce speeds for icy, wet, graveled or soft roadway surfaces; and check and
follow local regulations for equipment size, lighting and marking before driving on public roadways.

## Prerequisites

1. Transport the machine at the narrowest tread adjust setting only
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].
2. It is recommended to remove the StarFire receiver when transporting the machine; follow all local
   laws to meet height requirements [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].
3. Weigh the machine and check tire inflation against the Wheels and Tires section — see [[tire]]
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].

## Steps

1. Be sure that a slow-moving vehicle (SMV) emblem is installed on the boom (or rear of machine)
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
2. Start the engine — see [[start-the-engine--a12543|Start the Engine]]
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
3. Press the road light button (A) [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
4. Press the hazard light button (B) [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).

   ![[N153023.png]]
   **Figure N153023** — A: Road Light Button; B: Hazard Light Button
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

5. Check that the amber warning lights are flashing and that all lights are clean and visible
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
6. Adjust tread width to the narrowest position (see the Chassis System Application section for tread
   adjust procedures) [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
7. Fold the booms into transport position [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
   Boom Return to Height will remain active after the boom is folded. When lowering the boom onto
   the cradles, the boom may stop at the set point if it is above support rest height; it can be
   lowered beyond the set point by pressing the boom lower switch again
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].
8. If nozzles are dripping, relieve boom pressure — see [[relieve-spray-pressure]]
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26). The Boom Pressure Relief function's
   main purpose is to minimize the possibility of the sprayer nozzle bodies leaking while in transit
   [[pressure-relief--da45ff|Pressure Relief]].
9. Push the transport lock switch to put the machine in transport mode
   [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26).
10. Rephase the steering cylinders [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-26): turn
    the wheels full left, then turn the steering wheel three additional revolutions while slowly
    moving forward at around 0.8 km/h (0.5 mph); turn the wheels full right and do the same; repeat
    these steps 1—3 more times
    [[check-front-axle-toe-in-annually-or-as-needed--b4cc6e|Check Front Axle Toe-In—(Annually or as Needed)]].
    See [[steering]].
11. Push the high idle switch to achieve maximum engine speed
    [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-27).

    ![[N137031.png]]
    **Figure N137031** — High Idle Switch [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

12. Select appropriate target speed range. When the machine is started, the F1 range is automatically
    selected with a target speed of 16 km/h (10 mph)
    [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-27).
13. Press the park brake switch to release the park brakes
    [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-27).

    ![[N137032.png]]
    **Figure N137032** — Park Brake Switch [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]]

14. Push the multi-function lever slowly forward to start forward travel. Continue pushing the
    multi-function lever forward to increase ground speed. When engine reaches maximum rpm, rotate the
    speed range adjustment wheel to increase speed, if desired
    [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-27).
15. To reverse direction of travel, move the multi-function lever to neutral and press the brake pedal
    as required to bring the machine to a complete stop, then move the multi-function lever to the
    right and rear to back up the machine. Reverse speed available is proportional to the distance
    the multi-function lever is moved [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]] (p. 50-27).

## What the manual does not specify

- The transport height of each machine and boom configuration is not given in this procedure; the
  Safety section only tells you to know it
  [[transport-and-operate-safely--a0035b|Transport and Operate Safely]].
- The manual does not give a maximum transport speed per tire size in this procedure; it sends you
  to the tire tables — see [[tire]] [[transport-machine--4216ec|Transport Machine]].
