# Clean Flowmeter—As Required

**Interval:** as required — when application rates fluctuate
[[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]. **Models:** all; the topic has
no option condition [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: Standard flow pump systems have a single flowmeter. High flow systems have two flowmeters. [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

Step 3 refers to the flowmeter inserts in the plural
[[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]; on a high flow machine that
means both flowmeters.

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Do not close the flowmeter shutoff valve (B) with the solution pump running. This can trap pressure in the plumbing that can be hazardous when removing flowmeters for cleaning.
> [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Spray system can contain hazardous material, which can cause serious injury or death to you or others. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves. Clean flowmeter in an area where people, animals, vegetation, and water supply, and so on, cannot be contaminated.
> [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

For what the manual says about trapped pressure elsewhere in the spray system, see
[[relieve-spray-pressure]].

## Steps

![[NX1432688.png]]

**Figure NX1432688** — A: Flowmeter Insert; B: Flowmeter Shutoff Valve
[[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

1. Shut off solution pump [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].
2. Close flowmeter shutoff valve (B) — only after the pump is off (see the first CAUTION)
   [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].
3. Unscrew flowmeter inserts and remove
   [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].

   ![[N136012.png]]

   **Figure N136012** — A: Turbine [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

4. Clean insert with John Deere Brake and Parts Cleaner (Part No. TY26101) by spraying cleaner
   through turbine (A) and bearing area
   [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].

   > [!warning] NOTE
   > NOTE: Clean the flowmeter thoroughly with water to ensure none of the cleaner sits in the flow meter.
   > [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

5. Install insert in flowmeter [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].
6. Open flowmeter shutoff valve [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: If the flowmeter issue is not resolved with the brake cleaner, contact a John Deere dealer or other service provider for flowmeter reconditioning.
> Do not allow solution to remain in flowmeters for extended periods of time as chemicals can solidify and cause performance issues.
> [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]]

## What the manual does not specify here

- The topic does not say whether the flowmeter must be recalibrated after cleaning. A flowmeter
  calibration exists as its own display procedure
  [[flowmeter-calibration-procedure--44ce32|Flowmeter Calibration Procedure]], but the cleaning topic
  does not refer to it.
- How much solution will come out when the insert is removed is not stated; the CAUTION requires a
  location where nothing can be contaminated [[clean-flowmeter-as-required--75e4f8|Clean Flowmeter—As Required]].
