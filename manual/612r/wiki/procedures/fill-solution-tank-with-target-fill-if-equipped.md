# Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped

This procedure applies only to machines equipped with **Target Fill**; it is written for the single
solution machine
[[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].
Target Fill automatically fills the tank to a desired level; the correct volume must be entered to
ensure proper concentration of chemical and to have enough solution to complete your job
[[target-fill--096808|Target Fill]]. The manual prints no Dual Product version of this procedure.

Target Fill uses the sprayer's own solution pump to pull solution from the nurse tank: step 4 selects
the pull-on fill button, and the procedure warns about running the solution pump dry
[[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].
Target fill will not function when filling the tank with the nurse pump (push on) — use
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-tank-pump--129396|Fill Solution Tank with Nurse Tank Pump]] or
[[fill-solution-tank-with-nurse-pump-and-front-quik-fill-if-eq]] for that
[[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].
The steps below show the side Quik-Fill; the front fill procedure is similar
[[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].

**The product label and local regulations govern.** Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions
for mixing the spray solution
[[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION — read the label (p. 60-14)
> CAUTION: Read carefully the directions printed on the chemical manufacturer's labels before handling chemicals to prevent exposure to hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — when to add chemical (p. 60-14)
> IMPORTANT: Do not add chemical to the tank until just before field use. Follow the chemical manufacturer's instructions for mixing the spray solution to obtain the desired application rate and effect. [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] CAUTION — Quik-Fill valve (p. 60-14)
> CAUTION: Quik-Fill valve (A) can contain hazardous materials that could cause injury or be poisonous. Before removing Quik-Fill cap (B), verify that Quik-Fill valve is closed. [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — do not run the pump dry (p. 60-14)
> IMPORTANT: To prevent solution pump damage caused by running pump dry, observe the following guidelines: [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]
>
> - Ensure that the fill hose is the shortest length possible.
> - If solution tank is empty, perform a low volume rinse cycle to prime the pump. See Operate Rinse
>   System in this section.
> - Rinse cycle can be ended after pump priming is achieved.
> - The water level in the nurse tank must be at or above the midline of the solution pump during
>   entire fill procedure.

> [!conflict] How high the nurse tank level must be
> The Target Fill procedure says the water level in the nurse tank must be "at or above the midline
> of the solution pump" during the entire fill
> [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].
> The solution pump topic says the nurse tank solution level "must be above that of the sprayers
> pump, or solution pump will not prime leading to seal damage", and adds that 76.2 mm (3 in) hose
> and fittings must be used (if possible) on the nurse equipment
> [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]].
> Neither is a generic module. The manual does not reconcile them; the stricter reading is a level
> above the pump. Confirm with your dealer if your nurse setup cannot meet it.

## Prerequisites

1. Wear the protective equipment the chemical label calls for
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].
2. If the solution tank is empty, prime the pump first with a low volume rinse cycle — see
   [[operate-rinse-system--738e0d|Operate Rinse System]]; the rinse cycle can be ended once priming
   is achieved
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-14).
3. Position the nurse tank so its level stays at or above the pump (see the conflict above) and keep
   the fill hose as short as possible
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]].

## Steps

1. Verify that the Quik-Fill valve is closed
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-14).
2. Open lock levers and remove the cap
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-14).
3. Connect nurse tank fill hose to Quik-Fill adapter, close lock levers, and open nurse tank valve
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-14).
4. Open the Quik-Fill valve and press pull-on fill button (A)
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-14).

   ![[NX1435136.png]]
   **Figure NX1435136** — A: Pull-On Fill Button
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

5. Press target fill button (A)
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).

   ![[NX1440130.png]]
   **Figure NX1440130** — A: Target Fill Button
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

   If the target fill volume is set using the CommandCenter display or is the same as the last loaded
   volume, press enter button (A) to confirm the fill volume and begin the fill procedure
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).

   ![[NX1440131.png]]
   **Figure NX1440131** — A: Enter Button; B: Clear Button; C: Target Fill Volume; D: Back Button
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

6. If necessary, press clear button (B) to reset the volume and set the desired target fill volume (C)
   using the keypad
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).
7. Press the enter button to confirm and begin fill procedure. Press back button (D) to return to the
   previous screen view
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).
8. During the fill procedure, press pump speed button (A) to change the speed of the solution pump
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).

   ![[NX1440138.png]]
   **Figure NX1440138** — A: Pump Speed Button; B: Cancel Button
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]]

9. Press cancel button (B) to end and exit the target fill procedure if desired
   [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).
10. After the solution tank has filled to preset level, close nurse tank and Quik-Fill valves
    [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).
11. Remove the fill hose from the Quik-Fill adapter and replace cap
    [[fill-solution-tank-with-target-fill-if-equipped--8ee583|Fill Solution Tank with Target Fill—If Equipped]] (p. 60-15).

## Setting the target from the cab display

In the Tank System application, verify that the desired fill level is shown and select the Input Box
to change the target fill preset; select Start to begin filling and Stop to end before the selected
level is reached [[target-fill--096808|Target Fill]]. The status indicator shows Active (green) while
the target fill system is filling, and Inactive (gray) when it is not — in which case you must
monitor the solution level using the sight gauge
[[status-indicator-target-fill--830a38|Status Indicator | Target Fill]].
