# Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours

**Interval:** as printed in the topic title, "—1000 Hours"
[[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
**Applies to:** all models; the source topic is not tagged to a model or option. Two figures differ
by engine: 6.8 L Engine and 9.0 L Engine
[[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
The 408R has the PowerTech Plus 6.8 L engine; the 410R, 412R, 400R and 612R have the JD9 and the
616R/600R the PowerTech PSS 9.0L, with 9.0 L (549 in³) displacement
[[machine-specification--bfe721|Machine Specification]].

The hydrostatic system and hydraulic system use the same reservoir
[[check-hydraulic-oil-level-daily--edf17c|Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily]]. The filter change ends
with a charge-pressure start-up check; the manual also prints that start-up as a separate Hydraulic
Start-Up Procedure, to be performed when changing the hydraulic or hydrostatic filters and oil
[[hydraulic-start-up-procedure--8db98e|Hydraulic Start-Up Procedure]]. The two versions give
different pressures — see the conflict below before you start.

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Avoid injury or death to others. Keep away all bystanders while performing the start-up procedure. Machine could move unexpectedly. [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]]

Escaping fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin causing serious injury; avoid the hazard by
relieving pressure before disconnecting hydraulic or other lines
[[avoid-high-pressure-fluids--3fc79d|Avoid High-Pressure Fluids]] (generic Deere-wide module).

## Prerequisites

1. Park the machine and stop the engine: [[park-machine-safely]]. Never lubricate, service, or
   adjust machine while it is moving; disengage all power and operate controls to relieve pressure;
   lower equipment to the ground; stop the engine and secure the machine; if equipped, remove the
   key, disconnect the battery, and allow machine to cool
   [[practice-safe-maintenance--f22083|Practice Safe Maintenance]] (generic Deere-wide module).
2. For the oil-level checks in this procedure, the level is read in the sight tube with the machine
   on level surface, tread adjusted to narrow position, booms in cradle, and engine stopped
   [[check-hydraulic-oil-level-daily--edf17c|Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily]].

> [!inference] Battery disconnect
> Practice Safe Maintenance says to disconnect the battery, but this procedure later needs the
> ignition key turned to crank the engine. The filter-change topic itself does not mention the
> battery. Reconnect it before step 7; on Final Tier 4/Stage V machines wait for the DEF purge LED
> before disconnecting (see [[park-machine-safely]]).

## Steps

![[N155093.png]]

**Figure N155093** — A: Hydrostatic Filter; B: Hydraulic Filter (2 used)
[[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]]

1. Remove hydrostatic filter (A) and hydraulic filters (B)
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
2. Lubricate the new filter packing with John Deere Torq-Gard Supreme 10W-30 or equivalent
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
3. Install and hand-tighten the filter elements
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
4. Check the oil level and add as required. Do not install the reservoir cap at this time
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].

   ![[N121582.png]]

   **Figure N121582** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle; B: Fuel Injector Harness

   ![[N121583.png]]

   **Figure N121583** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle; B: Fuel Injector Harness — printed with
   the label "6.8 L Engine" (408R)

   ![[N121584.png]]

   **Figure N121584** — A: Charge Pressure Receptacle; B: Fuel Injector Harness — printed with
   the label "9.0 L Engine" (all other models)
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]]

5. Install a 6894 kPa (68.9 bar) (1000 psi) pressure gauge to the charge pressure receptacle (A)
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
6. Disconnect fuel injector harness (B) on the cylinder head
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
   The following steps could require two people
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
7. Turn the ignition key for 15 seconds at 1-minute intervals. Monitor the charge pressure while
   starting the engine. Repeat until the charge pressure consistently reaches the specified pressure
   — Charge Pump Pressure: 207—345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi) in this topic; **see the
   conflict below**
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
8. Turn the ignition key for three additional 15-second cycles
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
9. Check the oil level and add as required
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
10. Reconnect the fuel injector harness
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
11. Keep bystanders away (CAUTION above). Start the engine and run at a low idle for 5 minutes
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
12. Check the charge pressure and verify that it is within specification — Charge Pump Pressure:
    2344—2827 kPa (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi)
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
13. Check the oil level and add as required
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
14. Operate the engine at a high idle
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
15. Verify that the machine drive system operates properly. Move the machine in forward and reverse
    directions
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
16. Check for leaks
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
17. Check the oil level and add as required. Install the reservoir cap
    [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].

> [!conflict] Charge pump pressure during the start-up check
> The 1000-hour filter topic (p. 115-48) gives a cranking-phase Charge Pump Pressure of
> 207—345 kPa (2.07—3.45 bar) (30—50 psi), then a running check after 5 minutes at low idle of
> 2344—2827 kPa (23.44—28.27 bar) (340—410 psi)
> [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
> The Hydraulic Start-Up Procedure (p. 105-2), which the manual says to perform when changing the
> hydraulic or hydrostatic filters and oil, gives the cranking-phase Charge Pump Pressure as
> 3102—3861 kPa (31.02—38.61 bar) (450—560 psi) and has no running-phase pressure check
> [[hydraulic-start-up-procedure--8db98e|Hydraulic Start-Up Procedure]]. Both are machine-specific
> topics. The cranking-phase ranges do not overlap. This page does not choose — confirm the correct
> charge pressure with your John Deere dealer before judging the result.

> [!conflict] Crank the engine, or turn the ignition key
> The filter topic says to "Turn the ignition key for 15 seconds at 1-minute intervals"
> [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]],
> the start-up procedure says to "Crank the engine for 15 seconds at 1 minute intervals"
> [[hydraulic-start-up-procedure--8db98e|Hydraulic Start-Up Procedure]]. With the fuel injector
> harness disconnected both describe the same action, but the wording is reported as printed.

## What the manual does not specify here

  Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily
  [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours--98578b|Replace Hydrostatic and Hydraulic Filters —1000 Hours]].
- What to do if the charge pressure never reaches specification; the procedure only says to repeat
  until it does. Ask your dealer.
