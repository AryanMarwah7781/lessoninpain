# Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually

This task is for machines with the **high flow** solution pump; the procedure's pump-removal figure
is labelled "High Flow Solution System" and the torque given is for "High Flow Pump Mounting Nuts"
[[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
The high flow system is optional: the manual describes the high flow valve as "(optional)" and used
with a high flow solution system [[solution-system--544787|Solution System]]. The manual prints no
equivalent seal-chamber procedure for the standard flow pump. Interval as printed: Annually.

The product label governs chemical handling: check the manufacturer's label of the last used
chemical to see if special cleaning procedures are necessary, and follow the chemical manufacturer's
instructions for disposal
[[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]] (p. 115-44).

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION — decontaminate first (p. 115-44)
> CAUTION: Decontaminate all spraying equipment before servicing. Decontamination should be done in a safe area by washing with water, neutralization, or by means recommended by the manufacturer of chemical last used. [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]]
>
> Spray solutions or vapors may be extremely dangerous. Treat all spray chemicals, solutions, or
> solution residues with great caution. Do not take chances. When in doubt, proceed as though
> contamination is present.
>
> Keep spray material from contacting skin. If spray material contacts skin, wash off immediately
> with clean water and detergent or follow instructions of manufacturer of chemical last used.

> [!warning] CAUTION — pump and motor weight (p. 115-44)
> CAUTION: Approximate weight of the solution pump and motor assembly is 40 kg (88 lb). Use two people to remove pump and motor assembly. [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]]

The solution pump topic adds two cautions that apply whenever the pump is serviced:

> [!warning] CAUTION — key switch (p. 55-3)
> CAUTION: Remove key from the key switch before servicing pump. [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]]

> [!warning] CAUTION — high-pressure fluid (p. 55-3)
> CAUTION: Escaping fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin causing serious injury. [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]]
>
> Avoid the hazard by relieving pressure before disconnecting hydraulic or other lines. Tighten all
> connections before applying pressure.

## Prerequisites

1. Park the machine safely and remove the key from the key switch — see [[park-machine-safely]]
   [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]].
2. Relieve pressure before disconnecting lines — see [[relieve-spray-pressure]]
   [[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]].
3. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment
   [[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]].
4. Have the supplied seal chamber fluid, medium-strength thread lock and sealer, and a suitable stand
   for the motor ready
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].

> [!inference] Order of the prerequisites
> Steps 3 to 7 of the procedure run the pump to circulate cleaning solutions, so the engine and pump
> must be operable during decontamination
> [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
> Parking with the key removed and relieving pressure therefore apply from step 9 (disconnecting the
> pump), not before step 1. The manual does not state this order explicitly.

## Steps — decontaminate (p. 115-44)

1. Check manufacturer's label of the last used chemical to see if special cleaning procedures are
   necessary
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
2. Safely dispose of any chemical solution remaining in the tank. Follow chemical manufacturer's
   instructions for disposal of chemical solution
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
3. Flush the tank and pump thoroughly with clean water and allow the water to drain out
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
4. Mix household detergent with 95 L (25 gal) of water or use Incide-Out Spray Tank Cleaner (PM434QT)
   and circulate through the system for 30 minutes, then drain. Discharge detergent solution through
   spray nozzles until the tank is empty
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
5. Fill tank with water. Add 0.95 L (1 qt) of household ammonia for every 95 L (25 gal) of water.
   Operate pump to circulate ammonia solution through the spray system for 15—20 minutes. Let stand
   overnight
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
6. Flush the ammonia solution out of sprayer. Discharge the solution through the spray nozzles until
   tank is empty
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
7. Fill tank with clean water and flush system by discharging the water through the open nozzle
   bodies
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
8. Clean the area around the solution pump and motor
   [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].

## Steps — replenish the seal chamber (p. 115-45)

The solution pump wet seal fluid capacity is approximately 23 oz
[[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].

![[N147089.png]]
**Figure N147089** — A: Plug (2 used)
[[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]]

12. Position the pump so that one of the plugs (A) is at the twelve o'clock position and remove plug
    if installed
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
13. While the plug is held in the twelve o'clock position, fill the pump with the supplied seal
    chamber fluid. Fill the pump until it is level with the bottom threads and seeps from hole
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
14. Apply thread lock and sealer (medium strength) to the plug
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
15. Install the plug and tighten to specification — Fill Plug—Torque: 13 N·m (119 lb·in)
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
16. Install pump in reverse order. Tighten pump mounting nuts to specification — High Flow Pump
    Mounting Nuts—Torque: 315 N·m (232 lb·ft)
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
17. Check the reservoir oil level and add as necessary (see Check Hydraulic Oil Level—Daily)
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
18. Flush the system with SprayMaster Winterizer Fluid (N305634) if the machine is to be stored in a
    climate colder than 0°C (32°F) — see [[prepare-machine-for-cold-weather-storage-winterizing]]
    [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].

## Why it matters

A mechanical seal leak caused by the pump running dry is fixed by replacing the seal; the manual
lists seal problems and their fixes in the pump's troubleshooting table
[[solution-pump-maintenance-and-operation-recommendations--a28f35|Solution Pump Maintenance and Operation Recommendations]].
See [[solution-pump]].

## What the manual does not specify

- The part number of the "supplied seal chamber fluid" is not given
  [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
- No inspection criterion (colour, level loss) is given for deciding whether the fluid needs
  replenishing; the procedure simply refills it
  [[inspect-and-replenish-high-flow-spray-pump-seal-chamber-flui--5cdd03|Inspect and Replenish High Flow Spray Pump Seal Chamber Fluid—Annually]].
