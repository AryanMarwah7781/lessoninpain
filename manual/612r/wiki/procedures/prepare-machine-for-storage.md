# Prepare Machine for Storage

**Applies to:** all models [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
Two parts are conditional and are marked where they occur: the nozzle-turret plugs (ExactApply with
carbon fiber booms only) and the battery/DEF steps (Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines)
[[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. For machines that are stored in
cold temperatures, a winterizing procedure is also performed (step 4)
[[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: DO NOT use gasoline for washing. Gasoline is highly flammable and can ignite if exposed to sparks or open flame, causing a fire that can cause serious injury or death. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: On the Final Tier 4/Stage V engine equipped machines, do not disconnect the battery until the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is off. Selective catalyst reduction system automatically purges lines of diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) immediately after the machine is stopped. If adequate time is not allowed for lines to be purged, any DEF remaining in lines can crystallize and plug the lines. In freezing weather, DEF freezes and possibly bursts lines. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

> [!warning] IMPORTANT
> IMPORTANT: Long-term storage in the vehicle (over six months) is not recommended. If long-term storage is necessary, periodic testing of the diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) is recommended to ensure that the urea concentration does not fall out of specification. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

## Prerequisites

- Several steps are done with the engine stopped (filters, hub oil, crankcase drain, battery). Stop
  and secure the machine as in [[park-machine-safely]]; its battery/DEF-purge condition is the same
  one printed in step 21 below [[park-the-machine--a7f533|Park the Machine]].

## Steps

### Solution system and nozzles

1. Clean the machine thoroughly, inside and outside (See Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals,
   Including Pesticides) [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]],
   [[clean-vehicle-of-hazardous-chemicals-including-pesticides--37d899|Clean Vehicle of Hazardous Chemicals, Including Pesticides]].
2. Clean and flush the solution system (See Clean Solution System)
   [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]],
   [[clean-solution-system--d8c29a|Clean Solution System]].
3. Remove, clean, and install the fill strainer, pressure strainers, and flowmeters
   [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
4. For machines that are stored in cold temperatures, perform a winterizing procedure (See Prepare
   Machine for Cold-Weather Storage – Winterizing)
   [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]],
   [[prepare-machine-for-cold-weather-storage-winterizing--6354cc|Prepare Machine for Cold-Weather Storage – Winterizing]].
5. Remove and clean the nozzle tips and screens. Store the tips and screens off the machine in a dry
   place [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

> [!note] Only if your machine has ExactApply **and** carbon fiber booms
> NOTE: Plugs are only needed for sprayers equipped with ExactApply and carbon fiber booms. The two open turrets in the spray position on the nozzle body are the only turrets that require a plug. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]
>
> ![[N151449.png]]
>
> **Figure N151449** — A: Plug
>
> 6. Insert plugs (A) into the open turrets of nozzle bodies on the outer boom and breakaway
>    sections that will be upside-down during storage
>    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
>
> Machines without ExactApply and carbon fiber booms skip step 6.

7. Prepare the foam marker system for cold weather (See Prepare Foam Marker System for Cold-Weather
   Storage) [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

### Hydraulics, hubs and chassis

![[N155093.png]]

**Figure N155093** — A: Hydrostatic Filter; B: Hydraulic Filter (2 used)
[[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

8. Replace hydrostatic filter (A) and hydraulic filters (B)
   [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. The full filter-change
   procedure, including the start-up check, is on
   [[replace-hydrostatic-and-hydraulic-filters-1000-hours]].

![[N154938.png]]

**Figure N154938** — A: Planetary Hub
[[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

9. Drain the planetary hubs (A) (See Change Planetary Hub Oil in the Lubrication and Maintenance
   section) [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]; the drain steps
   and torques are on [[change-planetary-hub-oil-after-first-50-hours]].
10. Fill the planetary hub oil to the proper level
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
11. Lubricate all grease fittings. Apply grease to the exposed area of the hydraulic cylinder rods
    and chrome suspension rods [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
12. Paint as necessary to prevent rust
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
13. Treat exposed surfaces with Fluid Film (TY22032 or TY27529) for prevention of corrosion and
    rust [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
14. Lift each corner of the machine and support with a JDG10393 Jackstand or equivalent to take
    weight off of the tires. Do not deflate the tires
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. Instructions for lifting
    the machine can be found in Install Optional Tires in the Wheels and Tires section
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

### Engine

15. Wash the engine using a safe solvent — not gasoline (CAUTION above)
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
16. Remove, clean, and install the air cleaner elements
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]] — see the conflict below.
17. With the engine warm, drain the oil from the crankcase. Replace the oil filter and fill the
    crankcase to the proper level [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
18. Drain and flush the cooling system (engine block and radiator)
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

    > [!warning] NOTE
    > NOTE: (For warm-weather climates only.) If antifreeze has not been, and will not be, added in the engine, be sure to remove the plug to drain the engine oil cooler. Add clean soft water with John Deere Summer Engine Coolant Conditioner, or equivalent, in place of antifreeze. [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]

19. Fill the cooling system with antifreeze to the proper level. Use antifreeze that does not
    contain stop-leak additives [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
20. Operate the engine at a low idle for a few minutes to circulate the coolant and crankcase oil
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

### Battery and DEF

21. Disconnect the negative (-) battery cables and secure them away from the battery terminal
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. **Final Tier 4/Stage V
    engine equipped machines:** only after the LED indicator at the battery shutoff is off (IMPORTANT
    above) [[battery-disconnect-switch--c9c8e5|Battery Disconnect Switch]].
22. Diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) has a limited shelf life, but it may be stored in the vehicle for as
    long as six months depending upon storage conditions. If necessary, drain the DEF tank (See
    Drain and Flush Diesel Exhaust Fluid [DEF] Tank)
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]],
    [[drain-and-flush-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-tank--7579fe|Drain and Flush Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) Tank]].

### Seal and label

23. Seal the following openings with tape and/or plastic bags: exhaust pipe, air cleaner intake,
    engine dipstick tube, engine oil fill tube, crankcase breather tube
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
24. Leave a note in the cab detailing what was done for winter protection so that the next person to
    use the machine will know what kind of preparation is needed for field operation
    [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].

> [!conflict] How long DEF can stay in the tank
> The storage procedure says long-term storage in the vehicle (over six months) is not recommended,
> and that DEF may be stored in the vehicle for as long as six months depending upon storage
> conditions [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. The Storing Diesel
> Exhaust Fluid (DEF) topic — a generic Deere-wide module (`generic: true`) — says long-term storage
> in the DEF tank (over 12 months) is not recommended, and that under ideal conditions DEF is
> expected to remain useable for a minimum of 18 months
> [[storing-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def--18be20|Storing Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)]]. The machine-specific
> figure is six months, the generic one 12 months. This page does not choose; both say to test DEF if
> storage runs long.

> [!conflict] Clean the engine air cleaner elements, or never clean them
> Step 16 of the storage procedure says to "Remove, clean, and install the air cleaner elements"
> [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]]. The air intake service topic
> carries an IMPORTANT block that says "Do not attempt to clean filters"
> [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually--590bfa|Service Air Intake System—1000 Hours or Annually]].
> Both are machine-specific. This page does not choose — ask your dealer, and see
> [[service-air-intake-system-1000-hours-or-annually]].

## What the manual does not specify here

- A storage temperature below which the winterizing procedure is required; step 4 only says "cold
  temperatures" [[prepare-machine-for-storage--fd6f6c|Prepare Machine for Storage]].
- The reverse procedure is Remove Machine from Storage (p. 165-7), a separate topic.
