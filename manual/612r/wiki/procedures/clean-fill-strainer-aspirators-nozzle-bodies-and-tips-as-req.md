# Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required

This task is written for the base machine and applies to all models. It has two parts: cleaning the
fill strainer behind the side Quik-Fill valve, and cleaning aspirators, nozzle bodies and tips
[[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--f9977d|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]].
On the base machine the fill strainer is "Fill Strainer- 20 Mesh (Standard)"
[[solution-strainers--3295d0|Solution Strainers]].

> [!note] Where this is printed
> The manual prints this procedure twice under two module IDs: in the Wet System section
> (p. 55-23 to 55-24) and in Lubrication and Maintenance (p. 115-14 to 115-16)
> [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--f9977d|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]]
> [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--c01b81|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]].
> The numbered steps are identical; the caution wording differs (see the conflict below). Each step
> gives its page in both copies, in the order Wet System · Lubrication and Maintenance.

The product label and local regulations govern chemical handling; the manual tells you to treat all
spray chemicals, solutions, or solution residues with great caution
[[service-spray-equipment-safely--ce61b7|Service Spray Equipment Safely]].

## Prerequisites

1. Wear protective clothing, eyewear, and gloves
   [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--f9977d|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]].
2. Have a suitable container ready; every draining step drains into a container, never onto the
   ground
   [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--f9977d|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]].
3. Before cleaning aspirators, nozzle bodies or tips, relieve boom pressure — see
   [[relieve-spray-pressure]]
   [[clean-fill-strainer-aspirators-nozzle-bodies-and-tips-as-req--f9977d|Clean Fill Strainer, Aspirators, Nozzle Bodies, and Tips—As Required]] (p. 55-24 · 115-15).

## If your machine has ExactApply

> [!warning] IMPORTANT — strainers must stay installed (ExactApply)
> IMPORTANT: 50 and 80 mesh strainers are installed in the pressure plumbing of the machine. These strainers must remain installed during all applications to prevent plugging of spray tips and solenoid valves. [[exactapply-strainer-requirements--c2ea09|ExactApply Strainer Requirements]]

A minimum 20 mesh strainer is required in the machine fill lines when equipped with ExactApply;
machines come equipped with a 20 mesh strainer in the fill line from the side Fill Station
[[exactapply-strainer-requirements--c2ea09|ExactApply Strainer Requirements]]. If the machine is
equipped with front fill, a 20 mesh strainer must be used when filling; it can be installed on the
machine or located in the nurse tank fill line
[[exactapply-strainer-requirements--c2ea09|ExactApply Strainer Requirements]]. Reinstall the fill
strainer after cleaning — the fill-line strainer is required, not optional, on ExactApply machines [[exactapply-strainer-requirements--c2ea09|ExactApply Strainer Requirements]].

## If your machine has Individual Nozzle Control (INC) Pro

The same strainer rules are printed for INC Pro: 50 and 80 mesh strainers in the pressure plumbing
must remain installed during all applications, and a minimum 20 mesh strainer is required in the
machine fill lines
[[individual-nozzle-control-inc-pro-strainer-requirements--86bfb4|Individual Nozzle Control (INC) Pro Strainer Requirements]].
