# Field Operation

Starting to spray in the field, and stopping and restarting at row ends. This is a true
step-by-step procedure in the manual. The main steps are the base-machine topic in Operate the
Machine [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]. Machines with ExactApply have their own version
with a few differences — see [the conditional section](#if-your-machine-has-exactapply) at the end.

## Before you spray — label and weather

Always follow all label directions for effective, safe, and legal use of agricultural chemicals
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
The product label may set stricter limits than the manual. From the generic Deere-wide
chemical-sprayer safety module
[[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]]:

- Do not spray when winds exceed 16 km/h (10 mph)
  [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
- Do not spray when wind is blowing towards a nearby sensitive crop, garden, or populated area
  [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].
- Minimize risk of spray drift; use large nozzle tips operated at lower pressures
  [[service-and-operate-chemical-sprayers-safely--6b47f1|Service and Operate Chemical Sprayers Safely]].

For wind, drift and pressure settings, see the settings pages (wind and drift, droplet size and
pressure) and [[components/spray-tip]], which carries the conflict between the
generic 50 psi limit and the flat fan tip range.

## Warnings

> [!warning] CAUTION
> CAUTION: Do not turn on the machine until you are sure that nobody is in the danger zone. [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

> [!warning] NOTE
> NOTE: If no solution flow is detected, the solution pump will automatically shut off after a brief time to prevent overheating and damage to seals.
> [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

## Steps

1. Unfold the boom and press raise/lower switch (A) to position the boom at desired operating height
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

   ![[N155116.png]]

   **Figure N155116** — A: Raise/Lower Switch [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

2. Set up the display for your particular application (see the CommandCenter Display Applications
   section for proper setup procedure) [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].
3. Verify that the tank is loaded with solution [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].
4. Push solution pump switch (A) to engage the solution pump
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

   ![[N155117.png]]

   **Figure N155117** — A: Solution Pump Switch [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

5. View spray operation on the display [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].
6. Select rate control switch (A) programmed for desired application rate (1, 2, or 3) or the manual
   rate switch (B); the rate selected is highlighted on the display
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

   ![[N155118.png]]

   **Figure N155118** — A: Rate Control Switch; B: Manual Rate Switch
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

7. Push multi-function lever (A) slowly forward to start forward motion
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

   ![[N166439.png]]

   **Figure N166439** — A: Multi-Function Lever [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

8. Verify that desired boom sections are enabled (see Boom Sections | Custom in the Boom and Nozzles
   Application section); the status of each boom section is indicated on the display
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].
9. Press spray system on/off switch (A) to start spraying operation. Indicators (B and C) are
   illuminated when the spray system on/off switch is activated on the multi-function lever
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

   ![[N167055.png]]

   **Figure N167055** — A: Spray System On/Off Switch; B: Indicator; C: Indicator
   [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

   > [!warning] NOTE
   > NOTE: Spray system shutdown is not immediate. Solution flow will continue for a few seconds after pressing the spray system on/off switch. For best response time and coverage, turn off the spray system on/off switch while slowing for the row end.
   > [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

10. At the row end, press spray system on/off switch (A) to stop spraying. All other switches can
    remain in preset positions [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

    ![[N155121.png]]

    **Figure N155121** — A: Spray System On/Off Switch [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

    > [!warning] NOTE
    > NOTE: Spray system start-up is not immediate. To overcome delayed response, press the spray system on/off switch while moving forward to start spraying just before solution is needed.
    > [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]

11. When aligned with the next row, press the spray system on/off switch to resume spraying
    [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

## If your machine has ExactApply

The ExactApply section repeats the same eleven steps with these differences
[[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]:

- In step 6 the second choice is the **manual pressure setting** button (B) instead of the manual
  rate switch [[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]:

  ![[N127746.png]]

  **Figure N127746** — A: Rate Control Switch; B: Manual Pressure Setting Switch
  [[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]

  > [!warning] NOTE
  > NOTE: Rate selected is highlighted on the display. Only use manual pressure mode with fixed pulsing or pulsing turned off.
  > [[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]

- It adds a note on high pressure with low voltage:

  > [!warning] NOTE
  > NOTE: Plungers do not open properly when operating above 965.3 kPa (9.65 bar) (140 psi) when a low voltage condition occurs.
  > [[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]

- It does not repeat the base topic's notes on delayed spray shutdown and start-up
  [[field-operation--87b1c4|Field Operation]]; those notes come from the base topic
  [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]].

## What the manual does not specify here

- The operating boom height in step 1 ("desired operating height") is not a number in this topic
  [[field-operation--4b7bff|Field Operation]]; see [[components/boom-height]] and the tip guidance on
  [[components/spray-tip]].
- Which application rate to program is set by the product label and your tip chart, not by this
  topic.
